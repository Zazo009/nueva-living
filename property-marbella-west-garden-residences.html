The development

Marbella West Garden Residences brings together 139 homes around planted courtyards, pools and spaces for wellness, work and family life. It is a large, amenity-led project, but the architecture is arranged to keep outdoor living and natural light at the centre of each home.

The location is practical as well as attractive: the beach is described by the developer as a 10-minute walk, Puerto Banús is around five minutes by car, and San Pedro's everyday services are close by.

Architecture · Light-first architecture wrapped in Mediterranean planting

Huete Arquitectos has organised the buildings around landscaped spaces, with rounded terraces, integrated planters and a warm palette of stone, sand and wood tones. The layouts open living areas towards generous terraces and keep movement between indoors and outdoors simple.

The specification includes high-performance glazing, motorised bedroom shutters, ducted climate control, aerothermal hot water and an A-A energy rating. Selected corner homes and all Sky Villas add private pools.

  • Architecture by Huete Arquitectos and landscaping by Laura Pou Studio.
  • Large-format porcelain surfaces inside and on the terraces.
  • Thermal-break aluminium joinery and high-performance double glazing.
  • Private pools in Iconic Residences and Sky Villas.

Our view

What we would weigh before reserving here, and the checks we make on your behalf.

Why it stands out

  • Who it is forBuyers looking for a primary home, an easy second residence or a family base with wellness, social and work facilities on site.
  • What makes it differentThe amenity programme goes beyond a standard pool and gym, adding a full wellness hub, rooftop lounge, business space, cinema, kids' room and pet facilities.
  • Why location mattersThe setting connects a walkable beach lifestyle with quick access to Puerto Banús, San Pedro, Guadalmina and several leading golf courses.
  • Why check availabilityThe mix ranges from efficient 1-bedroom homes to duplex Sky Villas, so floor, orientation, terrace and release status make a meaningful difference.

What to plan for

  • Growing districtMarbella West is receiving new residential stock and infrastructure close to an established coastal lifestyle.
  • Broad buyer appealThe project ranges from compact homes to private-pool Sky Villas, which can serve several buyer profiles.
  • Long-stay usabilityWork, wellness, family and social facilities make the project relevant beyond short holiday visits.
  • Selection mattersThe value difference between homes is likely to come from orientation, privacy, outdoor space, views and position within the community.

Checks before any money moves

  • Project teamDeveloper entity: Residencial Puertalcantara S.L.; architecture by Huete Arquitectos; construction by Bonifacio Solis S.L.
  • Programme and licenceThe building licence is stated as granted. Construction is listed as started in Q1 2026, with estimated completion in Q2 2029.
  • Payment structureThe supplied terms state a EUR 6,600 reservation, 30% plus VAT on the private contract and 70% plus VAT on completion.
  • Buyer protectionThe material states that amounts paid are to be protected by a bank guarantee. Your lawyer should verify the guarantee and contract before funds are released.
