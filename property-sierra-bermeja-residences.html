The development

Sierra Bermeja Residences is a gated development of 36 apartments with one, two and three bedrooms, arranged so the blocks step down the slope and most homes look out over the sea and the mountains.

Every apartment comes with a garage space and a storeroom. Kitchens are fitted, hot water is produced by aerothermics and there is pre-installation for electric vehicle charging. Ground floor apartments have a private garden. The communal areas hold a swimming pool, gardens and a children's playground.

Architecture · Built into the slope

The blocks step down the hillside so that most homes keep an open outlook over the sea and the mountains.

  • Layout: Low-rise blocks arranged to follow the topography of the plot.
  • Outdoor space: Terraces on every home, from 8.46 sqm to 66.84 sqm, and private gardens on the ground floor.
  • Hot water: Produced by aerothermics.
  • Parking: One garage space and one storeroom per apartment, with EV charging pre-installation.
  • Kitchens: Supplied equipped.
  • Communal areas: Swimming pool, landscaped gardens and a children's playground.

On site

Casares is one of the Andalusian white villages, with more than two kilometres of beach below it and the Sierra Bermeja Nature Park behind.

Golf

Finca Cortesin is about six minutes away, with Sotogrande, Estepona and Marbella courses along the coast.

The village

Casares is one of the best known of the white villages, between the sea and the mountains.

Beaches and marinas

Sabinillas, La Galera, Bahia Dorada and Arroyo Vaquero beaches, with the marinas at Estepona and La Duquesa either side.
