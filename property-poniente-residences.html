Overview

Seventy-eight homes, 23 available

This is a low-density gated development of 78 one-, two-, three- and four-bedroom apartments in the west of Estepona. 23 homes are listed with a price: apartments on the ground, first and second floors. Ground-floor homes have a private garden, and the developer’s documents give the ground-floor and second-floor homes a private pool.

The curved terraces follow the movement of the coast, and the communal areas are set under green roofs among palms. The shared areas include an outdoor salt-water pool, a heated indoor pool, a spa with a sauna and a hammam, a gym and a coworking space. The developer’s listing says the building licence is available and construction starts in the fourth quarter of 2026, with the end of the build in the first quarter of 2029, subject to change.

Location

West Estepona, a short stroll to the beach

The development is in the west of Estepona, a short stroll from the beach and nearer still to local shops. The port and the old town are a few minutes by car or along the coastal footpath, and the neighbourhood has a supermarket, a new hospital, schools, an 18-hole golf course and a lagoon resort.

Quality specifications

What the homes are built with

The developer’s own quality specification, summarised by part of the home.

Doors and wardrobes
  • A reinforced entrance door with a three-point lock and a spyhole, white-lacquered interior doors with matt stainless-steel handles, and hinged white wardrobes fitted with shelves, drawers and rails.
  • A modular walk-in wardrobe with smoked-glass doors in the main bedroom.
Floors and bathrooms
  • Large-format 75 by 75 cm porcelain tiles or similar on the floors, with a choice of three colours for the bathroom walls, and top-quality sanitaryware, taps and shower screens.
Kitchen
  • Upper and lower units with soft-close fittings and integrated handles in a choice of three colours, with worktops in at least two colours.
  • Siemens appliances or equivalent: a ceramic hob, an extractor hood, a fridge, an oven, a microwave and a dishwasher.
Climate and energy
  • Ducted air conditioning with zoned temperature control, hot water from a high-efficiency aerothermal system and electric underfloor heating in the bathrooms.
  • Underfloor heating throughout the home is offered as an optional upgrade.
Technology
  • A video intercom to the main entrance, LED track lighting in the living room, kitchen and main bedroom, and a basic home-automation package with light control, a web server and air-conditioning control.
Private outdoor areas
  • Ground-floor gardens come with a private swimming pool and an outdoor shower, and second-floor solariums with a private swimming pool and an outdoor shower.
  • An outdoor bar or kitchen is listed as an upgrade.
Upgrades
  • Listed as upgrades: the slatted kitchen design shown in the images, a fully integrated induction hob, Miele appliances, and further home-automation functions.
Insurance and oversight
  • A ten-year structural warranty from an accredited insurer, continuous supervision by an accredited technical control body, and laboratory testing of materials.

Architecture

Curved terraces, green roofs

The apartments are sculptural but restrained, with curved terraces that echo the movement of the coast. The buildings are oriented for light and privacy, and the communal zones are grounded in nature, with green roofs, layered planting and the shade of palms.

Ground-floor corner homes have wrap-around gardens, and the second-floor homes have solariums with sea views.

  • Structure — Reinforced concrete designed to the Technical Building Code, on foundations set by the geotechnical study.
  • Facade — Ceramic brick with a continuous mortar render in varied textures and tones, and a ventilated cavity with thermal and acoustic insulation.
  • Glazing — Thermally broken aluminium or PVC frames with double glazing, and terrace balustrades in laminated safety glass.
  • Roofs — Roofs designed for their use and always waterproof, with non-slip paving on the walkable areas.

FAQ

Common buyer questions

Can foreigners buy property in Spain?

Yes. There are no restrictions on non-Spanish nationals buying property in Spain, whether as a resident or non-resident.

What is an NIE number and do I need one?

An NIE (Numero de Identificacion de Extranjero) is a tax ID number required for any property purchase in Spain by a non-Spanish national. Nueva Living can guide you through obtaining one before you reserve.

What costs should I budget for on top of the purchase price?

Buyers typically budget for transfer tax or VAT, notary fees, land registry fees and legal fees on top of the purchase price. Nueva Living provides a full, current cost breakdown for your chosen residence before you reserve.

Can I get a mortgage in Spain as a non-resident?

Many Spanish banks offer mortgages to non-resident buyers, typically financing a portion of the purchase price. Exact terms depend on the bank and your personal financial profile.

What is the difference between off-plan and completed properties?

Off-plan means the development is still under construction and is usually sold with staged payments through to completion. A completed property is ready to view and move into now.

How does the reservation and payment process work?

Reservation and payment structures vary by development and are set out in full before you reserve. Nueva Living reconfirms the current schedule for your chosen residence at every step.

Can I rent out the property after purchase?

This depends on the individual development and local regulations, which can vary by community and municipality. Nueva Living will confirm the specific rules for a development before you reserve.

Do I need a lawyer?

Yes, we strongly recommend independent legal representation for any property purchase in Spain. Nueva Living can put you in touch with independent lawyers experienced in Costa del Sol property.

Is VAT included?

What comes with the price?

Every home comes with one car space and one storage room in the sales price. Furniture and equipment shown in the images are not included.

When will it be finished?
