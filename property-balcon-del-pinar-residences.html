Overview

16 homes on the price list

The developer’s brochure presents a building of two- and three-bedroom homes on a raised site in Torremolinos, its shared areas turned towards the pine wood opposite, with large terraces and views over the town and the coast towards Malaga.

The price list of 1 November 2026 names 16 homes in six portals: six with two bedrooms, nine with three and one three-bedroom penthouse. Each comes with a parking space, two for three of them, and a storage room. No document we hold gives the number of homes in the building, a licence, the stage of the works or a completion date.

Quality specifications

What the homes are built with

The developer’s own quality specification, summarised by part of the home.

Floors and walls
  • Interior flooring in large-format SPC of a premium range (1800 x 230 mm), a water-resistant material, and porcelain tiles in the bathrooms and on the terraces.
  • Smooth paint on the interior walls, and large-format ceramic tiles on the wet walls of the bathrooms.
Kitchen
  • A fitted kitchen with wall and base units in a blend of wood, light tones and durable worktop materials, in one of two design options, with an island or a peninsula as an option where the layout allows.
  • Premium-brand appliances: an induction hob, an extractor hood, an electric oven, an integrated refrigerator, an integrated dishwasher, a washing machine and a microwave oven.
Bathrooms
  • Designer toilets with a dual-flush system, low-profile shower trays, a shower screen in the main bathroom, and a vanity unit with drawers, a countertop washbasin and a designer mirror.
  • Single-lever taps on the washbasins and thermostatic taps in the showers.
Windows, doors and wardrobes
  • Thermally broken aluminium frames with double glazing, blinds in the living room and bedrooms, and glass balustrades fixed with a profile.
  • A reinforced security door at each home, lacquered interior doors with designer handles, matching skirting boards and built-in wardrobes lined throughout, with a hanging rail and storage.
Climate and installations
  • An energy-efficient air-source heat pump for hot water and for heating and cooling, ducted through the suspended ceiling.
  • A telecommunications connection in every room except the bathrooms, and electrical fittings with designer mechanisms.
Communal areas
  • An outdoor communal pool with saltwater chlorination, a garden and a sun terrace, and an indoor spa with a heated pool, a sauna and a shower circuit, connected to the gym and the changing rooms.
  • A fully equipped gym, a coworking space with an office kitchen and restrooms for all residents, landscaped gardens with Mediterranean planting and a motorised garage door with remote control.

Source: the developer’s quality specification, version of 22 December 2025. It says it is for guidance only, that the developer may make changes for technical, commercial, legal, material-availability or design reasons, and that any change will use materials of comparable quality.

Architecture

Layered terraces, a pine wood opposite

The brochure says the architects used the raised position of the site to build the homes as a succession of horizontal layers, each one a viewing platform, with the communal areas facing the pine wood opposite. The large terraces act as sunshades: more protection from the sun in summer and more light in winter.

In the developer’s computer-generated images the building has long, curved terrace slabs, timber soffits, glass balustrades and a pergola on the top floor, set on a stone base above the pool. The price list numbers the homes by portal, 1 to 6, from the ground floor to the fourth floor, with a penthouse above.

FAQ

Common buyer questions

Can foreigners buy property in Spain?

Yes. There are no restrictions on non-Spanish nationals buying property in Spain, whether as a resident or non-resident.

What is an NIE number and do I need one?

An NIE (Numero de Identificacion de Extranjero) is a tax ID number required for any property purchase in Spain by a non-Spanish national. Nueva Living can guide you through obtaining one before you reserve.

What costs should I budget for on top of the purchase price?

Buyers typically budget for transfer tax or VAT, notary fees, land registry fees and legal fees on top of the purchase price. Nueva Living provides a full, current cost breakdown for your chosen residence before you reserve.

Can I get a mortgage in Spain as a non-resident?

Many Spanish banks offer mortgages to non-resident buyers, typically financing a portion of the purchase price. Exact terms depend on the bank and your personal financial profile.

What is the difference between off-plan and completed properties?

Off-plan means the development is still under construction and is usually sold with staged payments through to completion. A completed property is ready to view and move into now.

How does the reservation and payment process work?

Reservation and payment structures vary by development and are set out in full before you reserve. Nueva Living reconfirms the current schedule for your chosen residence at every step.

Can I rent out the property after purchase?

This depends on the individual development and local regulations, which can vary by community and municipality. Nueva Living will confirm the specific rules for a development before you reserve.

Do I need a lawyer?

Yes, we strongly recommend independent legal representation for any property purchase in Spain. Nueva Living can put you in touch with independent lawyers experienced in Costa del Sol property.

16 are on the developer’s price list of 1 November 2026: six two-bedroom apartments from EUR 425,000 to EUR 485,000, nine three-bedroom apartments from EUR 493,000 to EUR 601,000 and a three-bedroom penthouse at EUR 802,000.

Are parking and storage included?

Yes, the price list includes a parking space and a storage room in every price, and two parking spaces for 1-3-C, 5-3-C and 2-AT-B. We confirm them in writing for the home you choose.

Is furniture included?

No. The brochure says furniture is shown only to illustrate the use of the spaces and is not included. The kitchens come with the appliances listed in the quality specification.

Can the prices change?
