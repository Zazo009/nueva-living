Nueva Living in your assistant Search Costa del Sol new builds inside ChatGPT and Claude

Describe what you want in your own words. The assistant answers with up to six developments from our own project pages, with photos, list prices, delivery dates and a map, then narrows the search with you.

How It Works

Ask in your own words, get real developments back

Add Nueva Living to ChatGPT or Claude once. After that, describe the home you want in an ordinary message and the assistant searches the developments on this site for you.

Free, No Login

Nothing to sign up for and nothing to pay. You add one address and start asking.

Ten Languages

Ask in English, Spanish, French, German, Russian, Arabic, Dutch, Polish, Swedish or Norwegian. Names, prices and dates come back in your language.

The Same Figures as Our Pages

Every answer comes from our own project pages. Prices are the developer’s list prices before VAT and purchase costs, reconfirmed before any reservation. Images are mostly renders.

The Address

One address for every assistant

https://nuevaliving.com/mcp

No login, streamable HTTP. Any assistant that accepts a remote MCP server can use it.

How to Connect: Claude

Add it to Claude in four steps

1

Open Connectors

Go to claude.ai/customize/connectors, or open Customize and then Connectors.

2

Add a Custom Connector

Press + Add, then Add custom connector.

3

Paste the Address

Name it Nueva Living, paste the address above, choose no sign-in and press Add.

4

Switch It On and Ask

In a chat, press +, open Connectors, switch Nueva Living on and ask.

It works on every Claude plan. The free plan allows one custom connector. On Team and Enterprise plans, an owner adds it for the whole organisation.

How to Connect: ChatGPT

Add it to ChatGPT in two steps

1

Add the Server

Open chatgpt.com/plugins, press +, choose Add custom MCP server and paste the address above.

2

Ask by Name

Start your message with “Nueva Living”, then say what you are looking for.

Try Asking

Three ways to begin

“Nueva Living, a villa in Estepona under €2 million with a private pool and golf nearby.”

“Nueva Living, key-ready two-bedroom apartments in Mijas with sea views.”

“Nueva Living, what is being built in Nueva Andalucía right now?”

What It Can Do

What it searches, and what it does not

New Builds Only

The new-build and off-plan developments on nuevaliving.com, in Marbella, Nueva Andalucía, San Pedro de Alcántara, Benahavís, Estepona, Casares, Mijas and Fuengirola, Benalmádena and Torremolinos. No resale homes and no rentals.

Six at a Time

Up to six developments per search, with photos, list prices, delivery dates and a map. The assistant then narrows the search with you by area, budget, type, stage or a feature.

One Project in Full

Ask about one development and it opens the homes on the price list with bedrooms, size and price, the note on what the prices include, the images and an approximate map position.

Your Details

Nothing about you until you ask

A search sends us only the criteria the assistant passes on, such as area, budget and type. It does not include your name or the rest of your conversation, and we do not record who searched.

If you ask to be contacted, the assistant sends your name and e-mail, and your phone number and message if you give them, to a Nueva Living advisor. It does so only after you agree, and we handle those details under our privacy policy.

Common Questions

Before you connect

Is it free, and do I need an account?

It is free, and you need no account with Nueva Living. Whether you can add a custom connector depends on your assistant and your plan with it.

Which assistants can use it?

Claude and ChatGPT, as described above. Any other assistant that can add a remote MCP server by its address can use the same address.

What can I ask?

Whatever you would tell an advisor about the home you want: the area, your budget, the number of bedrooms, a villa or an apartment, key-ready or off-plan, sea views, golf or a private pool. You can also ask about one development in detail.

How many developments does it show?

Up to six per search. When more fit, the assistant suggests how to narrow the search, by area, budget or type, and searches again with you.

Are the prices current?

They are the list prices shown on each project page, with the date each was last checked. They are the developer’s prices before VAT and purchase costs, and we reconfirm price and availability before any reservation.

Does it make anything up?

The connector returns only what is on our project pages, and the assistant is instructed not to invent developments, homes, prices or dates. An assistant can still make a mistake, so check anything important on the project page or with us.

Is my data shared?

Only if you ask to be contacted. Then your name and e-mail, and your phone number and message if you give them, go to a Nueva Living advisor once you agree. Our privacy policy explains how we handle them.

How do I talk to a person?

Ask the assistant to put you in touch, or write to us through our contact page. An advisor normally replies within one working day.
