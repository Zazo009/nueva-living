Overview

Six homes, listed with prices

Cristalino Residences is a development of apartments at Las Lagunas, in the municipality of Mijas next to Fuengirola. The developer’s infosheet describes apartments with one, two and three bedrooms, while the availability table lists six three-bedroom homes of 131.09 to 150.20 sqm at EUR 470,900 to EUR 770,000. In the renders the terraces sit behind blue glass balustrades, and the shared areas listed are a swimming pool, a garden area, a gym, a sauna and a children’s playground.

The infosheet gives the building licence as granted and the status as under construction, in two blocks. Its two sets of dates do not agree: the banner gives handover of Block 1 in the first quarter of 2026 and of Block 2 in the fourth quarter of 2026, while the list gives the end of construction of Block 1 in the fourth quarter of 2025 and of Block 2 in the second quarter of 2027. The sheet does not say which block each listed home is in, or how many homes the development has.

Quality specifications

What the homes are built with

The developer’s own quality specification, summarised by part of the home.

Floors and walls
  • Laminate wood flooring over acoustic insulation in the living areas, porcelain stoneware tiles in the bathrooms, and exterior stoneware on the terraces and private sun decks.
  • Bathroom walls tiled in stoneware, ivory in the main bathroom and white in the secondary ones, and light plastic paint on the other walls and white on the ceilings.
Doors and wardrobes
  • An entrance door with a security lock, anti-lever hinges and a peephole, in enamelled sheet steel with a steel frame on the ground floor, and interior doors with chrome fittings and translucent glass to the hall and living room.
  • Modular wardrobes with hinged doors finished to match the other joinery.
Kitchen
  • A furnished kitchen with a fridge, washing machine, dishwasher, oven, ceramic hob and extractor hood by Bosch or similar, and an undercounter sink by Franke or similar.
Bathrooms
  • Grohe taps and a dual-flow Grohe shower set or similar, Laufen Pro sanitaryware or similar with pedestal or wall-hung basins, and resin shower trays.
Climate and energy
  • Heat-pump air conditioning, a multi-split system with units in the living room and bedrooms and the outdoor unit on the roof or on the ground floor, and hot water from an aerothermal heat pump.
  • Electric underfloor heating in the bathrooms with its own control. The building is designed for an A energy rating, with photovoltaic panels supporting the communal electricity.
Electrics and entry
  • Niessen or similar switches and an electronic door entry system, with the garage prepared for electric-vehicle charging points.
Lift, garage and storage
  • A lift with automatic doors for six people (450 kg), stopping at every floor of homes and at the basement, and a basement garage reached by a ramp with a remote-controlled motorised door.
  • Garage and storage-room floors in smoothed concrete.

Source: the developer’s quality specification for this development, in Spanish and English, which carries no date or version. It says materials may be replaced by others of equal or higher quality without changing the price, and that natural stone varies in tone and veining. The two languages differ in places: the English text gives the glazing as 6-12-16 and the Spanish text as 6-12-6, and the Spanish text adds a thermal break and the option of PVC frames. We follow the Spanish text. The specification gives the flat roofs as reached only for maintenance, while one render shows a furnished roof terrace; we ask which homes have one.

FAQ

Common buyer questions

Can foreigners buy property in Spain?

Yes. There are no restrictions on non-Spanish nationals buying property in Spain, whether as a resident or non-resident.

What is an NIE number and do I need one?

An NIE (Numero de Identificacion de Extranjero) is a tax ID number required for any property purchase in Spain by a non-Spanish national. Nueva Living can guide you through obtaining one before you reserve.

What costs should I budget for on top of the purchase price?

Buyers typically budget for transfer tax or VAT, notary fees, land registry fees and legal fees on top of the purchase price. Nueva Living provides a full, current cost breakdown for your chosen residence before you reserve.

Can I get a mortgage in Spain as a non-resident?

Many Spanish banks offer mortgages to non-resident buyers, typically financing a portion of the purchase price. Exact terms depend on the bank and your personal financial profile.

What is the difference between off-plan and completed properties?

Off-plan means the development is still under construction and is usually sold with staged payments through to completion. A completed property is ready to view and move into now.

How does the reservation and payment process work?

Reservation and payment structures vary by development and are set out in full before you reserve. Nueva Living reconfirms the current schedule for your chosen residence at every step.

Can I rent out the property after purchase?

This depends on the individual development and local regulations, which can vary by community and municipality. Nueva Living will confirm the specific rules for a development before you reserve.

Do I need a lawyer?

Yes, we strongly recommend independent legal representation for any property purchase in Spain. Nueva Living can put you in touch with independent lawyers experienced in Costa del Sol property.
