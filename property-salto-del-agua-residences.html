The development

Salto del Agua Residences is a single building of thirty-five apartments in the centre of San Pedro Alcántara, with the garages in the basement and commercial premises at street level. Fifteen of the thirty-five are still available, and the structure is already complete.

Every one of the fifteen has three bedrooms and two bathrooms, and they run from 123.34 to 253.94 sqm built. The two largest are duplexes on the top floor, each with a solarium of about 96 sqm looking over San Pedro, Nueva Andalucía and the sea, where the developer has planned a private saltwater pool.

A parking space and a storage room come with each apartment, as do underfloor heating and an aerothermal system. There is no communal pool and no gym in this building, and the community fee is EUR 90 a month.

Architecture · One building, white terraces

A single building on a town street, its white floors stepping back so each apartment gets a terrace, with commercial premises at street level and the garages below. The facade is finished in single-layer mortar with large-format ceramic panels in a wood finish on some elevations.

  • The building: Thirty-five apartments over four residential floors, with the garages and storage rooms in the basement.
  • Inside: Rectified porcelain stoneware floors throughout, plasterboard false ceilings, and smooth white paint.
  • The kitchens: German-made units with a Techlam worktop and Siemens appliances, including a built-in washer-dryer.
  • Climate: Ducted air conditioning and underfloor heating from an aerothermal system, zoned room by room.
  • Insulation: Mineral wool linings, brick party walls and laminated double glazing in aluminium with a thermal break.
  • The top floor: Duplexes with a solarium of about 96 sqm, where a private saltwater pool is planned.

On site

Salto del Agua sits in San Pedro Alcántara, a town with its own centre and everyday life, between Estepona and Marbella and a few minutes from Puerto Banús.

Day to day

The shops, schools, health centre and market of a working town are in the streets around the building.

The beach

San Pedro's beach and its promenade are about five minutes down through the town by car; the town centre itself is a twelve-minute walk.

Getting around

The A-7 runs past the town, which puts Puerto Banús seven minutes away and the Guadalmina and La Quinta courses five and seven.

Questions about Salto del Agua

How big are the homes?

From 123.34 to 253.94 sqm built.

How many bedrooms do they have?

All fifteen remaining apartments have three bedrooms and two bathrooms. Every two-bedroom home has been sold.

Is parking included?

Yes. A parking space and a storage room in the basement come with each apartment.

Is there a pool or a gym?

Not for the building as a whole. The top-floor duplexes have a solarium where a private saltwater pool is planned.

What are the running costs?

The community fee is €90 a month. The annual council tax and the refuse charge are roughly €400 and €200.
