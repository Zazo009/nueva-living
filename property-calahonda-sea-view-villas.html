The development

Calahonda Sea View Villas is a gated row of seven detached houses on the Mijas coast, each on its own plot with a private pool, two parking spaces and a solarium above the first floor.

The villas run from 627.44 sqm built on a 496.35 sqm plot to 729.50 sqm on 554.28 sqm, with 300.65 to 342.83 sqm of terraces. Four to six bedrooms depending on the house, and the lower level of every villa holds a multipurpose room of around 60 sqm that the renders show fitted out as a cinema or a gym. Five of the seven are released; the building licence has been granted and completion is set for Q1 2028.

Architecture · Built to a stated specification

Designed by Huete Arquitectos. The developer publishes a quality report that names its fittings brand by brand; the points below are taken from it, not from marketing copy.

  • Facade: Origen CACO3 Revosec projectable white mortar in a 20 mm layer, VOC-free, finished in CACO3 Easy to Clean paint.
  • Glazing: Reynaers CS77 and SP68 systems, with triple-laminated glass to the railings and garage door.
  • Flooring: Saloni Petralava Marfil 90x90 cm porcelain inside, the same in a non-slip finish outside, with Portuguese cobblestone and San Blas Alpujarra Marfil to the driveway.
  • Bathrooms: Roca The Gap Square sanitaryware, custom Roca Dash SURFEX washbasins, Ultra-flat STONEX shower trays and Cisal Xion and Zen mixers.
  • Interior joinery: Eclisse Syntesis Line flush doors with Olivari Loto handles, and Dicar 778S skirting.
  • Lighting and switches: LedsC4 and Artemide fittings inside and out, with Jung LS990 switches and sockets.

On site

Calahonda is a coastal stretch of Mijas between Marbella and Fuengirola, with its own beaches, shops and golf a few minutes inland.

The beach

The coast road runs below the plots, with beaches and beach restaurants along it.

Getting around

The A-7 passes alongside and the AP-7 toll road above, putting Marbella and Fuengirola within a short drive.

Calahonda has its own supermarkets, restaurants and golf, so daily life does not depend on driving into Marbella.

Questions about Calahonda

What do the villas cost?

Five of the seven are priced, from €1,650,000 to €1,900,000, excluding VAT. Villas 1 and 3 carry no price in the current list.

Does it have a building licence?

Yes. The developer states the building licence has been granted. Nueva Living confirms it in writing before any money moves.

How big are the villas?

From 627.44 sqm built on a 496.35 sqm plot to 729.50 sqm on 554.28 sqm, with 300.65 to 342.83 sqm of terraces.

What is on the lower level?

A multipurpose room of around 60 sqm, a bedroom and a bathroom, a plant room and a sunken patio. The renders show the room fitted out as a cinema or a gym; we confirm in writing what is delivered.

How many bathrooms are there?

The price list gives four for villas 4 to 7 and five for villas 1 to 3. The drawings count the lower-level bathroom as well, which is where the difference comes from.
