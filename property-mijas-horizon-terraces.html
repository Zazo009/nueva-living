The development

Mijas Horizon Terraces is a development of 142 apartments and penthouses with two and three bedrooms in Mijas Costa. The brochure describes sea views even from the ground floors, a central swimming pool with a chill-out area, a gym, a spa area, a social club and a coworking space, set in landscaped gardens.

The price list names 19 of the 142 homes. Six are priced and available, from a two-bedroom home at EUR 320,000 to a two-bedroom penthouse at EUR 480,000, all before VAT, and the other 13 are marked reserved. The payment terms put the title deed in the first quarter of 2029.

Architecture · Terraced blocks, terraces open to the view

In the renders the blocks are long and low, with a deep terrace on each floor, glass balustrades and sand-coloured walls above the communal pool. The brochure promises sea views even from the ground floors, and says that all the homes face south or south-west.

The plans show the ground-floor homes opening to a terrace or a front garden, and the penthouse with a single uncovered terrace across the whole width of the home.

  • Structure: A reinforced concrete foundation, designed to the geotechnical study of the land, and a reinforced concrete structure above it.
  • Facade: Perforated brick, projected polyurethane insulation, an air cavity and a plasterboard lining with mineral wool.
  • Glazing: PVC windows with double glazing and an insulating chamber, safety laminated glass on the terrace doors and manual roller blinds with thermal insulation in the bedrooms.
  • Terraces: Anti-slip, frost-resistant tiles on the floors, with a tap, a TV connection, electrical outlets and lighting on the terrace.

  • Interior doors in white-lacquered wood with chromed handles, and modular wardrobes with an upper shelf and a hanging bar, with doors in the same colour as the interior doors.

Our assessment

What we would weigh before reserving here, and what we check for you.

Why it stands out

  • Who it is forBuyers who want a new two- or three-bedroom apartment or penthouse in Mijas Costa with a terrace or a garden, to live in or as a second home.
  • What makes it differentThe six homes on the list are two ground-floor homes, three apartments on the first and second floors and one penthouse. One ground-floor home has a front garden of 49.54 sqm, and the penthouse has an uncovered terrace of 36.96 sqm. Each is listed with a garage space and a storage room.
  • Why location mattersThe brochure puts the development in Mijas Costa, 40 km from Marbella and 41 km from Málaga, and says that a large park with a navigable lake, a cycle path and an open-air amphitheatre is being built 800 m away.
  • Why check availabilityThe price list names 19 of the 142 homes, prices are before VAT, and the payment terms tie the private contract to obtaining the licence, whose status no document gives. We confirm the licence, the delivery date and the current list before any reservation.

What to plan for

  • VAT and taxesThe price list says that VAT is not included, and the reservation is given as EUR 6,000 plus VAT. At 10 per cent VAT and 1.20 per cent stamp duty, the EUR 320,000 home costs about EUR 356,000 and the EUR 480,000 penthouse about EUR 534,000, before notary and land registry fees.
  • Payment termsEUR 6,000 plus VAT on reservation; 15 per cent plus VAT at the private contract, less the reservation; 15 per cent plus VAT in three payments on 5 March 2027, 5 September 2027 and 5 March 2028; and 70 per cent plus VAT at the title deed.
  • Parking and storageThe price list gives a garage space and a storage room by number for each home, and the brochure speaks of parking and a storage room. We confirm that both are included in the price.
  • Dated instalmentsThe three instalments of 15 per cent have fixed dates, and the private contract before them is due on obtaining the licence. We ask what happens to the dates if the licence is late, and how money paid before completion is guaranteed.

Checks before any money moves

  • The homes listedThe price list names 19 of the 142 homes: six with a price and 13 marked reserved. It says nothing about the other 123, and it prints no date; the date on this page is the one recorded in the file. We ask for the complete list and the status of each home.
  • The licence and the delivery dateThe payment terms put the private contract at the grant of the licence and the title deed in the first quarter of 2029. No document gives the licence status or a start of works. We ask for the licence, the start of the works and the contractual delivery date.
  • The orientationThe brochure says that all the homes face south or south-west. The price list gives west for 13 of the 19 homes, including all six that are available, and south-east for the other six. We confirm the orientation of the home you choose from the plan and on site.

The plans add a gross area including a share of the common areas and the areas under the Andalusian decree 218/2005.   • The poolsThe brochure describes a central swimming pool with a chill-out area, and the specification lists salt-chlorinated splash pools. We ask for the number, the size and the type of the pools.
  • The images and the plansThe images are computer-generated and, the brochure says, based on an initial project that may change during the works. The plans say the same. The furniture, decoration and planting shown are not part of the offer.

Questions about Mijas Horizon Terraces

How many homes are there?

The brochure describes 142 apartments and penthouses with two and three bedrooms. The price list names 19 of them: six are available, from EUR 320,000 to EUR 480,000 before VAT, and 13 are marked reserved.

Is VAT included?

No. The price list says that VAT is not included. VAT at 10 per cent and stamp duty at 1.20 per cent are added, together with notary and land registry fees.

Are a garage and a storage room included?

The price list gives a garage space and a storage room by number for each home, and the brochure speaks of parking and a storage room. It does not say that they are included in the price, so we confirm that.

When will it be finished?

The payment terms put the title deed, with completion and delivery of keys, in the first quarter of 2029. No document gives a licence status or a start of works, so we ask for the licence and the contractual date.

What is the payment schedule?

EUR 6,000 plus VAT on reservation; 15 per cent plus VAT at the private contract, due on obtaining the licence, less the reservation; 15 per cent plus VAT in three payments on 5 March 2027, 5 September 2027 and 5 March 2028; and 70 per cent plus VAT at the title deed.
