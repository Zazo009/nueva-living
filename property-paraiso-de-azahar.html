The development

This is a residential building of 10 homes with one, two and three bedrooms, on the ground, first and second floors and a top floor, in the centre of Estepona, next to the old town. Six homes are listed with a price: a one-bedroom and a three-bedroom home on the ground floor, and four on the first floor, one of them with a patio and a private pool. Each has a garage space and a storage room.

The building has a lift and a car lift down to the garage. The four other homes, on the second floor and the top floor, are marked reserved on the developer’s price list. The developer’s documents give no delivery date.

Architecture · White walls, clay roofs and green shutters

The developer’s dossier describes a building that combines classical elements with contemporary design, with generous rooms, on a ground floor, a first and a second floor and a top floor.

In the images the building has white walls, clay-tile roofs, balconies with dark railings and sage-green shutters, and the upper homes have terraces with private pools set between the roofs.

  • Facade: Half-brick ceramic masonry, rendered, with a backing wall of double hollow brick and insulation sprayed into the cavity, and a lime-based mortar finish between the windows.
  • Glazing: Aluminium frames with a wood-effect finish and a thermal break, with double glazing and an air chamber.
  • Lift and garage: A lift for four people that stops at the garage, and a car lift at the garage entrance.
  • Energy: Aerothermal hot water in every home and, according to the dossier, a solar energy system and a high energy rating.

Residences · Six homes, four kinds

The six homes fall into four groups by floor, size and outdoor space. The table below lists each one with its price.

Specifications

The developer’s own specification, summarised by part of the home.

  • Rendered half-brick ceramic masonry on the facades, backed by double hollow brick with insulation sprayed into the cavity, and a lime-based mortar finish between the windows.
  • Partitions inside the home in plasterboard with thermal and acoustic insulation, and between homes in ceramic brick with sprayed insulation.
  • Aluminium windows with a wood-effect finish and a thermal break, in a colour the project management chooses, with double glazing and an air chamber.
  • An armoured front door panelled on the inside to match the interior doors, with a security lock and anti-lever bolts, smooth interior doors in solid wood or similar, and built-in modular wardrobes lacquered white and lined with melamine, with a luggage shelf and a hanging rail.
  • First-quality wood flooring inside the homes, non-slip porcelain stoneware on the terraces and first-quality porcelain tiling in the bathrooms.
  • Plasterboard false ceilings in the kitchen, the main bathrooms and the service areas, plaster and paint on the other ceilings, and a timber coffering on the living-room and main-bedroom ceilings.
  • A fitted kitchen with upper and lower units, a worktop in natural quartz or similar, and an island with a pull-out table that sits in several positions.
  • An oven, an induction hob, an extractor hood, a washing machine, a dishwasher and a fridge, all from premium brands, are included.
  • White glazed porcelain sanitaryware, a low-profile acrylic-resin shower tray and a tempered-glass shower screen.
  • Single-lever and thermostatic taps, in a finish the project management chooses.
  • An aerothermal system that helps to heat the water, and air conditioning by fan-coils in the bathroom false ceilings, with fibreglass ducts to each room and a programmable thermostat in the living room.
  • Pre-installation for electric-car charging in the garage, TV points in the living room and the main bedroom, a light point and a socket on the terraces, and a video intercom.
  • Fibre and polyester pools, with a size that the project management sets for each home, clad in natural stone on the outside.
  • An outdoor shower in the homes with a pool.
  • A lift for four people with automatic doors that stops at the garage, an automatic entrance gate with the safety systems the regulations require, and a car lift at the garage entrance.
  • Glass balustrades.

Source: the specification in the developer’s dossier, which is undated. It says that the materials may be changed by the project management if their quality is not reduced, that the images are illustrative and that the furniture shown is not included. For the interior flooring the Spanish text says “tarima de madera de 1.ª calidad” (first-quality timber flooring) and the English text of the same specification says “wood laminate flooring”; we ask which is used.

Location

The dossier places the building in the centre of Estepona, next to the old town, among shops, supermarkets, schools and sports facilities. Its map gives the marina as 3 minutes away, Punta Plata beach as 9 and Padrón beach as 10, without saying whether those are minutes on foot or by car. It also names the A-7 and the AP-7, which leads to Málaga airport.

Questions about Paraíso de Azahar

How many homes are available?

The building has 10 homes and six are currently listed with a price: one one-bedroom home, three two-bedroom homes and two three-bedroom homes, from EUR 495,000 to EUR 895,000 plus VAT. The other four are marked reserved.

Is VAT included?

No. The price list states that the prices exclude IVA and gives the rate as 10%. The prices on this page are the developer’s net figures, and IVA is not added to them.

Is there parking and storage?

Yes. The price list gives each of the six listed homes a garage space and a storage room, and the garage is reached by a car lift. The specification includes pre-installation for electric-car charging.

Does any home have a private pool?

Of the six listed homes, 1ºA has a private pool and a patio of 38.56 sqm. The four reserved homes on the upper floors are listed with pools too, and are not available.

When will it be finished?

None of the documents we hold gives a delivery date or the stage of the building. We ask for the contractual date and put it in writing.
