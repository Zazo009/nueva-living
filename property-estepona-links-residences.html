Overview

10 homes available, in two blocks

Estepona Links Residences is a development of 111 one, two and three-bedroom homes in Estepona Golf, which the developer’s dossier describes as an enclave where nature, tranquillity and lifestyle come together around the green. It describes contemporary architecture inspired by the Mediterranean, open views over the golf course and communal areas designed to be used all year.

The price list of 3 November 2026 covers 35 homes in two blocks and marks 25 of them as reserved. It lists ten with a price: one one-bedroom, eight two-bedroom and one three-bedroom apartment. None of the documents gives a completion date.

Location

Estepona Golf, Estepona

The dossier places the development inside Estepona Golf, in the municipality of Estepona. It puts Marbella 25 minutes away and Málaga airport under an hour, and says that Puerto Banús, Sotogrande and several white villages inland are a few minutes away. Its location map names Finca Cortesín and Valle Romano Golf Club, a new hospital, a shopping area, the beaches of Bahía Dorada and La Rada, the port, the town centre and a coastal path, without distances. The documents give no street address and no coordinate for the plot, so the map marker is at Estepona Golf and not on the plot.

Architecture

Contemporary design, on the course

The dossier describes contemporary architecture inspired by the Mediterranean, with open views over the golf course. In the developer’s computer-generated images the blocks are long and low, with deep balconies, glass balustrades and a lawn along their front.

On the plans of the ten listed homes the living, dining and kitchen areas are one open room that opens to a terrace, with a main bedroom and a second bedroom, each with a bathroom, and a third bedroom in the three-bedroom home. The dossier says the terraces face south-east and east.

FAQ

Common buyer questions

Can foreigners buy property in Spain?

Yes. There are no restrictions on non-Spanish nationals buying property in Spain, whether as a resident or non-resident.

What is an NIE number and do I need one?

An NIE (Numero de Identificacion de Extranjero) is a tax ID number required for any property purchase in Spain by a non-Spanish national. Nueva Living can guide you through obtaining one before you reserve.

What costs should I budget for on top of the purchase price?

Buyers typically budget for transfer tax or VAT, notary fees, land registry fees and legal fees on top of the purchase price. Nueva Living provides a full, current cost breakdown for your chosen residence before you reserve.

Can I get a mortgage in Spain as a non-resident?

Many Spanish banks offer mortgages to non-resident buyers, typically financing a portion of the purchase price. Exact terms depend on the bank and your personal financial profile.

What is the difference between off-plan and completed properties?

Off-plan means the development is still under construction and is usually sold with staged payments through to completion. A completed property is ready to view and move into now.

How does the reservation and payment process work?

Reservation and payment structures vary by development and are set out in full before you reserve. Nueva Living reconfirms the current schedule for your chosen residence at every step.

Can I rent out the property after purchase?

This depends on the individual development and local regulations, which can vary by community and municipality. Nueva Living will confirm the specific rules for a development before you reserve.

Do I need a lawyer?

Yes, we strongly recommend independent legal representation for any property purchase in Spain. Nueva Living can put you in touch with independent lawyers experienced in Costa del Sol property.

How many homes are available?

Ten of the 35 on the price list of 3 November 2026: one one-bedroom, eight two-bedroom and one three-bedroom apartment, from EUR 320,000 to EUR 495,000 plus VAT. The other 25 are marked reserved. The development has 111 homes in all.

Is VAT included?

No. The list says VAT is not included. VAT on a new home is 10 per cent and stamp duty in Andalusia is 1.20 per cent. We confirm the taxes and the purchase costs in writing.

Is there parking and storage?

The list gives each home one parking space and one storage room. The quality report adds a motorised garage door, a pre-installed charging point for electric vehicles and parking for golf buggies at course level.

Is furniture included?

No. The furniture and decoration in the images are not included. The kitchens are delivered with the appliances in the specification.

What is the payment schedule?

EUR 12,000 plus VAT on reservation, 20 per cent plus VAT less the reservation when the building licence is obtained, 10 per cent six months and 10 per cent twelve months after the private contract, and 60 per cent on completion. We confirm the schedule in writing.

When will it be finished?

None of the developer’s documents gives a completion date. We ask for the contractual date, the licence status and the stage of the works.

Is golf included?

The development is inside Estepona Golf, an 18-hole course, and has parking for golf buggies at course level. No document says whether membership or green fees are included, so we ask.
