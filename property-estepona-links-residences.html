The development

Estepona Links Residences is a development of 111 one, two and three-bedroom homes in Estepona Golf, which the developer’s dossier describes as an enclave where nature, tranquillity and lifestyle come together around the green. It describes contemporary architecture inspired by the Mediterranean, open views over the golf course and communal areas designed to be used all year.

The price list of 3 November 2026 covers 35 homes in two blocks and marks 25 of them as reserved. It lists ten with a price: one one-bedroom, eight two-bedroom and one three-bedroom apartment. None of the documents gives a completion date.

Architecture · Contemporary design, on the course

The dossier describes contemporary architecture inspired by the Mediterranean, with open views over the golf course. In the developer’s computer-generated images the blocks are long and low, with deep balconies, glass balustrades and a lawn along their front.

On the plans of the ten listed homes the living, dining and kitchen areas are one open room that opens to a terrace, with a main bedroom and a second bedroom, each with a bathroom, and a third bedroom in the three-bedroom home. The dossier says the terraces face south-east and east.

  • Structure: Reinforced concrete foundations to the site’s geotechnical study and a reinforced concrete slab structure on every floor.
  • Facade: A cavity wall of perforated brick with external render, sprayed polyurethane insulation, an air chamber and an inner lining with mineral wool insulation.
  • Glazing: PVC or aluminium window and door frames with double glazing, safety laminated glass on the terrace doors and insulated aluminium roller shutters in the bedrooms.
  • Security: An armoured entrance door, a video intercom at the main entrance and at the building, a master-key system and a gated complex with private access.

Location

The dossier places the development inside Estepona Golf, in the municipality of Estepona. It puts Marbella 25 minutes away and Málaga airport under an hour, and says that Puerto Banús, Sotogrande and several white villages inland are a few minutes away. Its location map names Finca Cortesín and Valle Romano Golf Club, a new hospital, a shopping area, the beaches of Bahía Dorada and La Rada, the port, the town centre and a coastal path, without distances. The documents give no street address and no coordinate for the plot, so the map marker is at Estepona Golf and not on the plot.

Our view

What we would weigh before reserving here, and the checks we make on your behalf.

Why it stands out

  • Who it is forBuyers who want a new one, two or three-bedroom apartment in Estepona Golf, with a terrace, a parking space and a storage room, to live in or as a second home.
  • What makes it differentThree pools, a gym, and a social club with a coworking area, together with parking for golf buggies at course level, all among the communal areas the developer lists.
  • Why location mattersThe development is inside Estepona Golf, an 18-hole course with a clubhouse restaurant. The dossier puts Marbella 25 minutes away and Málaga airport under an hour.
  • Why check availability25 of the 35 homes on the price list are marked reserved and ten have a price. The documents give no completion date, so we confirm it before any reservation.

What to plan for

  • Availability25 of the 35 homes on the list are marked reserved. Ten have a price, from EUR 320,000 to EUR 495,000 plus VAT, and the other 76 of the 111 homes are not on the list.
  • TaxThe list says VAT is not included. With VAT at 10 per cent and stamp duty at 1.20 per cent, the cheapest listed home comes to about EUR 356,000 and the dearest to about EUR 550,000, before notary and registry fees.
  • Payment termsEUR 12,000 plus VAT on reservation, then 20 per cent plus VAT less the reservation when the building licence is obtained, 10 per cent six months and 10 per cent twelve months after that contract, and 60 per cent on completion. The two middle instalments run from the contract, not from the build.
  • Licence and deliveryThe schedule makes the private contract depend on obtaining the building licence. No document says whether it has been granted, when work starts or when the homes are delivered, so we ask for all three in writing.

Checks before any money moves

  • The completion dateNone of the documents we hold gives a completion date, a start of works or the status of the licence, so this page says only that completion is to be confirmed. We ask for the contractual date and the licence status before any reservation.
  • The documentsThe price list is dated 3 November 2026 and the payment terms carry no date. The plan sheets are dated 16 March 2026, although their folder is named 13 October 2026, and the commercial dossier has 260618 in its file name. We use the price list for prices and availability and the other documents to describe the project.
  • The areasThe list and the plans disagree on a few figures. Home 14G is 79.11 sqm built on the list and 78.11 on its plan, and 15G is 51.14 and 51.17. For 13C, 13E, 15G and 24E the list’s terraces and gardens are larger than the plan’s usable outdoor areas: 105.50 against 99.12 sqm, 19.65 and 28.98 against 18.88 and 23.43, 13.94 against 12.50, and 19.43 against 17.62. The table follows the list, and we confirm which figures the contract uses.
  • The floorsThe list gives each home a floor from 0 to 5 and the plan sheets a level from P00 to P05. They agree for 13C, 20D, 21B, 22B and 25B. For 13E, 14G, 15G, 23H and 24E the plans say levels 3, 4, 5, 3 and 4 where the list says floors 0, 1, 2, 0 and 1. The table follows the list, and we confirm each home’s floor on site.
  • The imagesThe images are computer-generated, except the three photographs of the area. Furniture and decoration are not included, and the dossier says the images come from an initial project and may change during construction.
  • The mapThe documents give no address or coordinates, so the marker on the map is at Estepona Golf and not on the plot, and the road distances beside the map are measured from that marker. Questions about Estepona Links

How many homes are available?

Ten of the 35 on the price list of 3 November 2026: one one-bedroom, eight two-bedroom and one three-bedroom apartment, from EUR 320,000 to EUR 495,000 plus VAT. The other 25 are marked reserved. The development has 111 homes in all.

Is VAT included?

No. The list says VAT is not included. VAT on a new home is 10 per cent and stamp duty in Andalusia is 1.20 per cent. We confirm the taxes and the purchase costs in writing.

Is there parking and storage?

The list gives each home one parking space and one storage room. The quality report adds a motorised garage door, a pre-installed charging point for electric vehicles and parking for golf buggies at course level.

Is furniture included?

No. The furniture and decoration in the images are not included. The kitchens are delivered with the appliances in the specification.

What is the payment schedule?

EUR 12,000 plus VAT on reservation, 20 per cent plus VAT less the reservation when the building licence is obtained, 10 per cent six months and 10 per cent twelve months after the private contract, and 60 per cent on completion. We confirm the schedule in writing.
