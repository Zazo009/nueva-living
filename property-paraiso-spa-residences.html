Overview

88 homes, eight low blocks

Paraíso Spa Residences is a gated development of 88 two- and three-bedroom homes in eight low blocks between Atalaya and El Paraíso, on the New Golden Mile side of Estepona. The blocks are staggered around a central pool and gardens, so that most homes look over the communal areas or out to the hills and golf courses around them.

31 homes are currently available: ten ground-floor homes with a private garden, most of them with a private basement as well, two penthouses and seven further top-floor homes with a solarium, and twelve homes on the first and second floors. Six have two bedrooms and twenty-five have three.

Built areas, which include each home's share of the common areas, run from 134.66 to 357.15 sqm. Prices run from EUR 668,000 to EUR 1,240,000 and exclude the 10% IVA. The building licence was granted in September 2025 and delivery is scheduled for the third quarter of 2028.

Quality specifications

What the homes are built with

The developer's own quality specification, grouped by part of the home. Brand names are as specified, and "or similar" is the developer's own wording.

Structure and envelope
  • Reinforced concrete foundations and structure, checked by an independent technical control body.
  • Façades with a continuous exterior cladding and a double layer of thermal and acoustic insulation, lined inside with plasterboard on galvanised steel.
  • Terraces enclosed in a combination of masonry and glass, according to position and design.
  • Flat roofs with a double waterproofing layer, and double thermal insulation over living areas.
Windows and doors
  • Thermal-break joinery with double glazing, including Schüco-type casement and sliding windows.
  • Motorised blinds in the living room and bedrooms.
  • A reinforced front door, finished inside to match the interior joinery, and extra-height interior doors.
  • Modular wardrobes with extra-high doors, a top shelf and a hanging rail.
Walls, floors and ceilings
  • Between homes, ceramic masonry lined on both sides with insulation and double plasterboard.
  • Interior partitions in double laminated plasterboard with an insulated core.
  • Large-format Porcelanosa porcelain flooring throughout, with matching skirting.
  • False ceilings throughout with curtain recesses, and a cove with indirect lighting in the living and dining area.
Kitchen
  • Fitted wall and base units, in a choice of two colours offered by the developer.
  • An extra-thin Porcelanosa porcelain worktop, an undermount sink and a single-lever filter tap.
  • An induction hob, an extractor hood, an oven and microwave column, a dishwasher and an integrated Miele fridge or similar.
Bathrooms
  • Walls in Porcelanosa porcelain and paint, and the same large-format Porcelanosa floor as the rest of the home.
  • Wall-hung Villeroy & Boch toilets with concealed cisterns, Krion basins and Zucchetti taps.
  • Thermostatic taps, a built-in shower in the main bathroom and a shower column in the second, with a bathtub instead of a shower as an option.
  • Recessed LED lighting and a backlit mirror above the basin.
Climate and energy
  • Zoned underfloor heating.
  • Ducted air conditioning in the living room and bedrooms, controlled by an Airzone system or similar with a central screen and a sensor in each room.
  • Hot water from aerothermal heat pumps, and mechanical ventilation.
  • Energy rating A, with photovoltaic panels for the communal areas.
Electrical and security
  • Television and telephone sockets in the living room and bedrooms, and USB sockets in every room.
  • Video entry at the main gate and an entry intercom at each block entrance.
  • CCTV at the garage and pedestrian entrances, with a single entry and exit monitored all year round.
Outdoor and communal areas
  • Terraces and solariums in large-format non-slip Porcelanosa porcelain, with a water point on private terraces and gardens.
  • An underground garage with a motorised gate and pre-installation for electric-vehicle charging.
  • An outdoor pool with sun decks, a heated indoor pool, a social club, a gym, a hammam and a sauna.
  • Landscaped gardens with lounge areas and a street workout area.
  • LED lighting with presence detection in entrances, stairs, lift lobbies and the garage.

Source: the developer's quality specification, July 2026, checked against its Spanish original. Where the two differ, or the English is incomplete, only what both versions support is shown. The specification is indicative and may change for technical reasons during construction.

Architecture

Curves, staggered for the view

Eight low blocks are staggered across the site so that most homes face the central pool and gardens rather than each other. The terraces are drawn as deep white curves, the roofs are planted, and a perimeter road keeps cars away from the heart of the development, with the parking underground.

  • The homes — Gardens on the ground floor, terraces on the first, and solariums on the top floor of most blocks.
  • Inside — Large-format Porcelanosa flooring throughout, false ceilings and extra-height doors.
  • The communal spaces — An outdoor pool, a heated indoor pool, a hammam, a sauna, a gym and a social club around an interior courtyard.
  • Energy — Energy rating A, aerothermal hot water, mechanical ventilation and photovoltaic panels for the communal areas.

FAQ

Common buyer questions

Can foreigners buy property in Spain?

Yes. There are no restrictions on non-Spanish nationals buying property in Spain, whether as a resident or non-resident.

What is an NIE number and do I need one?

An NIE (Numero de Identificacion de Extranjero) is a tax ID number required for any property purchase in Spain by a non-Spanish national. Nueva Living can guide you through obtaining one before you reserve.

What costs should I budget for on top of the purchase price?

Buyers typically budget for transfer tax or VAT, notary fees, land registry fees and legal fees on top of the purchase price. Nueva Living provides a full, current cost breakdown for your chosen residence before you reserve.

Can I get a mortgage in Spain as a non-resident?

Many Spanish banks offer mortgages to non-resident buyers, typically financing a portion of the purchase price. Exact terms depend on the bank and your personal financial profile.

What is the difference between off-plan and completed properties?

Off-plan means the development is still under construction and is usually sold with staged payments through to completion. A completed property is ready to view and move into now.

How does the reservation and payment process work?

Reservation and payment structures vary by development and are set out in full before you reserve. Nueva Living reconfirms the current schedule for your chosen residence at every step.

Can I rent out the property after purchase?

This depends on the individual development and local regulations, which can vary by community and municipality. Nueva Living will confirm the specific rules for a development before you reserve.

Do I need a lawyer?

Yes, we strongly recommend independent legal representation for any property purchase in Spain. Nueva Living can put you in touch with independent lawyers experienced in Costa del Sol property.

What is available?

31 of the 88 homes: six with two bedrooms and twenty-five with three, including ten ground-floor homes with gardens and two penthouses.

Do the penthouses have a private pool?

The developer's website describes penthouses with a private pool, but the plans show a solarium, and on one penthouse an area set aside for a jacuzzi. We confirm in writing what is built before you reserve.

What is included in the price?

The home, at least one underground parking space, and a garden and basement or a storage room depending on the home. Prices exclude the 10% IVA.

When will it be finished?

The developer's legal information document gives the third quarter of 2028. The building licence was granted in September 2025 and the site was at the foundations stage in July 2026.

How is the payment structured?

A EUR 10,000 reservation, 30% on signing the purchase contract less the reservation, 10% on 5 November 2026 and 60% on completion. Payments before completion are guaranteed by an insurer.

What are the communal facilities?

An outdoor pool with sun decks, a heated indoor pool, a hammam and a sauna, a fully equipped gym, a social club, a street workout area and landscaped gardens, inside a gated development with CCTV at the entrances.
