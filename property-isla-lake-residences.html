The development

Isla Lake Residences is a complex of 63 apartments with two or three bedrooms and two bathrooms, at Atalaya, in Estepona. Two homes are currently available: a two-bedroom ground-floor apartment with a private garden facing the pool, and a three-bedroom first-floor apartment with a long terrace over the gardens.

Both are sold finished, with furniture, equipment and decoration included in the price list. Every home has underfloor heating and aerothermal energy, a fitted kitchen with appliances, and a parking space and storeroom in the basement. The shared grounds cover more than 6,700 sqm.

Architecture · Three kinds of home, one complex

The catalogue describes three types of home. Ground-floor homes have a large garden with direct access to the shared grounds. Middle-floor homes have a large covered terrace, part porch and part open to the sun. Top-floor homes have a private solarium with a barbecue, a sink, a shower and pre-installation for a jacuzzi.

Homes face south, east, south-east or west, and the catalogue says the layout allows cross ventilation. The shared areas are designed without architectural barriers, so that every space can be used by everyone.

  • Ground floor: A large garden with direct access to the shared grounds, as in Home 21, which has a private garden of 81 sqm.
  • Middle floor: A large covered terrace, part porch and part open, as in Home 50, which has a 27.95 sqm covered terrace.
  • Top floor: A private solarium with a barbecue, a sink, a shower and pre-installation for a jacuzzi. Neither home listed as available is a top-floor home.
  • Orientation: Homes face south, east, south-east or west, with views of the natural surroundings or of the shared grounds.

What we would weigh before reserving here, and what we check for you.

  • Why location mattersAtalaya sits between Marbella, Estepona and Benahavís, with ten golf courses within 4 km, Guadalmina beach about nine minutes away and Puerto Banús a few minutes by car.

What to plan for

  • Reservation and contractWe confirm the reservation deposit, the payment schedule and the contract terms with the developer before you commit.
  • Guaranteed paymentsWe ask the developer to confirm in writing how amounts paid on account are guaranteed through a bank, as Spanish building law requires.

Questions about Isla Lake

Is it finished?

The developer’s price list gives both homes as turnkey, without a date, and the grounds are shown built in the photographs. The catalogue gives a building licence granted on 26 August 2021. We ask for the occupation licence and the energy certificate for the home you choose.

What comes with the price?

The price list shows furniture, equipment and decoration as included for both homes, with terrace furniture also listed for Home 21, and no separate equipment figure. Each home has a parking space and a storeroom in the basement. We confirm the inventory in writing.

What are the shared grounds?

More than 6,700 sqm with a large adult pool, a children’s pool, a second heated adult pool, jacuzzis, water jets, a lake with a relaxation island, a yoga area, a gym, a pétanque court and a playground.

What furniture is included?

The price list shows furniture, equipment and decoration as included for both homes. The developer’s furniture catalogue of 3 November 2022 lists a package room by room: a living room with a smart TV, a chaise-longue sofa and a dining table, furnished bedrooms with TVs in the main one, bathroom accessories, and a kitchen with small appliances and kitchenware. We confirm the current inventory in writing.

How far is the beach and the golf?

The catalogue gives Guadalmina beach at about nine minutes, Estepona and Marbella at about twenty, Puerto Banús at a few minutes by car, and ten golf courses within 4 km.
