The development

Absolute Estepona is a gated boutique development of 24 apartments with two and three bedrooms in Estepona. The developer’s infosheet puts it 500 metres from the beach and local amenities, with panoramic sea views. The renders show two blocks of white terraces stepping down a slope towards a pool, and the plans number the entrances as portals 1 and 2. Five homes are listed with a price: two three-bedroom penthouses with a solarium and a jacuzzi, and three two-bedroom homes, one with a garden, one at garden level with a very large terrace and one on the first floor.

The infosheet gives the building licence as dated 18 September 2024 and the status as under construction, started in the third quarter of 2024, with the end of construction estimated for the first quarter of 2027. It leaves the first-occupation licence blank and carries no date, so we ask how far the build has come.

Architecture · White terraces, stepping down the slope

In the renders the two blocks have long white terrace slabs on every level, with glass balustrades, pergolas and planted edges, stepping down the slope towards a pool and a lawn, with the sea beyond.

The quality specification describes an ambitious avant-garde facade in innovative materials, for higher energy efficiency, with studied interior layouts and quality details in the entrance and the common areas.

  • Ceiling height: 2.70 m, according to the infosheet.
  • Flooring: Continuous large-format rectified porcelain stoneware throughout each home; the infosheet names Porcelanosa porcelain in 1 x 1 m format.
  • Glazing: Beige aluminium frames with double glazing for thermal and acoustic insulation and energy saving, and motorised blinds in the bedrooms.
  • Energy: Hot water, heating and cooling from an individual aerothermal system in each home, with ducted air conditioning.

Specifications

The developer’s own quality specification, summarised by part of the home.

  • Continuous large-format rectified porcelain stoneware flooring throughout the home.
  • The main bathroom is tiled in large-format stoneware with a white marble effect, and the second bathroom in a combination by PORCELANOSA or similar.
  • A wall-hung washbasin cabinet with two drawers and a mirror, a clear-glass shower screen, and in the main bathroom a wall-hung toilet and an illuminated anti-mist mirror.
  • Matte black taps and a thermostatic shower in the main bathroom, with a rain head, an anti-limescale system, a 40 °C safety button and an adjustable fixed shower angle.
  • White-lacquered interior doors with chrome fittings, and lined block-type wardrobes with an intermediate shelf, a hanging rail and a drawer unit.
  • Beige aluminium exterior frames with double glazing for thermal and acoustic insulation and energy saving, and motorised blinds with a push button in the bedrooms.
  • An induction hob, a microwave, an oven, an extractor hood, a fridge, a dishwasher, and a sink and tap.
  • LED strip lighting under the units to light the work area.
  • Hot water, heating and cooling from an individual aerothermal system in each home, which the specification says saves community costs because it is used only as needed, with ducted air conditioning whose machines sit in the false ceiling of the bathrooms.
  • Anti-impact acoustic insulation in all the homes, thermal and acoustic insulation in the exterior walls and between homes, and a three-layer soundproofed downpipe system.
  • Simon 100 series switches and sockets, LED lighting in the bathrooms and kitchen, a video intercom at the entrance and fibre optic in the building with outlets in the living room and bedrooms.
  • A motorised garage door with a remote control and a lift to the parking level that serves all floors, with controlled mechanical ventilation of the homes and constant water pressure from a storage tank and pumping unit.

Source: the quality specification in the developer’s documents, which is undated. It says that it is at the development stage and that its references may change for technical reasons without any reduction in the quality or design of the result, and that the kitchen furniture shown is a schematic sample and may vary.

Our assessment

What we would weigh before reserving here, and what we check for you.

Why it stands out

  • Who it is forBuyers who want a new two- or three-bedroom home with a large terrace in a small gated development in Estepona, to live in or as a second home, from a first-floor apartment to a penthouse with a rooftop jacuzzi.
  • What makes it differentThe five listed homes have terraces of 57.16 to 227.06 sqm on the plans, and each penthouse adds a solarium of 100.97 sqm with a jacuzzi.
  • Why location mattersThe infosheet puts the development 500 metres from the beach and local amenities and lists panoramic sea views. It gives no other distances.
  • Why check the detailsThe availability list and the plans disagree on the built area of two homes, the plans give two street addresses and the end of construction is only an estimate, so we confirm these with the developer before any reservation.

What to plan for

  • Reservation and paymentThe developer sets a reservation and a staged payment plan in its infosheet. We send you the current amounts and dates in writing before you reserve.
  • VATThe availability list gives the developer’s net prices and the infosheet adds VAT to each instalment, so we read the prices as excluding VAT. The rate is not stated, so we confirm it and the other purchase costs in writing.
  • What the price includesThe plans of 1.S01.A and 2.00.D show a storage room on the level below, and the quality specification gives a fitted kitchen with appliances. No document says whether a garage space comes with each home, or that the furniture in the images is included.
  • The specificationThe quality specification is at the development stage and may change for technical reasons without reducing the quality or design of the result. We ask for the final specification.

Checks before any money moves

  • Licence and worksBuilding licence granted. The infosheet gives a building licence dated 18 September 2024, works started in the third quarter of 2024 and the first-occupation licence blank. We ask for the licence and for the bank guarantee that covers amounts paid on account.
  • The completion dateThe infosheet estimates the end of construction for the first quarter of 2027 and carries no date. We ask how far the build has come and for the contractual date, and put both in writing.
  • The areasThe availability list and the plans agree on the built area of 1.02.A, 1.02.B and 2.01.A, but not on 1.S01.A, listed at 115.27 sqm against 161.34 sqm built and 115.51 sqm interior on the plan, or on 2.00.D, listed at 114.18 sqm against 160.07 sqm built and 113.46 sqm interior. The plans also give useful areas under Decree 218/2005, for example 107.94 sqm for 1.02.A and 1.02.B against 98.13 sqm. We confirm which figures the contract uses.
  • Address and pricesThe plans give one street address for the penthouses and the portal 2 homes and another for 1.S01.A, although the penthouses and 1.S01.A are both in portal 1. The two penthouse plans show the same areas, but the list prices them EUR 95,000 apart. We ask which address is registered and what explains the difference.

Questions about Absolute Estepona

How many homes are available?
