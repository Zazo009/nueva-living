Overview

Twenty-four homes, five listed

Absolute Estepona is a gated boutique development of 24 apartments with two and three bedrooms in Estepona. The developer’s infosheet puts it 500 metres from the beach and local amenities, with panoramic sea views. The renders show two blocks of white terraces stepping down a slope towards a pool, and the plans number the entrances as portals 1 and 2. Five homes are listed with a price: two three-bedroom penthouses with a solarium and a jacuzzi, and three two-bedroom homes, one with a garden, one at garden level with a very large terrace and one on the first floor.

The infosheet gives the building licence as dated 18 September 2024 and the status as under construction, started in the third quarter of 2024, with the end of construction estimated for the first quarter of 2027. It leaves the first-occupation licence blank and carries no date, so we ask how far the build has come.

Quality specifications

What the homes are built with

The developer’s own quality specification, summarised by part of the home.

Floors and tiling
  • Continuous large-format rectified porcelain stoneware flooring throughout the home.
  • The main bathroom is tiled in large-format stoneware with a white marble effect, and the second bathroom in a combination by PORCELANOSA or similar.
Bathrooms
  • A wall-hung washbasin cabinet with two drawers and a mirror, a clear-glass shower screen, and in the main bathroom a wall-hung toilet and an illuminated anti-mist mirror.
  • Matte black taps and a thermostatic shower in the main bathroom, with a rain head, an anti-limescale system, a 40 °C safety button and an adjustable fixed shower angle.
Doors, windows and wardrobes
  • White-lacquered interior doors with chrome fittings, and lined block-type wardrobes with an intermediate shelf, a hanging rail and a drawer unit.
  • Beige aluminium exterior frames with double glazing for thermal and acoustic insulation and energy saving, and motorised blinds with a push button in the bedrooms.
Kitchen
  • An induction hob, a microwave, an oven, an extractor hood, a fridge, a dishwasher, and a sink and tap.
  • LED strip lighting under the units to light the work area.
Climate, energy and insulation
  • Hot water, heating and cooling from an individual aerothermal system in each home, which the specification says saves community costs because it is used only as needed, with ducted air conditioning whose machines sit in the false ceiling of the bathrooms.
  • Anti-impact acoustic insulation in all the homes, thermal and acoustic insulation in the exterior walls and between homes, and a three-layer soundproofed downpipe system.
Electrical, telecoms and common areas
  • Simon 100 series switches and sockets, LED lighting in the bathrooms and kitchen, a video intercom at the entrance and fibre optic in the building with outlets in the living room and bedrooms.
  • A motorised garage door with a remote control and a lift to the parking level that serves all floors, with controlled mechanical ventilation of the homes and constant water pressure from a storage tank and pumping unit.

Source: the quality specification in the developer’s documents, which is undated. It says that it is at the development stage and that its references may change for technical reasons without any reduction in the quality or design of the result, and that the kitchen furniture shown is a schematic sample and may vary.

FAQ

Common buyer questions

Can foreigners buy property in Spain?

Yes. There are no restrictions on non-Spanish nationals buying property in Spain, whether as a resident or non-resident.

What is an NIE number and do I need one?

An NIE (Numero de Identificacion de Extranjero) is a tax ID number required for any property purchase in Spain by a non-Spanish national. Nueva Living can guide you through obtaining one before you reserve.

What costs should I budget for on top of the purchase price?

Buyers typically budget for transfer tax or VAT, notary fees, land registry fees and legal fees on top of the purchase price. Nueva Living provides a full, current cost breakdown for your chosen residence before you reserve.

Can I get a mortgage in Spain as a non-resident?

Many Spanish banks offer mortgages to non-resident buyers, typically financing a portion of the purchase price. Exact terms depend on the bank and your personal financial profile.

What is the difference between off-plan and completed properties?

Off-plan means the development is still under construction and is usually sold with staged payments through to completion. A completed property is ready to view and move into now.

How does the reservation and payment process work?

Reservation and payment structures vary by development and are set out in full before you reserve. Nueva Living reconfirms the current schedule for your chosen residence at every step.

Can I rent out the property after purchase?

This depends on the individual development and local regulations, which can vary by community and municipality. Nueva Living will confirm the specific rules for a development before you reserve.

Do I need a lawyer?

Yes, we strongly recommend independent legal representation for any property purchase in Spain. Nueva Living can put you in touch with independent lawyers experienced in Costa del Sol property.
