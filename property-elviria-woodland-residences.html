Overview

21 homes, each with its own private pool

Elviria Woodland Residences is a collection of 21 apartments spread across seven independent buildings, set within a private Mediterranean forest in Elviria, Marbella East, surrounded by pines, mastic trees and rosemary, and crossed by a natural stream.

Ground-floor residences open onto a front and rear garden; first-floor residences have their own rooftop terrace with a pergola, and one building is built exclusively as a duplex with its own solarium. Every residence, on any floor, has its own private swimming pool. A footbridge over the stream leads to a shared Wellness Hub with a dry sauna, hammam, gym and outdoor fitness area.

Architecture

Built into a working forest

The seven buildings are designed around the site's Mediterranean forest rather than in place of it, with rendered facades in light tones, floor-to-ceiling glazing and living rooms and kitchens up to 12 metres wide opening onto the terrace. Large-format porcelain flooring (90x90cm) runs at the same level between interior and exterior.

Structurally the buildings use sustainable Eco-Planet concrete with reinforced concrete walls and mixed foundations, and exterior carpentry is aluminium with a thermal break and lever-proof motorised roller shutters. Underfloor heating is paired with a double-flow ventilation system with a heat exchanger, and every residence is pre-installed for a home automation system covering temperature, lighting, blinds and video entry.

  • Private saline-chlorination swimming pool at every residence, with pre-installation for heating.
  • Living room and kitchen up to 12 metres wide, opening onto the terrace through floor-to-ceiling glazing.
  • Underfloor heating with a double-flow ventilation system and heat exchanger.
  • Pre-installation for a home automation system and for electric vehicle charging.

FAQ

Common buyer questions

Can foreigners buy property in Spain?

Yes. There are no restrictions on non-Spanish nationals buying property in Spain, whether as a resident or non-resident.

What is an NIE number and do I need one?

An NIE (Numero de Identificacion de Extranjero) is a tax ID number required for any property purchase in Spain by a non-Spanish national. Nueva Living can guide you through obtaining one before you reserve.

What costs should I budget for on top of the purchase price?

Buyers typically budget for transfer tax or VAT, notary fees, land registry fees and legal fees on top of the purchase price. Nueva Living provides a full, current cost breakdown for your chosen residence before you reserve.

Can I get a mortgage in Spain as a non-resident?

Many Spanish banks offer mortgages to non-resident buyers, typically financing a portion of the purchase price. Exact terms depend on the bank and your personal financial profile.

What is the difference between off-plan and completed properties?

Off-plan means the development is still under construction and is usually sold with staged payments through to completion. A completed property is ready to view and move into now.

How does the reservation and payment process work?

Reservation and payment structures vary by development and are set out in full before you reserve. Nueva Living reconfirms the current schedule for your chosen residence at every step.

Can I rent out the property after purchase?

This depends on the individual development and local regulations, which can vary by community and municipality. Nueva Living will confirm the specific rules for a development before you reserve.

Do I need a lawyer?

Yes, we strongly recommend independent legal representation for any property purchase in Spain. Nueva Living can put you in touch with independent lawyers experienced in Costa del Sol property.

How many residences are there in total?

21 apartments across seven independent buildings.

How many are currently available?

The developer has released 5 residences with confirmed pricing so far, from two-bedroom to three-bedroom layouts. Nueva Living reconfirms the current release before any viewing.

Is a private pool included?

Yes. The developer's specification states that every residence has its own private, saline-chlorination swimming pool.

Is the price inclusive of IVA?

No. The published payment structure adds VAT to each payment stage.

Is furniture included?

No. The images shown are computer-generated; furniture, styling and planting are indicative and are not included in the sale.

When is delivery?

Construction started in Q1 2025 with completion estimated for Q2 2027, and the First Occupation Licence is estimated for Q4 2027. The Building Licence has already been granted.
