The development

Atalaya Duplex Residences is a gated scheme of forty homes in three low blocks beside Atalaya Golf, on the inland side of the New Golden Mile between Estepona and San Pedro de Alcántara.

The homes are duplex apartments of four and five bedrooms and penthouses of three, from 179.26 to 279.84 sqm built, with terraces of 23.80 to 54.37 sqm. Most have their own pool of about 11 to 15 sqm, drawn on the unit plan, and the two ground-floor duplexes add a garden of 83.59 and 420.85 sqm. Underground parking and a storage room come with every home.

The communal areas are a pool in the gardens, a heated indoor pool, a spa with changing rooms, a gym, a padel court, a covered social lounge with co-working and a concierge at the entrance.

Architecture · White blocks, private water

Three low blocks step down the plot in a Mediterranean idiom: white render under tiled roofs, deep terraces and planting between the buildings rather than parking. Cars go underground, and the ground between the blocks is garden.

  • The duplexes: Two floors, three to five bedrooms, with the living room and kitchen opening onto a terrace and, on most, a private pool.
  • The penthouses: A single floor under the roof, three bedrooms, with the terrace running the width of the home and a plunge pool on some.
  • Private pools: A pool or plunge pool of about 11 to 15 sqm on most homes, drawn on the unit plans rather than promised in a brochure.
  • Terraces: From 23.80 to 54.37 sqm, and on the ground-floor duplexes a garden of up to 420.85 sqm.
  • Parking and storage: Underground parking and a store with every home, entered from inside the block.
  • Communal areas: An outdoor pool, a heated indoor pool, a spa, a gym, a padel court, a covered social lounge and a concierge at the gate.

Our assessment

What we would weigh before reserving here, and what we check for you.

Why it stands out

  • What it isForty duplex apartments and penthouses in three blocks, of which nine are still available.
  • Who it is forBuyers who want a private pool without buying a villa, and owners who intend to let the home when they are not in it.
  • What makes it differentAlmost every home has its own pool or plunge pool, which is unusual in a multi-unit scheme at this price.
  • Why location mattersAtalaya Golf is three minutes away and Puerto Banús eleven, on the New Golden Mile between Estepona and San Pedro de Alcántara.

What to plan for

  • What the operator projectsGross yields of 8-10% a year on a managed short-let, against the 4-5% the operator cites for a traditional Marbella letting.
  • The income assumptionsA three-bedroom home is modelled at €330-400 a night and 65-72% occupancy, a four-bedroom at €380-450, and a five-bedroom at €450-550 and 68-75%. That gives €80,000-90,000, €95,000-105,000 and €120,000-135,000 gross a year respectively.
  • What the management coversLetting and operations, dynamic pricing, check-in and check-out, cleaning, concierge and security, reported to the owner through a dashboard. Costs sit at community level rather than per home.
  • What is not promisedOccupancy, nightly rates and yields are projections. They move with the season, the market, the rules on holiday letting and how much the owner uses the home. Ask for the operator's contract and its fee schedule before you rely on any of it.

Checks before any money moves

  • The licenceThe building licence and, on a finished home, the first occupation licence, confirmed in writing before any money moves.
  • What is registeredThat the home, its parking and its storage are registered as described, and that the private pool is on the plan.
  • Letting rulesWhether the community and the local rules allow short-term letting, and on what terms. This matters more here than on most schemes.
  • The management contractThe term, the fees, the cancellation rights and what happens if you want to use the home yourself.

Questions about Atalaya Duplex

How many are left?

Nine of the forty. Thirty are sold and one is reserved.

Do they all have a private pool?

No. Most of the duplexes have a pool of about 11 to 15 sqm, and some penthouses have a plunge pool on the terrace. The unit drawing shows which.

How big are the homes?

From 179.26 to 279.84 sqm built, with terraces of 23.80 to 54.37 sqm and, on two ground-floor duplexes, a garden.

When is delivery?

The developer gives 2026, with the first homes already finished. We confirm the date in the purchase contract in writing.

Is parking included?

Yes. Underground parking and a storage room come with every home.

Can the home be let out?

The developer runs a managed short-let operation on the scheme. We check the community rules and the local licensing before you commit.

What returns can I expect?

The operator projects 8-10% gross. That is a target, not a guarantee, and it depends on occupancy, rates, costs and your own use of the home.
