The development

La Noria Golf Residences is the second phase of a gated scheme in La Cala de Mijas, opposite the La Noria golf course. The first phase, fifty homes in blocks one to five, is built, and its gardens, pools and gym are in use. This phase is blocks six and seven, thirty-two apartments of one and two bedrooms.

The homes run from 70.48 to 102.90 sqm built, with terraces of 24.12 to 35.18 sqm. The four homes on the top level trade the terrace for a private solarium of 117.70 sqm. A parking space and a storage room come with every home, and underfloor heating runs throughout.

The blocks step down the slope, so the lowest homes still sit about nine metres above the street and keep the view. Seventeen of the thirty-two are available.

Architecture · White terraces, down the slope

Two blocks step down the hillside so that each home looks over the roof of the one below. The facades are white render, the terraces are deep and the planting between the blocks is mature, because the first phase has been standing for years.

  • The blocks: Blocks six and seven, thirty-two homes over four levels, each level set back from the one under it.
  • The terraces: From 24.12 to 35.18 sqm off the living room, in porcelain paving.
  • The solariums: The four homes on the top level have a private roof terrace of 117.70 sqm instead.
  • Inside: Underfloor heating throughout, porcelain floors and large windows to the south.
  • Parking and storage: A parking space and a storage room with every home.
  • Communal areas: An infinity pool, an oasis pool, a children pool, a playground and outdoor training equipment, all built and in use.
