The development

Higueron Rooftop Villas is a gated scheme of thirty-six townhouses at El Higuerón, on the hillside between Fuengirola and Benalmádena, laid out in four stepped terraces so that every house faces south or south-west over the Mediterranean. Thirteen are still available.

Nine are three-bedroom and twenty-seven are four-bedroom. Every house has a private pool on its roof solarium, a private garden, two parking spaces and a storeroom.

Of the thirteen available, built areas run from 170.30 to 187.49 sqm with terraces of 80.61 to 111.29 sqm. Prices exclude the 10% IVA.

Architecture · Stone, stepped down the hill

Four terraces of houses follow the contour of the hillside, each row set back above the one below so that no house looks into its neighbour and every roof keeps its view. Natural stone is used in the cladding, and the scheme is built to a BREEAM environmental assessment, with the insulation and glazing specified to cut both energy use and the sound of the coast road below.

  • The houses: Three living levels plus the roof solarium, with a basement level on the upper terrace.
  • Inside: Large-format Porcelanosa flooring throughout, including the staircases, and full-height tiling in the bathrooms.
  • The roof: A private pool, a sun terrace and, on the show house, an outdoor kitchen and a fire pit.
  • Energy: BREEAM certified, aerothermal heating and cooling, and pre-installation for photovoltaic panels and a home battery.

Our assessment

What we would weigh before reserving here, and what we check for you.

Why it stands out

  • What it isThirty-six townhouses on a hillside at El Higuerón, thirteen still available, each with its own rooftop pool.
  • Who it is forBuyers who want a house with a pool and a garden but not the upkeep of a detached villa, within a few minutes of the beach and the train.
  • What makes it differentA private pool on the roof of every house, which almost no townhouse scheme on this coast offers.
  • Why location mattersThe Carvajal train station and two beaches are under five minutes away, and Málaga airport about twenty.

What to plan for

  • Why timing mattersTwenty-three of the thirty-six are already gone, and only two of the nine three-bedroom houses remain.
  • Current informationNueva Living reconfirms live prices, availability and payment terms before any reservation.
  • Long-term appealEl Higuerón is one of the few hillside positions on this stretch with a train station at the bottom of it, which supports demand year round.
  • Personal use and rentalSuits a main or second home, subject to the community rules and the Andalusian tourist-licence regime.

Questions about Higueron Rooftop

Does every house have its own pool?

Yes. Every house in the scheme has a private pool on its roof solarium, with a sun terrace around it.

What is included in the price?

The house, its garden, its rooftop pool, two parking spaces and a storeroom. Prices exclude the 10% IVA.

What are the communal facilities?

Three communal pools, a gym, a spa with an indoor pool and a relaxation room, a co-working lounge, and more than 100,000 sqm of Mediterranean gardens.

Which way do the houses face?

South or south-west. The four terraces step down the hillside so that each row looks over the one below towards the sea.
