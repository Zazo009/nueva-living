The development

The developer’s infosheet describes a finished, key-ready development of four apartments of two, three and four bedrooms on the beachfront in Estepona, fully furnished and with underground parking. The sales portal lists four apartments, AT-01, AT-03, AT-04 and AT-05, of 113.99 to 202.69 sqm, priced from EUR 3,200,000 to EUR 7,300,000.

The infosheet lists the building licence and the first-occupation licence as in place and the end of construction as finished. The shared areas it and the building specification describe are an outdoor pool, a heated indoor pool, a gym, a sauna, a Turkish bath, landscaped communal areas and a concierge service. The documents differ on the number of bedrooms and on one price, and the sections below say where.

Architecture · Stepped terraces, stone and timber

The photographs show terraced levels with dark pergolas, glass balustrades, large stone-paved terraces and timber decking, set directly above the beach. The specification gives large-format marble for the floors and shows a materials board with marble and white travertine, timber decking, and a choice of white or grey marble.

It describes the private terraces as having a mini-pool, a lounge area, a gas fireplace and water features, according to design. It does not say which apartment has which, and we confirm that for each one.

  • Structure: A reinforced-concrete foundation slab designed to the Technical Building Code and the geotechnical study, waffle slabs, and reinforced-concrete walls and pillars with some metal pillars.
  • Walls: Perforated-brick outer walls with an air chamber insulated with polyurethane foam and a water-repellent cement-mortar render, and soundproof perforated brick between apartments.
  • Glazing: Minimalist aluminium frames with a thermal break, double glazing with solar control, and glass balustrades set into the floor.
  • Terraces: Flat inverted roofs with floating marble paving and deck-type flooring, with the slopes and drains under the paving.

Specifications

The developer’s own building specification, summarised by part of the home.

  • Large-format marble on the interior floors, hardwood in the bedrooms, large non-slip marble on the private terraces, and large-format marble on the bathroom walls.
  • The other walls are plastered with projected perlite and painted, with anti-humidity paint in the wet areas.
  • A wooden armoured entrance door, interior doors in oak or lacquered finish with chrome fittings and a magnetic latch, and wardrobes with matching doors, lined inside and fitted with drawers, shelves, a shoe rack and hanging rails.
  • Minimalist aluminium frames set flush with the floor, with a thermal break and double glazing with solar control.
  • Air conditioning with heat recovery in which every room has its own quiet indoor unit and its own thermostat, water underfloor heating, and electric underfloor heating in the bathrooms. The same system produces the hot water.
  • A ventilation unit with heat recovery that renews and filters the air and can run while the home is empty.
  • A home-automation system for lighting scenes, shutters, climate and audio, controlled from touch screens and mobile devices, including remotely, with a water-leak alarm that shuts off the supply to the apartment.
  • Wi-Fi amplifiers inside and on the terraces, 5.1 surround sound in the lounges, a video door entry with a screen, a fingerprint reader for the block and for each apartment, and an electric-vehicle charging point for each apartment.
  • Low-energy LED lighting with recessed spotlights, strip lights and wall and floor fittings.
  • Suspended toilets with concealed cisterns and dark-platinum matt taps and shower fittings throughout.
  • A custom washbasin unit and a freestanding bath in the main bathroom, basins built into the countertop in the secondary bathrooms, and a freestanding basin with a backlit mirror in the guest bathrooms. Anti-fog mirrors in the wet rooms.
  • Drinking water pumped from a treated, recirculated and disinfected tank by a pressure group of three variable-speed pumps, and a self-cleaning filter and a decalcifier in each property that keep the hardness at about 10 °F.
  • The main shut-off valve of each apartment is in its storage room and is controlled by the home automation.
  • A motorised garage door operated by remote control, a car lift of special size and a turntable for cars, and a panoramic lift with double access at 180 degrees.

Source: the developer’s building specification for this development, dated 15 September 2026. It is written in the future tense, although the infosheet says the building is finished, and it names equipment brands “or similar”, which this summary leaves out. It has no section on the kitchen, the appliances or the furniture.

Questions about Estepona Seafront

Why is AT-01 shown at one price?

The portal lists it at EUR 4,200,000 and at EUR 3,237,000. The page shows EUR 4,200,000, which is also the price listed for AT-05, an apartment of the same size. We ask the developer which one applies and put the answer in writing.

Is VAT included?

The portal table does not say. The infosheet’s payment terms add VAT to the selling price, which indicates that the prices are before VAT, and the VAT rate is not stated. We confirm the taxes and purchase costs in writing.

Is the building finished?

The infosheet says the end of construction is finished, that the building licence and the first-occupation licence are in place and that the apartments are key ready. We ask for the licences and the certificate of completion before you reserve.

What are the payment terms?

The reservation is 10% of the selling price plus VAT, and the other 90% plus VAT is paid on signing the deed.

Are the apartments furnished?

The infosheet lists them as fully furnished. It does not say what is included in the price, and the furnished apartment in the photographs is not identified.
