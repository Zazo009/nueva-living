Overview

An urban resort between Fuengirola and Mijas

Miramar Urban Residences is a 233-home development in Las Lagunas, the most active and commercial district of Mijas, sitting between Fuengirola and Mijas town. The development spans several buildings arranged around landscaped communal gardens, with apartments and penthouses from 2 to 4 bedrooms.

Residents share two outdoor pools connected by a water feature, a rooftop skypool with coastal views, an indoor spa with a heated pool, a fully equipped gym and a coworking space -- amenities designed to bring a resort-style routine to everyday life, a short walk from the Parque Comercial Miramar shopping and leisure centre and five minutes' drive from Fuengirola.

Architecture

Contemporary design, organic massing

The development's stepped, terraced buildings are designed so that as many homes as possible have a private terrace and a clear line of sight, rather than facing directly onto a neighbouring block. Facades combine light stone-toned render with glass balustrades and planted terraces on every level.

Interiors use large-format wood-effect porcelain flooring throughout, with floor-to-ceiling glazing that floods living areas with natural light. Open-plan kitchens are fitted with Miele appliances, and every home is pre-installed for smart home control of lighting and climate. The development is BREEAM certified for sustainable construction, with an A energy efficiency rating.

  • Stepped, terraced massing giving most homes a private terrace and open outlook.
  • Large-format wood-effect porcelain flooring throughout.
  • Open-plan kitchens fitted with Miele appliances.
  • BREEAM certified, A energy efficiency rating.

FAQ

Common buyer questions

Can foreigners buy property in Spain?

Yes. There are no restrictions on non-Spanish nationals buying property in Spain, whether as a resident or non-resident.

What is an NIE number and do I need one?

An NIE (Numero de Identificacion de Extranjero) is a tax ID number required for any property purchase in Spain by a non-Spanish national. Nueva Living can guide you through obtaining one before you reserve.

What costs should I budget for on top of the purchase price?

Buyers typically budget for transfer tax or VAT, notary fees, land registry fees and legal fees on top of the purchase price. Nueva Living provides a full, current cost breakdown for your chosen residence before you reserve.

Can I get a mortgage in Spain as a non-resident?

Many Spanish banks offer mortgages to non-resident buyers, typically financing a portion of the purchase price. Exact terms depend on the bank and your personal financial profile.

What is the difference between off-plan and completed properties?

Off-plan means the development is still under construction and is usually sold with staged payments through to completion. A completed property is ready to view and move into now.

How does the reservation and payment process work?

Reservation and payment structures vary by development and are set out in full before you reserve. Nueva Living reconfirms the current schedule for your chosen residence at every step.

Can I rent out the property after purchase?

This depends on the individual development and local regulations, which can vary by community and municipality. Nueva Living will confirm the specific rules for a development before you reserve.

Do I need a lawyer?

Yes, we strongly recommend independent legal representation for any property purchase in Spain. Nueva Living can put you in touch with independent lawyers experienced in Costa del Sol property.

How many homes are in the development?

233 in total, across several buildings; 52 are currently shown as available by the developer.

What amenities are included?

Two outdoor communal pools, a rooftop skypool, an indoor spa with a heated pool, a gym, a coworking space and landscaped gardens, shared by all residents.

Are prices published per unit?

Not publicly. The developer quotes a starting price of EUR 458,000 for the collection; Nueva Living requests the current price for a specific residence before any viewing.

Is the price inclusive of VAT?

The developer's published information does not state this. Nueva Living confirms VAT treatment in writing before any reservation.

Is furniture included?

No. The images shown are computer-generated; furniture, styling and planting are indicative and are not included in the sale.

When is delivery?

The developer states the building licence is granted and active and the project is under construction; a confirmed delivery date has not been published. Nueva Living confirms the current build programme in writing before any reservation.
