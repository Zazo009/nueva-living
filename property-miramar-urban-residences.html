The development

Miramar Urban Residences is a 233-home development in Las Lagunas, the most active and commercial district of Mijas, sitting between Fuengirola and Mijas town. The development spans several buildings arranged around landscaped communal gardens, with apartments and penthouses with 2 or 3 bedrooms, some with a study.

Residents share two outdoor pools connected by a water feature, a rooftop skypool with coastal views, an indoor spa with a heated pool, a fully equipped gym and a coworking space -- amenities designed to bring a resort-style routine to everyday life, a short walk from the Parque Comercial Miramar shopping and leisure centre and five minutes' drive from Fuengirola.

Architecture · Contemporary design, organic massing

The development's stepped, terraced buildings are designed so that as many homes as possible have a private terrace and a clear line of sight, rather than facing directly onto a neighbouring block. Facades combine light stone-toned render with glass balustrades and planted terraces on every level.

Interiors use large-format wood-effect porcelain flooring throughout, with floor-to-ceiling glazing that floods living areas with natural light. Open-plan kitchens are fitted with Miele appliances, and every home is pre-installed for smart home control of lighting and climate. The development is BREEAM certified for sustainable construction, with an A energy efficiency rating. The developer states an energy use of 44.6 kWh/m² a year and emissions of 10 kg CO₂/m² a year, with thermally broken window frames, an insulated façade system and LED lighting in the communal areas.

Location

Miramar Urban Residences sits in Las Lagunas, the most active district of Mijas, between the coastal towns of Fuengirola and Mijas.
