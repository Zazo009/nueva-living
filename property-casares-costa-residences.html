The development

Casares Costa Residences is a gated scheme of twenty-five apartments in two low blocks at Casares Costa, between the sea and the hills behind.

The homes run from 120.09 to 184.42 sqm built and carry two to four bedrooms. Every one has a terrace, from 65.35 to 97.98 sqm, and the seven on the ground floor add a private-use garden of 138.80 to 441.34 sqm.

The communal areas are a pool in the gardens, a heated indoor pool, a spa, a gym and underground parking.

Architecture · Low blocks, long terraces

Two low blocks sit among planted gardens, their terraces stepping back so that each home keeps its own outlook. The homes are wide rather than deep, which is what allows every one of them a terrace of at least 65 sqm.

  • The blocks: Buildings 22 and 23, twenty-five homes over three floors.
  • The terraces: From 65.35 to 97.98 sqm, part covered and part open, with a planter on each.
  • The gardens: The seven ground-floor homes add a private-use garden of 138.80 to 441.34 sqm.
  • Inside: Two to four bedrooms, with the living room and kitchen opening to the terrace.
  • Parking and storage: Underground parking and a storage room with every home.
  • Communal areas: An outdoor pool in the gardens, a heated indoor pool, a spa and a gym.

On site

The coastal strip of Casares, between Estepona and Sotogrande, with the beach below and the Sierra Bermeja behind.

Golf

Finca Cortesín is about thirteen minutes away, with the Casares and Estepona courses in between.

Day to day

Estepona is about eighteen minutes for shops, schools and a hospital, and the beach is ten.

Getting around

The A-7 runs alongside, which puts Sotogrande, Marbella and Gibraltar within an easy drive; Gibraltar airport is about thirty-six minutes.
