The development

Mirlo Residences is a development of 29 homes with one, two and three bedrooms in Las Lagunas de Mijas, with terraces and a rooftop pool. The developer’s portal lists 17 as available, from a one-bedroom apartment on the ground floor to a two-bedroom penthouse with a 42.15 sqm terrace.

The portal gives June 2028 as the delivery date. The documents say neither whether the building licence is granted nor how far the works have gone, so we ask for both before any reservation.

Architecture · White walls, timber and planting

The brochure describes pure lines and Mediterranean white in dialogue with nature, with the design putting light and the use of space first. The renders show a timber pergola along the roof and planting along the balcony edges.

The images are computer-generated and the finished building may differ from them.

  • Facades: Rendered mortar or exposed brick over an air cavity and mineral-wool insulation, finished in white, and grey on the terraces.
  • Glazing: PVC window frames with double glazing, and lacquered aluminium roller shutters that black out the bedrooms.
  • Roofs: Flat roofs, paved in non-slip ceramic where they can be walked on and finished in gravel where they cannot.
  • Lift: A lift for six people serves every floor and connects directly to the garage levels.

Specifications

The developer’s own quality specification, summarised by part of the home.

  • A reinforced concrete structure with waffle-slab floors, to the building code.
  • Foundations designed from the geotechnical study, with reinforced concrete diaphragm walls and a reinforced concrete slab.
  • Partitions between homes and with the common areas in dry construction, lined on both sides over a self-supporting metal frame, with mineral-wool thermal and acoustic insulation.
  • Moisture-resistant plasterboard in the wet areas.
  • A solid entrance door lacquered white, with a security lock and an optical peephole, and interior doors lacquered white.
  • Built-in wardrobes in every bedroom, with sliding doors lacquered white, an inside lining, a luggage shelf and a hanging rail.
  • Large-format porcelain stoneware (at least 90 x 90 cm or 120 x 60 cm) in neutral grey, with a rectified edge and a satin finish, in the living room, the kitchen and the hallways.
  • Non-slip porcelain stoneware from the same series on the terraces.
  • Ceramic tiling in the bathrooms, in sand in the secondary bathroom and in grey in the main one.
  • Handleless cabinets in matt white with an anti-fingerprint finish, and a Calacatta-style porcelain worktop and island with a waterfall side. The brochure’s introductory text speaks of quartz worktops.
  • Appliances included: a built-in combined fridge-freezer, a multifunction oven and a microwave in a column, an induction hob, an extractor hood and a black undermount sink.
  • A vanity unit, sink and mirror by Porcelanosa, a toilet, a resin shower tray and a shower screen, with single-lever chrome taps with flow control.
  • Heat-pump air conditioning, ducted through a false ceiling with grilles in the living room, the kitchen and the bedrooms, and a thermostat in the living room.
  • Hot water from solar thermal panels and a storage tank with an electric backup.
  • Mechanical ventilation to the building code, with an individual duct to the roof for each home and a separate smoke outlet for the extractor hood.

Our assessment

What we would weigh before reserving here, and what we check for you.

Why it stands out

  • Who it is forBuyers who want a new apartment with a terrace between Mijas Costa and Fuengirola, as a main home or a second home.
  • What makes it differentA building of only 29 homes with a rooftop pool and solarium, and a garage space and a storeroom assigned to each listed home.
  • Why location mattersLas Lagunas de Mijas is an established residential area, a few minutes from the beaches of Mijas Costa and next to the A-7 and AP-7 motorways.
  • Why check availability17 of the 29 homes are listed. Nueva Living reconfirms price and availability with the developer before any viewing.

What to plan for

  • ReservationThe portal lists a reservation of EUR 3,000 plus VAT for national buyers and EUR 6,000 plus VAT for international buyers.
  • Payment scheduleFor national buyers: 10% plus VAT on the purchase contract, 10% plus VAT deferred, and 80% plus VAT on the deed. For international buyers: 20% plus VAT on the purchase contract, 10% plus VAT deferred, and 70% plus VAT on the deed.
  • VATThe price list shows prices without VAT and does not give the rate. Nueva Living confirms the taxes and purchase costs in writing.
  • Garage and storeroomThe price list assigns each home a garage space and a storeroom but does not say whether they are in the price, so we confirm it in writing.

Checks before any money moves

  • The delivery dateThe developer’s portal gives June 2028 and the brochure gives no date, so we ask for the contractual date.
  • Licence and guaranteesThe documents do not say whether the building licence is granted. We ask for the licence and for the bank guarantee that covers amounts paid on account.
  • The areasThe price list and the plans give different figures: for Home 2 the list gives 59.87 sqm interior and the plan 40.92 sqm. We confirm which figure the contract uses.
  • Which home is whichThe titles of seven plans name a floor one level above the floor on their own table and on the price list. We match each home by its number and confirm it in writing.
