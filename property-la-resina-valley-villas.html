Overview

Twelve villas, six still available

La Resina Valley Villas is a gated scheme of twelve detached houses in a wooded valley at La Resina, on the New Golden Mile between Estepona and Marbella. Six are available.

Each sits on its own plot of 1,000 to 2,010 sqm with a private pool, a garden, covered and uncovered terraces and parking. Every house has a four-stop lift running from the garage to the solarium.

The houses are delivered with three bedrooms, but the basement runs from 236 to 465 sqm and is fitted out to the buyer's brief, which the developer takes to as many as seven bedrooms and seven bathrooms.

Of the six available, built areas run from 508.43 to 976.52 sqm including the basement, terraces from 278.35 to 467.06 sqm. Prices are net and exclude VAT and transfer taxes.

Architecture

Twelve houses, one valley

The twelve houses sit in two rows either side of a private road, following the fall of a wooded valley so that each looks out over the roofs below it to the sea. The design is by Villarroel Torrico and the contractor is Casais. Facades are rendered and painted white, with limestone cladding and a large-format decorative panel on some elevations, and the terraces are shaded by pergolas.

  • The houses — Four levels: basement, ground, upper floor and solarium, served by a lift in every house.
  • Inside — Continuous plasterboard ceilings with recessed curtain tracks, 90 x 90 cm porcelain floors and 2.85 m sliding doors.
  • Outside — A private pool, covered and uncovered terraces, parking for two cars and room for three more.
  • Water — A Hydraloop system recycles shower and bath water for irrigation, flushing and the pool.

FAQ

Common buyer questions

Can foreigners buy property in Spain?

Yes. There are no restrictions on non-Spanish nationals buying property in Spain, whether as a resident or non-resident.

What is an NIE number and do I need one?

An NIE (Numero de Identificacion de Extranjero) is a tax ID number required for any property purchase in Spain by a non-Spanish national. Nueva Living can guide you through obtaining one before you reserve.

What costs should I budget for on top of the purchase price?

Buyers typically budget for transfer tax or VAT, notary fees, land registry fees and legal fees on top of the purchase price. Nueva Living provides a full, current cost breakdown for your chosen residence before you reserve.

Can I get a mortgage in Spain as a non-resident?

Many Spanish banks offer mortgages to non-resident buyers, typically financing a portion of the purchase price. Exact terms depend on the bank and your personal financial profile.

What is the difference between off-plan and completed properties?

Off-plan means the development is still under construction and is usually sold with staged payments through to completion. A completed property is ready to view and move into now.

How does the reservation and payment process work?

Reservation and payment structures vary by development and are set out in full before you reserve. Nueva Living reconfirms the current schedule for your chosen residence at every step.

Can I rent out the property after purchase?

This depends on the individual development and local regulations, which can vary by community and municipality. Nueva Living will confirm the specific rules for a development before you reserve.

Do I need a lawyer?

Yes, we strongly recommend independent legal representation for any property purchase in Spain. Nueva Living can put you in touch with independent lawyers experienced in Costa del Sol property.

What is available?

Six of the twelve villas: OV-3, OV-5, OV-6, OV-8, OV-10 and OV-11.

How many bedrooms?

Three as delivered, with three bathrooms and two WCs. The basement is fitted out to your brief and the developer takes it to as many as seven bedrooms and seven bathrooms.

What goes in the basement?

Whatever you brief: bedrooms and bathrooms, and the developer's own feature list names a gym. It runs from 236 to 465 sqm on the available villas.

Does every villa have a pool?

Yes. Each plot has its own outdoor pool, from 40.77 to 83.07 sqm on the available villas, with underwater colour lighting.

What is included in the price?

The villa, its plot, its pool and its parking. The price list is net, so VAT or transfer tax and stamp duty come on top, and on most plots the basement fit-out is priced separately.

When will it be finished?

Villas 1 to 4 are under construction and estimated for the third quarter of 2026. Villas 5 to 8 have their licence and are estimated for the fourth quarter of 2027. The developer gives no date for the last four.

Which way do the villas face?

South or south-west. The two rows follow the fall of the valley so that each house looks over the roofs below it.
