The development

La Resina Valley Villas is a gated scheme of twelve detached houses in a wooded valley at La Resina, on the New Golden Mile between Estepona and Marbella. Six are available.

Each sits on its own plot of 1,000 to 2,010 sqm with a private pool, a garden, covered and uncovered terraces and parking. Every house has a four-stop lift running from the garage to the solarium.

The houses are delivered with three bedrooms, but the basement runs from 236 to 465 sqm and is fitted out to the buyer's brief, which the developer takes to as many as seven bedrooms and seven bathrooms.

Of the six available, built areas run from 508.43 to 976.52 sqm including the basement, terraces from 278.35 to 467.06 sqm. Prices are net and exclude VAT and transfer taxes.

Architecture · Twelve houses, one valley

The twelve houses sit in two rows either side of a private road, following the fall of a wooded valley so that each looks out over the roofs below it to the sea. The design is by Villarroel Torrico and the contractor is Casais. Facades are rendered and painted white, with limestone cladding and a large-format decorative panel on some elevations, and the terraces are shaded by pergolas.

  • The houses: Four levels: basement, ground, upper floor and solarium, served by a lift in every house.
  • Inside: Continuous plasterboard ceilings with recessed curtain tracks, 90 x 90 cm porcelain floors and 2.85 m sliding doors.
  • Outside: A private pool, covered and uncovered terraces, parking for two cars and room for three more.
  • Water: A Hydraloop system recycles shower and bath water for irrigation, flushing and the pool.

The villas

  • Four floors served by a four-stop lift for six people, from the garage to the solarium.
  • Three bedrooms and three bathrooms as standard, with the basement customisable to as many as seven bedrooms and seven bathrooms.
  • A private pool on every plot, from 40.77 to 83.07 sqm, with underwater colour lighting.
  • Plots of 1,000 to 2,010 sqm, with standard planting and automatic irrigation, walled and gated with a remote-opening pedestrian door and a sliding vehicle gate.
  • A Modulnova kitchen with Siemens appliances, indoors and out.
  • Daikin aerothermal heating and cooling with two 230-litre tanks, water underfloor heating throughout and electric underfloor heating in the bathrooms.
  • Technal sliding aluminium doors and windows with solar-control double glazing, 2.85 m high in the living rooms and bedrooms.
  • 90 x 90 cm matte porcelain floors inside, the same in a non-slip finish on the terraces, and 120 x 280 cm slabs in the bathrooms.
  • Axor Hansgrohe and Villeroy & Boch bathroom fittings, with a wall-hung WC and LED-lit mirrors in the main bathroom.
  • Built-in wardrobes in the bedrooms, and storage at garage level.
  • A Hydraloop greywater system that recycles shower and bath water for irrigation, flushing and the pool, which the manufacturer puts at up to 45% of household water use.
  • Lutron home automation, a three-screen video intercom, Wi-Fi on every floor and a digital safe in the main dressing room.

Our assessment

What we would weigh before reserving here, and what we check for you.

Why it stands out

  • What it isTwelve detached villas in a wooded valley at La Resina, six still available, each on a plot of 1,000 sqm or more with its own pool and lift.
  • Who it is forBuyers who want a large house on a private plot rather than a unit in a scheme, and who want to decide what goes in the basement.
  • What makes it differentThe basement is between a third and a half of the house and is finished to the buyer's brief, which is how a three-bedroom villa becomes a seven-bedroom one.
  • Why location mattersLa Resina sits on the New Golden Mile between Estepona and Marbella, fifteen minutes from either and a fifteen-minute walk from the beach.

What to plan for

  • Why timing mattersSix of the twelve have gone, and the four villas of the first phase complete in 2026.
  • Current informationNueva Living reconfirms live prices, availability and payment terms before any reservation.
  • Long-term appealThe New Golden Mile has filled in steadily between Estepona and Puerto Banús, and detached plots of this size are no longer being released here.
  • Personal use and rentalSuits a main or second home, subject to the community rules and the Andalusian tourist-licence regime.

Questions about La Resina Valley

How many bedrooms?

Three as delivered, with three bathrooms and two WCs. The basement is fitted out to your brief and the developer takes it to as many as seven bedrooms and seven bathrooms.

What goes in the basement?

Whatever you brief: bedrooms and bathrooms, and the developer's own feature list names a gym. It runs from 236 to 465 sqm on the available villas.

Does every villa have a pool?

Yes. Each plot has its own outdoor pool, from 40.77 to 83.07 sqm on the available villas, with underwater colour lighting.

What is included in the price?

The villa, its plot, its pool and its parking. The price list is net, so VAT or transfer tax and stamp duty come on top, and on most plots the basement fit-out is priced separately.

Which way do the villas face?

South or south-west. The two rows follow the fall of the valley so that each house looks over the roofs below it.
