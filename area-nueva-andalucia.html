Nueva Andalucía, life around Golf Valley

Living in Nueva Andalucía

Marbella convenience with more residential calm

Nueva Andalucia ranges from walkable pockets near local services to quieter, elevated parts of Golf Valley. Proximity to Puerto Banus is a major advantage, while traffic, views and walkability vary noticeably between streets and communities.

Local Knowledge

What makes Nueva Andalucía different

Golf Valley's nickname is not marketing -- it describes how the area is actually laid out.

01

Golf Valley

Aloha, Las Brisas and Los Naranjos golf courses sit within a few minutes of each other, giving the area its everyday nickname and a genuinely dense concentration of golf real estate.

02

Walking distance to Puerto Banús

Close enough to the marina for an evening walk, without paying Puerto Banús prices or living inside the tourist density.

03

Centro Plaza and the local restaurant scene

A more residential, less polished stretch of restaurants and everyday shops than the coast road -- where people who actually live here tend to eat.

04

La Concha views

Many of the area's elevated plots share the same mountain backdrop as central Marbella, without sitting directly on the busier coast road.
