The development

A development of 96 homes in 12 blocks of up to three storeys in Marbella, set among more than 20,000 sqm of gardens. The developer places it five minutes from Marbella, with the sea in view from the homes.

The homes are apartments on the ground and first floors and penthouses above them, each penthouse with a private solarium over its terrace.

Architecture · Twelve blocks, three storeys at most

The 2022 dossier describes 96 homes in 12 blocks of at most three storeys: ground-floor homes, first-floor homes with large terraces, and penthouses with a solarium.

It calls the design modern and minimalist, with bright, open rooms, and puts more than 20,000 sqm of gardens with Mediterranean plants and trees around the blocks. It says the first phase builds the residential blocks, the communal areas and the gardens.

Our assessment

What we would weigh before reserving here, and what we check for you.

Why it stands out

  • A short road to the deedBoth payment sheets ask for the balance at the deed, 60 or 90 days after reservation, with no instalments in between.
  • Penthouses with a private solariumEach penthouse on the list has a solarium of 49.60 to 71.20 sqm above its terrace, with the sea on the horizon in the developer's photographs.
  • Three pools, a spa and two gymsThe June 2026 dossier describes three communal pools, a spa of more than 200 sqm, two gyms, a coworking room and a social club.

What to plan for

  • Tax is not in the priceThe price list is quoted before VAT. Ten per cent VAT and 1.20 per cent stamp duty add roughly sixty-four thousand euros to the cheapest apartment and a hundred and fifty-four thousand to the dearest penthouse, before notary and registry fees.
  • No completion date is statedThe documents give none. Ask which homes are finished, what the contract says about the completion date and what happens if it slips.
  • Two sheets of payment termsA reservation of EUR 10,000 applies to one group of homes and EUR 30,000 to the other. Which sheet applies to your home decides how much you put down and how long you have before the deed.
  • The price list is short-livedThe list says it is valid for two days from download. Prices can change and homes can move to reserved or sold, so we reconfirm before you commit.

Checks before any money moves

Questions about Monteros Heights

What is included in the price?

The price list gives each home a numbered parking space (two for a penthouse, one for an apartment) and a storeroom, and does not say whether they are in the price. Tax is not included: VAT at 10 per cent and stamp duty at 1.20 per cent are added, along with notary and land registry fees.

When will the homes be delivered?

No document gives a completion date. The payment sheets put the title deed 60 or 90 days after reservation, which suggests finished or nearly finished homes. Nueva Living asks which homes are finished and for the date in writing.

How many homes are for sale?

The scheme has 96 homes. The price list of 3 November 2026 shows fourteen of them: ten available, two sold (22A and 32B) and two reserved (72A and 92A). It does not show the other 82.

How does the payment schedule work?

On the sheet for Phase I and blocks 5 and 6, EUR 10,000 plus VAT on reservation and the balance plus VAT at the deed, at most 90 days after reservation. On the general sheet, EUR 30,000 plus VAT on reservation and the balance plus VAT on completion and key delivery, at most 60 days after reservation.

Is the penthouse pool included?

Not according to the plans: the plan sheets say the pool drawn on the solarium floor is not in the project or the sale price and is an option at the buyer's cost. The June 2026 dossier shows a jacuzzi on the solarium, and the price list marks the pool column YES for every penthouse. We ask what is in each price.

Do all the homes have sea views?

The dossiers speak of sea views and show photographs from penthouse terraces with the sea on the horizon. Which view a particular home has depends on its block and floor, and the price list does not state it, so it is worth checking on site.

Can the prices change?

Yes. The price list says it is valid for two days from download, and the developer can revise prices and availability at any point before a reservation is signed. Nueva Living reconfirms the figure before you commit to anything.
