The development

Vista Alta Suites is a collection of 72 apartments across seven 3-storey buildings on the New Golden Mile in Estepona, with 2, 3 and 4-bedroom layouts and sea and mountain views from most residences.

Ground-floor homes have private gardens, mid-floor apartments open onto covered terraces, and the top-floor penthouses have their own rooftop solarium -- every home can also add an optional private pool. Residents share two outdoor pools, an indoor heated pool, a gym, a spa and a coworking space, all within a gated complex with 24-hour entry control.

Architecture · Seven buildings, framed for the view

The complex is designed across seven 3-storey buildings, with exterior walls finished in a reinforced acrylic render and natural stone accents, and Technal aluminium windows with argon-filled double glazing for thermal and acoustic insulation.

Kitchens are fully fitted by Modulnova with Siemens appliances, and bathrooms combine Villeroy & Boch fittings with Hansgrohe taps. Heating and hot water are supplied by a Daikin air-to-water heat pump, with water-based underfloor heating throughout, and each home has a basic Schneider and Airzone home automation system for climate control and blinds.

  • Two outdoor pools, an indoor heated pool, gym, spa and coworking space.
  • Modulnova kitchens with Siemens appliances and one-piece porcelain countertops.
  • Daikin air-to-water heat pump with underfloor heating in every room.
  • OTIS, Thyssen or Schindler lifts, and underground parking and storage for every home.

Questions about Vista Alta

How many residences are there in total?

72 apartments across seven buildings.

Is the price inclusive of IVA?

No. The published payment structure adds VAT to each payment stage.

Is furniture included?

No. The images shown are computer-generated; furniture, styling and planting are indicative and are not included in the sale.

Can I add a private pool?

Yes. The developer offers an optional private pool upgrade on every home, on top of the complex's two outdoor pools and indoor heated pool.

What amenities are shared?

Two outdoor pools, an indoor heated pool, a gym, a spa, a coworking / business centre, and underground parking and storage for every home.
