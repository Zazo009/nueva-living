Overview

A hillside of 120 homes, ten on the list

Retama Residences is a gated development of 120 homes in low blocks on a plot of 31,000 sqm in Mijas Costa, close to the Cerrado del Águila golf course. The brochure arranges the buildings around a large garden, with paths linking them to the communal areas and to a viewpoint, and leaves part of the plot as natural land.

The developer’s price list of 14 September 2026 shows ten homes, all available: apartments with one to three bedrooms on the ground, first and second floors from EUR 341,000, and three penthouses with large terraces from EUR 780,000 to EUR 885,000, all before VAT. The developer’s portal gives the building licence as granted, the start of works as 11 November 2025 and completion as September 2028.

Quality specifications

What the homes are built with

The developer’s own specification, summarised by part of the home.

Facade and structure
  • A reinforced-concrete structure, and a double-leaf facade of rendered perforated brick with sprayed polyurethane insulation, lined inside with plasterboard and mineral wool.
  • Triple partition walls between homes, with an acoustic membrane, and plasterboard partitions with mineral-wool insulation inside each home.
Floors and tiles
  • Large-format porcelain stoneware floors throughout, with white lacquered skirting boards, and non-slip porcelain of the same model on the terraces.
  • Large-format rectified porcelain on the bathroom walls, and false ceilings in the hall, the corridor, the kitchen and the bathrooms.
Kitchen
  • High and low units in a choice of two colours, with a Silestone, Compac or similar worktop and splashback.
  • Bosch, Teka or similar appliances: an extractor hood, an induction hob, an electric oven, a microwave, a dishwasher, a fridge and a built-in steel sink.
Bathrooms
  • A drawer unit with an integrated basin, white vitrified porcelain sanitary ware and a low resin shower tray.
  • Single-lever chrome taps with water savers and a system that reduces hot-water use, and taps built into the shower walls.
Carpentry
  • A security front door with three anchor points, and smooth white lacquered interior doors with chrome-coloured handles.
  • Built-in wardrobes in white lacquered wood with sliding or folding doors, a shelf and a hanging rail, and lacquered aluminium blinds in the bedrooms.
Climate and hot water
  • Ducted air conditioning, hot and cold, by heat pump, with grilles in every room and one thermostat in the living room.
  • Hot water from an aerothermal unit for each home, and a single-flow ventilation system that brings in fresh air and extracts stale air.
Communal areas
  • A spa with a pool heated by aerothermal energy, a Finnish sauna, showers and changing rooms, and two outdoor pools with saltwater chlorination and underwater lighting.
  • A chill-out area with a barbecue, gardens of native species that need less water, rainwater collection for irrigation, and a concierge area for the building services.

Source: the developer’s building specification, in English and in Spanish, which carries no printed date. It says the finishes may be modified or replaced by elements of equal or higher quality, and that the furniture, decoration and planting in the images are not part of the offer.

Architecture

Low blocks around a garden

The brochure describes low buildings arranged around a large green space, with the garden as the main feature and paths running through it to the communal areas and a viewpoint. In the renders the blocks have deep terraces with glass balustrades, slatted pergolas and stone-coloured walls, set among olive trees, lavender and grasses.

The site plan numbers nine portals, and the price list names four levels: ground floor, first floor, second floor and penthouse. Two of the listed penthouses add a solarium above the home, reached by a spiral stair on the plans.

  • A fenced and enclosed complex with access control for pedestrians and vehicles and closed-circuit television.
  • Electric lifts for eight people to every floor, with automatic stainless-steel doors, suitable for people with reduced mobility.
  • An automated garage door with remote control, and a pre-installation for charging an electric vehicle from the home’s own meter.
  • Aluminium windows with a thermal break and low-emissive glazing, and photovoltaic panels on the roofs of the parking area for the communal areas.

FAQ

Common buyer questions

Can foreigners buy property in Spain?

Yes. There are no restrictions on non-Spanish nationals buying property in Spain, whether as a resident or non-resident.

What is an NIE number and do I need one?

An NIE (Numero de Identificacion de Extranjero) is a tax ID number required for any property purchase in Spain by a non-Spanish national. Nueva Living can guide you through obtaining one before you reserve.

What costs should I budget for on top of the purchase price?

Buyers typically budget for transfer tax or VAT, notary fees, land registry fees and legal fees on top of the purchase price. Nueva Living provides a full, current cost breakdown for your chosen residence before you reserve.

Can I get a mortgage in Spain as a non-resident?

Many Spanish banks offer mortgages to non-resident buyers, typically financing a portion of the purchase price. Exact terms depend on the bank and your personal financial profile.

What is the difference between off-plan and completed properties?

Off-plan means the development is still under construction and is usually sold with staged payments through to completion. A completed property is ready to view and move into now.

How does the reservation and payment process work?

Reservation and payment structures vary by development and are set out in full before you reserve. Nueva Living reconfirms the current schedule for your chosen residence at every step.

Can I rent out the property after purchase?

This depends on the individual development and local regulations, which can vary by community and municipality. Nueva Living will confirm the specific rules for a development before you reserve.

Do I need a lawyer?

Yes, we strongly recommend independent legal representation for any property purchase in Spain. Nueva Living can put you in touch with independent lawyers experienced in Costa del Sol property.

How many homes are there?

The brochure gives the development as 120 homes. The price list of 14 September 2026 shows ten, all available: seven apartments with one to three bedrooms from EUR 341,000 to EUR 595,000 and three penthouses from EUR 780,000 to EUR 885,000.

Is VAT included?

No. The price list says its prices are net and exclude VAT and transfer taxes. VAT at 10 per cent and stamp duty at 1.20 per cent are added, along with notary and land registry fees.

Are a garage space and a storage room included?

Each listed home has a numbered garage space and storage room on the same line as its price, and the specification describes the garage and its storage rooms. The documents do not say in words that both are in the price, so we confirm it in writing.

When will the homes be delivered?

The developer’s portal gives completion as September 2028, with the building licence granted and the works started on 11 November 2025. We ask for the date in the contract and for what happens if it slips.

How does the payment schedule work?

EUR 10,000 on reservation, 30 per cent at the private contract, 10 per cent six months later and 60 per cent at the first occupation licence and the title deed, with VAT added to each.

What is shared?

Two outdoor pools, a spa with a heated pool and a sauna, a gym, a social lounge with coworking, an outdoor lounge with a barbecue, a putting green, a sand court, a children’s area, vegetable gardens, a viewpoint and a concierge area, as the developer’s documents list them.

Can the prices change?

The price list is the developer’s and can be revised until the initial deposit is received. Nueva Living reconfirms the figure with the developer before you commit to anything.
