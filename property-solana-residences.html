The development

Eight apartments and penthouses are available in three blocks at El Higuerón, between Fuengirola and Benalmádena. The developer’s brochure calls them completed residences with immediate availability, and its portal lists them as ready to move in. Five have three bedrooms and three have two, all with two bathrooms and large terraces.

The shared facilities listed by the developer are an outdoor pool, a heated indoor pool, a gym, a coworking space, a social room and gardens, and the development has a show apartment that can be visited.

Architecture · Contemporary blocks, open to the sea

The developer describes contemporary architecture with elegant lines and large private terraces that extend the living area outdoors. In the photographs the blocks step back in white terraces with glass balustrades, facing the Mediterranean.

The homes are in Blocks 1, 2 and 3, on the ground, first and second floors and in penthouses on the fourth floor.

  • Architecture: Contemporary architecture with elegant lines, in the developer’s words.
  • Terraces: Large private terraces, with the covered and uncovered parts measured separately on each plan.
  • Private pools: Three ground-floor homes, 10A, 20B and 30A, have a private pool drawn on the terrace of their plan.
  • Views: Sea views, which the brochure calls panoramic Mediterranean views.

Our assessment

What we would weigh before reserving here, and what we check for you.

Why it stands out

  • Who it is forBuyers who want to move in soon and prefer to see a finished home and its facilities before they commit.
  • What makes it differentLarge terraces on every home, from 46 sqm on the penthouses to 177 sqm on the ground floor, and three ground-floor homes with a private pool on the plan.
  • Why location mattersEl Higuerón sits between Fuengirola and Benalmádena, beside the AP-7 on the developer’s map, with several beaches and golf courses marked around it.
  • Why check the detailsThe developer’s documents give no completion date, licence or payment terms, and two terrace areas differ between the price list and the plans, so we confirm each before any reservation.

What to plan for

  • PricesThe prices are those of the developer’s portal, and the documents do not say whether VAT and purchase costs are included. We confirm this in writing.
  • Furniture and decorationThe developer’s notice says the furniture, decorative items and landscaping in its images are not included in the price of the home.
  • Parking and storageThe documents do not say whether a parking space or a storage room comes with each home. We confirm what is included.
  • Reservation and guaranteeNo reservation amount or schedule is given. We ask for them, and for how any payment is guaranteed through a bank, before any payment.

Checks before any money moves

  • Completion and certificatesThe brochure and the portal both describe the homes as completed, but neither gives a completion date or mentions a licence. We ask for the first-occupation licence and the energy certificate for the home you choose.
  • Two terrace areasThe price list gives the terraces of home 24A as 44.47 sqm and of home 20B as 117.29 sqm; the plans give 46.47 and 177.29 sqm. We confirm which figures the contract uses.
  • Which area countsThe built area on the price list is the decree 218/2005 figure on each plan, and the interior area is a separate, smaller figure. We confirm which one the contract uses.
  • The private poolsThe plans of homes 10A, 20B and 30A draw a private pool on the terrace, but the price list does not mention one. We confirm it is built and included.

Questions about Solana

How many homes are available?

Eight are listed as available: 3 bedrooms in homes 10A, 20B, 30A, 32A and 34B, and 2 bedrooms in homes 24A, 31A and 34A, from EUR 645,000 to EUR 1,254,700. The documents do not say how many homes the development has in total.

Is it finished?

The developer’s brochure describes completed residences with immediate delivery, and its portal says turnkey and ready to move in. Neither gives a completion date, so we ask for the occupation licence and the energy certificate for the home you choose.

Is VAT included?

The portal does not say. We confirm in writing whether the prices include VAT, and what purchase costs come on top.

Which areas differ between the documents?

The terraces of home 24A are 44.47 sqm on the price list and 46.47 sqm on the plan, and those of home 20B are 117.29 sqm on the price list and 177.29 sqm on the plan. This page shows the plans’ figures.

What shared facilities are there?

The developer lists an outdoor pool, a heated indoor pool, a gym, a coworking space, a social room and gardens, and offers a show apartment.
