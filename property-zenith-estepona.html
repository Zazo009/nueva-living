Overview

Forty homes, eight available

This is a closed residential complex of 40 apartments with one, two and three bedrooms, in the El Calvario residential area of Estepona, near the new sports zone, the old town and the coastal path. Eight homes are listed with a price: two on the ground floor with a private garden, four on the second floor with a terrace and a solarium, one two-bedroom home with a terrace and one one-bedroom home. Each has a garage space and a storage room.

The shared areas are a salt-water pool, landscaped areas, a gym and a multipurpose room. The developer’s listing says the works have started, and none of the documents we hold gives a delivery date.

Location

El Calvario, in Estepona

The homes are in the El Calvario residential area of Estepona, a quiet and well-connected neighbourhood near the new sports zone, the old town, the coastal path and the Sierra Bermeja. The developer’s dossier gives the old town as 5 minutes away, the A-7 as 3 minutes, Puerto Banús as 20, Marbella as 30 and Málaga airport as an hour. The listing also names the hospital, the renewed port area and the schools as nearby.

Quality specifications

What the homes are built with

The developer’s own quality specification, summarised by part of the home.

Walls and partitions
  • Partitions inside the home in dry plasterboard on metal profiles with rock-wool insulation, and between homes and common areas in perforated solid brick lined with plasterboard and rock wool, with water-repellent plasterboard in the wet rooms.
  • Plasterboard false ceilings on suspended galvanised steel profiles with rock-wool insulation.
Floors, doors and wardrobes
  • Rectified porcelain stoneware floors with a 10 cm white skirting, and non-slip flooring on the terraces.
  • An armoured front door with a three-point security lock and anti-pry hinges, white-lacquered interior doors, and built-in wardrobes with hinged or sliding white-lacquered doors, a suitcase shelf and a hanging rail, all with chrome fittings.
Kitchen
  • A kitchen with large-capacity matt white base and wall units, a quartz-resin worktop, a single-bowl stainless-steel sink and a chrome single-lever tap.
  • An induction hob, an oven and an extractor hood are included.
Bathrooms
  • Bathrooms partly tiled in ceramic or porcelain stoneware, with chrome single-lever taps and forced extraction.
  • The main bathrooms have a vanity with a single basin, an anti-mist backlit mirror, a toilet and an extra-flat shower tray; the secondary bathroom has a single-basin vanity with a mirror, a toilet and a bath.
Climate and energy
  • Hot water from an individual aerothermal system, and individual hot-and-cold air conditioning by direct expansion, through ducts hidden in the false ceiling with grilles in the living room, bedrooms and kitchen.
  • A digital thermostat in the living room.
Electrical and telecoms
  • LED spotlights in the bathrooms and kitchen, a power point, a TV point and LED lighting on the terraces, a water point on each terrace, and a colour video intercom.
  • A digital network of integrated services with outlets in the living rooms, kitchens and bedrooms.
Garage and lifts
  • A garage with a remote-controlled door, a polished quartz-concrete floor, fire protection to the regulations and pre-installation for electric-car charging at the parking spaces.
  • Lifts that reach every floor and the garage floors directly, with automatic doors, overload detection, a telephone connection and room for 8 people.
Communal areas
  • A salt-chlorinated pool lined with gresite tiles and a solarium area, a multipurpose room and a gym area.
  • Entrance halls lit by LED, presence detectors with timers on the lighting of entrances, stairs and landings, and landscaping of native, low-water planting with automatic drip irrigation.

Source: the quality specification in the developer’s dossier, which is undated. It says that the images are an approximate representation, that the furniture shown is not included and that the project management may make changes for technical or legal reasons.

Architecture

Light facades, wide terraces

The developer’s dossier describes a reformulated Mediterranean architecture with clean, modern lines, large windows and wide terraces conceived as an extension of the interior.

In the images the blocks have white walls with bands of vertical slats in a sand tone, terraces with glass balustrades and pergolas on the upper levels.

  • Structure — Reinforced concrete built to current regulations and the Technical Building Code, on a foundation designed from the geotechnical study.
  • Facade — Multilayer brickwork with insulation and a self-supporting plasterboard lining, rendered and painted in smooth matt acrylic paint.
  • Glazing — PVC frames with double glazing and an air chamber, and thermal PVC slat blinds with injected insulation.
  • Roofs — Inverted flat roofs, waterproofed and insulated, with gravel in the areas nobody walks on and non-slip ceramic flooring where they are walkable.

FAQ

Common buyer questions

Can foreigners buy property in Spain?

Yes. There are no restrictions on non-Spanish nationals buying property in Spain, whether as a resident or non-resident.

What is an NIE number and do I need one?

An NIE (Numero de Identificacion de Extranjero) is a tax ID number required for any property purchase in Spain by a non-Spanish national. Nueva Living can guide you through obtaining one before you reserve.

What costs should I budget for on top of the purchase price?

Buyers typically budget for transfer tax or VAT, notary fees, land registry fees and legal fees on top of the purchase price. Nueva Living provides a full, current cost breakdown for your chosen residence before you reserve.

Can I get a mortgage in Spain as a non-resident?

Many Spanish banks offer mortgages to non-resident buyers, typically financing a portion of the purchase price. Exact terms depend on the bank and your personal financial profile.

What is the difference between off-plan and completed properties?

Off-plan means the development is still under construction and is usually sold with staged payments through to completion. A completed property is ready to view and move into now.

How does the reservation and payment process work?

Reservation and payment structures vary by development and are set out in full before you reserve. Nueva Living reconfirms the current schedule for your chosen residence at every step.

Can I rent out the property after purchase?

This depends on the individual development and local regulations, which can vary by community and municipality. Nueva Living will confirm the specific rules for a development before you reserve.

Do I need a lawyer?

Yes, we strongly recommend independent legal representation for any property purchase in Spain. Nueva Living can put you in touch with independent lawyers experienced in Costa del Sol property.

How many homes are available?

The complex has 40 homes and eight are currently listed with a price: one one-bedroom home, six two-bedroom homes and one three-bedroom home, from EUR 363,000 to EUR 566,000 plus VAT.

Is VAT included?

No. The price list states that prices exclude VAT, and the payment terms add VAT to each instalment. The list does not give the rate, so we confirm it and the other purchase costs in writing.

Is there parking and storage?

Yes. The price list gives each of the eight homes a storage room and one or two garage spaces, and the specification includes pre-installation for electric-car charging in the garage.

When will it be finished?
