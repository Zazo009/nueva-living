The development

Biubay is a seafront development of ten apartments with three or four bedrooms in Estepona, near the marina according to the developer’s infosheet. The five homes listed with a price all have four bedrooms, four bathrooms and a toilet, a private lift, and a terrace of 108.52 to 191.37 sqm on the plans with a small pool drawn on it, and they cost from EUR 6,500,000 to EUR 9,000,000 plus VAT.

Residents share an outdoor pool that faces the sea, a spa with an indoor pool in the renders, a gym, a cinema, a golf simulator, a games room and a multipurpose room, on the amenities level of the plans. The infosheet says the building licence is granted and that the works started in March 2026, with delivery in Q4 2028.

Architecture · Stepped terraces, facing the sea

In the developer’s computer-generated images the building rises in stepped white bands, with deep terraces behind glass balustrades, a stone base, and gardens with long reflecting pools running towards the sea.

The street entrance is a double-height glazed door framed in stone, with timber slatting, and opens into a tall entrance hall with a reception desk. The brochure says that every home faces south.

  • Orientation: The brochure says every home has a south-facing orientation.
  • Interior spans: The brochure gives interior spans of up to 20 metres.
  • Private lift: The plans of all five listed homes show a private lift that opens into the home.
  • Terraces: The brochure presents the large sea-facing terraces as a defining feature, and the plans draw a small pool on each of them.

Residences · Five homes, three layouts

The five homes listed fall into three groups by plan. The table below lists each one with its price.

Homes BY-02 and BY-04

  • Two four-bedroom homes of 288.94 sqm built on the list, with a covered terrace of 121.50 sqm and an uncovered terrace of 47.20 sqm on their data sheets, three parking spaces and a private lift.
  • The prices are EUR 9,000,000 for BY-02 and EUR 7,500,000 for BY-04.
  • Each plan has a living-dining room and kitchen of 117.62 sqm (BY-02) and 116.80 sqm (BY-04), an office, and four bedrooms, the main one of 42.56 sqm (BY-02) and 42.22 sqm (BY-04), and draws a storage room of 82.11 sqm.
  • The data sheets give the same areas for both homes, while the plans draw a terrace of 150.60 sqm for BY-02 and 108.52 sqm for BY-04. The BY-02 data sheet gives no storage room, and its plan draws one.

Home BY-06

  • A four-bedroom home of 299.19 sqm built, with 261.42 sqm of interior area, a share of 92.53 sqm of the communal areas, which makes 391.72 sqm in total, and a storage room of 28.42 sqm on the amenities level.
  • The price is EUR 6,500,000.
  • Its plan has a living-dining room and kitchen of 121.13 sqm, a main bedroom of 32.51 sqm, a laundry and a dirty kitchen.
  • The table on its plan gives porches of 118.50 sqm and terraces of 137.73 sqm, while the drawing labels a terrace of 191.37 sqm and an access terrace of 41.81 sqm.

Homes BY-07 and BY-08

  • Two four-bedroom homes with the same layout: a living-dining room and kitchen of 121.13 sqm, a dirty kitchen, a laundry, four bedrooms of 17.52 to 28.52 sqm and a terrace of 118.87 sqm on the plans.

  • The list gives 285.64 sqm built for both. The BY-08 data sheet agrees, and the BY-07 data sheet gives 249.46 sqm.

Our assessment

What we would weigh before reserving here, and what we check for you.

Why it stands out

  • Who it is forBuyers who want a large new home on the seafront in Estepona, to live in or as a second home, with pools, a spa and a gym in the building itself.
  • What makes it differentOnly ten homes in the building, built areas of 285.64 to 299.19 sqm on the list, a private lift in each of the five listed homes, and terraces of 108.52 to 191.37 sqm on the plans.
  • Why location mattersThe developer places the building directly on the seafront in Estepona, near the marina. Its brochure gives Puerto Banús as 20 minutes away and Málaga as 50, without saying how they are travelled.
  • Why check the worksThe infosheet says the licence is granted and the works started in March 2026, with delivery in Q4 2028. It is undated, so we ask for the contractual date and the stage of the works before any reservation.

What to plan for

  • AvailabilityFive of the ten homes are on the developer’s list with a price, from EUR 6,500,000 to EUR 9,000,000 plus VAT. The documents we hold do not say whether the other five are sold, reserved or not yet released.
  • VATThe list shows the developer’s net figure and the infosheet adds VAT to each payment, so the prices are plus VAT. The documents we hold do not state the rate, and Nueva Living confirms it and the other purchase costs in writing.
  • Payment termsThe developer sets a reservation amount and a staged payment schedule. We confirm the current terms in writing before any reservation.
  • Parking and storageThe data sheets give three parking spaces for BY-02, BY-04, BY-07 and BY-08, and the plans show a storage room of 28.07 to 82.11 sqm. The sheet for BY-06 gives no parking, so we confirm what each home includes.

Checks before any money moves

  • Licence and worksBuilding licence granted. The infosheet says the building licence is granted and that the works started in March 2026. It is undated, so we ask for the licence and the stage of the works as they stand.
  • The completion dateThe infosheet gives the end of construction as Q3 2028 and the delivery as Q4 2028. We ask for the contractual delivery date and put it in writing.
  • The number of homesThe infosheet and the brochure say ten homes, with three or four bedrooms. The availability list shows five, all with four bedrooms, and none of the documents says what has become of the other five.
  • The areasThe list and the plans agree on the built area of BY-02, BY-04, BY-06 and BY-08. For BY-07 the list gives 285.64 sqm and the plan’s data sheet gives 249.46 sqm. The data sheets of BY-02 and BY-04 give the same figures for two different plans, and the plan of BY-06 gives terraces of 137.73 sqm in its table against 191.37 sqm on its drawing.
