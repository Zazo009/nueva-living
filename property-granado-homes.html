The development

Granado Homes is a residential development of 144 one-, two- and three-bedroom homes in six low-rise buildings at Estepona, in the area the developer’s listing calls the new Golden Mile of Estepona. The homes face south, with large windows and views of the Bay of Estepona and the golf course.

Residents share a pool, an indoor gym and a gourmet room, set among green areas planted with native species. The complex is enclosed and watched from a security gatehouse, and the price of each home includes a garage space and a storage room. The developer’s listing gives delivery as May 2028.

Architecture · Six low-rise buildings, facing south

The development is made up of six low-rise buildings. The homes face south, and their large windows look towards the bay and the golf course.

The developer’s computer-generated views show terraced blocks set among planting. They are illustrations, not the finished buildings, and no specification of the finishes has been supplied to us.

What to plan for

Checks before any money moves

Questions about Granado

How many homes are available?

The development has 144 homes and 27 are listed with a price: 3 ground-floor homes with a garden, 21 apartments on the first and second floors and 3 penthouses, with one to three bedrooms, from EUR 321,000 to EUR 558,000 plus VAT.

Is VAT included?

No. The price list states that prices do not include VAT, which on a new home in Spain is normally 10%; the list does not state the rate. We confirm the taxes and purchase costs in writing.

Is there parking?

Yes. The price list shows a garage space and a storage room for every listed home, and the developer’s listing says that garage and storage are included in the price.

When will it be finished?

The developer’s listing gives delivery as May 2028 and no other document states a date, so we ask for the contractual date and the licence status.

What is the payment schedule?

There is no reservation payment. Buyers who are not resident in Spain pay 30% plus VAT on signing the private contract and 70% plus VAT at the notary; buyers resident in Spain pay 20% plus VAT, then 80% plus VAT.
