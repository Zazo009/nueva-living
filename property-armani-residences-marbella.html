The concept

Elegance lies in harmony, not in excess.

Giorgio Armani

Armani/Casa arose from the curiosity to explore new environments, and from a capacity to imagine subtle exchanges between different sensibilities and cultures. The brand is the practical extension of Giorgio Armani’s aesthetic ideal beyond fashion.

At Armani Residences Marbella that philosophy is translated into architecture, materials, proportions and atmosphere. The interiors are designed by the Armani/Casa Interior Design Studio, which works under the direct supervision of Giorgio Armani, according to the developer.

The site

The plot, June 2026

Drone footage of the plot on Avenida de las Piedras, with the Golden Mile and the sea below and Sierra Blanca behind. The land is still undeveloped.

The masterplan

Nine buildings, one vision

Nine buildings of four floors are arranged across three terraced rows, aligned with the natural topography. All vehicles are kept below grade, with 116 underground parking spaces and three independent entrances, so the whole surface of the plot is reserved for gardens, open space and quiet.

Interiors

Materials that age with grace

Each interior is conceived as a harmonious whole, where stone, timber, leather and woven fabrics are chosen for the way they mature over time. Floor-to-ceiling glazing brings Mediterranean light into the rooms.

The Armani/Casa bespoke service allows each residence to be personalised in detail, from furniture to spatial layout, while keeping the integrity of the design system. Turn-key delivery is available.

The team

Three names, one result

According to the developer, the project brings together a Marbella developer with four decades in luxury, an architecture studio ranked among the world’s top 100, and the interior design studio of Armani/Casa.

Developer

Sierra Blanca Estates (Palya Assets)

Founded in Marbella in 1986 by Pedro Rodríguez and led today by Carlos (CEO) and Luis (COO). The developer describes itself as a pioneer of branded residences in continental Europe, with EPIC Marbella by Fendi Casa, Karl Lagerfeld Villas and Design Hills by Dolce&Gabbana.

Architecture

HCP Arquitectos and GVI International

HCP Architecture & Engineering, founded in Málaga in 1986, was named in the WA100 list of the world’s 100 most important architecture firms in 2024 and has more than 3,000 projects in over 40 countries.

Interior design

Armani/Casa Interior Design Studio

Based in Milan and present in 29 countries, under the direct supervision of Giorgio Armani. Complete design work is listed in Miami, Istanbul, Mumbai, London and Beijing.

FAQ

Common buyer questions

Can foreigners buy property in Spain?

Yes. There are no restrictions on non-Spanish nationals buying property in Spain, whether as a resident or non-resident.

What is an NIE number and do I need one?

An NIE (Numero de Identificacion de Extranjero) is a tax ID number required for any property purchase in Spain by a non-Spanish national. Nueva Living can guide you through obtaining one before you reserve.

What costs should I budget for on top of the purchase price?

Buyers typically budget for transfer tax or VAT, notary fees, land registry fees and legal fees on top of the purchase price. Nueva Living provides a full, current cost breakdown for your chosen residence before you reserve.

Can I get a mortgage in Spain as a non-resident?

Many Spanish banks offer mortgages to non-resident buyers, typically financing a portion of the purchase price. Exact terms depend on the bank and your personal financial profile.

What is the difference between off-plan and completed properties?

Off-plan means the development is still under construction and is usually sold with staged payments through to completion. A completed property is ready to view and move into now.

How does the reservation and payment process work?

Reservation and payment structures vary by development and are set out in full before you reserve. Nueva Living reconfirms the current schedule for your chosen residence at every step.

Can I rent out the property after purchase?

This depends on the individual development and local regulations, which can vary by community and municipality. Nueva Living will confirm the specific rules for a development before you reserve.

Do I need a lawyer?

Yes, we strongly recommend independent legal representation for any property purchase in Spain. Nueva Living can put you in touch with independent lawyers experienced in Costa del Sol property.

What is Armani Residences Marbella?

A development of 33 residences in nine buildings on 50,000 sqm in Nagüeles, Marbella, with interiors by the Armani/Casa Interior Design Studio. Each residence has a private pool, and the development has its own spa and gym.

Who is behind the project?

The developer is Sierra Blanca Estates (Palya Assets), founded in Marbella in 1986. The architecture is by HCP Arquitectos and GVI International, and the interior design is by the Armani/Casa Interior Design Studio, which, according to the developer, works under the direct supervision of Giorgio Armani.

How big are the residences?

There are three types: Type A has 300 to 500 sqm of interior built area and four to seven bedrooms, Type B has 200 to 400 sqm and four to seven bedrooms, and Type C has 200 to 300 sqm and three or four bedrooms. Total built area, with terraces and gardens, runs from 400 to 1,400 sqm.

What does it cost and what is available?

The starting price is EUR 4,750,000. Phase 1 is on sale with ten residences, and four have been sold so far. We do not yet hold a price list or floor plans, and we do not know whether the price includes VAT.

When will it be finished?

The developer gives delivery as the second quarter of 2028. The development is sold off plan, and a delivery date can move, so we confirm it against the contract.

Can the interiors be personalised?

Yes. The Armani/Casa bespoke service lets each residence be personalised in detail, from furniture to spatial layout, and turn-key delivery is available as an option. We do not hold prices for it.

How far is it from Puerto Banús, the beach clubs and the airport?

The developer gives 5 to 7 minutes to the Marbella Club Hotel, 6 to Puente Romano, 8 to Puerto Banús and 40 to 45 minutes to Málaga Airport. The figures for the Marbella Club and the airport differ between the developer’s own sources, so we show the range.

Is Rafael Nadal involved in Armani Residences Marbella?

Yes. According to the launch announcement of May 2026, reported by Idealista and FashionUnited, Palya Assets, which develops the project together with Sierra Blanca Estates, is led by Rafael Nadal and Abel Matutes. Separately, the developer names Rafael Nadal and the Palladium Group as partners on another project, Sierra Blanca by the Sea in Estepona.

How do I see it?

By private appointment. We arrange the visit with the developer’s team and prepare the plans and terms in advance.
