The development

Acacia Residences is a finished residential complex at Nueva Andalucía, Marbella. The developer’s brochure describes 70 homes with two and three bedrooms, terraces with views of the Mediterranean and the mountains, and landscaped gardens with two pools, a gym and a wellness area. Ten two-bedroom homes are listed for sale today: three on the ground floor, four on the first floor and three duplex penthouses.

Every listed home has two bedrooms and two bathrooms, and the plans also show a separate toilet. Each comes with one parking space and one storage room. The developer’s portal calls the complex a finished building; the brochure is dated 2023 and gives no completion date.

Architecture · White blocks, terraces facing the view

The homes are in white blocks with curved clay roof tiles, stepping up a terraced hillside above the gardens and the pools. The brochure gives the orientation as south-east and south-west, with terraces looking over the Mediterranean and the mountains.

The developer describes a private complex with 24-hour security, and the portal lists underground garages and storage rooms.

  • Roof: Curved Arab roof tile.
  • Facade: Exterior paint in RAL 9003, a white.
  • Windows: PVC frames with Climalit glazing.
  • Terraces: The brochure says every home has a terrace with panoramic views of the Mediterranean and the mountains.

Our assessment

What we would weigh before reserving here, and what we check for you.

Why it stands out

  • Who it is forBuyers who want a finished two-bedroom home at Nueva Andalucía, with a large terrace and shared pools, gym and gardens, rather than a home still to be built.
  • What makes it differentTerraces of 38.56 to 64.54 sqm on the price list, a parking space and a storage room with every home, and a complex that is already built, so you can see the homes, the pools and the gym before you decide.
  • Why location mattersThe brochure counts three golf courses within a few minutes, and Puerto Banús is about ten minutes away by car.
  • What to checkThe price list adds 7% ITP rather than VAT, and its areas differ from those on the plans, so we confirm the tax and the contract areas before any reservation.

What to plan for

  • Payment scheduleThe portal gives EUR 4,900 on signing the private purchase contract and the rest on signing the title deed.
  • TaxThe price list does not add VAT. It says prices must be increased by 7% ITP, the property transfer tax. Nueva Living confirms the tax and the purchase costs in writing.
  • What the price includesEach home comes with one parking space and one storage room, as the price list says, and the brochure says the parking is underground. The price list does not mention furniture.
  • ReservationThe documents give no reservation amount or conditions. We ask for them, and for how the reservation is held, before any payment.

Checks before any money moves

  • Licences and completionThe portal lists the complex as a finished building and the brochure is dated 2023. The plans are titled as a project to fit out the interiors and the outdoor communal areas, dated 28 February 2023. We ask for the licence of first occupation and the energy certificate, and confirm that the homes are new.
  • The taxThe price list says prices must be increased by 7% ITP and does not mention VAT. We confirm which tax applies to each home and the total cost, in writing.
  • The areasFor the first-floor homes the price list gives 113.24 sqm interior, 145.68 sqm built and a 38.56 sqm terrace, and the plan gives 93.17 sqm useful, 113.13 sqm built and a 39.14 sqm terrace. We confirm which figures the contract uses.
  • The list of homesThe portal lists ten homes and the price list file lists eleven, the extra one being a penthouse in block 8. The brochure counts 70 homes of two and three bedrooms, and only two-bedroom homes are listed. We confirm what is available when you ask.
