Overview

Eight villas, three types

Valle Romano Crest Villas is a group of eight detached houses inside the Valle Romano golf resort in Estepona, in three types. Each stands on its own plot of 400 to 1,002 sqm and is drawn with a private infinity-edge pool, a garden and parking.

The houses are built on two floors into the slope, with the living room, the kitchen and the pool on the ground floor and the bedrooms above. Villas 1 and 2 are the compact Type I, Villas 3 to 7 the Type II, and Villa 8 is the single, larger Type III, with 433 sqm of interior on a plot of 1,002 sqm.

The project is off-plan. The developer's price list of October 2026 gives a favourable report on the building licence with the grant still pending, the construction tender completed and the contractor not yet chosen, and expects completion in Q4 2028.

Architecture

Contemporary, open to the terraces

The specification describes a silk-grey façade, graphite aluminium frames and pergolas in a wood-effect aluminium, with large glazed openings from the living rooms to the terraces and pools.

  • Included finish — The Contemporary specification: Keraben porcelain floors inside and on the terraces, a kitchen island with Siemens appliances and an induction hob with built-in extraction.
  • Glazing — Thermal-break aluminium by Exlabesa or similar, with double glazing and low-emissivity glass.
  • Comfort — LG ducted air conditioning with Airzone zone control, mechanical ventilation with heat recovery, and electric underfloor heating in the bathrooms.
  • Energy — Aerothermal hot water from a 210-litre Ariston heat pump and a pre-installation for electric car charging. Project rating B for energy use and A for emissions.
  • The pools — Each villa is drawn with an infinity-edge pool in reinforced concrete, lined in sand-coloured mosaic, with salt chlorination.
  • Options — At extra cost: a basement, wine cellar, gym, sauna, cinema, golf simulator, roof terrace, outdoor kitchen, pool heating, home automation, solar panels and a lift where feasible.

FAQ

Common buyer questions

Can foreigners buy property in Spain?

Yes. There are no restrictions on non-Spanish nationals buying property in Spain, whether as a resident or non-resident.

What is an NIE number and do I need one?

An NIE (Numero de Identificacion de Extranjero) is a tax ID number required for any property purchase in Spain by a non-Spanish national. Nueva Living can guide you through obtaining one before you reserve.

What costs should I budget for on top of the purchase price?

Buyers typically budget for transfer tax or VAT, notary fees, land registry fees and legal fees on top of the purchase price. Nueva Living provides a full, current cost breakdown for your chosen residence before you reserve.

Can I get a mortgage in Spain as a non-resident?

Many Spanish banks offer mortgages to non-resident buyers, typically financing a portion of the purchase price. Exact terms depend on the bank and your personal financial profile.

What is the difference between off-plan and completed properties?

Off-plan means the development is still under construction and is usually sold with staged payments through to completion. A completed property is ready to view and move into now.

How does the reservation and payment process work?

Reservation and payment structures vary by development and are set out in full before you reserve. Nueva Living reconfirms the current schedule for your chosen residence at every step.

Can I rent out the property after purchase?

This depends on the individual development and local regulations, which can vary by community and municipality. Nueva Living will confirm the specific rules for a development before you reserve.

Do I need a lawyer?

Yes, we strongly recommend independent legal representation for any property purchase in Spain. Nueva Living can put you in touch with independent lawyers experienced in Costa del Sol property.

What do the villas cost?

From €1,295,000 to €2,100,000 on the developer's price list of October 2026: €1,295,000 for Villas 1 and 2, €1,495,000 for Villas 3 to 5, €1,590,000 for Villas 6 and 7 and €2,100,000 for Villa 8.

Is VAT included?

The price list does not say. On a new-build villa in Spain VAT is charged at 10% on top of the purchase price; we confirm in writing which applies before you reserve.

How are the payments staged?

€10,000 on reservation, deducted from the first payment; 20% on signing the purchase contract, 20% when the structure is complete and 60% at the title deed.

Does it have a building licence?

The price list reports a favourable report with the grant still pending. Nueva Living confirms the grant in writing before any money moves.

When is it finished?

Completion is expected in Q4 2028. The construction tender is complete and the contractor has not yet been chosen; the date in the purchase contract is the one that governs.

How many bedrooms do the villas have?

The price list and the summary on every plan sheet give four bedrooms and four bathrooms, but the room schedules on every villa's floor plans name three bedrooms. Villas 3 to 8 have three bathrooms for the bedrooms and a fourth bathroom or guest WC; Villas 1 and 2 have three bathrooms and a dressing room, and Villa 8 adds a basement with two multi-use rooms. We confirm the layout of each villa against the contract drawings.

How big are the villas?

From 122 to 433 sqm of interior and 279 to 604 sqm built in total, on plots of 400 to 1,002 sqm.

What is included and what is optional?

The Contemporary specification is included, with air conditioning, ventilation with heat recovery, aerothermal hot water and the pool. A basement, cellar, gym, sauna, cinema, roof terrace, outdoor kitchen, pool heating, home automation, solar panels and a lift are paid options; the garden is delivered as topsoil.
