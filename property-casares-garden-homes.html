Overview

One hundred and thirty-four apartments, 14 listed

This is a development of 134 apartments with two, three or four bedrooms in Casares, in the province of Málaga, and it is under construction. 14 homes are listed with a size and a price. The developer’s masterplan places it on plot 56 of sector UR-12 in Casares and shows 9 blocks with 17 numbered entrances, with a communal swimming pool at the centre of the plan.

The infosheet lists an open-plan, fully fitted kitchen, hot and cold air conditioning, parking and storage, a communal pool, a gym, built-in wardrobes and a south and south-west orientation. The building specification lists a landscaped development with local plant species, a communal pool and premises for a gym.

Quality specifications

What the homes are built with

The developer’s own building specification, summarised by part of the home.

Floors and walls
  • Porcelain stoneware flooring in 60 by 60 cm format throughout the home, non-slip on the terraces, and matt paint on the walls.
  • Rectified stoneware wall tiling in 30 by 90 cm format in the bathrooms, showers and above the washbasins, and plasterboard false ceilings in the hall, kitchen and bathrooms.
Partitions and insulation
  • Dry partition walls of plasterboard on metal profiles, with mineral wool for thermal and acoustic insulation and water-resistant board in the wet areas.
  • Brick walls with plasterboard lining and insulation between homes, and between homes and the common areas.
Doors and wardrobes
  • A reinforced entrance door with a three-point security lock, anti-lever hinges and a panoramic spyhole, and lacquered semi-solid interior doors with stainless steel or chrome fittings.
  • Built-in wardrobes lined inside, with a hanging rail and doors matching the interior doors.
Kitchen
  • Fully furnished kitchens with large upper and lower units in laminate, a Silestone worktop and a large stainless steel sink.
  • Equipment: an integrated extractor hood, a stainless steel oven and an integrated microwave, a 3-zone induction hob, an integrated fridge, an integrated dishwasher, a washing machine (stainless steel or built-in, depending on the kitchen type) and LED lighting in the upper unit.
Bathrooms
  • Main and secondary bathrooms with a toilet, a washbasin and a shower tray, and toilets with a toilet and a washbasin. Suspended vitrified porcelain washbasins, a drawer unit with a matching top in the main bathroom, white non-slip rectangular shower trays and chrome taps.
  • A mirror in the main bathroom. Shower screens are offered as an optional extra at a cost.
Climate and installations
  • Air conditioning by heat pump, with ducts in the false ceiling and grilles in the living rooms and bedrooms, and hot water from an individual aerothermal unit.
  • A high degree of electrification, with telephone points in the living room, kitchen and bedrooms and a collective TV aerial with outlets in the living room, kitchen, bedrooms and ground-floor terraces.
Common areas
  • Landscaped grounds with local plant species, a communal swimming pool and premises for a gym.
  • Porcelain stoneware in the entrance halls, corridors and stairs, low-energy lighting, a garage with a remote-controlled motorised door, and lifts with automatic doors, a mirror and a telephone link.

Source: the developer’s building specification, dated 23 September 2024. The developer says it is indicative and may change for technical or legal reasons, or where the architect finds it necessary, in which case materials are replaced by others of equal or higher quality. Some tile and sanitaryware brands are named in it. The specification lists no loose furniture.

FAQ

Common buyer questions

Can foreigners buy property in Spain?

Yes. There are no restrictions on non-Spanish nationals buying property in Spain, whether as a resident or non-resident.

What is an NIE number and do I need one?

An NIE (Numero de Identificacion de Extranjero) is a tax ID number required for any property purchase in Spain by a non-Spanish national. Nueva Living can guide you through obtaining one before you reserve.

What costs should I budget for on top of the purchase price?

Buyers typically budget for transfer tax or VAT, notary fees, land registry fees and legal fees on top of the purchase price. Nueva Living provides a full, current cost breakdown for your chosen residence before you reserve.

Can I get a mortgage in Spain as a non-resident?

Many Spanish banks offer mortgages to non-resident buyers, typically financing a portion of the purchase price. Exact terms depend on the bank and your personal financial profile.

What is the difference between off-plan and completed properties?

Off-plan means the development is still under construction and is usually sold with staged payments through to completion. A completed property is ready to view and move into now.

How does the reservation and payment process work?

Reservation and payment structures vary by development and are set out in full before you reserve. Nueva Living reconfirms the current schedule for your chosen residence at every step.

Can I rent out the property after purchase?

This depends on the individual development and local regulations, which can vary by community and municipality. Nueva Living will confirm the specific rules for a development before you reserve.

Do I need a lawyer?

Yes, we strongly recommend independent legal representation for any property purchase in Spain. Nueva Living can put you in touch with independent lawyers experienced in Costa del Sol property.
