The development

This is a development of 134 apartments with two, three or four bedrooms in Casares, in the province of Málaga, and it is under construction. 14 homes are listed with a size and a price. The developer’s masterplan places it on plot 56 of sector UR-12 in Casares and shows 9 blocks with 17 numbered entrances, with a communal swimming pool at the centre of the plan.

The infosheet lists an open-plan, fully fitted kitchen, hot and cold air conditioning, parking and storage, a communal pool, a gym, built-in wardrobes and a south and south-west orientation. The building specification lists a landscaped development with local plant species, a communal pool and premises for a gym.

Architecture · Terraced blocks, gardens and a central pool

The developer’s renders show blocks of stepped terraces with ground-floor patios and lawns, glass balustrades on the upper terraces and slatted shading above them, set around a curving communal swimming pool and planted slopes.

The masterplan is version 8, of January 2024. The developer marks it as not definitive: it follows the execution project and may change for technical or legal reasons, and the areas on it are approximate.

  • Structure: Reinforced concrete foundations set by the geotechnical study, and a reinforced concrete structure except for some metal pillars, to the building code.
  • Facade: A multi-layer facade with brick masonry outside, insulation and an inner lining, finished in painted render in several colours.
  • Glazing: PVC exterior windows with Climalit-type double glazing, treated according to the facade, and blinds in the bedrooms.
  • Roofs: Flat roofs waterproofed and insulated with rigid boards, finished in gravel where they cannot be walked on and in non-slip ceramic paving where they can.

Specifications

The developer’s own building specification, summarised by part of the home.

  • Porcelain stoneware flooring in 60 by 60 cm format throughout the home, non-slip on the terraces, and matt paint on the walls.
  • Rectified stoneware wall tiling in 30 by 90 cm format in the bathrooms, showers and above the washbasins, and plasterboard false ceilings in the hall, kitchen and bathrooms.
  • Dry partition walls of plasterboard on metal profiles, with mineral wool for thermal and acoustic insulation and water-resistant board in the wet areas.
  • Brick walls with plasterboard lining and insulation between homes, and between homes and the common areas.
  • A reinforced entrance door with a three-point security lock, anti-lever hinges and a panoramic spyhole, and lacquered semi-solid interior doors with stainless steel or chrome fittings.
  • Built-in wardrobes lined inside, with a hanging rail and doors matching the interior doors.
  • Fully furnished kitchens with large upper and lower units in laminate, a Silestone worktop and a large stainless steel sink.
  • Equipment: an integrated extractor hood, a stainless steel oven and an integrated microwave, a 3-zone induction hob, an integrated fridge, an integrated dishwasher, a washing machine (stainless steel or built-in, depending on the kitchen type) and LED lighting in the upper unit.
  • Main and secondary bathrooms with a toilet, a washbasin and a shower tray, and toilets with a toilet and a washbasin. Suspended vitrified porcelain washbasins, a drawer unit with a matching top in the main bathroom, white non-slip rectangular shower trays and chrome taps.
  • A mirror in the main bathroom. Shower screens are offered as an optional extra at a cost.
  • Air conditioning by heat pump, with ducts in the false ceiling and grilles in the living rooms and bedrooms, and hot water from an individual aerothermal unit.
  • A high degree of electrification, with telephone points in the living room, kitchen and bedrooms and a collective TV aerial with outlets in the living room, kitchen, bedrooms and ground-floor terraces.
  • Landscaped grounds with local plant species, a communal swimming pool and premises for a gym.
  • Porcelain stoneware in the entrance halls, corridors and stairs, low-energy lighting, a garage with a remote-controlled motorised door, and lifts with automatic doors, a mirror and a telephone link.

Source: the developer’s building specification, dated 23 September 2024. The developer says it is indicative and may change for technical or legal reasons, or where the architect finds it necessary, in which case materials are replaced by others of equal or higher quality. Some tile and sanitaryware brands are named in it. The specification lists no loose furniture.

Our assessment

What we would weigh before reserving here, and what we check for you.

Why it stands out

  • Who it is forBuyers who want an apartment of two to four bedrooms in Casares, in a development that is already under construction, with a communal pool and a gym, and who accept that the contractual date is still to be confirmed.
  • What makes it differentA communal swimming pool at the centre of a masterplan of 9 blocks, a building licence the infosheet lists as in place, and show-home photographs of ground-floor and duplex homes.
  • Why location mattersThe masterplan places it on plot 56 of sector UR-12 in Casares. The infosheet lists a 15-minute walk to the beach, and its map marks the site inland from the coast, to the north-west of Casares del Sol and Casares Costa. It gives no other distances.
  • Why check availability14 of the 134 homes are currently listed, and prices differ between homes of the same size. Nueva Living reconfirms price and availability with the developer before any viewing.

What to plan for

  • Payment scheduleFor non-residents, 30% plus VAT on signing the private purchase contract and 70% plus VAT on completion. For residents, 20% plus VAT on signing and 80% plus VAT on completion. The infosheet gives no reservation amount.
  • GuaranteeThe infosheet says the amounts paid are covered by a bank guarantee. We ask for the guarantee document before any payment.
  • VATThe infosheet states the instalments as plus VAT, which indicates that the listed prices are before VAT. The availability table does not say, so we confirm it in writing.
  • What the price includesThe infosheet lists parking and storage and a fully fitted kitchen. It does not say whether a parking space and a storage room come with each home, or which floor each home is on. The specification offers shower screens as an optional extra at a cost.

Checks before any money moves

  • Licences and guaranteesBuilding licence granted. The infosheet lists the building licence as in place and the first occupation licence as estimated for Q4 2026. We ask for both documents, and for the guarantee that covers amounts paid before completion.
  • The datesThe infosheet estimates the end of construction for Q4 2026 and says the delivery date in the private purchase contract will prevail. We ask for the contractual date.
  • The areasThe availability table gives one size per home and does not say whether it is built or interior area, and the developer says its areas are approximate. We confirm which figure the contract uses, and the terrace and garden areas, which the table does not list.
  • Plans, floors and parkingWe ask for the floorplan of each home, the floor it is on, and whether a parking space and a storage room are included. None of these is in the documents we hold, which have only a general masterplan.

Questions about Casares Garden

Is VAT included?

The availability table does not say. The infosheet states the payment instalments as plus VAT, which points to prices before VAT. We confirm the taxes and purchase costs in writing.

Where exactly is it?

The masterplan says plot 56 of sector UR-12 in Casares, Málaga. The infosheet’s sales office is in Estepona, and its map marks the site inland from the coast, near Casares del Sol and Casares Costa.

What amenities are listed?

A communal swimming pool, a gym (the specification lists premises for one), landscaped grounds with local plant species and lifts.

Are parking and storage included?

The infosheet lists parking and storage as features. It does not say whether a space and a room come with every home, so we ask the developer before any reservation.

Are there floorplans?

Not for the homes, in the documents we hold: the developer’s masterplan shows the blocks but not the homes. We ask for the plan of each home you are interested in.
