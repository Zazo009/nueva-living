The development

This is a gated off-plan development of 116 one-bedroom apartments at Las Lagunas in Mijas. 10 homes are listed with a size and a price. The renders and the placement diagram on the floorplan show two blocks, and the developer lists a swimming pool and gardens, a gym, a coworking area, a barbecue area and parking.

The infosheet lists the building licence as granted, construction starting in the third quarter of 2026 and ending in the fourth quarter of 2028. It does not say whether works have started.

Architecture · Curved balconies, gardens and a pool

The developer’s renders show white blocks with curved balconies behind blue glass balustrades, set beyond formal gardens of hedged beds and palms. The specification describes how they are built.

The renders are indicative, and the specification allows materials to be replaced by others of equal or superior quality.

  • Structure: Foundations of a concrete slab or piles, and a reinforced concrete structure of pillars and bi-directional waffle slabs.
  • Facade: Rough brick walls finished in stoneware and/or painted mortar and/or single-layer render, according to the project design, with an insulating air chamber and dry lining with thermal and acoustic insulation.
  • Glazing: Lacquered aluminium with a thermal break, or PVC, with thermal-acoustic glazing, in sliding or hinged openings, and compact PVC blinds in the rooms the project defines. Ground-floor windows have security protection.
  • Roofs: Flat roofs with thermal insulation under a layer of pebbles, reached only for maintenance.

Specifications

The developer’s own building specification, summarised by part of the home.

  • Laminate or PVC flooring in the main rooms, laid over sound insulation, porcelain tiles or PVC in the bathrooms, and outdoor tiles, PVC or artificial grass on private terraces and solariums.
  • Bathroom wall tiles in 30 by 60 cm format and floor tiles in 45 by 45 cm format by Pamesa or similar, or PVC, plain light-coloured paint on untiled walls and white paint on ceilings.
  • An entrance door with a security lock, anti-lever hinges and an optical spyhole, in enamelled sheet steel with a steel frame on the ground floor, and interior doors with chrome fittings, with translucent glass in those to the hall and living room.
  • Modular wardrobes with hinged doors in the same finish as the other joinery.
  • A furnished kitchen with a fridge, a washing machine, a dishwasher, an oven, a ceramic hob and an extractor hood by Bosch or similar, and a Franke undercounter sink or similar with a single-lever tap.
  • Laufen Pro toilet and pedestal or wall-mounted washbasin or similar, in glazed porcelain, with resin shower trays.
  • Grohe Eurosmart mixer taps and a Tempesta 100 dual-flow shower set, or similar.
  • Ducted air conditioning, and hot water from a heat pump (aerothermal).
  • Cross-linked polyethylene water pipes, an electrical installation to regulations with Niessen switches or similar, TV, telephone and telecom sockets, and an electronic door entry system.
  • Large communal areas with a swimming pool and gardens, a gym, a coworking area and a barbecue area. The English column says barbecue area; the Spanish column says “zona de Chiringuito”.
  • A basement garage reached by a ramp with a remote-control door, with preinstallation for electric vehicle charging points as the regulations require.
  • A lift with automatic doors stopping on all home floors and the basement. The Spanish column gives it as 8 persons (650 kg) and the English column as 6 persons (450 kg).
  • The building is designed for an A energy rating, with photovoltaic panels supporting the electricity of the communal areas.

Our assessment

What we would weigh before reserving here, and what we check for you.

Why it stands out

  • Who it is forBuyers who want a one-bedroom apartment in a gated development with a pool, a gym and a coworking area, and who accept that the home is off-plan, with completion listed for the fourth quarter of 2028.
  • What makes it differentEvery home is a one-bedroom apartment, and the specification lists ducted air conditioning, aerothermal hot water and a furnished kitchen with Bosch appliances or similar.
  • Why location mattersThe documents place it at Las Lagunas in Mijas, and the infosheet map names Fuengirola as the nearest town it marks. They give no distances or travel times.
  • Why check availability10 of the 116 homes are currently listed, and prices differ widely between homes of similar size. Nueva Living reconfirms price and availability with the developer before any viewing.

What to plan for

  • ReservationThe reservation is EUR 12,000. The infosheet does not say whether it is deducted from the first instalment.
  • Payment schedule50% plus VAT on signing the private purchase contract, 10% plus VAT 12 months after signing it, and 40% plus VAT on signing the deed of purchase.
  • VATThe infosheet states every instalment as plus VAT, which indicates that the listed prices are before VAT. The availability table does not say, so we confirm it in writing.
  • What the price includesThe documents do not say whether a car space is included or how many each home has, or whether there is a storage room. The kitchens are listed as furnished with appliances; loose furniture is not listed.

Checks before any money moves

  • Licence and guaranteesBuilding licence granted. The infosheet lists the building licence as granted. We ask for the licence itself and for the guarantee that covers amounts paid before completion.
  • The datesThe infosheet lists the start of construction as the third quarter of 2026 and the end as the fourth quarter of 2028. It does not say whether works have started, so we ask for the progress of the build and the contractual completion date.
  • The areasThe availability table gives one size per home and does not say whether it is built or usable area. The plan we hold gives 34.69 sqm usable and 52.02 sqm built, with a terrace shown separately, for a home that is not listed. We confirm which figure the contract uses, and the terrace area of each home.
  • Plans, floors and parkingWe ask for the floorplan of each home, the block and floor it is on, and whether a car space and a storage room are included. We also ask which wording of the specification applies to the lift and to the last communal area.
