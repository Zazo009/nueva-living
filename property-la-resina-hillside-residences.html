The development

La Resina Hillside Residences is a gated scheme of fifty apartments in four low blocks on the hillside above Estepona, beside La Resina Golf and a short drive from the coast. Thirteen of the fifty are still available.

The available homes run from 97.73 to 138.61 sqm built with 84.01 to 113.16 sqm of usable interior, and have two or three bedrooms and two bathrooms. Every one has a terrace, from 34.46 to 86.47 sqm. Three of the ground-floor homes add a private garden of 33.70 to 77.70 sqm, and the three top-floor homes add a solarium of 69.58 to 73.15 sqm reached by its own stair.

The communal areas include a saltwater pool in the gardens, a gym, a co-working room, a community room, bicycle storage and an underground garage. Parking spaces and storage rooms are sold separately from the homes.

Architecture · Four blocks, stepping down

Four low blocks follow the slope, their terraces curving out and stepping back so each home keeps its own outlook over the coast. The architects are HCP Architects.

  • The blocks: Four blocks over eight portals and three floors, fifty homes in all, stepping down the slope.
  • The terraces: From 34.46 to 86.47 sqm, part covered and part open, with water, drainage, lighting and a TV socket on each.
  • The gardens: Three of the available ground-floor homes have a private garden of 33.70 to 77.70 sqm.
  • The solariums: The three available top-floor homes add a solarium of 69.58 to 73.15 sqm, reached by an internal stair.
  • Inside: Porcelain stoneware floors, false ceilings throughout, a fitted and equipped kitchen, and a double washbasin in the main bathroom.
  • Energy: Rooftop solar panels, an aerothermal heat pump for hot water, double glazing and thermally broken aluminium; the drawings carry an A rating.
