Overview

Fourteen villas, one shared club

Fairway Grove Villas is a collection of 14 detached villas at El Campanario, on the New Golden Mile between Puerto Banus and Estepona. The villas are arranged in three sizes -- Premium, Deluxe and Superior -- sharing the same contemporary design language of travertine-clad volumes, white render and full-height glazing.

Every villa comes with a private pool, a rooftop solarium with a jacuzzi, and an annual membership to the golf and country club at the centre of the urbanisation, giving owners use of the 9-hole golf course, gym, spa, indoor pool and restaurant without a separate membership fee.

Architecture

Contemporary design, built for outdoor living

The villas follow a contemporary design language from a Marbella-based architecture studio with more than 15 years of experience across the Costa del Sol, Sotogrande and inland Malaga. The design favours travertine-clad volumes over white render, full-height glazing and a fluid relationship between indoor and outdoor space.

Facades combine float-finished, water-repellent rendering with travertine marble and porcelain cladding. Entrance doors are solid iroko wood with a smart lock, and flooring throughout the main living areas is large-format natural stone.

  • Travertine-clad volumes over white rendered facades.
  • Full-height glazing opening living areas onto the garden and pool.
  • Large-format natural stone flooring throughout the ground floor.
  • Rooftop solarium with a jacuzzi on every villa.

FAQ

Common buyer questions

Can foreigners buy property in Spain?

Yes. There are no restrictions on non-Spanish nationals buying property in Spain, whether as a resident or non-resident.

What is an NIE number and do I need one?

An NIE (Numero de Identificacion de Extranjero) is a tax ID number required for any property purchase in Spain by a non-Spanish national. Nueva Living can guide you through obtaining one before you reserve.

What costs should I budget for on top of the purchase price?

Buyers typically budget for transfer tax or VAT, notary fees, land registry fees and legal fees on top of the purchase price. Nueva Living provides a full, current cost breakdown for your chosen residence before you reserve.

Can I get a mortgage in Spain as a non-resident?

Many Spanish banks offer mortgages to non-resident buyers, typically financing a portion of the purchase price. Exact terms depend on the bank and your personal financial profile.

What is the difference between off-plan and completed properties?

Off-plan means the development is still under construction and is usually sold with staged payments through to completion. A completed property is ready to view and move into now.

How does the reservation and payment process work?

Reservation and payment structures vary by development and are set out in full before you reserve. Nueva Living reconfirms the current schedule for your chosen residence at every step.

Can I rent out the property after purchase?

This depends on the individual development and local regulations, which can vary by community and municipality. Nueva Living will confirm the specific rules for a development before you reserve.

Do I need a lawyer?

Yes, we strongly recommend independent legal representation for any property purchase in Spain. Nueva Living can put you in touch with independent lawyers experienced in Costa del Sol property.

Is club membership really included?

Yes. The developer states that the purchase of any villa includes an annual membership to the golf and country club, covering the golf course, gym, spa, indoor pool, beauty centre and restaurant.

How many villas are available?

13 of the 14 villas in the collection. One Premium villa, Celestia, is already reserved.

What are the three villa types?

Premium (6 bedrooms, 545 sqm built, from EUR 3,500,000), Deluxe (5 bedrooms, 376 sqm built, from EUR 2,650,000) and Superior (5 bedrooms, 287 sqm built, from EUR 1,950,000).

Is the price inclusive of VAT?

The developer's published price list does not state this. Nueva Living confirms VAT treatment in writing before any reservation.

Is furniture included?

No. The images shown are computer-generated; furniture, styling and planting are indicative and are not included in the sale.

When is delivery?

The developer estimates First Phase delivery by Summer 2027, with Phase 2 completion expected by Q4 2027. The building licence is granted and active. Nueva Living reconfirms the current build programme in writing before any reservation.
