The development

Lagunas Park Residences is a building of 76 apartments with one to three bedrooms and kitchens fitted by the developer, at Las Lagunas de Mijas, between Mijas and Fuengirola. Three homes are currently listed: three-bedroom, two-bathroom apartments on the first, second and third floors of Portal 4, each with a terrace.

The complex has a communal pool in a landscaped patio, a children’s play area, underground parking with storage rooms and lifts from the garage to the roof. The developer’s listing gives completion for the first quarter of 2027, subject to change.

Architecture · Terraced floors, a landscaped patio

The building steps back as it rises, with large terraces framed by glass balustrades on a white and grey facade. Ground-floor homes have private gardens and the penthouses have the option of a private pool on their roof terraces.

The communal pool and the planted patio sit inside the building and are reached from the common stairs. Garage, storage rooms and lifts are separated from the homes by an independent lobby.

  • Facade: Pre-cast concrete or cement render with paint, with colours set by the architect.
  • Terraces: Terraces with glass, masonry or metal separation, and non-slip ceramic paving on the penthouse terraces.
  • Ground floor: Ground-floor homes with private gardens in some cases.
  • Penthouses: Penthouses with the option of installing a private pool.

Specifications

The developer’s own quality memorandum, summarised by part of the home.

  • Floating laminate flooring of class AC4 inside the homes, with a skirting in the same material or white-lacquered board.
  • A reinforced entrance door and white-lacquered interior doors with glass in the kitchen, hall and living room, and fitted wardrobes with a rail and a top shelf.
  • PVC or lacquered aluminium windows with a thermal break and double glazing with an air chamber, and a micro-ventilation system.
  • Base and wall units with a worktop, a single-bowl sink and taps, equipped with a ceramic hob, an oven and an extractor hood.
  • Connections for a washing machine and a dishwasher in the kitchen or utility area.
  • A prefabricated acrylic-resin shower in the main bathroom and a bath or shower in the second, a vanity unit with a built-in basin, white vitrified porcelain sanitaryware and single-lever taps.
  • Low-consumption inverter air conditioning with a heat pump and fibreglass ducts, with the outdoor unit on the roof.
  • Hot water from an aerothermal system with an individual storage tank, and mechanical ventilation of the wet rooms.
  • A video door-entry system and an electrical installation of high electrification, with pre-installation for electric-car charging at every parking space.
  • Garage access through an automated door, with parking spaces and storage rooms, and bicycle racks in the ground-floor common areas.
  • At no cost the buyer chooses the colour of the floor, the bathroom wall finish and the kitchen furniture, and can swap the bath for a shower tray.
  • At extra cost the developer lists an upgraded kitchen, and an upgraded kitchen with all appliances.

Source: the developer’s quality memorandum for this development, dated 20 February 2025 and described as including improvements. It is the developer’s document, and its materials may be changed by the architect where the quality is not reduced. Furniture is not included.

Location

Las Lagunas is a residential area of Mijas next to Fuengirola, with shops and restaurants within a short walk in both towns. The motorways A-7 and AP-7 are close, and Málaga airport is about 20 minutes away.

Our assessment

What we would weigh before reserving here, and what we check for you.

Why it stands out

  • Who it is forBuyers who want three bedrooms and a terrace at a lower price than the coast, with parking and storage included.
  • What makes it differentEvery home in the price list comes with one car space and one storage room in the price, and the developer lists a choice of finishes at no cost.
  • Why location mattersThe building is a short walk from Fuengirola, less than ten minutes from the beaches and beside the planned Gran Parque de la Costa del Sol.
  • Why check availabilityThree of the 76 homes are currently listed. Nueva Living reconfirms price and availability with the developer before any viewing.

What to plan for

  • ReservationThe reservation is EUR 6,600, VAT at 10% included, deducted from the first instalment.
  • Payment schedule40% plus VAT, less the reservation, on signing the private purchase contract, and 60% plus VAT on completion at the notary.
  • VATThe price list does not include VAT, which it gives as 10%. Nueva Living confirms the taxes and purchase costs in writing.
  • What the price includesEach home comes with one car space and one storage room in the sales price. Furniture and decoration shown in the images are not included.

Checks before any money moves

  • Licence and guaranteesThe listing says the building licence is available. We ask for the licence and for the bank guarantee that covers amounts paid on account.
  • The completion dateThe developer’s listing gives the end of the build as the first quarter of 2027 and marks it as subject to change, so we ask for the contractual date.
  • The areaThe price list gives 79.3 sqm of built indoor area, and the plan gives 67.18 sqm of useful interior area and 97.27 sqm built including common areas. We confirm which figure the contract uses.
  • The parkingWhich car space and storage room belong to each home, as the price list numbers them.
