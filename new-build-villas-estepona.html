Buying A Villa In Estepona

What a new-build villa here includes

A new-build villa in Estepona is a house on its own plot, sold from the plans or while it is being built. Most current releases include a private pool and a garden, and many add a rooftop solarium and a basement level. The plot and the built area are published for every development, so you can compare like with like.

The range is wide, from semi-detached houses with a private garden to large detached villas. The starting price on each card below shows where a development sits, and the figures above cover the developments that sell villas only.

Estepona in detail

Three settings, three kinds of villa

01

El Campanario and Bel Air

The villa collections on the New Golden Mile sit mostly in El Campanario, around the golf and country club, with Bel Air at the Marbella end of the strip, nearest Puerto Banús.

02

Atalaya and the golf urbanisations

Inland golf addresses at Atalaya and Estepona Golf, with both detached villas and semi-detached houses. The semi-detached houses have a private garden and usually a communal pool.

03

La Resina, Valle Romano and the seafront

Hillside plots in La Resina and Valle Romano trade the walk to the beach for space and views. A mixed development at Playa del Ángel includes villas on the seafront.

Choosing Between Them

Detached or semi-detached?

01

Detached villa

A house on its own plot, with its own pool and garden and no shared walls. More privacy and usually the largest plots and built areas, with more to maintain: budget for a gardener and pool service whether you are here or not.

02

Semi-detached house

Shares one wall with its neighbour and has a private garden, often with a communal pool rather than a private one. Usually a lower entry price and less to look after, which suits owners who are not here all year.

Common Questions

What buyers usually ask us

How much land comes with a villa in Estepona?

It depends on the development and is published for each one, from a private garden beside a semi-detached house to plots of well over a thousand square metres around the largest detached villas. The plot and built-area figures are in the quick facts of every project page.

What decides the price of a villa in Estepona?

The main drivers are plot size and built area, the outlook (golf frontline or sea view) and whether the house is detached or semi-detached. Basements, lifts, solariums and private pools add to the price when they are included, so compare specifications, not only the headline figure.

Are these villas off-plan, or can I move in now?

It varies by development. Most current releases are off-plan or under construction, sold with staged payments through to completion; where a villa is finished, the card says so. The delivery line on each card shows the status the developer states, and Nueva Living reconfirms it before you reserve.

What taxes and costs come on top of the price?

On a new-build bought from the developer you pay 10% VAT (IVA) and 1.2% stamp duty (AJD) in Andalucía, 11.2% in total, before notary, land registry and legal fees. Some prices on this site are shown plus VAT, so check the basis of the figure you are comparing.
