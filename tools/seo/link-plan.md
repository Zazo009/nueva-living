# nuevaliving.com link-building plan (drafted 2026-11-10)

Scope: earned or legitimately listed links only. No paid links, exchanges, PBNs, forum link drops,
fake reviews, or optimised-anchor guest posts. Nothing here has been submitted, sent or posted.
Research only; the owner sends everything personally.

Status labels: VERIFIED = I loaded the URL or page text in this session. UNVERIFIED = from a search
snippet, memory, or not loadable (reason given). Link follow status was checked in page source only
where stated; otherwise assume UNVERIFIED and expect nofollow or sponsored on most listings.

Google's own rule, for the record (https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies,
section "Link spam"): "Link spam is the practice of creating links to or from a site primarily for the
purpose of manipulating search rankings." The same section lists "links with optimized anchor text in
articles, guest posts, or press releases distributed on other sites" and says paid or sponsored links
must be "qualified" with rel="nofollow" or rel="sponsored". So: brand-name or plain-URL anchors only,
and a nofollow link is fine and still brings readers and brand mentions.

Honest expectation: most directory links are nofollow or low weight. They give entity consistency
(NAP, Maps, Apple Maps) and referral traffic, not much ranking. The links that can move a site at
position 20 are editorial mentions of the data page (sections 3 and 4). Do directories fast, spend the
real effort on the data page.

## 5. Developers' and partner sites

Deliberately not done. Telling you which of our developers publish a partners page requires reading
`crm.realDeveloper`, which must not appear in public-facing output, and the benefit does not justify
touching it. Also, a developer's partners page is not a link source we should ask for from an
anonymised relationship.

If the owner wants to do this privately: for each developer, open its public site and look for
"Partners", "Colaboradores", "Agents" or "Distributors". Keep the result in a private note outside this
repo. Do not contact any developer about links from this plan. Prefer a page that already lists
independent advisors with no fee; skip anything that sells placement.
