The development

The developer’s dossier describes homes of one, two and three bedrooms set within the Estepona Golf course, with open views over the green, privacy and natural light through the day. It calls them modern and connected with the landscape.

The price list of 3 November 2026 names 43 homes in two blocks. It shows 8 with a price and marks the other 35 as reserved; one of the 8 has since been sold, so 7 are available. The homes with a price have two or three bedrooms and each comes with one parking space and one storage room. No document we hold gives a completion date, a licence date or the stage of the works.

Architecture · Curved terraces, glass and planting

In the developer’s computer-generated images the building has long, curved terrace slabs in a pale stone colour and a timber tone, with glass balustrades, palms and planted edges, and stands on a grass slope above the course. The price list and the plans name two blocks.

The quality report describes a cavity facade of perforated brick with render, flat roofs with double waterproofing, and acoustic brickwork between homes, with aerothermal hot water and photovoltaic panels on the roofs.

  • Facade: A cavity wall of perforated brick with external render, sprayed polyurethane insulation, an air chamber and an inner lining of mineral wool and plasterboard.
  • Between homes: Acoustic brickwork with plasterboard on both sides over galvanised steel frames and mineral wool insulation.
  • Glazing: PVC or aluminium frames, tilt-and-turn or sliding, with Climalit-type double glazing, safety laminated glass on the terrace doors and insulated aluminium roller shutters in the bedrooms.
  • Energy: Aerothermal hot water, photovoltaic panels on the roofs as the Spanish Building Code requires, and ducted hot and cold air conditioning.

Specifications

The developer’s own quality report, summarised by part of the home.

  • 60 x 60 cm ceramic stoneware floor tiles throughout the home, and 30 x 90 cm wall tiles in the shower area and on the wall behind the washbasin.
  • Anti-slip, frost-resistant exterior tiles on the terraces and patios. The dossier shows a base tone of floor and wall tile and two further tones as options at no extra cost.
  • A fitted kitchen with high and low cabinets in pressure-laminated board and a panelled refrigerator matching the cabinets.
  • A ceramic hob, an oven-microwave, an extractor hood, a refrigerator, a dishwasher and a washing machine, Bosch or similar. The quality report names a Silestone-type worktop and the dossier a porcelain-type one, in both cases to the project design.
  • White vitrified porcelain sanitaryware, a resin shower tray in the main bathroom and an enamelled steel bath in the second.
  • A shower column with a single-lever mixer tap, Grohe or similar, and in the main bathroom a vanity unit and a shower screen.
  • PVC or aluminium frames with Climalit-type double glazing, safety laminated glass on the terrace doors and insulated aluminium roller shutters in the bedrooms.
  • White lacquered interior doors with chrome handles or sliding systems, and built-in modular wardrobes with doors that match them.
  • Aerothermal hot water, ducted hot and cold air conditioning in the living room and bedrooms with an air supply in the kitchen, and individual mechanical ventilation in the bathrooms and kitchens.
  • Photovoltaic panels on the roofs as the Spanish Building Code requires.
  • TV and ethernet sockets in the living room, kitchen and bedrooms, outdoor lighting on the terraces and patios, a water tap on the terraces and a pre-installed charging point for an electric vehicle.
  • An armoured entrance door, a master-key system, a video intercom at the entrance and at the building, lifts with automatic doors and disabled access, and a gated complex with private access.

Source: the quality report dated 14 April 2026 (revision 01) and the quality dossier, both in the developer’s name. Our assessment

What we would weigh before reserving here, and what we check for you.

Why it stands out

  • Who it is forBuyers who want a new two- or three-bedroom apartment at Estepona Golf, with a terrace or a garden, a parking space and a storage room, to live in or as a second home.

  • Why check availability35 of the 43 homes on the list are marked reserved, and the list says its prices are valid for two days after it is downloaded. We confirm price and availability before any reservation.

What to plan for

  • Availability7 of the 43 homes on the list are available: three two-bedroom apartments from EUR 325,000 to EUR 447,000 and four three-bedroom apartments from EUR 470,000 to EUR 730,000, plus VAT. The list marks 35 as reserved, and one more has been sold since it was issued.
  • Tax is not in the priceThe list says VAT is not included. With VAT at 10% and stamp duty at 1.20%, EUR 325,000 becomes about EUR 361,000 and EUR 730,000 about EUR 812,000, before notary and land registry fees.

Questions about Estepona Fairway

Are parking and storage included?

The price list says each home has one parking space and one storage room. It does not number them, so we confirm in writing that they are included in the price.

How does the payment schedule work?

EUR 12,000 + VAT on reservation (EUR 13,200 with VAT), then 20% + VAT at the private contract, less the reservation, 10% + VAT six months after the contract, 10% + VAT twelve months after it, and 60% + VAT at the deed, on completion and delivery of the keys. The private contract falls due when the building licence is obtained.

When will it be finished?

No document gives a completion date, a licence date or the stage of the works, so this page says only that completion is to be confirmed. We ask for the contractual date in writing before any reservation.

Do the plans and the price list give the same areas?

Not for four of the seven homes. The table uses the price list’s areas and the note under it gives both sets. There is no plan for 24C.

Is furniture included?

No. The furniture, decoration and planting in the images and on the plans are not part of the offer. The kitchens come with the appliances listed in the quality report.
