The development

Aire is a development of 57 homes with two or three bedrooms in two residential blocks at Cancelada, on Estepona’s New Golden Mile, between Estepona and Marbella. The dossier describes ground-floor homes with a private garden, mid-floor homes with large terraces and penthouses with a solarium, and says that every home has a garage space and a storage room.

The price list names three of the 57 homes and marks all three as available: a three-bedroom home at EUR 487,500, a two-bedroom home with a garden at EUR 492,000 and a three-bedroom home on the sixth floor with a 142.9 sqm terrace at EUR 922,000. None of the documents we hold gives a completion date.

Architecture · Stepped blocks, terraces open to the view

The dossier describes two residential blocks of contemporary architecture, and says that every home has open views and an orientation chosen to catch the light. In the renders the white terrace edges step back floor by floor, with glass balustrades over sand-coloured walls.

The ground-floor homes open to a private garden, the mid-floor homes have large terraces and the penthouses a private solarium, as the dossier lists them.

  • Structure: Foundations and structure in reinforced concrete.
  • Facade: Ceramic brick with a continuous coating and white paint, an air chamber, thermal insulation and a plasterboard lining on the inside.
  • Glazing: Windows and exterior doors with a thermal break and thermo-acoustic double glazing, with insulated blinds in the bedrooms.
  • Terraces: Private terraces tiled in outdoor stoneware, and technical roofs that are not walkable, with thermal insulation and a gravel finish.

Specifications

The developer’s own building specification, summarised by part of the home.

  • Rectified porcelain tile, 90 x 90 cm, in cream, with grey as an option, throughout the home, and outdoor stoneware on the terraces.
  • Bathroom walls tiled in porcelain, in matt white 40 x 120 cm or in cream or grey 30 x 90 cm, in the shower and bath area, and plastic paint on the other walls.
  • Plasterboard false ceilings with access panels for the air-conditioning and ventilation units, painted smooth.
  • An armoured entrance door with a security lock, smooth interior doors lacquered in white, and modular wardrobes lined inside with a luggage shelf and a hanging rail.
  • A fitted kitchen with a quartz or porcelain worktop, equipped with appliances.
  • Options without extra cost are the colour combination of the units and the worktop model; options at extra cost include appliance panelling, LED lighting under the wall units, better appliances, an island, an osmosis unit and a wine cooler.
  • Vitrified porcelain sanitaryware, a wall-hung basin with built-in taps and a mirror over it in the main and secondary bathrooms, a shower tray in the main bathrooms and a bathtub in the secondary ones, and thermostatic taps in the shower.
  • Options at extra cost include a backlit mirror, black taps, a shower screen, a bidet tap and full tiling of the bathroom.
  • A heat-pump air-conditioning system for cooling and heating, ducted through the false ceiling, with the outdoor unit on the technical roof, and hot water from an individual aerothermal system.
  • Controlled air renewal, with extraction outlets in the bathrooms and kitchens.
  • The quality specification gives the homes the highest rating, A, for both non-renewable primary energy use and carbon emissions, and the plans note that the final rating of the sustainability seals is obtained only after the execution project.
  • An electrical installation to the low-voltage regulations with a high degree of electrification, television, telephone and telecommunications sockets in the living room and bedrooms, and a colour video intercom.
  • A communal swimming pool, an equipped communal gym, an outdoor sports area and a zen area, and large gardens designed for low water use, with automatic irrigation and walking and rest areas.
  • Bicycle racks at the entrance, an access-control booth and lifts with automatic doors for eight people.
  • Covered and uncovered car parks with a concrete floor and an automatic vehicle door, steel storage-room doors with ventilation and a lock, fire detection and alarm in all garages, and provision for electric-car charging in line with regulations.
  • The home comes with an ownership share in a Club House service building, with a spa, a sauna, an equipped gym and community rooms, managed through the owners’ associations as an extension of the common areas.

Source: the developer’s quality specification and the specification pages of its dossier, neither of which carries a date. Location

The homes are at Cancelada, a residential area between Estepona and Marbella. The developer’s dossier says that Málaga airport and the high-speed train station are less than 45 minutes away, gives 8 km to San Pedro de Alcántara and 58 km to Gibraltar airport, and mentions schools, health centres, supermarkets and pharmacies nearby, a secondary school 190 m away and the Cancelada cycle path. The documents give no address or coordinate for the plot, so the map shows the Cancelada area.

On site

Life here is outdoors: the terrace or garden of the home, the communal pool, the park in front of the development and the Club House a short walk away.

The terrace or garden

The three homes on the list have terraces of 27.8, 48.5 and 142.9 sqm, and one of them has a 44.9 sqm garden.

The pool and the gym

A communal swimming pool with a swimming zone, landscaped gardens, an equipped gym with an indoor and an outdoor area, a sports area and a zen area, as the dossier lists them.

The Club House

A residents’ Club House 450 m from the development, a seven-minute walk, which the dossier describes as already open, with a gym, a sauna, a spa, changing rooms, a café or restaurant area and a raised terrace with sea views. It is owned jointly through a share that comes with the home.

The park and the cycle path

A large tree-lined park lies in front of the development, and the bicycle racks connect to the cycle path that runs round the plot.

The golf

The dossier lists nine golf courses within 11 km: Alferini Golf at 2 km, El Paraíso at 5 km, Atalaya at 6 km, and Marbella Golf Club, El Campanario and El Coto at 7 km.

Our assessment

What we would weigh before reserving here, and what we check for you.

Why it stands out

  • Who it is forBuyers who want a new two- or three-bedroom home with a garden or a large terrace, a garage space and a storage room on the New Golden Mile, to live in or as a second home.
  • What makes it differentThe three homes on the list are three different kinds: a second-floor home with a covered terrace, a first-floor home with a covered and an uncovered terrace and a 44.9 sqm garden, and a sixth-floor home with a 142.9 sqm uncovered terrace. Each has a garage space and a storage room.
  • Why location mattersCancelada is a residential area between Estepona and Marbella. The dossier puts a large tree-lined park in front of the development, the Cancelada cycle path beside it and the Club House 450 m away, and names golf courses from 2 km.
  • Why check availabilityThe price list covers three of the 57 homes and says nothing about the other 54. The documents give no completion date, licence status or VAT treatment, so we ask for all three before any reservation.

What to plan for

  • VATThe price list does not say whether VAT is included. This page shows its prices as listed, and we confirm the VAT treatment and the purchase costs in writing before any reservation.
  • Payment termsThe documents we hold give no reservation amount and no payment schedule. We ask for both, with the dates, before any reservation.
  • GuaranteeThe dossier says that amounts paid in advance are guaranteed in accordance with the law, and that the guaranteeing entity and the bond or insurance policy are still being arranged. We ask for the policy and the account details before any payment.
  • Parking and storageThe price list shows one garage space and one storage room for each home, and the dossier says that every home has both. We confirm that they are included in the price.

Checks before any money moves

  • The homes listedThe price list names three of the 57 homes and marks all three as available. It says nothing about the other 54, so we ask for the complete list and the status of each home. It carries no date other than September 2026 in its file name.
  • The completion dateNone of the documents we hold gives a completion date. The dossier says it was drawn up from the basic project submitted to obtain the building licence, and does not say whether the licence has been granted, so we ask for the licence, the start of the works and the contractual completion date.
  • The areasFor homes 1-2-6-B and 2-4-2-B the price list gives built areas of 121.9 and 100.6 sqm, where their plans give 150.8 and 120.9 sqm, the interior area plus a share of the communal areas. For home 2-5-1-B both give 100.7 sqm. The plans also give a second set of areas under the Andalusian decree 218/2005. We confirm which figures the contract uses.
  • The Club House shareThe dossier and the specification say that the purchase of a home includes an ownership share in the Club House service building, managed through the owners’ associations. They do not say what that adds to the price or to the community charges, so we ask.
  • The orientationThe price list gives the orientation of two homes as west and that of home 2-4-2-B as “NO”, which can be read as north-west or as none. We confirm it from the plans and on site.
  • The images and the plansThe images are computer-generated, and the plans are project-stage plans that the developer says are not definitive and may change for technical or legal reasons. The furniture in the images is not included.
