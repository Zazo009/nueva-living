Costa del Sol Områdeguide Benahavís: where to buy above the coast

A low-density hillside market centred on privacy, golf, space and gated residential communities.

Å bo i Benahavís

A quieter setting, still close to Marbella

Benahavis is not one single residential centre. La Quinta, Los Arqueros, Los Flamingos and the more secluded estates each offer a different balance of driving time, elevation, views and security. A car is essential in most communities.

01

Best For

Buyers prioritising privacy, security, golf and larger indoor-outdoor spaces.

02

Important Trade-off

Most homes require a car, and access times differ considerably between communities.

03

Property Profile

Villas and elevated apartments often offer larger terraces, pools and open views.

04

Location Choice

La Quinta is closer to Nueva Andalucia; higher and western communities feel more secluded.

Lokal kunnskap

What makes Benahávis different

Small, hillside and quietly wealthy -- Benahávis works on its own terms.

01

The dining capital claim

Benahávis has one of the highest restaurant-to-resident ratios on the coast, and locals genuinely mean it when they call it the area's culinary centre -- worth a lunch visit before you view anything.

02

The village itself

Built into the hillside with views down the valley toward the coast, the old centre still feels like a mountain village rather than a Marbella suburb.

03

The Río Verde gorge walk

A well-known hiking route following the river gorge down toward San Pedro -- one of the few genuine walking trails this close to major development.

04

La Zagaleta and the gated hills

Some of the most private, security-gated communities on the Costa del Sol sit within Benahávis's boundaries, which shapes the area's reputation for discretion as much as its scenery does.
