Overview

72 homes, sixteen listed

This is a residential development of 72 homes close to Estepona Golf, in Estepona, with two- and three-bedroom apartments, ground-floor apartments with gardens and duplex penthouses. The brochure lists 108 garages and 72 storerooms in the basement, limited places for golf buggies, a communal pool, a coworking area and a chill-out terrace, and every home has an individual aerothermal system and a furnished, equipped kitchen.

The sales portal marks the development as works started, while the brochure calls it a construction project and the plans we hold are marked basic-project or execution-project stage. None of the documents gives a completion date, so we ask for the licence, the state of the works and the contractual date before any reservation.

Quality specifications

What the homes are built with

The developer’s own quality specification, summarised by part of the home.

Floors and walls
  • Stoneware ceramic flooring throughout with a matching skirting, first-quality ceramic tiling in the bathrooms and the kitchen, and smooth false ceilings where services run, with an accessible false ceiling in the bathroom for the air-conditioning unit.
  • Smooth plastic paint in a soft colour on the interior walls and in white on the ceilings.
Doors and wardrobes
  • An armoured white-lacquered front door with security fittings, an outside handle and an optical peephole, flat-panel lacquered interior doors, and modular wardrobes, hinged or sliding by home type, with a melamine interior, a luggage shelf and a hanging rail.
Kitchen
  • A furnished kitchen with a compact-material worktop, a stainless-steel sink and a single-lever tap, laid out according to the home type.
  • Appliances included: an electric oven, an induction hob, a pull-out extractor hood, a fridge and a dishwasher.
Bathrooms
  • White sanitaryware, extra-flat shower trays, a screen and a thermostatic shower tap in the main bathroom, single-lever taps in the second bathroom and at the basins, a vanity unit with a basin in the main bathroom, and mirrors in the bathrooms.
  • Taps on the terraces of the ground-floor homes and the penthouses, depending on the type.
Climate and energy
  • An individual aerothermal system for hot water, and air conditioning by a heat pump for cooling and heating, through ducts with grilles in the living room and bedrooms, with an indoor and an outdoor unit.
  • The development is shown with an energy rating of B for consumption and for emissions at project stage, which is informative and provisional.
Electrical and telecoms
  • An electrical installation to the low-voltage regulations, telecoms sockets in the living room, bedrooms, kitchen and terrace, an automatic video intercom at the entrances to the development and an intercom at each portal, and a photovoltaic contribution to the communal services.
Walls and sound
  • Interior partitions of plasterboard on a metal frame with thermal and acoustic insulation, and walls between homes of brickwork with a plasterboard lining and thermal and acoustic insulation on both faces.
Communal areas
  • A fully enclosed and fenced residential complex with low-energy lighting at the entrances, on the internal roads and in the gardens, and landscaped areas with lawns, trees and shrubs.
  • An outdoor communal pool with night lighting and salt chlorination, a gastroteca space and multi-sport courts in the communal area, power pre-installed in the basements for electric-car charging according to regulations, and limited places for buggies.
Choices
  • The buyer can choose between finishing options for the general flooring, the bathroom tiling and flooring and the kitchen worktop, up to a deadline set by the progress of the works.

Source: the developer’s quality specification, version V-0.2 dated 5 June 2024. It is provisional and may change with licensing requirements or the criteria of the project management, without compromising quality.

FAQ

Common buyer questions

Can foreigners buy property in Spain?

Yes. There are no restrictions on non-Spanish nationals buying property in Spain, whether as a resident or non-resident.

What is an NIE number and do I need one?

An NIE (Numero de Identificacion de Extranjero) is a tax ID number required for any property purchase in Spain by a non-Spanish national. Nueva Living can guide you through obtaining one before you reserve.

What costs should I budget for on top of the purchase price?

Buyers typically budget for transfer tax or VAT, notary fees, land registry fees and legal fees on top of the purchase price. Nueva Living provides a full, current cost breakdown for your chosen residence before you reserve.

Can I get a mortgage in Spain as a non-resident?

Many Spanish banks offer mortgages to non-resident buyers, typically financing a portion of the purchase price. Exact terms depend on the bank and your personal financial profile.

What is the difference between off-plan and completed properties?

Off-plan means the development is still under construction and is usually sold with staged payments through to completion. A completed property is ready to view and move into now.

How does the reservation and payment process work?

Reservation and payment structures vary by development and are set out in full before you reserve. Nueva Living reconfirms the current schedule for your chosen residence at every step.

Can I rent out the property after purchase?

This depends on the individual development and local regulations, which can vary by community and municipality. Nueva Living will confirm the specific rules for a development before you reserve.

Do I need a lawyer?

Yes, we strongly recommend independent legal representation for any property purchase in Spain. Nueva Living can put you in touch with independent lawyers experienced in Costa del Sol property.

How many homes are there?

The development has 72 homes. The developer’s price list shows 16 of them with a price, from EUR 330,000 to EUR 525,000 plus VAT: 6 ground-floor apartments with gardens, 4 first-floor apartments and 6 duplex penthouses, with two or three bedrooms.

Is VAT included?

No. The price list states its prices without VAT, and the payment terms give VAT at 10%. We confirm the taxes and purchase costs in writing.

Is there parking?

The price list gives each home a basement garage space, two for the three-bedroom homes, and a storeroom, and three homes also have a buggy space. We confirm in writing that they are included in the price.

Are the gardens landscaped?

The ground-floor plans mark the garden as unlandscaped space, and the quality specification describes landscaping only for the communal areas. We ask what is delivered in each private garden.

When will it be finished?

No document we hold gives a completion date. The sales portal says works have started and the brochure calls it a construction project, so we ask for the licence, the state of the works and the contractual date.

What is the payment schedule?

The reservation is EUR 6,000 plus 10% VAT. Then 30% plus VAT on the purchase contract and 70% plus VAT at the notary on completion.
