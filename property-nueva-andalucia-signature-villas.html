Overview

Twenty-six villas, three typologies

Nueva Andalucia Signature Villas is a gated community of twenty-six detached houses in central Nueva Andalucía, laid out along a private road with the golf valley below and the mountains behind. Fifteen are still available.

Twenty-three of them share a size: 461 sqm built on the Typology A houses and 488 sqm on Typology B, each with five bedrooms, a private infinity pool, a landscaped garden, a lower entertainment level and a roof solarium. The remaining three are the largest in the scheme, at 701 sqm built with six bedrooms on double plots.

Plots run from 900 to 1,674 sqm and terraces from 198 to 301 sqm. Each house has either a private carport or a private garage, reached from the road that runs beneath the houses.

Architecture

Three houses, one street

The houses step down the slope on either side of a private road, their upper floors cantilevered over the terraces so that each one looks over the garden below rather than into its neighbour. The facades combine a rendered external insulation system in off-white with stone cladding, composite panels and aluminium sheeting that runs on from the window frames, and the terrace balustrades are frameless laminated glass on a recessed aluminium channel.

  • The houses — Three levels plus a roof solarium, over a lower entertainment level with the garage or carport reached from the road.
  • Inside — 120 x 120 porcelain on the main level, oak parquet in the bedrooms, and full-height porcelain in the bathrooms.
  • The terraces — The same porcelain in a non-slip finish, laid level with the interior floor so the two read as one surface.
  • Energy — Aerothermal hot water and underfloor heating, photovoltaic panels, external insulation and an A-rated thermal-break glazing system.

FAQ

Common buyer questions

Can foreigners buy property in Spain?

Yes. There are no restrictions on non-Spanish nationals buying property in Spain, whether as a resident or non-resident.

What is an NIE number and do I need one?

An NIE (Numero de Identificacion de Extranjero) is a tax ID number required for any property purchase in Spain by a non-Spanish national. Nueva Living can guide you through obtaining one before you reserve.

What costs should I budget for on top of the purchase price?

Buyers typically budget for transfer tax or VAT, notary fees, land registry fees and legal fees on top of the purchase price. Nueva Living provides a full, current cost breakdown for your chosen residence before you reserve.

Can I get a mortgage in Spain as a non-resident?

Many Spanish banks offer mortgages to non-resident buyers, typically financing a portion of the purchase price. Exact terms depend on the bank and your personal financial profile.

What is the difference between off-plan and completed properties?

Off-plan means the development is still under construction and is usually sold with staged payments through to completion. A completed property is ready to view and move into now.

How does the reservation and payment process work?

Reservation and payment structures vary by development and are set out in full before you reserve. Nueva Living reconfirms the current schedule for your chosen residence at every step.

Can I rent out the property after purchase?

This depends on the individual development and local regulations, which can vary by community and municipality. Nueva Living will confirm the specific rules for a development before you reserve.

Do I need a lawyer?

Yes, we strongly recommend independent legal representation for any property purchase in Spain. Nueva Living can put you in touch with independent lawyers experienced in Costa del Sol property.

What is available?

Fifteen of the twenty-six houses. Thirteen are Typology A or B with five bedrooms, and two are the six-bedroom Typology C houses on the largest plots. One house is reserved and ten are sold.

Why is there one plan per typology rather than one per house?

Because that is what the developer publishes. The houses within a typology differ in orientation, plot and whether they have a carport or a garage, not in layout. We confirm which plan applies to the specific house before any reservation.

What is included in the price?

The house, its plot, its private pool and garden, and its carport or garage. The prices exclude the 10% IVA and the 1.2% stamp duty.

When will it be finished?

The developer’s payment schedule puts completion in the first quarter of 2028.

What are the communal facilities?

A communal building with a gym, a heated indoor pool, a steam room, a sauna, a cold room and changing rooms, set in landscaped gardens, with a gatehouse, controlled access and 24-hour security.

How are the payments staged?

EUR 50,000 on reservation, 30% on the private purchase contract within thirty days, 10% ten months later, 10% ten months after that, and the remaining 50% at completion. The reservation counts towards the 30%.
