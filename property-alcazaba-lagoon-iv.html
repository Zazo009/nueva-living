Overview

Phase IV, one home available

Alcazaba Lagoon IV is Phase IV of Alcazaba Lagoon, in Casares on the Costa del Sol. The developer’s information sheet describes 59 apartments of one, two and three bedrooms, and the plans of the phase show blocks 19, 20 and 21. One home is currently listed as available: AL4-1924, a two-bedroom, two-bathroom apartment on the second floor of Block 19, with terraces of 55.52 sqm and a solarium of 109.60 sqm.

The 2024 information sheet lists a swimming pool of modern design, tropical gardens, a children’s playground and what it calls exclusive use of a crystal-clear lagoon, together with large double-glazed windows, an aerothermal system, and one parking space and one storage room with each home. The availability list gives the stage of the development as built.

Quality specifications

What the homes are built with

The developer’s own building specification, summarised by part of the home.

Structure and walls
  • Reinforced-concrete footings or slab, basement walls and two-way floor slabs, with an anti-impact layer.
  • Solid-brick external walls, plastered outside with cement mortar, lined inside with sprayed polyurethane thermal and acoustic insulation, an air gap and a hollow-brick partition.
Sound and partitions
  • Between homes, a double leaf of perforated acoustic brick with mineral wool, and inside each home a thick double hollow-brick partition.
  • Gypsum plaster on walls and ceilings, with plaster false ceilings in wet areas and where services run, and removable false ceilings where the air-conditioning units sit.
Floors and tiling
  • Porcelain stoneware floors inside, in a 20 x 120 cm wood-effect plank, and non-slip porcelain stoneware outside, 60 x 60 cm.
  • Bathrooms and toilets tiled in porcelain stoneware, with smooth plastic paint.
Bathrooms
  • Floor-standing toilets with a soft-closing seat and a concealed cistern, a vanity unit with a drawer, a bath screen and a mirror.
  • The main bathroom has a single-lever mixer with two outlets, a wall-mounted rain shower head and a hand shower; the secondary bathroom has an overhead shower, a hand shower, a thermostat, a hose and a slider.
Doors and windows
  • A reinforced front door with a steel sheet, a security lock and a peephole, and interior doors lacquered matt white, hinged or sliding depending on the plan.
  • Sliding or casement windows with double glazing and an air gap, and glass balustrades on the terraces.
Kitchen
  • Fitted and fully equipped kitchens with a washing machine, a dishwasher, a fridge, a hob, an electric oven, a sink and an extractor hood.
Climate and installations
  • Hot water from a high-efficiency Altherma system, and air conditioning by heat pump through ducts in every room.
  • Mechanical ventilation in the kitchens, bathrooms and toilets, TV, telephone and internet points, an electronic door entry system and a pre-installation for a basic home-automation system.
Shared areas
  • Pools, and gardens planted with subtropical and native species, with an irrigation system and lighting for the whole complex, exterior lighting, and a metal garage door with a photocell and a remote-control receiver.

Source: the developer’s building specification for Phase IV. It is in its development stage, and the developer may alter it for technical reasons without reducing the quality or design of the result. Most items read “or similar” in the original.

FAQ

Common buyer questions

Can foreigners buy property in Spain?

Yes. There are no restrictions on non-Spanish nationals buying property in Spain, whether as a resident or non-resident.

What is an NIE number and do I need one?

An NIE (Numero de Identificacion de Extranjero) is a tax ID number required for any property purchase in Spain by a non-Spanish national. Nueva Living can guide you through obtaining one before you reserve.

What costs should I budget for on top of the purchase price?

Buyers typically budget for transfer tax or VAT, notary fees, land registry fees and legal fees on top of the purchase price. Nueva Living provides a full, current cost breakdown for your chosen residence before you reserve.

Can I get a mortgage in Spain as a non-resident?

Many Spanish banks offer mortgages to non-resident buyers, typically financing a portion of the purchase price. Exact terms depend on the bank and your personal financial profile.

What is the difference between off-plan and completed properties?

Off-plan means the development is still under construction and is usually sold with staged payments through to completion. A completed property is ready to view and move into now.

How does the reservation and payment process work?

Reservation and payment structures vary by development and are set out in full before you reserve. Nueva Living reconfirms the current schedule for your chosen residence at every step.

Can I rent out the property after purchase?

This depends on the individual development and local regulations, which can vary by community and municipality. Nueva Living will confirm the specific rules for a development before you reserve.

Do I need a lawyer?

Yes, we strongly recommend independent legal representation for any property purchase in Spain. Nueva Living can put you in touch with independent lawyers experienced in Costa del Sol property.
