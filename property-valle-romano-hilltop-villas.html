Overview

Five villas, four levels each

Valle Romano Hilltop Villas is a group of five detached houses on the high side of Valle Romano in Estepona, each on its own plot of about 884 sqm with a private pool, a garden and parking.

Every villa is laid out over four levels: a lower level, the ground floor with the living room and kitchen, a first floor of bedrooms, and a solarium on top. The drawings we hold cover four of the five plots and run from 590.76 to 648.05 sqm built. Three bedrooms, each with its own bathroom, and the position on the upper part of the urbanisation is what gives the houses their outlook over the golf course and out to the sea.

The houses are sold before construction, which means the layout and the finishes can still be adjusted. The developer states that the finishes are specified throughout from the Porcelanosa range.

Architecture

Contemporary, and still open

The houses are contemporary: flat roofs, deep terraces and full-height glass to the living rooms, with the upper floor set over the terrace to shade it.

  • Four levels — A lower level, the living floor, a bedroom floor and a solarium above, so the house gains its space upwards rather than across the plot.
  • Glazing — Full-height glass to the living room and the terrace, with the outlook carried through the plan.
  • The solarium — A roof terrace with room for dining and a hot tub, facing the course and the sea.
  • Finishes — The developer states that the finishes throughout come from the Porcelanosa range.
  • Personalisation — Layouts and finishes can be adjusted during construction, which is the practical advantage of buying at this stage.
  • Outside — A private pool, garden and parking on each plot, all within a gated urbanisation.

FAQ

Common buyer questions

Can foreigners buy property in Spain?

Yes. There are no restrictions on non-Spanish nationals buying property in Spain, whether as a resident or non-resident.

What is an NIE number and do I need one?

An NIE (Numero de Identificacion de Extranjero) is a tax ID number required for any property purchase in Spain by a non-Spanish national. Nueva Living can guide you through obtaining one before you reserve.

What costs should I budget for on top of the purchase price?

Buyers typically budget for transfer tax or VAT, notary fees, land registry fees and legal fees on top of the purchase price. Nueva Living provides a full, current cost breakdown for your chosen residence before you reserve.

Can I get a mortgage in Spain as a non-resident?

Many Spanish banks offer mortgages to non-resident buyers, typically financing a portion of the purchase price. Exact terms depend on the bank and your personal financial profile.

What is the difference between off-plan and completed properties?

Off-plan means the development is still under construction and is usually sold with staged payments through to completion. A completed property is ready to view and move into now.

How does the reservation and payment process work?

Reservation and payment structures vary by development and are set out in full before you reserve. Nueva Living reconfirms the current schedule for your chosen residence at every step.

Can I rent out the property after purchase?

This depends on the individual development and local regulations, which can vary by community and municipality. Nueva Living will confirm the specific rules for a development before you reserve.

Do I need a lawyer?

Yes, we strongly recommend independent legal representation for any property purchase in Spain. Nueva Living can put you in touch with independent lawyers experienced in Costa del Sol property.

What do the villas cost?

From €1,590,000, excluding VAT. We confirm the price for each plot before any viewing.

Is VAT included?

No. VAT is added at 10% to each stage of the payment schedule, including the €20,000 reservation.

How are the payments staged?

€20,000 on reservation, 30% on the private purchase contract, 10% in staged payments during construction and 60% at title deed, each plus VAT at 10%.

Does it have a building licence?

The developer’s pack does not state it. Nueva Living confirms in writing where the application stands before any money moves.

When is it finished?

Delivery is given as Q4 2027. The date that appears in the purchase contract is the one that governs, and we confirm it in writing.

How big are the villas?

From 590.76 to 648.05 sqm built across the four plots we hold drawings for, on plots of about 884 sqm.

How many bedrooms are there?

Three, each with its own bathroom, plus a cloakroom. On two of the plots the ground-floor room is drawn as a study or a third bedroom.

Can the house be changed?

Yes. The houses are sold before construction, so layouts and finishes can still be adjusted. We confirm in writing what each change costs.
