Overview

Two homes available, beside the dunes

Dunique Marbella is a residential complex of semi-detached villas and apartments in Marbella, beside the protected dune system of El Barronal de la Morena. The developer’s brochure, dated August 2024, describes 32 semi-detached villas and 64 apartments, a social hub with a spa, gyms, a coworking space and a rooftop lounge, and tiered gardens with native planting. Those counts are the brochure’s, not current ones: the sales list we hold shows two homes.

The two homes listed are villa DU-04, with four bedrooms, and apartment DU-202, on the ground floor with three bedrooms, each with a private pool and garden. None of the documents gives a completion date, and the sales list shows the project as under construction.

Quality specifications

What the homes are built with

The developer’s own quality specification, summarised by part of the home.

Floors and ceilings
  • Large-format 120 x 120 cm porcelain tiles throughout the house and the basement, with an anthracite aluminium skirting built into the wall, and rectified 60 x 60 cm porcelain stoneware or polished concrete in the private garage, depending on the type.
  • Plasterboard false ceilings throughout the house, tray ceilings with rounded corners in the living room and the master bedroom, and curtain pelmets on the windows that face outwards.
Windows and blinds
  • Cortizo aluminium frames with a thermal break and double glazing with solar control, floor to ceiling, with the frames flush with the floor in the living rooms, and automatic security blinds in the rear bedrooms.
Doors and wardrobes
  • A white oak front door at least 2.50 m high, with a fingerprint-reader opening, and interior doors up to the ceiling, at least 2.50 m high on the upper floors.
  • Wardrobes lined inside, with drawers, shelves, a hanging rail and LED lighting with an opening sensor, and smoked-glass doors in the master bedroom.
Bathrooms
  • In the master bathroom, large-format porcelain tiling from floor to ceiling, and in the second bathroom, tiling from floor to ceiling in the wet areas and half-height tiling with wallpaper or paint elsewhere.
  • A free-standing solid-surface bathtub, Duravit suspended toilets and bidets, with a Sensowash toilet in the main bathrooms only, double sinks in mineral resin in the main bathrooms, matt black taps with a thermostatic shower and a hand shower, and an anti-fog backlit mirror.
Kitchen
  • Kitchen units in E1-class melamine with 20 mm matt lacquered fronts, and Gaggenau series 200 appliances: an oven, a microwave, an induction hob with integrated extraction according to the type, a dishwasher, a refrigerator and a freezer, with a wine cooler.
  • Outdoor kitchens with a porcelain worktop, a stainless-steel sink and water and electricity points, and a utility room with a heat-pump dryer and a washing machine.
Home automation
  • An Iddero system on the KNX standard that controls the lighting, the blinds and the climate, with a 10.1 inch touch panel at the entrance that doubles as the video entry screen, a 4.3 inch panel in each room, and flood alarms with a general water cut-off.
Climate and energy
  • Mitsubishi air conditioning with temperature zoning and concealed linear diffusers, under-floor heating throughout the house except the basement, and hot water from an aerothermal heat pump.
  • Ventilation with heat recovery, an electronic water softener, a system that treats greywater for the cisterns, and a charging point for electric vehicles in the garage.
Security, garage and lifts
  • A perimeter security system with cameras and heat sensors, monitored by the development’s security team, and CCTV in the common areas, the garages and the lifts.
  • A basement garage with remote-controlled doors, a polished concrete floor waterproofed for pressure washing and a direct pedestrian entry to the house, and electric lifts with four stops: for six people in the villas, according to the type, and for eight in the apartments.

Contemporary lines, beside the sea

The brochure describes a contemporary complex of semi-detached villas and apartments with large terraces, in tiered gardens that slope down towards the sea, with native, low-water planting, ponds and small waterfalls.

FAQ

Common buyer questions

Can foreigners buy property in Spain?

Yes. There are no restrictions on non-Spanish nationals buying property in Spain, whether as a resident or non-resident.

What is an NIE number and do I need one?

An NIE (Numero de Identificacion de Extranjero) is a tax ID number required for any property purchase in Spain by a non-Spanish national. Nueva Living can guide you through obtaining one before you reserve.

What costs should I budget for on top of the purchase price?

Buyers typically budget for transfer tax or VAT, notary fees, land registry fees and legal fees on top of the purchase price. Nueva Living provides a full, current cost breakdown for your chosen residence before you reserve.

Can I get a mortgage in Spain as a non-resident?

Many Spanish banks offer mortgages to non-resident buyers, typically financing a portion of the purchase price. Exact terms depend on the bank and your personal financial profile.

What is the difference between off-plan and completed properties?

Off-plan means the development is still under construction and is usually sold with staged payments through to completion. A completed property is ready to view and move into now.

How does the reservation and payment process work?

Reservation and payment structures vary by development and are set out in full before you reserve. Nueva Living reconfirms the current schedule for your chosen residence at every step.

Can I rent out the property after purchase?

This depends on the individual development and local regulations, which can vary by community and municipality. Nueva Living will confirm the specific rules for a development before you reserve.

Do I need a lawyer?

Yes, we strongly recommend independent legal representation for any property purchase in Spain. Nueva Living can put you in touch with independent lawyers experienced in Costa del Sol property.
