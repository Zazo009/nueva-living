The development

Meridian Golf Villas is a boutique collection of 14 semi-detached villas next to El Campanario golf course, on the New Golden Mile between Estepona and San Pedro de Alcantara, with 4-bedroom, 4-bathroom layouts across four levels.

Every villa has a private garden, a basement, spacious terraces and a solarium with a pergola, with underground parking for two cars. Residents share a communal outdoor pool with an infinity edge, a fully equipped gym and a wellness area with spa and jacuzzi, within a gated complex with monitored entry.

Architecture · Four levels, designed around the golf course

The complex is designed as 14 semi-detached villas with a communal garage and amenities at the centre, an organic layout that adapts to the topography to enhance views, orientation and privacy for each home.

Each villa spans a basement, ground floor, first floor and solarium. Ground floors are fully open-plan, integrating the living-dining room and kitchen with the garden and terrace; the four bedrooms are upstairs, each with large windows for natural light.

  • Communal outdoor pool with an infinity edge, a fully equipped gym and a wellness area with spa and jacuzzi.
  • Private garden, basement and solarium with pergola in every villa.
  • Underground parking for two cars per villa.
  • Personalisation options: a private pool, a private lift connecting all floors, and an EV charging point.
