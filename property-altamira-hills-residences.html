The development

Altamira Hills Residences is a development of apartments and duplex penthouses in the upper part of Los Monteros, east of Marbella. The listed homes have two or three bedrooms, two bathrooms and large terraces with sea views, and some ground-floor homes also have a private garden. The buildings step up the hillside in four heights.

The community has an outdoor pool, a heated indoor pool, a spa, a gym with its own terrace, a pool bar and a coworking area, set in landscaped gardens. The developer’s documents do not give a completion date.

Architecture · White terraces, stepped to the sea

The buildings are designed around light and views, in four heights with a duplex penthouse with a solarium at the top. Wide terraces run along each floor, and the living rooms open onto them through large sliding doors.

The renders show white volumes with dark accents, glass balustrades and planted gardens between the blocks.

  • Facade: A continuous mortar coating over high-quality thermal insulation, with insulated plasterboard lining inside.
  • Terraces: Large slabs of non-slip porcelain stoneware, with continuity between the terrace and the living room.
  • Glazing: Lacquered aluminium frames with a thermal break and sound-insulating double glazing.
  • Structure: Reinforced concrete foundations and structure.

Specifications

The developer’s own quality specification, summarised by part of the home.

  • Large-slab porcelain stoneware floors, in three colour options, with white lacquered skirting boards, and insulated plasterboard interior walls.
  • A security entrance door with a security lock, lacquered interior doors and modular lacquered wardrobes with a top shelf, a hanging rail and interior lining.
  • A kitchen with large floor and wall units and a Solid Surface worktop.
  • Appliances included: an oven, a microwave, an induction hob, a washing machine, a dishwasher and a fridge.
  • The main bathroom is tiled from floor to ceiling, with a double Solid Surface washbasin, a backlit mirror, a suspended toilet and a Solid Surface shower tray.
  • Glass shower screens in every bathroom, and wall-mounted thermostatic shower taps.
  • An aerothermal system for hot water and underfloor heating, and air conditioning with a heat pump, ducts and zoned control.
  • The developer estimates an energy rating of B; the certification is in progress.
  • Anthracite switches, LED lights on the terraces and a video intercom.
  • Home automation for the blinds, fire detection and living-room lighting, controlled from a phone or a voice assistant.
  • A community pool, large garden areas, and a gym with an outdoor terrace and a room for scheduled activities.
  • A community garage with an automated door, and storage rooms.

Source: the developer’s quality specification for this development, which is undated. It is illustrative and not contractual, and its materials may be replaced by others of equal or higher quality.

Our assessment

What we would weigh before reserving here, and what we check for you.

Why it stands out

  • Who it is forBuyers who want a spacious two- or three-bedroom home with a large terrace and a sea view, and a pool, spa and gym in the community.
  • What makes it differentLarge terraces: 13 of the 18 homes have 49.70 sqm or more, and the penthouses up to 121.90 sqm.
  • Why location mattersThe development is in the upper part of Los Monteros, about 10 minutes from the centre of Marbella and its old town, on a hillside with sea views.
  • Why check availabilityThe prices come from the developer’s list as shown on its portal in October 2026, and the homes are not built yet. Nueva Living reconfirms price and availability before any viewing.

What to plan for

  • Reservation and payment scheduleThe documents we hold give no reservation amount and no payment schedule. We obtain the developer’s terms and send them to you in writing before you reserve.
  • VAT and taxesThe price list does not say whether VAT is included. Nueva Living confirms the taxes and purchase costs in writing.
  • Parking and storageThe specification describes a community garage with an automated door and storage rooms. It does not say whether a space or a room is included in the price, so we ask for the home you choose.
  • The specificationThe quality specification says its materials may be replaced by others of equal or higher quality, and that it is not contractual. We ask for the final specification.

Checks before any money moves

  • Licence and worksThe documents we hold do not say whether the building licence has been granted or whether the works have started; the plans are drawn from the basic project. We ask for the licence, its status and the start date of the works.
  • The completion dateNone of the developer’s documents gives a completion date, so we ask for the contractual date before any reservation.
  • Which homes, which phaseThe developer’s page presents the release as phase 4. The plans of 12 of the 18 homes are headed phase IV, in blocks 1 to 3; the other six have references in portals 15 and 16, their plans name no phase, and home 150B has no plan. The brochure describes 39 homes in three blocks as the first phase. We ask the developer how many homes each phase has.
  • The areasThe price list gives one built area and one terrace figure for each home, but not on one basis: for the blocks 1 to 3 homes it is the interior built area, and for the portal 15 and 16 homes it includes a share of the common areas. The plans give a larger figure with the common areas, and areas under Decree 218/2005. We confirm which figure the contract uses.
