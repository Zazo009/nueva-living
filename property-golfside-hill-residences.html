Overview

Nine homes, three kinds

Golfside Hill Residences is one phase of a larger gated scheme on rising ground beside a golf course in east Marbella, built as white low blocks with the sea in front. Nine of its homes are available: two ground-floor garden apartments, four first-floor penthouses with a roof solarium, and three semi-detached villas on three levels.

The available homes run from 173.98 to 264.44 sqm built with 139.52 to 213.48 sqm of usable interior, and have three or four bedrooms. Their terraces run from 134.11 to 220.63 sqm, counting the solarium and the pool where there is one. All nine face south-west.

Residents share the communal areas of the whole scheme: the brochure lists four outdoor pools and an indoor pool, a spa with a sauna and a Turkish bath, a gym, a co-working area, a coffee shop, a concierge service and an e-bike centre. Two parking spaces and a storage room come with each home, priced separately.

Architecture

White blocks, deep terraces

The brochure describes a contemporary reading of Mediterranean architecture: white buildings with large pergolas for sun and shade, courtyards that bring in light and cross ventilation, and blocks set into the slope so that each home looks towards the sea or the golf course.

  • The homes — Two-storey blocks of garden apartments and penthouses, and semi-detached villas on three levels, all facing south-west.
  • The terraces — From 134.11 to 220.63 sqm including solarium and pool, each with a water point, a power socket and non-slip porcelain paving.
  • The pools — A private pool of 11.80 to 15.55 sqm on the drawing of every available home, in the garden or on the roof.
  • Inside — Large-format porcelain floors, a fitted kitchen with induction hob, oven, microwave, dishwasher, fridge and washer-dryer, and Silestone worktops.
  • Comfort — Underfloor heating and ducted air conditioning from an aerothermal system, zoned control and home automation in the living area.
  • Energy — Solar panels in support, double glazing with a thermal break and motorised blinds in the bedrooms; the drawings carry an A rating.

FAQ

Common buyer questions

Can foreigners buy property in Spain?

Yes. There are no restrictions on non-Spanish nationals buying property in Spain, whether as a resident or non-resident.

What is an NIE number and do I need one?

An NIE (Numero de Identificacion de Extranjero) is a tax ID number required for any property purchase in Spain by a non-Spanish national. Nueva Living can guide you through obtaining one before you reserve.

What costs should I budget for on top of the purchase price?

Buyers typically budget for transfer tax or VAT, notary fees, land registry fees and legal fees on top of the purchase price. Nueva Living provides a full, current cost breakdown for your chosen residence before you reserve.

Can I get a mortgage in Spain as a non-resident?

Many Spanish banks offer mortgages to non-resident buyers, typically financing a portion of the purchase price. Exact terms depend on the bank and your personal financial profile.

What is the difference between off-plan and completed properties?

Off-plan means the development is still under construction and is usually sold with staged payments through to completion. A completed property is ready to view and move into now.

How does the reservation and payment process work?

Reservation and payment structures vary by development and are set out in full before you reserve. Nueva Living reconfirms the current schedule for your chosen residence at every step.

Can I rent out the property after purchase?

This depends on the individual development and local regulations, which can vary by community and municipality. Nueva Living will confirm the specific rules for a development before you reserve.

Do I need a lawyer?

Yes, we strongly recommend independent legal representation for any property purchase in Spain. Nueva Living can put you in touch with independent lawyers experienced in Costa del Sol property.
