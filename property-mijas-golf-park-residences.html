The development

Six portals in three blocks, stepped along a landscaped park on the edge of Mijas Golf, eight minutes from Fuengirola.

One, two and three bedrooms, ground-floor homes with private gardens and penthouses with solariums, the largest of them 229.64 sqm.

Architecture · The architecture

How the scheme is built and how it sits on the site.

The design staggers six portals along the slope so that each steps past the one in front of it and keeps its frontage onto the course.

  • The layout: Six portals in three blocks, staggered so that each steps past the one in front of it and keeps its view of the course.
  • The envelope: Reinforced concrete with a continuous rendered facade, an air chamber and thermal insulation, checked by an independent technical control body.
  • Inside: Porcelanosa floors throughout, a living room with a stepped false ceiling, and full-height passage doors.
  • Energy: Rooftop photovoltaic panels for the common areas, centralised aerothermal hot water, dual-flush cisterns and aerated taps.

Residences · The homes

What every home includes as standard.

The homes

  • One, two and three bedrooms across ground, first, second and third floors, with penthouses above.
  • Ground-floor homes have private gardens; penthouses have solariums, the largest of them 229.64 sqm.
  • Kommerling PVC windows with double glazing and a thermal break, tilt-and-turn, with motorised blinds in living rooms and bedrooms.
  • Terrace doors in living rooms and bedrooms are flush with the floor, and ground-floor windows carry anti-intrusion protection.
  • Porcelanosa porcelain floors throughout, in a non-slip finish on the terraces.
  • A fitted kitchen with a compact quartz worktop and matching splashback, soft-close hardware and a Bosch induction hob, extractor and oven.
  • Laufen sanitaryware and Hansgrohe taps, with thermostatic mixers and rain showerheads.
  • Ducted air conditioning and heating with an Airzone-type control panel in the living room and a sensor in every habitable room.
  • Centralised aerothermal hot water with an individual meter for each home.
  • Mechanical ventilation, an armoured entrance door, full-height passage doors and modular wardrobes with damped doors.

Questions about Mijas Golf Park

How many bedrooms?

One, two or three. Ground-floor homes have private gardens and penthouses have solariums.

What does the quoted area include?

The developer's built area includes a share of the common elements. A home listed at 97.34 sqm is 70.99 sqm interior plus a 15.54 sqm terrace.

What is included in the price?

The home, its terrace and its parking space. The price list is net, so VAT and stamp duty come on top.

What are the communal facilities?

A pool, a gym, a sauna, a community room, a coworking room, landscaped social areas and underground parking, all gated.
