Overview

Seven villas, each on its own plot

Atalaya Pool Villas is a development of seven detached villas in Atalaya, Estepona, on the stretch of the Costa del Sol between Estepona and Marbella. Each villa stands on its own walled plot of 536 to 795 sqm, with a private garden, an infinity pool and a rooftop solarium, and each has a layout of its own.

Every villa is arranged over a basement, a ground floor, a first floor and a solarium reached by a covered staircase. The ground floor opens the living and dining room, which has a double-height section, onto a covered porch and the pool, and has a bedroom with access to the terrace. Five of the seven villas are currently available: four with three bedrooms and one with four.

Location

Atalaya, between Estepona and Marbella

Atalaya is an established residential area of golf and large plots, on the stretch of the Costa del Sol between Estepona, Marbella and Benahavís. The road to Benahavís is 500 m away, the A-7 is 1 km away and the AP-7 is 8 km away, and Málaga airport is about 40 minutes by car.

Quality specifications

What the homes are built with

The developer’s own specification, summarised by part of the home.

Floors and interior
  • Large-format porcelain floors and large-format ceramic wall tiling.
  • Interior doors 2.20 m high.
Climate and energy
  • Air conditioning and hot water by aerothermal energy, with room-by-room control.
  • Underfloor heating throughout the house except the basement, and electric underfloor heating in the bathrooms.
  • An electrical installation of 11.50 kW.
Kitchen and laundry
  • Designer kitchen with Siemens appliances included.
  • Basement laundry room with washer and dryer included.
Bathrooms
  • Wall-hung vanity units with recessed LED lighting and a mirror, backlit and anti-fog in the main bathroom.
  • Wall-hung toilets with concealed cisterns. The main bathroom has a shower and a built-in bath, except in Villas 06 and 07, where it has a shower only.
Windows, doors and security
  • Large lifting sliding doors in the living room, with a motorised roller blackout, and motorised shutters on the other windows and doors.
  • A video entry phone, and an alarm installation connected to a monitoring centre.
Garden, pool and solarium
  • A private garden with natural lawn, planting, lighting and automatic irrigation.
  • An infinity pool with interior lighting and pre-installation for heating: 10 x 4 m on the four-bedroom villa and 8 x 4 m on the others.
  • An integrated gas barbecue on the porch.
  • A solarium reached by a covered staircase with a motorised roof, with a shower, a built-in storage cupboard, TV and power outlets and pre-installation for a hot tub.
What is optional
  • Home automation linked to climate, multimedia, alarm, water, lighting and blinds is available at extra cost.
  • The structure is pre-installed for a lift stopping at every level except the solarium; the lift itself is not included.
  • Customisation of materials, with and without extra cost, is possible through the developer’s catalogue within set deadlines. Furnishing packages are priced separately.

Source: the developer’s circular for these villas, dated May 2024. The final specification is the developer’s quality memorandum, which we ask for in writing before any reservation. Images, furniture and landscaping in the renders are illustrative.

Investment Case

Reservation and contract

The developer’s May 2024 circular set the reservation deposit at EUR 50,000, with the staged payments following in the purchase contract.

Guaranteed payments

The developer undertakes to guarantee the amounts paid on account through a bank, under Spanish building law, within 20 days of each payment.

Costs on top of the price

The buyer pays the notary, the property registry and the purchase taxes. The seller pays the plusvalía tax and the registration of the new build and its division into units.

Annual fees

The seven plots belong to the area’s urbanisation maintenance body, with an annual fee of roughly EUR 183 to EUR 272 per plot, according to the developer’s 2024 circular.

Architecture

Contemporary lines, Mediterranean materials

The facades combine white cement render with cladding panels and screens in a timber-effect finish, and pergolas with timber-effect beams shade the terraces. Large sliding doors and windows in the living room and bedrooms bring in light and give cross ventilation, and balustrades in masonry and glass keep the view of the pool and garden from inside the house.

  • Facades — White cement render and paint, with cladding panels and exterior screens in a timber-effect finish.
  • Terraces — Pergolas with timber-effect beams, and masonry and glass balustrades facing the pool and garden.
  • Light and air — Large openings in the living room and bedrooms, and cross ventilation through the house.
  • Orientation — Every villa faces south, with the main rooms looking onto the garden.

FAQ

Common buyer questions

Can foreigners buy property in Spain?

Yes. There are no restrictions on non-Spanish nationals buying property in Spain, whether as a resident or non-resident.

What is an NIE number and do I need one?

An NIE (Numero de Identificacion de Extranjero) is a tax ID number required for any property purchase in Spain by a non-Spanish national. Nueva Living can guide you through obtaining one before you reserve.

What costs should I budget for on top of the purchase price?

Buyers typically budget for transfer tax or VAT, notary fees, land registry fees and legal fees on top of the purchase price. Nueva Living provides a full, current cost breakdown for your chosen residence before you reserve.

Can I get a mortgage in Spain as a non-resident?

Many Spanish banks offer mortgages to non-resident buyers, typically financing a portion of the purchase price. Exact terms depend on the bank and your personal financial profile.

What is the difference between off-plan and completed properties?

Off-plan means the development is still under construction and is usually sold with staged payments through to completion. A completed property is ready to view and move into now.

How does the reservation and payment process work?

Reservation and payment structures vary by development and are set out in full before you reserve. Nueva Living reconfirms the current schedule for your chosen residence at every step.

Can I rent out the property after purchase?

This depends on the individual development and local regulations, which can vary by community and municipality. Nueva Living will confirm the specific rules for a development before you reserve.

Do I need a lawyer?

Yes, we strongly recommend independent legal representation for any property purchase in Spain. Nueva Living can put you in touch with independent lawyers experienced in Costa del Sol property.

How many villas are available?

Seven villas make up the development and five are currently listed as available: Villa 02 with four bedrooms and Villas 03, 05, 06 and 07 with three, from EUR 1,530,000 to EUR 2,220,000. Villas 01 and 04 are no longer listed.

When will the villas be ready?

The developer’s current price list gives a delivery month for each villa: October 2027 for Villa 05, November 2027 for Villa 03, January 2028 for Villa 07 and February 2028 for Villas 02 and 06. Earlier developer material gave March 2026, which the current list supersedes.

Is the solarium included?

Yes. Each villa has a rooftop solarium of 49 to 95 sqm, reached by a covered staircase, with a shower, a storage cupboard and pre-installation for a hot tub. The hot tub itself is not included.

Where is the parking?

Villas 02, 03, 05 and 06 have a basement garage for two cars. Villa 07 has two spaces on the surface under a pergola, according to the plans and the developer’s circular, while the developer’s brochure describes one villa with one space in the basement and one outside. We confirm Villa 07 in writing.

Is each plot private?

Each villa stands on its own walled plot with a private garden and its own access. The plots share party walls with their neighbours and belong to the area’s urbanisation maintenance body.
