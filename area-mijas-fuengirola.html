Costa del Sol Area Guide Mijas & Fuengirola: connected coastal living, at lower prices

Two distinct coastal markets offering beaches, established services and easier access towards Málaga.

Living in Mijas & Fuengirola

More choice, easy access and daily convenience

Fuengirola is urban, walkable and rail-connected. Mijas ranges from the village and La Cala to golf and hillside communities, where a car is usually needed. The right choice depends more on the exact setting than the municipality name.

Local Knowledge

What makes Mijas & Fuengirola different

The most functional stretch of this coast, and increasingly one of the most interesting.

01

Mijas Pueblo

A whitewashed hillside village above Fuengirola with donkey taxis, viewpoints back over the coast and a genuinely old-Andalusia feel that the beach towns below it don't have.

02

The Cercanías train line

A direct commuter rail connection to Málaga and the airport -- a practical advantage this stretch of coast has that Marbella and Estepona don't.

03

Fuengirola's paseo marítimo and Bioparc

A long beachfront promenade and a well-regarded zoo built around natural habitats, both genuinely used by residents rather than just visitors.

04

Castillo Sohail

A restored 10th-century fortress at the mouth of the Río Fuengirola, now used as a summer concert venue -- one of the coast's more distinctive landmarks.

Where to Buy

Four settings, one price range apart

01

Las Lagunas

The most active district of Mijas, between Fuengirola and Mijas Pueblo, with the Parque Comercial Miramar shopping and leisure centre directly alongside it. The most urban and the most everyday-practical of the settings here.

02

Mijas Pueblo and the hills

A whitewashed hillside village above Fuengirola with viewpoints back over the coast and a genuinely old-Andalusia feel the beach towns below do not have. Hilltop plots nearby sit next to Cerrado del Águila golf, within reach of La Cala, Fuengirola and Marbella.

03

Fuengirola

Urban, walkable and rail-connected, with a long beachfront promenade and services used by residents rather than only visitors. It asks €4,483 per m² against €3,773 in Mijas, and it is the address to look at if the train matters.

04

Torremuelle and the Benalmádena edge

On the coast at Benalmádena, a short walk from the beach through a landscaped public park. It sits on the airport side of this stretch, which shortens the transfer further again.

FAQ

What buyers ask us about Mijas & Fuengirola

How much does property cost in Mijas and Fuengirola?

The average asking price for resale homes in June 2026 was €3,773 per m² in Mijas and €4,483 in Fuengirola. Both sit below Marbella's €5,608 on the same measure and month, which is the clearest reason buyers look at this stretch.

Mijas or Fuengirola — what is the difference?

Fuengirola is urban, walkable and rail-connected. Mijas ranges from the hillside village and La Cala to the inland golf communities, where a car is usually needed. The right choice depends far more on the exact setting than on which municipality the address falls in.

Can you reach Málaga airport by train from here?

Yes. The Cercanías commuter line runs directly to Málaga and the airport — a practical advantage this stretch of coast has that Marbella and Estepona do not. It is the main reason buyers who expect frequent short trips end up looking here.

Is this stretch cheaper than Marbella?

Yes, and measurably so: both municipal averages sit below Marbella's on the same Idealista measure for the same month. What you trade is prestige rather than access — the airport is closer from here than it is from Marbella.

Does it work outside the summer season?

It is the most functional stretch of this coast for year-round living, precisely because it is not built primarily around visitors. Fuengirola's promenade, shops and services are used by residents rather than only by tourists, and the rail line runs all year.

What kind of property is available?

A wider range than most of the coast: urban apartments in Fuengirola, coastal townhouses, and hillside and golf villas in Mijas. That range is the other half of why the price averages sit where they do.
