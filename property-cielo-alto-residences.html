The development

Cielo Alto Residences is a collection of 17 duplex Sky Villas set within six boutique buildings on an elevated plot in Benahavis, in the Golden Triangle between Marbella, Benahavis and Estepona. Five buildings hold three Sky Villas each, and the sixth holds two.

Each residence spans one or two full floors with its own private lift running from the living floors down to a private garage, combining the scale of a villa with the security of an apartment. Terraces are large and south-facing, with the option to add a private pool, and residents share a Sky Villa pool and a clubhouse with an indoor pool, spa, gym and coworking space.

Architecture · Organic architecture, built into the hillside

The Sky Villas are designed to sit within their hillside setting rather than against it, with rendered facades, floor-to-ceiling glazing and large terraces oriented to the view. Reinforced concrete construction follows a waffle-slab structure with steel and concrete pillars and beams.

Exterior carpentry is aluminium by Cortizo or similar, with sliding doors and thermal-break double glazing throughout. Air-conditioning and water-based underfloor heating are controlled room by room, and every residence is pre-installed for a home automation system.

  • Private lift access from the living floors to a private garage.
  • Floor-to-ceiling glazing opening living areas onto large terraces.
  • Water-based underfloor heating with independent room-by-room control.
  • Pre-installation for a home automation system.

Questions about Cielo Alto

How many Sky Villas are there in total?

17, across six buildings. Buildings 1 to 5 hold three Sky Villas each and building 6 holds two.

Is a private pool included?

No. The developer states that a private pool on the terrace is an optional extra, at additional cost, for each residence.

Is the price inclusive of IVA?

No. The published payment structure adds IVA to each payment stage.

Is furniture included?

No. The images shown are computer-generated; furniture, styling and planting are indicative and are not included in the sale.
