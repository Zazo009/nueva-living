# First-contact notice for an introduced person (GDPR Art. 14)

Send this as the **first** message, not a follow-up. Everything Article 14 requires is in the body:
who we are, what we hold, where it came from, the basis, how long, the rights, the right to object
stated separately, and the supervisory authority. A link to the privacy policy does not replace it.

Three languages below. The other six follow the same shape — ask and they will be written to match
the site's settled form of address per language.

---
