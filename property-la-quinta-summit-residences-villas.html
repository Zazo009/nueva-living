Overview

Sixty-six homes, forty available

La Quinta Summit is a gated community of 50 apartments and penthouses and 16 villas at the top of La Quinta, in Benahavís, on the southern edge of El Madroñal. The developer describes panoramic views of the bay of Marbella, the Mediterranean and the mountains, 24-hour security and shared amenities.

Every apartment has its own private pool, two covered parking spaces and a storage room. The apartments are duplex ground-floor homes with a private basement, middle-floor homes and duplex penthouses, with two to five bedrooms. The villas have five or six bedrooms over a basement, a ground floor and an upper floor, with a lift, a private pool and a basement that can hold a gym, a massage room, a sauna and a hammam.

The price lists mark 26 homes as Reserved: 21 apartments and 5 villas. They are not listed here. The 40 homes still priced are listed with their plans, from EUR 1,495,000 to EUR 8,950,000, excluding VAT.

Location

La Quinta, Benahavís

The community stands at the top of La Quinta in Benahavís, on the southern edge of El Madroñal, in the Golden Triangle of Marbella, Estepona and Benahavís. The developer’s brochure gives the AP-7 motorway and the first golf course as 5 minutes away, Puerto Banús as 15, Sotogrande as 30 and Málaga airport as 45, and names five golf courses 5 to 15 minutes by car: La Quinta, Los Arqueros, Los Naranjos, Las Brisas and Aloha. Its description gives Puerto Banús as ten minutes.

Quality specifications

What the homes are built with

The developer’s two quality specifications, one for the apartments and one for the villas, group by group, with the product brands as they name them.

Apartments: Structure and walls
  • The foundations combine footings with a raised slab and piled foundations with pile caps.
  • The structure is reinforced concrete walls and columns, using waffle slabs or solid concrete slabs as required, in accordance with current building regulations.
  • Exterior walls are perforated solid brick, with a ventilated or non-ventilated cavity depending on position, thermal insulation in line with current regulations and a self-supporting plasterboard inner lining on an auxiliary frame.
  • Interior partitions between rooms are laminated plasterboard, finished in wallpaper, wood or paint depending on the design.
Apartments: Finishes and paintwork
  • Exterior walls are finished in render or extruded aluminium slats, depending on the design.
  • The slab edges of the overhangs follow an organic, wave-like geometry, clad in a continuous sequence of Corian solid-surface pieces, or similar.
  • Interior walls and ceilings are finished in plaster, paint or wood panelling, depending on the design.
  • False ceilings are plasterboard, with perimeter recesses for curtains and indirect LED lighting, according to the design.
  • A continuous Aquapanel false ceiling, or similar, extends from the interior onto the terrace, creating visual continuity between inside and outside while standing up to outdoor conditions.
  • The exterior is painted in white or cream exterior paint, or similar, according to the design.
  • Interior walls and ceilings are painted in smooth white or cream plastic paint, or similar, where applicable.
Apartments: Flooring and wall cladding
  • The main living areas have natural wood parquet laid in a chevron pattern, by Gunni&Trentino, or similar.
  • Bathrooms, WCs, laundry, plant room and porches have large-format porcelain flooring by Gunni&Trentino, or similar, laid continuously without breaks at doorways, with joints kept as discreet as possible.
  • Bathroom and WC walls are clad in the same large-format Gunni&Trentino porcelain, or similar, in a cream travertine look, creating continuity between floor and walls. It is combined with three different porcelains to distinguish the guest WC, the secondary bathrooms and the main bathroom.
  • All cladding is installed in floor-to-ceiling formats, according to the design.
  • In two-level apartments, the staircase treads are finished in wood to match the flooring.
Apartments: Exterior joinery
  • Sliding doors to walkable terraces are slim-profile aluminium, Schüco or similar, metallic lacquered in a champagne tone, floor-to-ceiling, with concealed tracks flush with the floor, thermal break and double glazing with solar control, low emissivity and a clear polyvinyl butyral layer.
  • All other doors and windows are also aluminium, with tilt-and-turn or sliding opening depending on the design, Schüco or similar, with the same finish and performance.
Apartments: Interior joinery
  • The entrance door is a floor-to-ceiling pivoting door, tall and wide, with no architrave, finished in natural oak, with concealed security hinges, acoustic seals, stainless steel hardware and a designer handle.
  • Floor-to-ceiling interior doors (excluding the private basement) are lacquered wood, with concealed hinges, privacy lock, stainless steel hardware and a steel-finish designer handle.
  • Decorative panelling and/or porcelain or wood mosaics in selected areas, according to the design.
  • Bespoke floor-to-ceiling wardrobes designed by Gunni&Trentino, with handles integrated into aluminium profiles, fully finished interiors, a modular accessory system in leather- or textile-effect and LED lighting to highlight selected areas.
  • In the master suite, a bespoke walk-in wardrobe by Poliform, or similar: a lightweight aluminium-profile structure, textile or leather finishes and floor-to-ceiling glass folding doors, with indirect LED lighting and full accessories for a sense of lightness.
  • Flush skirting throughout, Filomur type or similar, in aluminium, wood or porcelain according to the design.
Apartments: Lighting, sound and telecommunications
  • The lighting design combines different moods and smart home controls tailored to each space, and the electrical connection boxes and electrical and telecoms cabinets are positioned to remain concealed.
  • The main rooms have two lighting moods: indirect, recessed LED lighting with diffuser panels for a calm, even light, and, in kitchens and bathrooms, LED accents to highlight specific elements. This is combined, according to the design, with ceiling-level track lighting for different dimmable scenes, plus independent spotlights to highlight selected areas.
  • Niessen smart controls, or similar, with a main touchscreen in the living room and master bedroom for control of lighting, air conditioning, underfloor heating, sound and access throughout the home. All other rooms have a Niessen smart keypad, or similar, for temperature and lighting.
  • Everything can be managed from the central panel or from any fixed or mobile device with internet access, from anywhere in the world: simulate daily routines, adjust the temperature, schedule lighting and view the video-intercom camera.
Apartments: Plumbing, air conditioning and underfloor heating
  • Hot and cold water installations comply with current regulations, using first-quality pipework for cold water and thermally insulated pipework for hot water, with shut-off valves in every wet room and per fixture.
  • An air-source heat pump (aerothermal) system produces domestic hot water and supplies underfloor heating in living areas and bedrooms.
  • Ducted hot and cold air conditioning in the living room and bedrooms, individually zoned per room and integrated into the home-automation system, with linear diffusers flush-mounted to ceiling or wall.
  • Water-based underfloor heating in living areas and bedrooms through embedded pipework that also improves sound insulation, and electric underfloor heating in the guest WC and in the secondary and main bathrooms.
Apartments: Bathrooms and WCs
  • Wall-hung toilets in matte white porcelain in the guest WC and bathrooms, Laufen or similar, with soft-close seat, concealed cistern and Geberit dual-flush front button, or similar.
  • In the main bathroom, according to the design, a freestanding bathtub with a contemporary, minimalist design and Dornbracht taps, or similar.
  • Shower trays fully flush with the floor, in the same porcelain as the flooring, with an anti-slip finish, drained through the joints, and a ceiling-mounted rain shower in all bathrooms, or similar, with floor-to-ceiling glass screens.
  • Laufen washbasins, or similar, on Trentino Bath designer vanity units by Gunni&Trentino, with matching accessories.

  • The top-level villa-type penthouse includes a private sauna and hammam by Effe, or similar, supplied by Gunni&Trentino.

Architecture

Two designs, one setting

The apartments are a curved, terraced building with wave-shaped slab edges, glass balustrades and a private pool for each home. The villas are flat-roofed houses in render, porcelain and wood, with a pool, planted terraces and a covered outdoor living area.

Each product line has its own specification: the apartments have chevron parquet throughout the living areas, the villas large-format porcelain downstairs and herringbone parquet upstairs. The full lists are in the specification section.

  • Structure — Reinforced concrete walls and columns with waffle or solid slabs, on shallow and deep foundations as each plot requires.
  • Facade — Perforated solid brick with insulated cavity, finished in render, porcelain, aluminium slats or natural wood according to the design.
  • Glazing — Thermally broken aluminium with solar-control, low-emissivity double glazing and floor-level sliding doors.
  • Climate — Aerothermal hot water, ducted air conditioning zoned room by room and water-based underfloor heating.
  • Control — Home automation for lighting, climate and, in the apartments, sound and access, from a touchscreen or a mobile device.

FAQ

Common buyer questions

Can foreigners buy property in Spain?

Yes. There are no restrictions on non-Spanish nationals buying property in Spain, whether as a resident or non-resident.

What is an NIE number and do I need one?

An NIE (Numero de Identificacion de Extranjero) is a tax ID number required for any property purchase in Spain by a non-Spanish national. Nueva Living can guide you through obtaining one before you reserve.

What costs should I budget for on top of the purchase price?

Buyers typically budget for transfer tax or VAT, notary fees, land registry fees and legal fees on top of the purchase price. Nueva Living provides a full, current cost breakdown for your chosen residence before you reserve.

Can I get a mortgage in Spain as a non-resident?

Many Spanish banks offer mortgages to non-resident buyers, typically financing a portion of the purchase price. Exact terms depend on the bank and your personal financial profile.

What is the difference between off-plan and completed properties?

Off-plan means the development is still under construction and is usually sold with staged payments through to completion. A completed property is ready to view and move into now.

How does the reservation and payment process work?

Reservation and payment structures vary by development and are set out in full before you reserve. Nueva Living reconfirms the current schedule for your chosen residence at every step.

Can I rent out the property after purchase?

This depends on the individual development and local regulations, which can vary by community and municipality. Nueva Living will confirm the specific rules for a development before you reserve.

Do I need a lawyer?

Yes, we strongly recommend independent legal representation for any property purchase in Spain. Nueva Living can put you in touch with independent lawyers experienced in Costa del Sol property.

How many homes are available?

The development has 66 homes: 50 apartments and penthouses and 16 villas. Forty are listed as available, 29 apartments and penthouses and 11 villas, and 26 are marked Reserved in the developer’s price lists. We confirm the current position before any viewing.

What do the homes cost?

According to the price lists, apartments and penthouses cost from EUR 1,495,000 to EUR 4,950,000 and villas from EUR 6,495,000 to EUR 8,950,000, each plus VAT. We confirm the price of each home in writing.

Is VAT included?

No. The payment terms add VAT to every instalment. The documents do not state the rate or other purchase costs, so we confirm them in writing.

What is the payment schedule?

For apartments, a reservation fee of EUR 20,000, EUR 35,000 or EUR 50,000 depending on the price band, then 35% at the private purchase contract (less the reservation), 15% two months later and 50% at the deed. For villas, EUR 50,000, then 35% (less the reservation), 15% three months later and 50% at the deed and handover. All plus VAT.

When will the homes be finished?

The documents disagree: the October 2026 description gives the third quarter of 2027 for both, the Word version gives the second quarter for villas and the third for apartments, and a public sales listing gives the second and the fourth. We ask for the contractual date.

What sizes are the homes?

The listed apartments run from 129.22 sqm built for a 2-bedroom middle-floor home to 362.93 sqm for the available 5-bedroom penthouse, with terraces and porches on top. The villas have 811 to 937 sqm built on plots of 690 to 1,705 sqm.

Are the villa photographs of a real villa?

Yes. The villa pictures are photographs of a pilot villa that the developer supplies as Villa 10, furnished and styled. The apartment and amenity pictures are computer-generated, and furniture is not included in the price.

What does Reserved mean?

The price lists mark 26 homes Reserved and give no price for them. We do not list them. If one becomes available again we confirm it with the developer.
