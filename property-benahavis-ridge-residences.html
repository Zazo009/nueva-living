The development

Benahavís Ridge is twenty-one homes on a hillside above the golf valley, arranged in staggered blocks so that no home stands in another's shadow.

Interiors run from 95 to 186 m², with terraces of 35 to 75 m² facing south and south-east towards the Mediterranean.

Architecture · Architecture that lets the coast breathe

The architecture uses clean horizontal lines, shaded outdoor areas and wide windows that connect the rooms to the terraces.

The design is calm rather than showy, with a focus on proportion, shade, views and natural materials.

  • Large-format openings designed around light and sea orientation.
  • Generous terraces with a natural connection between indoor and outdoor spaces.
  • Warm natural textures balanced by crisp architectural volumes.
  • Private pool on the ground-floor and top-floor homes.
  • Large-format 90x90cm porcelain flooring throughout, with solid wood interior doors and matte black ironmongery.
  • Underfloor heating powered by an aerothermal system, with individual room-by-room climate control.
  • Home automation for lighting and climate, with photovoltaic panels ready for self-consumption.

Location

The site sits in the hills of Benahavís, the inland corner of the triangle it forms with Marbella and Estepona -- the densest concentration of golf courses in Europe. Nueva Andalucía and Puerto Banús are about ten and twelve minutes down the valley.

  • Who it is forBuyers who want privacy and good design without being far from the coast's services.
  • What makes it differentThe focus is on large terraces, natural light and elevation rather than a busy beachfront setting.
  • Why location mattersBenahavís keeps the golf valley and Puerto Banús close while staying quieter than the coast road.
  • Why check availabilityHomes, views and payment plans can change quickly, so it is worth checking the latest details before you shortlist.
