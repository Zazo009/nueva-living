Overview

113 homes, built into the hillside

Laurel Hill Residences is a collection of 113 terraced homes on an elevated plot in Elviria Sur, Marbella, on a site with slopes of up to 59%. Rather than levelling the terrain, the architecture is staggered to follow it, so that around half of the homes have direct sea views and the other half lateral sea or mountain views.

Every home is organised across 4 levels connected by a private lift running from the garage to the rooftop solarium: a basement with two parking spaces and storage, a ground floor social area opening onto the garden, an upper floor with 3 bedrooms, and a rooftop solarium with a jacuzzi and panoramic views. Residents also share a wellness centre at the top of the plot, with an outdoor heated pool, an indoor heated pool, a gym, a sauna and hammam, and five outdoor sports areas.

Architecture

Cross View Design, staggered into the slope

The development's architecture is built around 'Cross View Design', a layout strategy that keeps visual continuity between the interior, exterior and surrounding landscape. Homes are staggered up the site's steep slope rather than terraced flat, maximising natural light and sea or mountain views for every residence.

Interiors use large-format Italian tiling and flooring, natural wood veneer kitchens and doors, and Strugal windows with a thermal break. Kitchens are fitted with Siemens appliances, with the option to upgrade to a superior range, and bathrooms combine neutral-tone stone finishes with Hansgrohe fixtures and backlit mirrors.

  • Private lift in every home, connecting the garage, ground floor, bedrooms and rooftop solarium.
  • Rooftop solarium with a jacuzzi and panoramic sea and mountain views in every home.
  • Large-format Italian tiling and flooring, with natural wood veneer kitchens and doors.

FAQ

Common buyer questions

Can foreigners buy property in Spain?

Yes. There are no restrictions on non-Spanish nationals buying property in Spain, whether as a resident or non-resident.

What is an NIE number and do I need one?

An NIE (Numero de Identificacion de Extranjero) is a tax ID number required for any property purchase in Spain by a non-Spanish national. Nueva Living can guide you through obtaining one before you reserve.

What costs should I budget for on top of the purchase price?

Buyers typically budget for transfer tax or VAT, notary fees, land registry fees and legal fees on top of the purchase price. Nueva Living provides a full, current cost breakdown for your chosen residence before you reserve.

Can I get a mortgage in Spain as a non-resident?

Many Spanish banks offer mortgages to non-resident buyers, typically financing a portion of the purchase price. Exact terms depend on the bank and your personal financial profile.

What is the difference between off-plan and completed properties?

Off-plan means the development is still under construction and is usually sold with staged payments through to completion. A completed property is ready to view and move into now.

How does the reservation and payment process work?

Reservation and payment structures vary by development and are set out in full before you reserve. Nueva Living reconfirms the current schedule for your chosen residence at every step.

Can I rent out the property after purchase?

This depends on the individual development and local regulations, which can vary by community and municipality. Nueva Living will confirm the specific rules for a development before you reserve.

Do I need a lawyer?

Yes, we strongly recommend independent legal representation for any property purchase in Spain. Nueva Living can put you in touch with independent lawyers experienced in Costa del Sol property.

How many homes are there in total?

113 terraced homes on a single staggered hillside plot.

How many are currently available?

The developer has not yet published a public price list or release status for individual residences. Nueva Living reconfirms current availability before any viewing.

Does every home have a sea view?

The developer states that around 50% of homes have direct sea views, with the remainder having lateral sea views or mountain views, due to the staggered 'Cross View Design' layout.

Is a private lift included?

Yes. The developer states that every home includes a private lift connecting the garage, ground floor, bedrooms and rooftop solarium.

Is furniture included?

No. The images shown are computer-generated; furniture, styling and planting are indicative and are not included in the sale.
