Overview

Thirty-two homes, seventeen listed

The Privilege is one residential building of 32 homes with two and three bedrooms next to the old town of Estepona. It has a ground floor, four upper floors and a rooftop solarium level, with two basement levels for parking and storage rooms. The ground-floor homes have a private garden and a private pool, and the penthouses have a terrace and a private solarium with a pool on the roof.

Seventeen homes are listed with a price and the other 15 are marked as reserved on the developer’s price list headed September 2026. The shared areas are a communal pool with night lighting, a gym, a spa with a sauna, a social club and a coworking space. The developer’s documents give no completion date.

Location

Estepona, next to the old town

The building is in one of Estepona’s residential areas, next to the old town and in the heart of the Costa del Sol, close to the A-7 and the AP-7. The developer’s brochure puts the marina, Playa Beach and La Rada beach under ten minutes away, with shops, supermarkets, schools and sports facilities close by and swift access to Málaga and its airport. Its location map adds times of 4 minutes to the Estepona marina, 6 to Punta Plata beach and 8 to Padrón beach, without saying how they are travelled.

Quality specifications

What the homes are built with

The developer’s own quality specification, summarised by part of the home.

Facade and glazing
  • A multilayer facade of an outer brick wall, interior insulation, an air cavity and a plasterboard lining, finished in glass and cladding such as lacquered aluminium or GRC panels.
  • A curtain wall of lacquered aluminium with a thermal break and Climalit-type double glazing with low-emissivity and solar-control treatment, with sliding and opening panels to the terraces and motorised blackout blinds in the bedrooms and living rooms.
Walls and partitions
  • Partitions inside the home in dry plasterboard on a metal frame with mineral-wool insulation, and water-repellent plasterboard in the wet rooms.
  • Between homes and common areas, a leaf of perforated brick with a self-supporting plasterboard lining and thermal and acoustic insulation with an anti-impact layer.
Floors, doors and wardrobes
  • Rectified porcelain stoneware floors in 120 x 120 cm format by PORCELANOSA or equivalent, in the rooms, the bathrooms and, in a non-slip finish, on the terraces, with timber skirting boards matching the doors.
  • An armoured front door with a three-point lock and a smart lock, full-height frameless lacquered interior doors, and built-in lacquered wardrobes with a suitcase shelf and a hanging rail. Some walls are clad in wood panelling with a choice of finishes.
Kitchen
  • A fully furnished kitchen with high-pressure laminate units, a worktop and splashback in SILESTONE compact quartz or porcelain stoneware, a central island with a porcelain or CORIAN top and a pull-out GROHE tap or similar.
  • GAGGENAU or equivalent appliances: an oven, an induction hob with an integrated extractor, a dishwasher, a fridge and a wine cooler, and a laundry area with a washing machine, a tumble dryer and an ironing centre.
Bathrooms
  • Electric underfloor heating in the main and the second bathroom, a continuous washbasin in compact quartz, porcelain or Corian with a wall-hung unit, and low-profile shower trays with glass screens.
  • Thermostatic wall-mounted GROHE or similar shower fittings with a sliding bar and a handheld shower head, large-format rectified wall tiles by PORCELANOSA or equivalent combined with microcement, and textured ceilings.
Climate and energy
  • Hot water from an aerothermal heat pump in each home.
  • Air conditioning and heating by a direct-expansion heat pump, through ducts in the false ceiling with grilles in the living room, kitchen and bedrooms, and a thermostat in the living room and in every bedroom with Airzone zoning.
Electrical and telecoms
  • A high level of electrification, TV, radio and telephone points in the living rooms, kitchens and bedrooms, a colour video intercom, and recessed LED lights in the kitchens, corridors and bathrooms.
  • LED lighting, a socket and a water point on the terraces, the rooftop solariums and the ground-floor gardens.
Terraces, gardens and solariums
  • Private built-in pools on some terraces, clad in natural stone with exterior LED lighting and individual to each home, and outdoor showers in the gardens and solariums.
  • Gardens and terraces paved with exterior porcelain stoneware and planted with natural grass and an integrated irrigation system.
Common areas, garage and lifts
  • A communal pool with night lighting, landscaped areas with native low-water planting and automatic drip irrigation, a solarium, a fully equipped gym, a spa with a sauna, a social club and a coworking space.
  • Entrance halls with stone, ceramic or large-format artificial stone floors and presence detectors with timers on the lighting of halls, stairs and landings.
  • A garage with a remote-controlled door, a polished quartz-concrete floor with an epoxy coating, fire protection and pre-installation for electric-car charging, and lifts that reach every floor and the garage levels directly, with automatic doors, overload detection and a telephone connection.

Architecture

Bronze-toned frames, planted terraces

The developer’s brochure calls the design innovative and avant-garde, and eminently contemporary. In its computer-generated images the building has angular bronze-toned frames around the terraces on every floor, planted balustrades and a large frame around the roof.

The specification describes a curtain-wall facade with sliding and opening panels where the living room and kitchen meet the terrace, with the tracks hidden in the floor, so that the room runs on into the outdoors.

  • Structure — Reinforced concrete built to current regulations and the Technical Building Code, on foundations designed from the geotechnical study.
  • Facade — Multilayer: an outer brick wall, interior insulation, an air cavity and a plasterboard lining, finished in glass and cladding such as lacquered aluminium or GRC panels, to be fixed by the project management.
  • Glazing — Lacquered aluminium frames with a thermal break and double glazing with low-emissivity and solar-control treatment, and motorised blackout blinds in the bedrooms and living rooms.
  • Roofs — Inverted flat roofs, waterproofed and insulated, with gravel where nobody walks and non-slip ceramic flooring where they are walkable.

FAQ

Common buyer questions

Can foreigners buy property in Spain?

Yes. There are no restrictions on non-Spanish nationals buying property in Spain, whether as a resident or non-resident.

What is an NIE number and do I need one?

An NIE (Numero de Identificacion de Extranjero) is a tax ID number required for any property purchase in Spain by a non-Spanish national. Nueva Living can guide you through obtaining one before you reserve.

What costs should I budget for on top of the purchase price?

Buyers typically budget for transfer tax or VAT, notary fees, land registry fees and legal fees on top of the purchase price. Nueva Living provides a full, current cost breakdown for your chosen residence before you reserve.

Can I get a mortgage in Spain as a non-resident?

Many Spanish banks offer mortgages to non-resident buyers, typically financing a portion of the purchase price. Exact terms depend on the bank and your personal financial profile.

What is the difference between off-plan and completed properties?

Off-plan means the development is still under construction and is usually sold with staged payments through to completion. A completed property is ready to view and move into now.

How does the reservation and payment process work?

Reservation and payment structures vary by development and are set out in full before you reserve. Nueva Living reconfirms the current schedule for your chosen residence at every step.

Can I rent out the property after purchase?

This depends on the individual development and local regulations, which can vary by community and municipality. Nueva Living will confirm the specific rules for a development before you reserve.

Do I need a lawyer?

Yes, we strongly recommend independent legal representation for any property purchase in Spain. Nueva Living can put you in touch with independent lawyers experienced in Costa del Sol property.

How many homes are available?

The building has 32 homes and 17 are listed with a price: five with two bedrooms and twelve with three, from EUR 775,500 to EUR 2,084,500 plus VAT. The other 15 are marked as reserved on the developer’s list.

Is VAT included?

No. The price list states that its prices exclude the 10% IVA. We confirm the tax and the other purchase costs in writing.

Is there parking and storage?

Yes. The price list gives each of the 17 homes two garage spaces and a storage room, and the specification includes pre-installation for electric-car charging in the garage.

When will it be finished?

None of the developer’s documents gives a completion date. We ask for the contractual date and the stage of the works and put them in writing.

What is the payment schedule?

The documents we hold do not give one. We ask the developer and confirm it in writing before you reserve.
