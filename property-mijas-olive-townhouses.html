The development

A development of 140 townhouses with three or four bedrooms at Mijas Costa, facing a natural area of dense pines. The dossier presents it as a resort-style development with communal pools, a gym, a social club and a chill-out area, and the payment sheets show two phases.

The phase 1 price list names three homes, all with three bedrooms and a private garden: two at EUR 495,000 and one at EUR 505,000, plus VAT. It does not cover phase 2 or the other homes of phase 1, and the documents do not say how many homes each phase has.

Architecture · Rows of townhouses, each with its own garden

The dossier describes 140 townhouses facing a natural area of dense pines, designed to bring out the setting, with communal services on a resort scale and good fittings in each home.

In the renders the homes stand in white rows of two floors with timber-effect panels and flat roofs, and the ground-floor terraces open onto lawns behind clipped hedges. The dossier says the homes are positioned to catch the light through the day.

  • Two floors: the living areas and the garden on the ground floor, the bedrooms on the first.
  • A frontal parking space of about 26 sqm drawn on the plot of each of the three homes listed.
  • Photovoltaic panels on the home roofs for self-consumption, and a pre-installed charging point for an electric car.
  • An enclosed development with an access-control booth and a video intercom at the entrance.

Residences · Three homes, one house type

The three homes on the price list share one layout over two floors, with three bedrooms and a private garden, and differ in position, orientation and garden size.

The three-bedroom townhouse

  • The living room, kitchen, hall and a toilet on the ground floor; three bedrooms, two bathrooms and a dressing room on the first.
  • 100.37 sqm of interior floor area, 53.05 sqm on the ground floor and 47.32 sqm on the first, and 124.05 or 124.08 sqm built.
  • An uncovered terrace of 9.15 or 11.55 sqm and a private garden, and a frontal parking space of about 26 sqm drawn on the plot.

What differs between the three

  • Townhouse 31, in block 3, faces north-east and has a 21.34 sqm garden, for EUR 495,000 + VAT.
  • Townhouse 39, in block 4, faces north-east and has a 29.51 sqm garden, for EUR 495,000 + VAT.
  • Townhouse 10, in block 1, faces south-west and has a 15.86 sqm garden, for EUR 505,000 + VAT.

Specifications

The developer’s own building specification, summarised by part of the home.

  • A reinforced-concrete foundation, as the geotechnical study requires, and a reinforced-concrete structure.
  • A facade of perforated brick with projected polyurethane or XPS insulation, an air cavity, and a plasterboard lining on a galvanised steel frame with mineral-wool insulation.
  • A non-walkable inverted roof, waterproofed and insulated.
  • Laminate flooring in the living room, bedrooms, kitchen, hallways and hall, with white skirting boards.
  • Ceramic tiles on the bathroom floors, on the staircase and on the shower walls, and plastic paint on the other walls and the plasterboard ceilings.
  • Anti-slip outdoor tiles on the patios.
  • PVC windows with double glazing, hinged, sliding or tilt-and-turn, laminated safety glass in the patio doors, and manually operated insulated roller blinds in the bedrooms.
  • White-lacquered interior doors, and modular wardrobes with a shelf and a hanging rail.
  • A security entrance door with a peephole and a lock, and anti-pull fittings and security glass on the ground-floor windows and doors.
  • Wall and floor units in high-pressure laminate, with a Silestone worktop and sink.
  • A fridge, a washing machine, a dishwasher, an induction hob, an oven, a microwave and an extractor hood.
  • White vitrified porcelain sanitaryware, resin shower trays in both bathrooms and single-lever taps.
  • The basin is built into a vanity unit in the master bathroom and wall-hung in the second bathroom and the toilet.
  • Hot water from an aerothermal system.
  • Hot and cold air through ducts to the living room, bedrooms and kitchen, with the kitchen alone served by a heat pump, and mechanical ventilation in the kitchens and bathrooms.
  • Photovoltaic panels on the roofs of the homes for self-consumption, as the Spanish Technical Building Code requires.
  • TV and ethernet connections to the ICT 2 telecommunications standard in the living room, kitchen and bedrooms, and a pre-installed charging point for an electric car.
  • Lighting on the walls facing the garden, and a TV connection, sockets and a water tap on the patio.
  • Three salt-chlorinated pools, of which the specification describes one for swimming and one for relaxation, and a fully equipped gym and a social club.
  • Gardens with lighting and automatic irrigation, and a portable defibrillator.
  • An enclosed development with private access only and an access-control booth, a video intercom at the entrance, master keys at several security levels, and an automatic entrance gate with remote opening.

Source: the developer’s quality specification in English, dated 6 February 2026 (revision 02). It says the materials may be changed at the discretion of the project management and replaced by others of similar or higher quality, and that natural stone and wood vary in shade and colour.

Our assessment

What we would weigh before reserving here, and what we check for you.

Why it stands out

  • Who it is forBuyers who want a three-bedroom house with its own garden rather than an apartment, to live in or as a second home on the coast of Mijas.
  • A garden of its ownThe three homes on the list each have a private garden of 15.86 to 29.51 sqm and an uncovered terrace of 9.15 to 11.55 sqm, on the ground floor beside the living room.
  • Shared facilitiesThree salt-chlorinated pools, a fully equipped gym, a social club and a chill-out area, in landscaped gardens inside an enclosed development with controlled access.
  • Most of the price falls at the deedSixty-five per cent of the price falls at the title deed in the third quarter of 2028, after 20 per cent at the private contract and 15 per cent in three instalments in 2027 and 2028.

What to plan for

  • Tax is not in the priceThe price list is quoted before VAT. Ten per cent VAT and 1.20 per cent stamp duty add roughly 55,000 euros to a 495,000 euro home and 57,000 to the 505,000 euro one, before notary and registry fees.
  • Only phase 1 is pricedPhase 2 has its own payment sheet, with the title deed in the second quarter of 2029, but no price list yet. We show no phase 2 price and do not estimate one.
  • Parking and storageThe plans draw a frontal parking space of about 26 sqm on each plot. The price list does not say what the price includes, so we confirm it in writing.
  • The delivery dateThe payment sheet gives the third quarter of 2028 for the title deed of phase 1. No document gives a licence status or a start of works, so ask what the contract says happens if the date slips.

Checks before any money moves

  • The homes listedThe phase 1 price list names three homes and has a blank fourth row. It has no status column and no date, and says nothing about the other homes of phase 1 or about phase 2, so we ask for the complete list and the status of every home.
  • Three bedrooms or fourThe dossier describes townhouses of three and four bedrooms. The three homes on the price list, and every plan we hold, have three bedrooms, so we ask where the four-bedroom homes are and when they are priced.
  • The licence and the worksNone of the documents we hold gives the status of the building licence or of the works. The payment sheets give only the title-deed quarter, so we ask for the licence, the start of works and the contractual completion date.
  • Your moneyThe payment sheets do not say how payments on account are guaranteed. We ask to see the guarantee itself, and the account details, before any payment.
  • The images and the plansThe images are computer-generated and for guidance only, and the furniture, decoration and planting in them are not part of the offer. The plans are project plans that the developer says may change for technical or legal reasons.

Questions about Mijas Olive

How many homes are there?

The development has 140 townhouses with three or four bedrooms, in two phases. The phase 1 price list names three, all with three bedrooms: two at EUR 495,000 and one at EUR 505,000, plus VAT.

When will the homes be delivered?

The phase 1 payment sheet gives the title deed and the delivery of keys for the third quarter of 2028, and phase 2 for the second quarter of 2029. No document gives a licence status or a start of works, so Nueva Living asks for the date in writing and for the contract to say what happens if it slips.

Is there a price list for phase 2?

Not yet. Phase 2 has a payment sheet but no price list, so we show no phase 2 home or price. We will add them when the developer issues one.

Does each home have a parking space?

The plans draw a frontal parking space of about 26 sqm on each plot. The price list does not say whether it is included in the price, so we confirm it in writing.

What is shared?

Three salt-chlorinated pools, a fully equipped gym, a social club, a chill-out area and a solarium, in landscaped gardens inside an enclosed development with controlled access.
