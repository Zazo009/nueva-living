Overview

Fifteen independent villas on the New Golden Mile

The Bel Air Villa Collection is a development of 15 independent villas in the established Bel Air urbanisation, on the New Golden Mile between Estepona and Marbella. Each villa sits on its own plot with a private pool, landscaped garden and covered pool bar.

Every villa shares the same 581.72 m² built layout across ground and first floors: four en-suite bedrooms, an open-plan kitchen and a main living room with ceilings approaching six metres. A basement and a rooftop solarium are available as options on each plot.

Why This Project

Why this project matters

The Bel Air Villa Collection suits buyers who want an independent villa on its own plot, rather than a unit in an apartment block, within walking distance of the coast on the New Golden Mile.

Why It Fits

Who it is for

Buyers looking for a detached, four-bedroom family villa with a private pool and garden, either as a main residence or a lock-and-leave second home.

What makes it different

A near six-metre living room ceiling and a south-west orientation give these villas a volume and light level unusual at this price on the New Golden Mile.

How it is bought

Sold off-plan on a staged payment schedule, with buyer payments guaranteed by an insurance policy and delivery expected 18 months from the private contract.

What to confirm

Plot position, whether the basement and solarium options are taken, and the current build licence status. Nueva Living confirms all three before any reservation.

Investment Case

Why timing matters

Four villas are currently released from a total of 15, with the remainder held for later phases.

Payment protection

Amounts paid on account of the house are guaranteed by an insurance company, as set out in the developer terms.

Early completion discount

The developer applies an 8% discount where the full price including taxes is paid during construction, before keys are handed over.

Personal use and rental

A detached villa with four en-suite bedrooms suits full-time living, a family second home, or a long-term hold.

Trust & Diligence

Developer information

Full developer and company details are provided on request before any reservation.

How the purchase works

5% reservation deposit, 25% on signing the purchase contract and obtaining the building licence, 20% across 15 monthly payments during construction, and 50% at the deed of sale.

Guarantees

Buyer payments on account of the house are guaranteed by an insurance company.

What is optional

The basement, the rooftop solarium and the four-stop lift are priced separately and are not included in the headline price.

Architecture

Volume, light and a considered specification

Each villa is arranged over a ground and first floor with an optional basement and rooftop solarium. The entrance opens into a main living room with ceilings approaching six metres, with a staircase connecting the levels and full-height glazing onto the garden.

The specification is consistent across the collection: large-format 90x90 cm porcelain floors, oak flooring in the first-floor bedrooms, TECHNAL exterior carpentry, an aerothermal system, home automation and an individual security system.

  • Living room ceilings approaching six metres, with full-height glazing to the garden.
  • Kitchen by KÖK Studio with SANTOS cabinetry and MIELE appliances.
  • Master dressing room by UECKO, alongside an en-suite master bathroom.
  • Private pool of approximately 56 m² connecting the house to the garden.
  • Covered pool bar on the ground floor terrace, pre-installed for an outdoor kitchen.
  • Two covered parking spaces with an electric vehicle charging point.

FAQ

Common buyer questions

Can foreigners buy property in Spain?

Yes. There are no restrictions on non-Spanish nationals buying property in Spain, whether as a resident or non-resident.

What is an NIE number and do I need one?

An NIE (Numero de Identificacion de Extranjero) is a tax ID number required for any property purchase in Spain by a non-Spanish national. Nueva Living can guide you through obtaining one before you reserve.

What costs should I budget for on top of the purchase price?

Buyers typically budget for transfer tax or VAT, notary fees, land registry fees and legal fees on top of the purchase price. Nueva Living provides a full, current cost breakdown for your chosen residence before you reserve.

Can I get a mortgage in Spain as a non-resident?

Many Spanish banks offer mortgages to non-resident buyers, typically financing a portion of the purchase price. Exact terms depend on the bank and your personal financial profile.

What is the difference between off-plan and completed properties?

Off-plan means the development is still under construction and is usually sold with staged payments through to completion. A completed property is ready to view and move into now.

How does the reservation and payment process work?

Reservation and payment structures vary by development and are set out in full before you reserve. Nueva Living reconfirms the current schedule for your chosen residence at every step.

Can I rent out the property after purchase?

This depends on the individual development and local regulations, which can vary by community and municipality. Nueva Living will confirm the specific rules for a development before you reserve.

Do I need a lawyer?

Yes, we strongly recommend independent legal representation for any property purchase in Spain. Nueva Living can put you in touch with independent lawyers experienced in Costa del Sol property.
