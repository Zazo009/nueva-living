The development

Monteros Garden Residences is a private development of 57 homes in Marbella. The developer’s dossier describes one-, two- and three-bedroom homes with large terraces and bright interiors, ground-floor homes and penthouses among them. Every penthouse has a hot tub, and so do some of the ground-floor homes.

The price list of 3 November 2026 names three of the 57 homes and shows each as available: a two-bedroom home on the second floor at EUR 590,000 and two three-bedroom homes on the first floor at EUR 685,000 and EUR 720,000, plus VAT. None of the documents gives a completion date.

Architecture · Clean lines, large terraces

The dossier describes clean lines and top-quality materials that create large, harmonious spaces opening to the outdoors, with large terraces and bright interiors.

In the renders, white and sand-coloured blocks with deep terraces stand round the communal pool.

  • Structure: Reinforced concrete foundations, in line with the geotechnical study of the site, and reinforced concrete slabs.
  • Facade: Rendering and paint combined with cladding, with high-performance thermal insulation, an air chamber and a plasterboard lining on the inside.
  • Glazing: PVC or thermally broken aluminium windows, as the project management decides, with low-emission thermo-acoustic glass, and motorised insulated roller blinds in the bedrooms.
  • Terraces: Large-format non-slip porcelain tile outdoors, and a jacuzzi with a shower on the penthouse floor.

Specifications

The developer’s own building specification, summarised by part of the home.

  • Large-format porcelain stoneware in the living areas, bedrooms, kitchen, hall and bathrooms, and non-slip porcelain on the terraces.
  • Shower areas tiled in ceramic from leading brands, and smooth plastic paint on the other walls and on the ceilings.
  • An armoured entrance door with a security lock, solid white lacquered interior doors and modular wardrobes lined inside, with a shelf and a hanging rail.
  • Wall and base units in high-pressure laminate with self-closing drawers and a quartz worktop of the Silestone or Neolith type.
  • An induction hob, integrated dishwasher, oven, microwave, fridge, extractor hood, washing machine and tumble dryer, and in the penthouses a wine cellar and a coffee maker.
  • White vitrified porcelain sanitaryware, resin shower trays with safety-glass screens, a double basin in the main bathroom and premium-brand mixer taps with thermostatic shower taps.
  • Ducted heat-pump air conditioning with zone control, a thermostat in the living room and one in each bedroom, and remote control by smartphone.
  • Underfloor heating throughout, supplied by the central aerothermal unit, and hot water from a high-quality aerothermal system.
  • A smartphone-controlled system for the air conditioning and the lighting, a colour video entry system, fibre-optic internet, ethernet sockets in every room except the bathrooms and a pre-installation for electric-vehicle charging.
  • A gated complex with private access, an exterior concierge, access control and 24-hour video surveillance with perimeter cameras, and a motorised garage door with remote opening.
  • Swimming pools with saline chlorination, a heated indoor pool, an equipped social room, a coworking room and a well-equipped gym.
  • Gardens with lighting and automatic irrigation.

Source: the developer’s building specification, dated 20 September 2023. It says that the specification may be changed by the project management and replaced with others of similar quality, and that natural stone varies in tone and colour.

Our assessment

What we would weigh before reserving here, and what we check for you.

Why it stands out

  • Who it is forBuyers who want a new two- or three-bedroom apartment with a large terrace in Marbella, with shared facilities that include a heated indoor pool, a gym and a coworking room.
  • What makes it differentThe specification gives every home underfloor heating, ducted air conditioning and a smartphone-controlled home system, and the developer offers a Custom Manager service for upgrades and personalisation.
  • Why location mattersThe dossier presents Marbella as a cosmopolitan resort with over 12 km of blue-flag beaches, shopping streets and a wide gastronomic offer, in a safe setting with good transport links.
  • Why check availabilityThe price list covers three of the 57 homes and the documents give no completion date, so we confirm both before any reservation.

What to plan for

  • Tax is not in the priceThe price list is quoted before VAT. At 10 per cent VAT and 1.20 per cent stamp duty, the standard rates, the cheapest home costs about EUR 66,000 more and the dearest about EUR 81,000 more, before notary and registry fees.
  • Delivery is not statedNo document gives a completion date, and 60 per cent of the price falls due on completion and key delivery. Ask what the contract says happens if the date slips.
  • Three of 57 are listedThe price list covers three homes. Which of the others are sold, reserved or not yet released is not stated, so a viewing starts with the complete list.
  • The middle instalments are date-drivenTen per cent at six months and ten per cent at twelve months are counted from the private contract, not from the progress of the build.

Checks before any money moves

  • The completion dateNone of the documents we hold gives a completion date, a licence status or a stage of works. The specification is dated 20 September 2023 and the plans are revision 02 of 5 January 2024, so we ask for the licence, the start of the works and the contractual date.
  • Your moneyThe documents we hold say nothing about a guarantee for amounts paid on account. Spanish law requires one for off-plan payments, so we ask to see the guarantee itself before any payment.
  • The figuresPrices and areas are transcribed from the price list of 3 November 2026 and agree with the plans. The list does not say what has become of the other 54 homes, so we ask for the complete list.
  • Prices can moveThe price list says its prices are valid for two days after it is downloaded. We reconfirm the figure with the seller before you reserve.
  • The images and the plansThe images are computer-generated, and the plans say that they may change during the works for technical or legal reasons. The furniture shown is not included.

Questions about Monteros Garden

How many homes are there?

The development has 57 homes with one, two or three bedrooms. The price list names three of them, all available: a two-bedroom home at EUR 590,000 and three-bedroom homes at EUR 685,000 and EUR 720,000, plus VAT.

Is VAT included?

No. The price list says that VAT is not included. VAT on a new home is 10 per cent and stamp duty in Andalusia 1.20 per cent, standard rates that the documents do not state.

Are a garage and a storage room included?

The price list shows one garage space with each of the three homes and a storage room with two of them, 11B and 122B. We confirm that they are included in the price.

When will it be finished?

None of the documents gives a completion date, a licence status or a stage of works. We ask for the licence and the contractual date.

How does the payment schedule work?

EUR 20,000 plus VAT on reservation, 20 per cent plus VAT at the private contract 60 days later, less the reservation, 10 per cent at six months and 10 per cent at twelve months after the private contract, and 60 per cent plus VAT on completion and key delivery.
