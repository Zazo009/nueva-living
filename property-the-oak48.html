The development

The OAK48 is a residential building of 48 homes in three blocks in Estepona, close to the old town. The developer’s list gives a price for 15 of them and marks the other 33 as reserved. The 15 are two- and three-bedroom homes: three on the ground floor with a patio, six on the first and second floors with a terrace, and six penthouses with a solarium on the roof.

Residents share a pool, a gym, a coworking room and a social lounge in landscaped gardens, with two basement levels of parking spaces and storage rooms. The developer’s documents give no delivery date and the images are computer-generated, so we treat the project as off-plan on the documents we hold.

Architecture · Curved terraces, slatted screens

The developer’s dossier says the building was designed by the studio Mónaco Arquitectos around four principles: accessibility, sustainability, contemporary design and functionality. It describes contemporary architecture with large terraces and views of the Sierra Bermeja and the sea.

In the images the blocks rise four storeys, with curved terraces behind vertical slats in a pale grey, timber-effect panels around the openings and glass balustrades, and a roof level of solariums on top.

  • Facades: Half-brick ceramic masonry, rendered and painted, with exposed brickwork in places, lined inside with double hollow brick and sprayed insulation in the cavities, and lime-based mortar between the window panels.
  • Terraces: Non-slip porcelain stoneware floors, ceilings in PVC or aluminium with outdoor finishes, timber-effect finishes, and lattice screens between the terraces of neighbouring homes.
  • Glazing: Aluminium frames with a thermal break and double glazing with an air chamber.
  • Roofs: Walkable private roofs with thermal and acoustic insulation, finished in non-slip porcelain stoneware, and a metal staircase to the solarium in the penthouses.

Residences · Fifteen homes, four kinds

The fifteen homes fall into four groups by floor and size. The table below lists each one with its price.

Ground-floor homes with a patio

  • Homes 1-0-D, 2-0-C and 3-0-D have two bedrooms and two bathrooms, one of them en suite, with 64.05, 65.65 and 69.81 sqm built and 55.10, 55.97 and 58.48 sqm interior.
  • Each has an open patio of 10.89, 10.89 and 9.96 sqm, with a utility space of 2.55 sqm on the plan, and no terrace.
  • All three are EUR 375,000.

Two-bedroom homes on the first and second floors

  • Homes 1-1-D, 2-1-D, 2-2-C, 3-1-D and 3-2-D have two bedrooms and two bathrooms, with 66.02 to 76.35 sqm built, 55.09 to 64.62 sqm interior and a terrace of 11.88 to 15.14 sqm.
  • The prices run from EUR 380,000 for 3-1-D to EUR 410,000 for 2-2-C.

The three-bedroom home on the first floor

  • Home 1-1-C has three bedrooms and two bathrooms, 91.68 sqm built, 76.89 sqm interior and a terrace of 37.54 sqm.
  • The price is EUR 517,000.

Penthouses with a solarium

  • Two larger penthouses: 1-3-B has two bedrooms, 91.36 sqm built and a solarium of 106.67 sqm, and 3-3-A has three bedrooms, 89.19 sqm built and a solarium of 97.46 sqm. The price list marks both with a sea and pool view, and their plans show a pool on the roof. They are EUR 890,000 and EUR 886,000.
  • Four smaller two-bedroom penthouses, 1-3-D, 2-3-C, 2-3-D and 3-3-D, with 66.02 to 76.35 sqm built, terraces of 11.88 to 15.14 sqm and solariums of 20.57 to 25.80 sqm. Their plans show a solarium and no pool, and the prices run from EUR 552,000 to EUR 559,000.

Specifications

The developer’s own quality specification, summarised by part of the home.

  • Interior partitions in plasterboard on a metal structure with thermal and acoustic insulation.
  • Separation between homes in ceramic brick with sprayed insulation.
  • Rectified first-quality porcelain flooring of 60 x 60 cm inside the homes, porcelain stoneware in the bathrooms and non-slip porcelain on the terraces.
  • Plasterboard false ceilings in the kitchen, the main bathrooms and the service runs, painted plaster elsewhere, and plastic paint on walls and ceilings.
  • An armoured front door with a security lock and anti-pry bolts, and smooth solid-wood interior doors.
  • Built-in wardrobes lacquered in white and lined with melamine, with a suitcase shelf, drawers and a hanging rail.
  • A fitted kitchen with upper and lower units and a worktop in Silestone or similar.
  • An oven, a ceramic hob, an extractor hood and a washing machine are included.
  • First-quality porcelain tiling in the bathrooms and the kitchen, from Saloni or similar.
  • White glazed porcelain sanitaryware from Roca or similar, a low-profile acrylic shower tray with a tempered-glass screen, and single-lever and thermostatic taps.
  • The dossier lists air conditioning and heating in every home, and the specification describes the system: fan-coil units in the bathroom false ceilings, fibreglass ducts to each room and a programmable thermostat in the living room.
  • Pre-installation of a communal solar thermal system that helps produce the hot water of each home, and pre-installation for electric-car charging in the garage.
  • TV aerial sockets in the living room and bedrooms, a light point and a power socket on the terraces, and telephone and broadband sockets.
  • Recessed lights in the kitchen and the main bathroom, and a video intercom for access to the home.
  • A private walkable roof with thermal and acoustic insulation, finished in non-slip porcelain stoneware and reached by a metal staircase.
  • The specification lists a 3.00 x 2.00 m pool and an outdoor shower as exclusive to penthouses; the plan sheets show a pool on two of the six listed penthouses.
  • Lifts for four people with automatic doors, stopping at the garage, an automatic gate for vehicles and a garage with the safety systems the regulations require.
  • CCTV in the communal areas and the garages, a paved interior courtyard, glass balustrades, and a communal pool, a coworking room and a sports area.

Source: the quality specification in the developer’s dossier, which is undated. It says that the materials may be changed by the project management provided the quality is not reduced, that the images are not contractual and that the furniture in the 3D images is not included.

Location

The dossier places the building in one of the most distinguished residential areas of Estepona, close to the old town and in the heart of the Costa del Sol, well connected to the A-7 and the AP-7, with views of the Sierra Bermeja and the Mediterranean. Its location map gives the marina as 6 minutes away, the Punta Plata beach as 8, the Padrón beach as 10 and Estepona Bay as 12, and its text gives Málaga airport as 45 minutes away. It does not say how these times are travelled, and it gives no street address, so the map places the project at area level.

Our assessment

What we would weigh before reserving here, and what we check for you.

Why it stands out

  • Who it is forBuyers who want a new two- or three-bedroom home in Estepona, from a ground-floor apartment with a patio to a penthouse with a solarium, to live in or as a second home.
  • What makes it differentPenthouses with solariums of 20.57 to 106.67 sqm, and a pool, a gym, a coworking room and a social lounge shared by every home, at prices from EUR 375,000 to EUR 890,000 plus VAT.
  • Why location mattersThe dossier puts the building close to the old town and well connected to the A-7 and the AP-7, with the marina 6 minutes away on its location map, and describes views of the Sierra Bermeja and the Mediterranean.
  • Why check the detailsThe documents give no delivery date or stage of works, and 33 of the 48 homes are already reserved, so we confirm availability, the contractual date and the licence before any reservation.

What to plan for

  • Reservation and paymentsNo document we hold gives the reservation amount, the instalments or their dates. We ask for them and put the answers in writing before you reserve.
  • VATThe price list does not mention VAT. We show the prices as net figures plus VAT, as is usual for new homes in Spain, and confirm the rate and the other purchase costs in writing.
  • What the price includesThe price list gives one price per home and does not say what it covers. The dossier shows two basement levels of parking spaces and storage rooms, and we confirm in writing whether a space and a room come with the home you choose. The furniture shown in the images is not included.

Checks before any money moves

  • Licence and worksThe documents we hold do not say whether the building licence has been granted or whether work has started, and the images are computer-generated. We ask for the licence, the start date of the works and the bank guarantee that covers amounts paid on account.
  • The completion dateNo document we hold gives a delivery date, so we ask for the contractual date for the block you choose and put it in writing.
  • The areasThe price list and the floor sheets agree on the built, interior and terrace areas of all 15 homes. The roof-level sheets of five of the six penthouses print a different interior total, 58.97 sqm against 57.48 for 3-3-D, and the plans give the solarium of three penthouses slightly differently: 106.77 sqm against 106.67 for 1-3-B, 22.70 against 22.73 for 2-3-D and 20.55 against 20.57 for 3-3-D. The dossier also gives larger areas under Decree 218/2005, 87.79 sqm interior and 99.66 built for 1-1-C against 76.89 and 91.68 on the list. We confirm which figures the contract uses.
  • The penthouse poolsThe specification lists a 3.00 x 2.00 m pool and an outdoor shower as exclusive to penthouses, but the plan sheets show a pool only on 1-3-B and 3-3-A, which are also the two the price list marks with a pool view. The other four show a solarium and no pool. We confirm in writing which penthouses include a pool.
  • The bedroomsThe dossier describes the homes as having two and three bedrooms in one passage and one, two and three in another. The price list has one one-bedroom home, and it is reserved; all 15 listed homes have two or three bedrooms.
  • The distancesThe times on this page are the developer’s, from its location map and text, and neither says how they are travelled. The road figures beside the map are measured from an approximate point in Estepona and differ from them, so we confirm the distances on a visit.

Questions about The OAK48

When will it be finished?

Do the penthouses have a private pool?

The specification lists a 3.00 x 2.00 m pool for penthouses, but the plans show one on only two of the six listed, 1-3-B and 3-3-A. We confirm which include one.
