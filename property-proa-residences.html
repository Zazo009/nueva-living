The development

Proa Residences is a development of 33 apartments with one, two or three bedrooms in Arroyo de la Miel, Benalmadena. The developer’s infosheet places it in front of Benalmadena marina, with panoramic sea views, natural light in every home and an outdoor pool. Three homes are listed with a price: two three-bedroom apartments of 120.30 and 125.25 sqm and one two-bedroom apartment of 90.15 sqm, at EUR 890,000 to EUR 1,750,000.

The infosheet gives the building licence as in place and the status as under construction, started in the fourth quarter of 2024, with the end of construction estimated for the fourth quarter of 2026. It leaves the first-occupation licence blank and gives the construction company as to be confirmed. The infosheet is headed 2025, so we ask where the build stands now.

Architecture · Curved terraces, slatted timber

In the renders the building is white, and every level has a curved terrace with a slatted timber soffit, so the front reads as a stack of curves. The glass balustrades sit on a stainless-steel rail, and the pool garden is planted with shrubs and tropical plants.

The architect’s sheets, dated July 2023, show two basement levels with 78 garage spaces and 45 storage rooms, and the area table lists a ground level, five upper levels and a level under the roof. Ground level has private terraces and gardens in that table.

  • Levels: Two basement levels, a ground level, five upper levels and a level under the roof, as the architect’s area table lists them.
  • Facade: An exterior thermal insulation system under a two-coat mortar render.
  • Glazing: Graphite-coloured thermally broken aluminium, floor to ceiling in the living room and kitchen, with double glazing for solar control or low emissivity depending on the facade, and laminated-glass terrace balustrades.
  • Energy: Built to the Technical Building Code, with a project energy certificate of B, and heating, cooling and hot water from an aerothermal system.

Specifications

The developer’s own quality specification, summarised by part of the home.

  • Large-format 100 by 100 cm porcelain tiles run through the interior and onto the terraces without a break, with a non-slip finish outside.
  • Bathroom floors in 59.6 by 59.6 cm porcelain in the main bathroom and 59.6 by 120 cm in the second, wet-zone walls in rectified porcelain and a decorative tile at the basin. The guest toilet floor matches the rest of the home.
  • A reinforced entrance door with a three-point lock and stainless-steel fittings, graphite outside and panelled inside to match the interior doors.
  • Floor-to-ceiling interior doors in smoked oak, sliding or hinged according to the design, with satin-chrome handles, and wardrobes with lacquered, water-repellent doors and a printed textile lining.
  • Graphite aluminium frames from floor to ceiling with no shutters in the living room and kitchen, and tilt-and-turn windows with motorised electric blinds in the bedrooms.
  • Double glazing with solar control or low emissivity depending on the facade, and acoustic laminated glass where the window design calls for it.
  • A quartz-resin worktop and splashback, 18 mm melamine units with soft-close hinges opening to 155 degrees, a rectangular sink with an extendable tap, and Siemens appliances or similar. The specification does not list which appliances.
  • In the main bathroom, a wall-hung toilet with a concealed cistern, a wall-hung vanity unit, a concealed thermostatic shower with a 30 cm head and a fixed laminated-glass screen.
  • In the second bathroom, a low-cistern toilet and a bar mixer with a hand shower. Dual-flush plates with an anti-odour system, and a hygienic shower kit as an option.
  • Air conditioning, underfloor heating and hot water from a high-efficiency aerothermal system, which the specification says draws 75% of its energy from the outside air and 25% from electricity.
  • Ducted fan-coil units with zoning by motorised grilles and a thermostat in each room, and mechanical ventilation with hidden extractors.
  • A 9,200 W single-phase supply, a video intercom, data sockets in every room, terrestrial and satellite antennas and fibre optic cable to the home, with LED lighting in the kitchens, bathrooms and toilets.
  • A home-automation system controlled from a phone for the lighting, air conditioning, underfloor heating and blinds, with flood detection that shuts off the water and a smoke detector that alerts the owner.
  • Cameras on the car park floor, pivoting motorised garage doors opened by remote control or from a phone, and a pre-installation for an electric-vehicle charging point at each space.
  • Perimeter walls of solid brick topped with metal, which reduces the view into the complex from outside, and printed concrete with LED lighting at the car park entrances.

Source: the developer’s quality specification for this development, in English and Spanish, which carries no date. It says its references are in development and may change for technical reasons without lowering the quality or design. The two languages differ on two points: the English text says the parking spaces will have electric-vehicle charging points where the Spanish text says a pre-installation for a charging point, and the English text says fibre optic cable runs as far as the entrance where the Spanish text says as far as the home. We follow the Spanish text.

Questions about Proa

How many homes are available?

Three are listed: one two-bedroom apartment of 90.15 sqm and two three-bedroom apartments of 120.30 and 125.25 sqm, from EUR 890,000 to EUR 1,750,000. The development has 33 homes.

Is VAT included?

The availability table does not say. The infosheet gives every instalment as plus VAT, so we read the prices as excluding VAT, and we confirm it in writing before you reserve.

What comes with the price?

The quality specification gives a fitted kitchen with appliances. The documents do not say whether a garage space and a storage room are included, and none says that the furniture in the images is included. We confirm both.

When will it be finished?

The infosheet, headed 2025, gives construction as started in the fourth quarter of 2024 and the end of construction as estimated for the fourth quarter of 2026. We ask how far the build has come and for the contractual date.

What is the payment schedule?

A reservation of EUR 6,000, then 30% plus VAT on the private purchase contract, 10% plus VAT four months later, 10% plus VAT eight months later and 50% plus VAT on completion.
