The development

El Campanario Golf Villa is a single detached house on the golf front line at El Campanario, Estepona, arranged over a semi-basement, ground floor and first floor with a private pool and garden.

The ground floor holds an open living and dining room of 37.90 sqm with a separate 23.60 sqm dining area and a 16.00 sqm kitchen, opening to a 28.06 sqm covered porch, a 31.75 sqm solarium and the pool. Two bedrooms sit on the first floor and two more in the semi-basement, alongside a 24.30 sqm media room and a 14.34 sqm games room. Planning permission has been granted and the developer gives delivery as Q4 2026 to Q1 2027.

Architecture · Built to a stated specification

The developer publishes a full quality specification. The points below are taken from it, not from marketing copy.

  • Structure: Reinforced concrete slab foundation, metal pillars and waffle slabs, with 25 cm reinforced basement walls.
  • Glazing: Anodised black aluminium with thermal break, low-emissivity outer glazing and 4+4 acoustic inner glazing.
  • Climate: Ducted inverter heat pump across three zones, underfloor heating and aerothermal hot water.
  • Floors: 70x70 cm porcelain on the ground floor, wood-effect vinyl above, wood-effect porcelain on the porch and terraces.
  • Kitchen: Handleless units with a 4 cm laminate worktop; oven, induction hob, fridge freezer, extractor and dishwasher included.
  • Pool and plot: Gunite pool lined in porcelain with salt chlorinator, printed concrete driveway, automated sliding gate and irrigated planting.

Questions about El Campanario

Is the price inclusive of VAT?

No. The developer quotes EUR 2,350,000 and the payment schedule adds VAT at 10% to each stage.

How many villas are left?

One. This is the last villa available in its phase.

Does it have planning permission?

The developer states that planning permission has been granted. Nueva Living confirms the licence in writing before reservation.

Is the gym included?

No. The semi-basement is described as having the potential to fit out a small gym and sauna; it is not fitted as part of the sale.

What appliances are included?

Oven, induction hob, fridge freezer, extractor and dishwasher. Washing machine and dryer are not included.
