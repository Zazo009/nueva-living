The development

Eight rows of terraced houses stepping down a south-facing slope above La Cala de Mijas, six of the rows with a basement garage and storage room.

Three and four bedrooms, 137 to 203 sqm built, every house with a garden, a terrace and a solarium, and many with sea views.

Architecture · The architecture

How the scheme is built and how it sits on the site.

Eight rows step down a south-facing slope so that each row looks over the roofs of the one below it towards the sea.

  • The layout: Eight rows of terraced houses, six of them with a basement garage and storage room, around a pool, a Fit space and a spa.
  • The envelope: Reinforced concrete with a waffle slab, a coloured mortar facade with wood-effect cladding, checked by an independent technical control body and covered by ten-year insurance.
  • Inside: Porcelain stoneware floors at 60 x 60, plasterboard suspended ceilings in the hallways, bathrooms and kitchen, and an armoured entrance door.
  • Energy: An A energy rating from a high-insulation envelope, thermal-break windows, aerothermal hot water and forced ventilation.

Residences · The houses

What every house includes as standard.

The houses

  • Three or four bedrooms and two bathrooms, over ground and first floors, all of them facing south.
  • Every house has a garden, a paved terrace and a solarium; six of the eight rows add a basement garage and storage room.
  • PVC or aluminium windows in the facade colour, with double glazing and an air chamber, and insulated blinds in every bedroom.
  • Porcelain stoneware floors at 60 x 60 throughout, with ceramic tiling in the bathrooms, kitchen, laundry and terraces.
  • A fitted kitchen with a compact quartz worktop and matching splashback, and Bosch hob, oven, extractor, dishwasher, washing machine, fridge and microwave.
  • Hansgrohe taps in chrome with thermostatic shower mixers, screens and backlit mirrors in both bathrooms.
  • Ducted heat-pump heating and cooling, aerothermal hot water, and electric underfloor heating in the first-floor bathrooms.
  • Individual forced ventilation drawing from the bathrooms and kitchen and feeding the bedrooms and living rooms.
  • An armoured entrance door, modular wardrobes with hinged doors, and a plasterboard suspended ceiling in hallways, bathrooms and kitchen.
  • Energy rating A, which the developer puts at about 80% less energy than an equivalent F-rated home.

Our assessment

What we would weigh before reserving here, and what we check for you.

Why it stands out

  • What it isSixty-eight south-facing terraced houses above La Cala de Mijas, thirty still available, in eight rows with a pool, a Fit space and a spa.
  • Who it is forBuyers who want a house with its own garden and garage rather than an apartment, without the price or the upkeep of a detached villa.
  • What makes it differentEvery house faces south and has a garden, a terrace and a solarium, and the scheme carries an A energy rating rather than the C or D usual on the coast.
  • Why location mattersLa Cala de Mijas is a working coastal village rather than a resort strip, with the village under a kilometre down the hill, the beach about two and golf either side of the gate.

What to plan for

  • Why timing mattersThe developer's November 2026 list shows 40 of the 68 houses: 30 are available and 10 are reserved, eight of them four-bedroom houses. The other 28 are not on the list.
  • Current informationNueva Living reconfirms live prices, availability and payment terms before any reservation.
  • Long-term appealLa Cala de Mijas has grown steadily between Fuengirola and Marbella, and land this close to the village with sea views is largely built out.
  • Personal use and rentalSuits a main or second home, subject to the community rules and the Andalusian tourist-licence regime.

Checks before any money moves

  • The delivery dateThat a completion date exists in writing, since the developer's own documents give none.
  • The licenceThat the building licence has been granted, since the specification is still described as provisional and subject to licence requirements.
  • What the area includesThat you know which area you are buying: the price list quotes a built area that includes a share of the common areas, and the private area is smaller.
  • What is on top of the priceThe price list is net: the 10% VAT, stamp duty, the community fee and the annual council tax all come on top.

Questions about La Cala Sun

How many bedrooms?

Three or four, with two bathrooms. Every house has a garden, a terrace and a solarium.

What does the quoted area include?

The developer's built area includes a share of the common areas. A house listed at 137 sqm is 134 sqm of private built area and 111.10 sqm usable.

What is included in the price?

The house, its garden, its terrace and its solarium, and on six of the eight rows a basement garage and storage room. The price list is net, so the 10% VAT and stamp duty come on top.

What are the communal facilities?

A pool with saline chlorination, a Fit space, a spa, a chill-out zone, landscaped gardens and a concierge on the gate, with wifi across the complex.
