Why This Project

Why this project matters

A factual look at what makes these homes relevant now, without any promise of future returns.

Why It Fits

What it is

Sixty-eight south-facing terraced houses above La Cala de Mijas, twenty-three still available, in eight rows with a pool, a Fit space and a spa.

Who it is for

Buyers who want a house with its own garden and garage rather than an apartment, without the price or the upkeep of a detached villa.

What makes it different

Every house faces south and has a garden, a terrace and a solarium, and the scheme carries an A energy rating rather than the C or D usual on the coast.

Why location matters

La Cala de Mijas is a working coastal village rather than a resort strip, with the village under a kilometre down the hill, the beach about two and golf either side of the gate.

Investment Case

Why timing matters

Forty of the 68 were gone before this price list was issued and five more are reserved, four of them four-bedroom houses.

La Cala de Mijas has grown steadily between Fuengirola and Marbella, and land this close to the village with sea views is largely built out.

Personal use and rental

Suits a main or second home, subject to the community rules and the Andalusian tourist-licence regime.

Architecture

The architecture

How the scheme is built and how it sits on the site.

Eight rows step down a south-facing slope so that each row looks over the roofs of the one below it towards the sea.

  • The layout — Eight rows of terraced houses, six of them with a basement garage and storage room, around a pool, a Fit space and a spa.
  • The envelope — Reinforced concrete with a waffle slab, a coloured mortar facade with wood-effect cladding, checked by an independent technical control body and covered by ten-year insurance.
  • Inside — Porcelain stoneware floors at 60 x 60, plasterboard suspended ceilings in the hallways, bathrooms and kitchen, and an armoured entrance door.
  • Energy — An A energy rating from a high-insulation envelope, thermal-break windows, aerothermal hot water and forced ventilation.

FAQ

Common buyer questions

Can foreigners buy property in Spain?

Yes. There are no restrictions on non-Spanish nationals buying property in Spain, whether as a resident or non-resident.

What is an NIE number and do I need one?

An NIE (Numero de Identificacion de Extranjero) is a tax ID number required for any property purchase in Spain by a non-Spanish national. Nueva Living can guide you through obtaining one before you reserve.

What costs should I budget for on top of the purchase price?

Buyers typically budget for transfer tax or VAT, notary fees, land registry fees and legal fees on top of the purchase price. Nueva Living provides a full, current cost breakdown for your chosen residence before you reserve.

Can I get a mortgage in Spain as a non-resident?

Many Spanish banks offer mortgages to non-resident buyers, typically financing a portion of the purchase price. Exact terms depend on the bank and your personal financial profile.

What is the difference between off-plan and completed properties?

Off-plan means the development is still under construction and is usually sold with staged payments through to completion. A completed property is ready to view and move into now.

How does the reservation and payment process work?

Reservation and payment structures vary by development and are set out in full before you reserve. Nueva Living reconfirms the current schedule for your chosen residence at every step.

Can I rent out the property after purchase?

This depends on the individual development and local regulations, which can vary by community and municipality. Nueva Living will confirm the specific rules for a development before you reserve.

Do I need a lawyer?

Yes, we strongly recommend independent legal representation for any property purchase in Spain. Nueva Living can put you in touch with independent lawyers experienced in Costa del Sol property.

What is available?

Twenty-three of the 68 houses: 13 with three bedrooms and 10 with four.

How many bedrooms?

Three or four, with two bathrooms. Every house has a garden, a terrace and a solarium.

What does the quoted area include?

The developer's built area includes a share of the common areas. A house listed at 137 sqm is 134 sqm of private built area and 111.10 sqm usable.

What is included in the price?

The house, its garden, its terrace and its solarium, and on six of the eight rows a basement garage and storage room. The price list is net, so the 10% VAT and stamp duty come on top.

When will it be finished?

The developer gives no completion date in its price list, dossier or specification. Nueva Living asks for it in writing before any reservation.

What are the communal facilities?

A pool with saline chlorination, a Fit space, a spa, a chill-out zone, landscaped gardens and a concierge on the gate, with wifi across the complex.
