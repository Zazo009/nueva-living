The development

This is a gated development of 77 apartments with one, two or three bedrooms on the New Golden Mile in Estepona. 30 homes are listed with a size and a price. The developer’s level 7 masterplan numbers 16 buildings around a large central swimming pool and landscaped gardens, and the developer lists solariums, panoramic views and spacious terraces and gardens.

The shared areas listed in the quality specification are a full spa with an indoor pool, a hammam, a sauna and relaxation areas, a fully equipped gym, a coworking area, a golf simulator, a games room and a cinema room. The documents give no building licence, start date or completion date: all three are listed as to be confirmed, and the construction firm is listed as pending.

Architecture · Terraces, gardens and views

The developer’s renders show blocks of terraced apartments with timber-slatted shading and planted edges, set around a central swimming pool and landscaped gardens, and the developer lists solariums, panoramic views and spacious terraces and gardens.

The renders are indicative, and the developer reserves the right to change the project for technical or legal reasons.

  • Structure: Reinforced concrete foundations set by the geotechnical study, with reinforced concrete waffle and solid slabs and concrete and metal columns.
  • Facade: A ceramic brick leaf, an air chamber, thermal insulation and a rock wool layer, lined with gypsum fibre board or plasterboard, and finished in cement mortar render or cladding to be defined by the project management.
  • Glazing: Cortizo aluminium or equivalent, with thermally broken profiles and double glazing, sliding or casement doors, and glass railings on the terrace parapets.
  • Roofs: Walkable flat roofs prepared for non-slip porcelain tiling, waterproofed and insulated to current regulations.

Specifications

The developer’s own quality specification, summarised by part of the home.

  • Porcelain tiles in 90 by 90 cm format indoors and non-slip porcelain outdoors, or similar or superior quality, white washable paint on walls and ceilings, and plasterboard false ceilings throughout, with a cement board false ceiling outdoors.
  • A security entrance door in water-resistant MDF with concealed hinges and a spyhole, white-lacquered MDF interior doors with concealed hinges and an interior lock on the bathroom doors, and hinged lacquered MDF wardrobes lined in grey melamine, with hanging rails and drawers on retractable runners.
  • Fully furnished kitchens with lacquered base and wall units in a minimalist style that open by a gola channel system, and a porcelain worktop.
  • Bosch appliances or equivalent: a washer-dryer, a dishwasher, a fridge, a hob, an electric oven, a sink and an extractor hood.
  • Porcelain stoneware wall tiles in 40 by 120 cm format or similar, wall-hung Laufen sanitaryware or equivalent, a continuous resin shower tray by Cifre, a vanity unit with an integrated basin by Unibaño, mirrors and shower screens.
  • Hansgrohe taps and showers in matt black: a built-in shower in the main bathroom and an exposed shower in the secondary bathrooms.
  • Air conditioning by heat pump, with ducts to all rooms, hot water from high-efficiency aerothermal heat pumps with a hot-water tank, and mechanical ventilation in the kitchens, bathrooms and toilets.
  • Electrical fittings by Gira or equivalent, with TV, telephone and data outlets.
  • Landscaped community gardens with native and subtropical species and programmed irrigation in prefabricated planters, LED lighting on timers, a stainless steel video intercom at the access from the public road and a motorised entrance gate with remote control.

Source: the developer’s quality specification for this development, dated 15 September 2026 on its last page. Brands are given as “or similar” or “or equivalent”, and the project management may define some finishes. The specification lists no loose furniture.

Our assessment

What we would weigh before reserving here, and what we check for you.

Why it stands out

  • Who it is forBuyers who want an apartment of one to three bedrooms in a gated development with a spa, a gym and a cinema room on site, and who accept that the licence and the dates are still to be confirmed.
  • What makes it differentA full spa with an indoor pool, a hammam and a sauna, a golf simulator and a cinema room are listed for the shared areas, around a large central pool on the masterplan.
  • Why location mattersThe developer places it on the New Golden Mile in Estepona, close to amenities, golf, schools and services. It gives no distances.
  • Why check availability30 of the 77 homes are currently listed, and prices vary widely between homes of similar size. Nueva Living reconfirms price and availability with the developer before any viewing.

What to plan for

  • ReservationThe reservation is EUR 6,000. The infosheet does not say whether it is deducted from the first instalment.
  • Payment schedule30% plus VAT on signing the private purchase contract and 70% plus VAT on completion.
  • VATThe infosheet states both instalments as plus VAT, which indicates that the listed prices are before VAT. The availability table does not say, so we confirm it in writing.
  • What the price includesThe documents do not say whether a car space or a storage room is included, or which floor each home is on. The kitchens are listed as fully equipped; loose furniture is not listed.

Checks before any money moves

  • Licence and guaranteesThe infosheet lists the building licence as to be confirmed. We ask for the licence and for the guarantee that covers amounts paid before completion.
  • The datesThe infosheet lists the start and the end of construction as to be confirmed and the construction firm as pending, so we ask for the contractual dates.
  • The areasThe availability table gives one size per home and does not say whether it is built or interior area. We confirm which figure the contract uses, and the terrace and garden areas, which the table does not list.
  • Plans, floors and parkingWe ask for the floorplan of each home, the floor it is on, and whether a car space and a storage room are included. None of these is in the documents we hold.
