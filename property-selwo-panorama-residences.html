Overview

Thirty-seven homes, two buildings

Selwo Panorama Residences is a gated development of thirty-seven apartments and penthouses under construction on the New Golden Mile, the stretch of coast between Estepona and Marbella.

The homes run from 201.81 to 305.38 sqm including their terraces, and the terraces are the point of the design: from 27.55 to 115.92 sqm, every one facing the sea. Three, four and five bedrooms, each home with two parking spaces and a storeroom included in the price.

The two buildings share an amenity floor built to resort scale: an outdoor pool on the terrace, a heated indoor pool, a spa, a gym, a yoga room, a social lounge with a cafeteria, a coworking room and a golf simulator.

Architecture

Terraces facing the sea

The buildings step down the hillside so that each floor sits back from the one below, which is what gives every home a deep terrace and an open outlook.

  • Orientation — Every home faces south-west or south-east, which is what the layout was planned around.
  • Terraces — From 27.55 to 115.92 sqm, treated as a room rather than a balcony.
  • Structure — A reinforced concrete frame, with exterior walls in continuous mortar cladding over high-performance insulation and an air chamber.
  • Insulation — Interior partitions in plasterboard with mineral wool; party walls in brick, lined both sides with plasterboard and rock wool.
  • Amenity floor — Outdoor pool, heated indoor pool, spa, gym, yoga room, social lounge, cafeteria, coworking room and a golf simulator.
  • Included — Two parking spaces and a storeroom with every home.

FAQ

Common buyer questions

Can foreigners buy property in Spain?

Yes. There are no restrictions on non-Spanish nationals buying property in Spain, whether as a resident or non-resident.

What is an NIE number and do I need one?

An NIE (Numero de Identificacion de Extranjero) is a tax ID number required for any property purchase in Spain by a non-Spanish national. Nueva Living can guide you through obtaining one before you reserve.

What costs should I budget for on top of the purchase price?

Buyers typically budget for transfer tax or VAT, notary fees, land registry fees and legal fees on top of the purchase price. Nueva Living provides a full, current cost breakdown for your chosen residence before you reserve.

Can I get a mortgage in Spain as a non-resident?

Many Spanish banks offer mortgages to non-resident buyers, typically financing a portion of the purchase price. Exact terms depend on the bank and your personal financial profile.

What is the difference between off-plan and completed properties?

Off-plan means the development is still under construction and is usually sold with staged payments through to completion. A completed property is ready to view and move into now.

How does the reservation and payment process work?

Reservation and payment structures vary by development and are set out in full before you reserve. Nueva Living reconfirms the current schedule for your chosen residence at every step.

Can I rent out the property after purchase?

This depends on the individual development and local regulations, which can vary by community and municipality. Nueva Living will confirm the specific rules for a development before you reserve.

Do I need a lawyer?

Yes, we strongly recommend independent legal representation for any property purchase in Spain. Nueva Living can put you in touch with independent lawyers experienced in Costa del Sol property.

What do the homes cost?

From €1,442,000 to €2,155,000, excluding VAT. Two parking spaces and a storeroom are included in every price.

Is VAT included?

No. VAT is added at 10% to each stage of the payment schedule, including the €25,000 reservation.

How are the payments staged?

€25,000 on reservation, 30% less the reservation on the private purchase contract, 20% in a single instalment within a year of that contract, and 50% at title deed, each plus VAT at 10%.

How many are still available?

25 of the thirty-seven. The other twelve are reserved, and one of the 25 has no price in the current list.

When is it finished?

Delivery is given as Q4 2027. The date that appears in the purchase contract is the one that governs, and we confirm it in writing.

How big are the homes?

From 201.81 to 305.38 sqm including the terrace, with interiors of 143.34 to 216.91 sqm and terraces of 27.55 to 115.92 sqm.

Can the show apartment be viewed?

Yes. It is finished and furnished, and we arrange the visit.

What is on the amenity floor?

An outdoor pool, a heated indoor pool, a spa, a gym, a yoga room, a social lounge with a cafeteria, a coworking room and a golf simulator.
