Overview

Thirty-six homes, five available

Velaris Residences is a boutique development of 36 apartments on the New Golden Mile, next to a golf community between Marbella and Estepona. The developer describes every home as a corner unit, in an L-shaped layout of cascading terraces facing south-east. Five three-bedroom, two-bathroom apartments on the second floor are currently listed, each with a terrace and, on the roof, a solarium and a private pool.

The shared areas include an outdoor pool, a spa with a heated pool, sauna and hammam, a gym and a coworking space. The developer’s listing says the building licence is available, that the development is under construction and that the end of the build is the third or fourth quarter of 2028, subject to change.

Quality specifications

What the homes are built with

The developer’s own quality specification, summarised by part of the home.

Doors and wardrobes
  • An armoured entrance door with a three-point lock, and white-lacquered interior doors with matt stainless-steel handles, sliding in the bathrooms.
  • Hinged white wardrobes with shelves, drawers and rails, and a walk-in wardrobe with smoked-glass doors in the main bedroom.
Windows
  • Thermally broken aluminium windows with double glazing, micro-ventilation openings and motorised shutters in the bedrooms and in ground-floor living rooms.
Kitchen
  • Upper and lower units with soft-close mechanisms and integrated handles, with Silestone worktops or equivalent in three colours.
  • Siemens appliances or similar: ceramic hob, fridge, oven, microwave and dishwasher.
Bathrooms
  • Large-format porcelain tiles on floors and walls, with taps, sanitaryware and shower enclosures chosen for durability.
Climate and energy
  • Ducted air conditioning with zoned temperature control, hot water from a high-efficiency aerothermal system and electric underfloor heating in the bathrooms.
  • The specification lists aerothermal underfloor heating throughout the home in one place and as an optional upgrade in another, so we confirm which applies.
Technology
  • A video intercom to the development entrance, LED track lighting in the living rooms, kitchens and dressing rooms, and a basic home-automation package with lighting, web server and air-conditioning control.
Upgrades
  • Listed as upgrades: indirect LED lighting, an induction hob built into the worktop, Miele appliances, a custom kitchen, further home-automation functions and an electric-vehicle charging point.
  • For outdoor areas the upgrades are a jacuzzi and outdoor shower on first-floor terraces and an outdoor bar or kitchen on second-floor solariums.
Insurance and oversight
  • A ten-year structural insurance from a certified insurer, supervision by an accredited technical control body, and laboratory testing of materials.

Source: the developer’s quality specification for this development, version 5, dated 8 April 2025. It is the developer’s document, and its materials may be changed by the project management where the quality is not reduced. Furniture is not included, except the kitchen units.

Architecture

Cascading terraces, corner homes

The blocks step back as they rise in an L-shaped plan, with white cantilevered terraces framed by glass balustrades and planted at each level. Because every home is a corner unit, living areas are lit from more than one side.

Above the second floor the apartments continue to a roof level with a solarium and a pool, set behind planted screens.

  • Structure — Reinforced concrete designed to the Technical Building Code, on foundations set by the geotechnical study.
  • Facade — Ceramic brick with an air cavity holding thermal and acoustic insulation, finished in rendered and painted mortar.
  • Glazing — Thermally broken aluminium frames with double glazing, motorised shutters in the bedrooms and glass balustrades on the terraces.
  • Roof level — Walkable roof areas with non-slip flooring, carrying the solarium and private pool of each second-floor home.

FAQ

Common buyer questions

Can foreigners buy property in Spain?

Yes. There are no restrictions on non-Spanish nationals buying property in Spain, whether as a resident or non-resident.

What is an NIE number and do I need one?

An NIE (Numero de Identificacion de Extranjero) is a tax ID number required for any property purchase in Spain by a non-Spanish national. Nueva Living can guide you through obtaining one before you reserve.

What costs should I budget for on top of the purchase price?

Buyers typically budget for transfer tax or VAT, notary fees, land registry fees and legal fees on top of the purchase price. Nueva Living provides a full, current cost breakdown for your chosen residence before you reserve.

Can I get a mortgage in Spain as a non-resident?

Many Spanish banks offer mortgages to non-resident buyers, typically financing a portion of the purchase price. Exact terms depend on the bank and your personal financial profile.

What is the difference between off-plan and completed properties?

Off-plan means the development is still under construction and is usually sold with staged payments through to completion. A completed property is ready to view and move into now.

How does the reservation and payment process work?

Reservation and payment structures vary by development and are set out in full before you reserve. Nueva Living reconfirms the current schedule for your chosen residence at every step.

Can I rent out the property after purchase?

This depends on the individual development and local regulations, which can vary by community and municipality. Nueva Living will confirm the specific rules for a development before you reserve.

Do I need a lawyer?

Yes, we strongly recommend independent legal representation for any property purchase in Spain. Nueva Living can put you in touch with independent lawyers experienced in Costa del Sol property.
