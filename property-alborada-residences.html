Overview

66 homes on the list, 60 available

Alborada Residences is a gated development in Mijas of two- and three-bedroom apartments, ground-floor homes with a private garden or a terrace, and penthouses. The architecture follows the slope of the land in flowing curves, with long terraces in stone and wood tones. The phase II price list names 66 homes, of which 60 are listed with a price and 6 are shown as reserved.

The shared areas include two outdoor pools, an indoor spa with a heated pool, a gym and a coworking space with a kitchen. The developer’s listing says the building licence is available and that the end of the build is the third quarter of 2028, subject to change.

Mijas, between the sea and the mountains

The development is in Mijas, with sea and mountain views, and supermarkets, pharmacies, medical and school services close by. The developer gives the beaches of Mijas Costa and Fuengirola as 15 minutes away, Marbella 25 minutes, and Málaga and its airport under 30 minutes.

Quality specifications

What the homes are built with

The developer’s own quality specification, summarised by part of the home.

Floors and walls
  • Premium SPC flooring in a wood look inside the homes, large-format porcelain tiles in the bathrooms and non-slip porcelain paving on the terraces.
  • Interior walls in smooth paint, and large-format ceramic tiles on the wet walls of the bathrooms.
Doors
  • A reinforced security door at each entrance, lacquered interior doors with designer handles, matching skirting boards and built-in wardrobes fully lined, with a hanging rail and storage.
Kitchen
  • Wall and base units in a wood-look finish with durable worktops, and two design options offered under the developer’s kitchen programme, with an island or a peninsula depending on the layout.
  • Premium appliances: an electric oven and a microwave oven, an induction hob, an extractor hood, a fridge, a dishwasher and a sink with a mixer tap.
Bathrooms
  • Designer fixtures with double-flush toilets, countertop washbasins on vanity units with drawers and a designer mirror, and a low-profile shower tray with an enclosure in the main bathroom.
Climate and energy
  • Ducted hot and cold air conditioning with grilles in the living room and bedrooms, and hot water from an aerothermal system.
Technology
  • A video door-entry system and a telecommunications installation that complies with current regulations.
Shared areas
  • Secure access control and a remote-controlled garage door, landscaped areas, two outdoor pools with salt chlorination, an indoor spa with a heated pool, sauna, cold-water bath and shower circuit, a gym, a pool bar with a chill-out area and a coworking space with a kitchen.

Architecture

Flowing curves, long terraces

The blocks follow the slope instead of cutting into it. Curving terraces run along the facades, and stone, wood and other natural textures tie the buildings to the landscape.

The buildings leave the ground between them open for communal gardens, planted with native species, a citrus orchard and aromatic herbs.

  • Facade — A designer facade in warm tones, with thermal and acoustic insulation that meets the Technical Building Code.
  • Glazing — Thermally broken aluminium frames with double glazing, blinds in the living room and bedrooms and glass railings with vertical tubular profiles.
  • Floors — Premium SPC flooring in a wood look inside the homes and non-slip porcelain paving on the terraces.
  • Certification — The developer says the development will have a BREEAM rating.

FAQ

Common buyer questions

Can foreigners buy property in Spain?

Yes. There are no restrictions on non-Spanish nationals buying property in Spain, whether as a resident or non-resident.

What is an NIE number and do I need one?

An NIE (Numero de Identificacion de Extranjero) is a tax ID number required for any property purchase in Spain by a non-Spanish national. Nueva Living can guide you through obtaining one before you reserve.

What costs should I budget for on top of the purchase price?

Buyers typically budget for transfer tax or VAT, notary fees, land registry fees and legal fees on top of the purchase price. Nueva Living provides a full, current cost breakdown for your chosen residence before you reserve.

Can I get a mortgage in Spain as a non-resident?

Many Spanish banks offer mortgages to non-resident buyers, typically financing a portion of the purchase price. Exact terms depend on the bank and your personal financial profile.

What is the difference between off-plan and completed properties?

Off-plan means the development is still under construction and is usually sold with staged payments through to completion. A completed property is ready to view and move into now.

How does the reservation and payment process work?

Reservation and payment structures vary by development and are set out in full before you reserve. Nueva Living reconfirms the current schedule for your chosen residence at every step.

Can I rent out the property after purchase?

This depends on the individual development and local regulations, which can vary by community and municipality. Nueva Living will confirm the specific rules for a development before you reserve.

Do I need a lawyer?

Yes, we strongly recommend independent legal representation for any property purchase in Spain. Nueva Living can put you in touch with independent lawyers experienced in Costa del Sol property.

How many homes are available?

Is VAT included?

No. The price list states that prices are net and do not include VAT or property transfer taxes. We confirm the taxes and purchase costs in writing.

When will it be finished?

What is the payment schedule?
