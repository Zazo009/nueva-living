The development

Higueron Seaside Residences is a finished development of sixty apartments and penthouses in three blocks at El Higuerón, Fuengirola, a short distance from Carvajal beach. Five homes are currently available: three ground-floor apartments with a private garden and two third-floor penthouses with a rooftop solarium.

Every home comes with two parking spaces and a storeroom in the basement, faces south-west, and is served by aerothermal heating and cooling with underfloor heating. The shared grounds hold a pool that can be heated, a children’s pool, a jacuzzi, a gym and a coworking room.

Architecture · Three blocks, stepped down the slope

The blocks follow the slope in stepped platforms, with large terraces framed by irregular volumes in the manner of porticoes and finished with glass balustrades, so that most homes look over the sea, the mountains or the shared gardens. Part of the top-floor terraces are covered by pergolas that filter the light, while the ground floors have covered porches and open terraces.

The facades are cement render and paint, with lacquered metal screens picking out some volumes, stairwells and galleries. Blocks 2 and 3 are set lower on the slope than Block 1, which keeps the sea views of the homes above.

  • Facades: Cement render and paint in combined colours, with lacquered metal screens on some volumes, stairwells and galleries.
  • Terraces: Large terraces and covered porches, glass balustrades, and pergolas that filter the light on the top floor.
  • Orientation: Every home faces south-west, with views of the sea, the mountains or the shared gardens depending on the home.
  • Gardens: The ground-floor homes have a private garden of artificial lawn, enclosed by a wall and a metal fence with a cypress hedge.

Location

El Higuerón is a residential area of Fuengirola near Carvajal beach, on the Costa del Sol between Fuengirola and Benalmádena. The N-340 is 450 m away, the AP-7 is 2 km away and Málaga airport is about 20 minutes by car.

Questions about Higueron Seaside

Is it finished?

The developer’s price list gives delivery as key ready and its quality memorandum of 8 October 2026 says the same. Its February 2023 circular gave 30 November 2024 as the delivery date. We ask for the occupation licence and the energy certificate for the home you choose.

What comes with the price?

Each home includes two parking spaces and a storeroom in the basement. The price list shows a separate equipment figure on top of the price, EUR 31,700 for the two-bedroom homes and EUR 45,000 for the three-bedroom apartment, which we confirm before any reservation.

What do the penthouses have?

Each third-floor penthouse has an open terrace and a rooftop solarium of 94.75 or 97.48 sqm, reached by a private staircase, with a barbecue, a shower with hot and cold water and pre-installation for a jacuzzi.

How far is the beach?

The developer’s February 2023 circular says 350 m to Carvajal beach and its 2025 catalogue says 700 m. We walk the route with you at a viewing.
