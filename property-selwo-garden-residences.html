The development

Selwo Garden Residences is the name we give to two later phases of a larger gated scheme in Estepona, beside the Selwo Aventura wildlife park, with low white blocks set around gardens and pools. Twenty-six apartments in six of its blocks are on the developer’s agent portal as available.

The available homes have one, two or three bedrooms and run from 87.89 to 148.27 sqm built, with 63.20 to 105.52 sqm of usable interior. Every one has a terrace, from 18.37 to 155.46 sqm. On their plans, five of the ground-floor homes add a private garden of 8.78 to 82.23 sqm.

The developer’s documents list two outdoor pools for adults and children, a spa, a gym, a coworking room, a cinema room and a youth club among the communal areas. Each home is listed with at least one parking space and one storage room, priced as separate lines.

Architecture · White blocks, deep terraces

In the developer’s images the blocks are low and white, with deep terraces framed in white and grey, glass balustrades and planting on the upper terraces, set above the gardens and pools on stone-faced walls.

The homes are in three-storey blocks over an underground garage. The developer numbers them by block, portal, floor and door, which is how the table refers to them.

  • The blocks: Ground floor and two upper floors, with lifts, over an underground garage with storage rooms.
  • The terraces: From 18.37 to 155.46 sqm, with water, drainage, lighting, a power socket and a TV socket on each.
  • The gardens: Five of the available ground-floor homes have a private garden on their plans, of 8.78 to 82.23 sqm.
  • Facades: Two-leaf brick facades with an insulated air cavity, rendered and painted for exteriors.
  • Glazing: Thermally broken aluminium frames with double glazing, and motorised blinds in the bedrooms.
  • Energy: Hot water from an aerothermal heat pump, insulated facades and large windows for daylight.

Specifications

The developer’s own building specification, summarised by part of the home.

  • Porcelain stoneware floors in the hall, living room, bedrooms and passage, with white skirting to match the interior doors.
  • Smooth emulsion paint on the walls, and suspended ceilings throughout the home.
  • Fitted with wall and base units in a contemporary design, porcelain floor, a worktop with a splashback between the units, and a sink with a mixer tap.
  • An induction hob, an extractor hood or filter unit, an electric oven, a panelled dishwasher, a refrigerator and a washing machine.
  • Ceramic tiled walls and porcelain stoneware floors in both bathrooms.
  • Thermostatic taps with a shower column, resin shower trays with screens, white sanitaryware, and a ceramic washbasin on a vanity unit.
  • Non-slip stoneware paving, with a water point, drainage, lighting, a power socket and a TV socket on every terrace.
  • The specification gives homes on the top floor a large sun terrace.
  • Thermally broken aluminium frames with double glazing, and motorised insulated aluminium roller blinds in the bedrooms.
  • An armoured entrance door with a spyhole, white lacquered interior doors with chrome handles, and wardrobes with a hanging rail, a shelf and drawers.
  • Ducted air conditioning for heating and cooling with a thermostat, and hot water from a high-efficiency aerothermal heat pump.
  • A ventilation system, a video door entry phone to the portals and the community gate, LED lighting in the bathrooms, kitchen and passages, and pre-installation for electric vehicle charging.
  • A gated community, with porcelain floors and motion-sensor lighting in the communal interiors, and lifts with automatic doors.
  • An underground garage with automatic doors worked by remote control, and painted storage rooms with metal doors.

Source: the developer’s building specification, which carries no printed date and says the images in it are an approximation of the finished homes, that changes may be made for technical or legal reasons, and that furniture is not included. The same document was supplied for both phases.
