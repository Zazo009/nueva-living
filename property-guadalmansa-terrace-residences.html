Overview

19 homes, three blocks

Guadalmansa Terrace Residences is a gated scheme on the hillside at La Resina, on the New Golden Mile between Estepona and Marbella, where the Guadalmansa valley opens towards the sea. The blocks step back floor by floor so that each home keeps a deep, largely uninterrupted terrace.

19 of the 66 homes in the current phase are available: four on the ground floor, three of them with a private garden, four penthouses with a solarium, and eleven on the first and second floors. Nine have two bedrooms and ten have three.

Built areas, which include each home's share of the common areas, run from 101.01 to 134.71 sqm, with terraces from 14.98 to 82.44 sqm. Prices run from EUR 414,000 to EUR 774,000 and exclude the 10% IVA. The building licence is granted and delivery is scheduled for the first quarter of 2028.

Quality specifications

What the homes are built with

The developer's own quality specification, grouped by part of the home.

Structure and envelope
  • Reinforced concrete foundation, as a slab or footings depending on the geotechnical study, and a reinforced concrete structure built to the Spanish building code.
  • Rendered facades finished in exterior-grade paint, with thermal and acoustic insulation to the building code.
  • Separating walls between homes in ceramic brick, lined on both sides with self-supporting plasterboard and insulation.
  • Interior partitions in plasterboard on a metal frame, with acoustic and thermal insulation.
Windows and doors
  • Lacquered aluminium joinery with a thermal break and double glazing with an air chamber.
  • Motorised blinds in the living room and the main bedroom.
  • A reinforced entrance door with a security lock, finished inside to match the interior doors.
  • Lacquered interior doors and fitted wardrobes finished to match, lined inside with a hanging rail and shelf.
Floors, walls and ceilings
  • Large-format flooring with a rigid limestone and polymer core in a natural-wood finish, water-repellent and hard-wearing.
  • Non-slip porcelain stoneware on the terraces.
  • Ceramic wall tiling in the bathrooms.
  • False ceilings in plasterboard or plaster throughout, and in bathrooms where the air conditioning units sit, with smooth paint in soft tones on the walls and white on the ceilings.
Kitchen
  • A fitted kitchen with wall and base units in a contemporary design.
  • Equipped with an oven, a microwave, an induction hob, an extractor hood, a fridge and a dishwasher.
  • A sink with a mixer tap.
Bathrooms
  • White vitrified porcelain sanitary ware.
  • Light-coloured resin shower trays, with a shower screen in the main bathroom.
  • Mixer taps from a high-quality brand.
Climate and energy
  • Ducted hot and cold air conditioning, with supply grilles in the living room and the bedrooms.
  • Hot water produced by aerothermal heat pumps.
  • A BREEAM rating for the scheme, an independent assessment of construction quality and running costs.
Electrical and communications
  • Electrical and telecommunications installation to current Spanish regulations.
  • A video entry system.
Communal areas
  • Extensive landscaped gardens, with outdoor walkways in non-slip ceramic paving and roads finished for outdoor use.
  • Two communal outdoor pools.
  • A spa with an indoor pool and a hydromassage area, a cold plunge bath, a sauna, changing rooms, toilets and showers.
  • A fully equipped gym for residents.
  • An automatic gate to the development, opened by remote control, with access control.

Source: the developer's quality specification for this phase, in its English and Spanish versions, read against each other. The specification is indicative and the developer reserves the right to change it for technical, legal or material-supply reasons during construction.

Architecture

Stepped back, towards the sea

The blocks follow the slope, each floor set back from the one below so the terraces stay deep and open rather than shaded by the balcony above. Facades are rendered and painted, insulated to the Spanish building code, and the parking sits underneath, which keeps cars out of the gardens between the blocks.

  • The homes — Gardens on the ground floor, deep terraces on the floors above, and a solarium on each penthouse.
  • Inside — Large-format flooring with a limestone and polymer core, false ceilings throughout and fitted wardrobes.
  • The communal spaces — Two outdoor pools, an indoor spa pool with a hydromassage area, a sauna, a gym and a co-working lounge.
  • Energy — Aerothermal hot water, ducted air conditioning and a BREEAM rating for the scheme.

FAQ

Common buyer questions

Can foreigners buy property in Spain?

Yes. There are no restrictions on non-Spanish nationals buying property in Spain, whether as a resident or non-resident.

What is an NIE number and do I need one?

An NIE (Numero de Identificacion de Extranjero) is a tax ID number required for any property purchase in Spain by a non-Spanish national. Nueva Living can guide you through obtaining one before you reserve.

What costs should I budget for on top of the purchase price?

Buyers typically budget for transfer tax or VAT, notary fees, land registry fees and legal fees on top of the purchase price. Nueva Living provides a full, current cost breakdown for your chosen residence before you reserve.

Can I get a mortgage in Spain as a non-resident?

Many Spanish banks offer mortgages to non-resident buyers, typically financing a portion of the purchase price. Exact terms depend on the bank and your personal financial profile.

What is the difference between off-plan and completed properties?

Off-plan means the development is still under construction and is usually sold with staged payments through to completion. A completed property is ready to view and move into now.

How does the reservation and payment process work?

Reservation and payment structures vary by development and are set out in full before you reserve. Nueva Living reconfirms the current schedule for your chosen residence at every step.

Can I rent out the property after purchase?

This depends on the individual development and local regulations, which can vary by community and municipality. Nueva Living will confirm the specific rules for a development before you reserve.

Do I need a lawyer?

Yes, we strongly recommend independent legal representation for any property purchase in Spain. Nueva Living can put you in touch with independent lawyers experienced in Costa del Sol property.

What is available?

19 of the 66 homes in the current phase: nine with two bedrooms and ten with three, including four penthouses with a solarium.

What is included in the price?

The home, at least one underground parking space, and a garden, a solarium or a storage room depending on the home. Prices exclude the 10% IVA.

When will it be finished?

The developer gives the first quarter of 2028 for this phase. The building licence is granted and the earlier phases of the scheme are already built.

How is the payment structured?

A EUR 6,600 reservation with VAT included, 25% plus VAT on signing the private purchase contract less the reservation, 10% plus VAT six months later, and 65% plus VAT on completion at the notary.

What are the communal facilities?

Two outdoor pools, an indoor spa pool with a hydromassage area, a sauna, changing rooms, a fully equipped gym, a co-working lounge and landscaped gardens, behind a gate with controlled access.

Is the scheme certified?

The developer states that the scheme will carry a BREEAM rating, an independent assessment of how the building is built and run. We ask for the certificate when it is issued.
