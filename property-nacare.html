Overview

Twenty homes, six available

Nacare is a building of 20 apartments and penthouses with three and four bedrooms in Estepona, with views of the sea and the mountains. The brochure describes south-facing homes with cross ventilation, open-plan layouts and generous terraces. Six homes are listed as available: five apartments on the first floor and one penthouse.

The shared areas include a spa with an indoor heated pool, a gym, an owners’ lounge and bar, and a sky pool with a rooftop lounge, with an owners’ club and a list of services. The developer’s sheet says the building licence and the first-occupation licence are pending and that the start and end of construction are to be confirmed.

Architecture

A terraced building, open to the sea

In the images the building is a terraced block with curved sand-coloured floor slabs that project over the terraces, glass balustrades, planting on every level and gardens around it.

The brochure describes an architecture defined by a Mediterranean essence: south-facing homes with cross ventilation, open-plan layouts with generous terraces, and penthouses with direct lift access from the parking level.

  • Layouts — Open-plan living, kitchen and dining areas of 69 to 86 sqm, with a large terrace beside them, according to the plans.
  • Bedrooms — A main bedroom of 27 to 34 sqm with its own bathroom, and two or three further bedrooms of 18 to 22 sqm.
  • Lifts and parking — Lifts serve the homes, and the penthouses have direct lift access from the parking level. The plans show underground garage floors.
  • Rooftop — The brochure lists a sky pool and a rooftop lounge, and the plan of penthouse 12 adds a rooftop floor of its own with an uncovered solarium, a covered living room and a jacuzzi.

FAQ

Common buyer questions

Can foreigners buy property in Spain?

Yes. There are no restrictions on non-Spanish nationals buying property in Spain, whether as a resident or non-resident.

What is an NIE number and do I need one?

An NIE (Numero de Identificacion de Extranjero) is a tax ID number required for any property purchase in Spain by a non-Spanish national. Nueva Living can guide you through obtaining one before you reserve.

What costs should I budget for on top of the purchase price?

Buyers typically budget for transfer tax or VAT, notary fees, land registry fees and legal fees on top of the purchase price. Nueva Living provides a full, current cost breakdown for your chosen residence before you reserve.

Can I get a mortgage in Spain as a non-resident?

Many Spanish banks offer mortgages to non-resident buyers, typically financing a portion of the purchase price. Exact terms depend on the bank and your personal financial profile.

What is the difference between off-plan and completed properties?

Off-plan means the development is still under construction and is usually sold with staged payments through to completion. A completed property is ready to view and move into now.

How does the reservation and payment process work?

Reservation and payment structures vary by development and are set out in full before you reserve. Nueva Living reconfirms the current schedule for your chosen residence at every step.

Can I rent out the property after purchase?

This depends on the individual development and local regulations, which can vary by community and municipality. Nueva Living will confirm the specific rules for a development before you reserve.

Do I need a lawyer?

Yes, we strongly recommend independent legal representation for any property purchase in Spain. Nueva Living can put you in touch with independent lawyers experienced in Costa del Sol property.

How many homes are available?

The building has 20 homes and six are currently available: one four-bedroom home and five three-bedroom homes, from EUR 2,500,000 to EUR 4,800,000 plus VAT.

Is VAT included?

No. The price list states that prices are net and do not include VAT, and it does not give the rate. We confirm the rate and the other purchase costs in writing.

Is there parking?

Yes. The price list gives each home garage spaces: two for homes 1, 5, 9 and 13, three for home 17 and four for penthouse 12.

When will it be finished?

No document we hold gives a date. The developer’s sheet marks the building licence and the first-occupation licence as pending and the start and end of construction as to be confirmed. We ask for the contractual date and put it in writing.

What does the building offer?

The brochure lists a spa, an indoor heated pool, massage and beauty rooms, a gym and flex room, an owners’ lounge and bar, a meeting room, a sky pool and a rooftop lounge, and landscaped gardens. We ask which services are included and which are charged.
