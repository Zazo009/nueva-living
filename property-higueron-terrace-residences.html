Overview

Terraces first, and a club below

Higueron Terrace Residences is the first phase of a larger scheme at El Higuerón, in Fuengirola, between the town and Benalmádena. It is made of apartments, garden homes and top-floor homes in six portals, every one with a large terrace, and the developer describes the whole scheme as 198 homes of one, two and three bedrooms.

25 of the 87 homes in this phase are listed as available, from EUR 499,000. The shared spaces include several pools, a gym, a sauna and a spa with a contrast zone, a lounge and a coworking room, and the railway station and the nearest beaches are within five minutes.

Location

El Higuerón, between two towns

El Higuerón is a residential area of Fuengirola, between the centre of Fuengirola and Benalmádena. The developer’s dossier gives under five minutes to the Cercanías station, the beaches of Carvajal and Torreblanca and the centre of Benalmádena, ten minutes to the centre of Fuengirola and Mijas Pueblo, and 15 to 20 minutes to Málaga airport.

Quality specifications

What the homes are built with

The developer’s own specification, summarised by part of the home.

Floors and tiling
  • Large-format porcelain floors in a light stone or timber finish, with oak boards available in the dry rooms.
  • Large-format light stone porcelain on the terraces and top-brand tiling in the bathrooms.
Climate and energy
  • Ducted aerothermal air conditioning with brands such as Samsung or Daikin, and a zone system that controls each room.
  • Underfloor heating through the whole home on the top floor, and in the main bedroom and bathroom of the apartments and garden homes.
Kitchen
  • Kitchen delivered fully fitted and furnished, with high and low cabinets and an island by Lube and a Dekton worktop by Cosentino.
  • Electrolux fridge, ceramic hob, oven, extractor hood, microwave, sink and dishwasher, with a washing machine and a dryer in the laundry room.
Bathrooms
  • Duravit, Laufen and Hansgrohe sanitary ware and taps, bathroom furniture and mirrors with LED backlighting.
  • A jacuzzi in every home, according to the specification.
Windows and doors
  • Cortizo or Technal windows with high thermal and acoustic performance, sliding or tilt-and-turn.
  • Wardrobes in a white or oak finish with shelves, drawers, a hanging rail and lighting.
Technology and lifts
  • Home automation with flood detectors in the bathrooms and the kitchen, a kitchen smoke detector, alerts for power cuts and floods, and monitoring of electricity and water use, climate, underfloor heating and air quality.
  • Cable TV and network in the living room and bedrooms, and Otis lifts.
Warranties
  • Under the Spanish Building Regulation Law, a 10-year warranty on structural defects and a 3-year warranty on defects affecting habitability.

Source: the developer’s quality specification, version 5, in English and Spanish. It says that materials and brands may be replaced by others of equal or higher quality, and Nueva Living asks for the current version in writing before any reservation.

Architecture

White blocks, timber and glass

The blocks are four storeys, stepped across the hillside, with terraces on every home, glass balustrades and timber-coloured soffits, behind landscaped gardens and palms.

The facades are smooth or textured monolayer render, and the sloped roofs sit on the top floor while the terraces have flat, non-slip floors.

  • Facades — Smooth or textured monolayer render with glass balustrades around the open areas.
  • Terraces — A large terrace with every home, with a watertight electrical outlet.
  • Top floor — Sloped roofs on the top floor, and underfloor heating through the whole of those homes.
  • Structure — Foundations of footings or slabs and a structure of pillars, slabs and retaining walls, to the geotechnical study.

FAQ

Common buyer questions

Can foreigners buy property in Spain?

Yes. There are no restrictions on non-Spanish nationals buying property in Spain, whether as a resident or non-resident.

What is an NIE number and do I need one?

An NIE (Numero de Identificacion de Extranjero) is a tax ID number required for any property purchase in Spain by a non-Spanish national. Nueva Living can guide you through obtaining one before you reserve.

What costs should I budget for on top of the purchase price?

Buyers typically budget for transfer tax or VAT, notary fees, land registry fees and legal fees on top of the purchase price. Nueva Living provides a full, current cost breakdown for your chosen residence before you reserve.

Can I get a mortgage in Spain as a non-resident?

Many Spanish banks offer mortgages to non-resident buyers, typically financing a portion of the purchase price. Exact terms depend on the bank and your personal financial profile.

What is the difference between off-plan and completed properties?

Off-plan means the development is still under construction and is usually sold with staged payments through to completion. A completed property is ready to view and move into now.

How does the reservation and payment process work?

Reservation and payment structures vary by development and are set out in full before you reserve. Nueva Living reconfirms the current schedule for your chosen residence at every step.

Can I rent out the property after purchase?

This depends on the individual development and local regulations, which can vary by community and municipality. Nueva Living will confirm the specific rules for a development before you reserve.

Do I need a lawyer?

Yes, we strongly recommend independent legal representation for any property purchase in Spain. Nueva Living can put you in touch with independent lawyers experienced in Costa del Sol property.

When will the homes be ready?

What does the specification include?
