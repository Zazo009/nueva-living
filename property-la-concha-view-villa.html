The development

La Concha View Villa is a single detached house under construction in Nueva Andalucía, Marbella, arranged over a lower level, a ground floor and a rooftop solarium, with a private pool and a landscaped garden on a plot of 1,009.47 sqm.

The developer gives 458.64 sqm of enclosed built area and 403.83 sqm of terraces, 862.47 sqm in total, with 311.79 sqm of garden and a 48.62 sqm pool. There are five bedrooms, five bathrooms and two guest WCs, a gym and parking for two cars. The building licence is granted and completion is given as Q3 2026.

Architecture · Built to a stated specification

The developer publishes a quality report that names its fittings brand by brand. The points below are taken from it, not from marketing copy.

  • Flooring: Petralava Marfil 90x90 cm porcelain throughout, in a non-slip finish indoors, with Alpujarra Marfil to the access road.
  • Facade: White CACO3 projectable mortar, screeded and trowelled in a 20 mm layer.
  • Glazing: Reynaers HiFinity sliding doors and ConceptPatio 68 windows, in RAL 7016 anthracite matt.
  • Bathrooms: Jacob Delafon Bastille wall-hung WCs and bidets, Duscholux Gravity screens, Grohe Skate Cosmopolitan S in black.
  • Kitchen and mixers: Icónico A-LE12 and D1 mixers, Gunni Trentino Bowl Glaceé, and a Vola 2121C in black at the kitchen.
  • Lighting and switches: Artemide, Leds C4 and VIBIA fittings inside and out, with Jung LS990 switches and sockets.

What does the villa cost?
