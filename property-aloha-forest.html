The development

Aloha Forest brings 16 apartments and penthouses to a gated setting in Marbella's Golf Valley, within walking distance of Aloha Golf and the international school, and minutes from Puerto Banús, Las Brisas Golf and the Golden Mile.

The development is being delivered in two phases. Phase I -- the 16 residences currently available -- began construction in 2024, has a fully finished show apartment, and is due for handover in 2026. A second, larger phase is planned to follow.

Architecture · Considered design, real materials

The two Phase I blocks -- Palm Tree and Olive Tree -- are built with a reinforced concrete structure, travertine marble facade cladding and flat roofs with polystyrene insulation, designed for a low-density, contemporary Mediterranean silhouette within the gated grounds.

Interiors use large-format 90x90 cm natural Palancar stone flooring, off-white oak carpentry and custom LED-lit dressing rooms, with Iroko wood entrance doors and Cortizo thermal-break aluminium windows to the exterior.

  • Villeroy & Boch sanitaryware with Fantini taps and freestanding tubs.
  • DAIKIN aerothermal hot water, underfloor heating and ducted air conditioning with smart thermostats.
  • Full LED lighting, internet/TV points and Wi-Fi video intercom.
