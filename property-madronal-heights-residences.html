Overview

One hillside, three phases

Madroñal Heights Residences is the name we give to a scheme of 107 apartments and penthouses on a hillside in Benahavís, above Golf Valley, with El Madroñal behind it. It is being built in three phases. The first, 49 homes in seven blocks, is finished; the second and third, 58 homes between them, are under construction, and their owners share the first phase’s health club, pools and kids’ club.

Twenty homes are available on the developer’s price lists. Four are in the first phase and ready for keys: two four-bedroom penthouses and two three-bedroom homes on the ground and first floors of the circular building. One is in the second phase and fifteen are in the third, from two-bedroom apartments to four-bedroom penthouses.

Ground-floor homes in the later phases have a garden with a private pool, and penthouses have a roof terrace, with a pool on every later-phase plan. Every price is the developer’s net figure; VAT at the current 10 per cent is added on top.

Quality specifications

What the homes are built with

The developer’s specification for the first phase, summarised by part of the home. Where the circular building differs, it is said.

Structure and insulation
  • Reinforced concrete foundations and frame, with insulated reinforced-concrete outer walls: 8 cm of insulation outside and 4 cm inside.
  • Flat inverted roofs with continuous insulation and a double asphalt waterproof sheet; insulated plasterboard partitions with rock wool.
Floors and glazing
  • Large-format porcelain floors inside, 100 by 100 cm, and the same collection, non-slip, on the terraces; in the circular building, porcelain with a timber finish.
  • Lacquered aluminium frames with double glazing and solar protection, and glass balustrades 110 cm high.
Kitchen and laundry
  • Floor-to-ceiling kitchen units with a central island and a porcelain splashback, with Miele appliances or similar; Gaggenau or similar in the circular building.
  • A separate laundry room with a washing machine and a tumble dryer.
Bathrooms
  • Porcelain tiling floor to ceiling in the showers, suspended toilets and wall-hung vanity units.
  • A freestanding bath and a ceiling rain shower in the master bathroom.
Climate
  • Ducted hot and cold air conditioning with a thermostat in the living room and each bedroom.
  • Water-based underfloor heating in the living room and bedrooms, electric underfloor heating in the bathrooms, and hot water from an accumulator tank.
Doors and technology
  • An oak front door 265 cm high with security bolts, and white lacquered interior doors 240 cm high.
  • Home automation that works with Android and iOS, a video intercom, alarm pre-installation and fibre pre-installation.
Garage and community
  • Two garage spaces and a storage room with each home, one of each for certain two-bedroom homes, and pre-installation for electric car charging on selected spaces.
  • A gated plot with a security post at the main entrance watching the perimeter by camera, and a concierge office at the health club.

Source: the developer’s quality specifications for Blocks 1 to 6 and for Block 7, the circular building, of the first phase. They carry no date, say the specifications may change for technical reasons, and add that the developer offers a customisation plan priced separately. We hold no specification for Phases II and III and ask for it before reservation.

Why It Fits

What it is

Twenty apartments and penthouses available in a three-phase scheme above Golf Valley, four of them finished and sixteen under construction.

Who it is for

Buyers who want to move into a finished home now, and buyers who would rather buy off-plan in a later phase with a garden pool or a roof terrace.

What makes it different

The amenities of the first phase are built and open: a health club with a 25-metre indoor pool, a 25-metre outdoor pool and round pools in the gardens. And one block is circular, with one home on each floor.

Why location matters

The hillside looks over Golf Valley to the coast, with the mountains behind, and the developer gives about eight minutes by car to Puerto Banús.

Architecture

Curved terraces, a circular block

The first phase is built: white blocks with long curved terraces and glass balustrades, stepped down the slope so that the buildings sit lower as the ground falls, around terraced gardens planted with olive trees.

Block 7 is circular, with one home on each of three floors around a round central atrium that brings in light, and a roof terrace with a pool for the penthouse. The later phases keep the curved terraces and add gardens with private pools and pools on the roof terraces.

  • The first phase — Forty-nine homes in seven blocks, finished and open.
  • The circular building — Three homes, one per floor, around a central atrium of 22 sqm.
  • The later phases — Fifty-eight homes in further blocks, under construction.
  • Terraces — From 37.99 to 232.39 sqm on the available homes.
  • Gardens and pools — Private-use gardens with pools on the later-phase ground floors.

FAQ

Common buyer questions

Can foreigners buy property in Spain?

Yes. There are no restrictions on non-Spanish nationals buying property in Spain, whether as a resident or non-resident.

What is an NIE number and do I need one?

An NIE (Numero de Identificacion de Extranjero) is a tax ID number required for any property purchase in Spain by a non-Spanish national. Nueva Living can guide you through obtaining one before you reserve.

What costs should I budget for on top of the purchase price?

Buyers typically budget for transfer tax or VAT, notary fees, land registry fees and legal fees on top of the purchase price. Nueva Living provides a full, current cost breakdown for your chosen residence before you reserve.

Can I get a mortgage in Spain as a non-resident?

Many Spanish banks offer mortgages to non-resident buyers, typically financing a portion of the purchase price. Exact terms depend on the bank and your personal financial profile.

What is the difference between off-plan and completed properties?

Off-plan means the development is still under construction and is usually sold with staged payments through to completion. A completed property is ready to view and move into now.

How does the reservation and payment process work?

Reservation and payment structures vary by development and are set out in full before you reserve. Nueva Living reconfirms the current schedule for your chosen residence at every step.

Can I rent out the property after purchase?

This depends on the individual development and local regulations, which can vary by community and municipality. Nueva Living will confirm the specific rules for a development before you reserve.

Do I need a lawyer?

Yes, we strongly recommend independent legal representation for any property purchase in Spain. Nueva Living can put you in touch with independent lawyers experienced in Costa del Sol property.

What do the homes cost?

From EUR 999,000 to EUR 2,789,500 for the home, plus VAT at 10 per cent and purchase costs, as listed in the developer’s price lists of 23 September, 5 November and 10 November 2026.

How many are available?

Twenty: four key-ready homes in the first phase, one in the second and fifteen in the third.

Can I move in now?

Into a Phase I home, yes: the developer’s lists call all four key-ready. The later phases are under construction.

When are the later phases delivered?

For the first part of Phase II, the disclosure document gives the fourth quarter of 2026 and the developer’s 2026 films the third quarter of 2027. No document gives a date for Phase III. We ask for it in writing.

Do I share the amenities?

Yes. The disclosure document for Phase II and the developer’s website say owners of the later phases share the first phase’s health club, pools and kids’ club, and the new communal areas are shared too.

Is parking included?

The price lists give each home one to three parking spaces and a storage room. An extra space costs EUR 50,000, with limited availability.

What are the payment terms?

For the later phases: EUR 20,000 to reserve, 30% at the private contract less the deposit, 20% six months later, 10% nine months later and 40% on completion, each plus VAT.
