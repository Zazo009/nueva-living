Overview

Twenty-two homes, nine listed

This is one residential building of 22 homes near the centre of Estepona. It has a ground floor, four upper floors and a penthouse level, with two basement levels for parking and storage rooms. Every home has three bedrooms, a living and dining room with a kitchen and two bathrooms, and the main bedroom has its own bathroom.

The four ground-floor homes have a terrace, a private garden and a private pool, and every upper floor has four homes with large terraces. Some of the upper homes have a private rooftop solarium with its own pool, and the roof also has a communal pool, a solarium and services. Nine homes are listed with a price; the developer’s documents do not give a completion date.

Location

Estepona, under ten minutes from the marina and the beach

The building is in one of Estepona’s residential areas, close to the historic centre and to the A-7 and the AP-7. The developer’s brochure puts the Estepona marina and La Rada beach under ten minutes away, with shops, supermarkets, schools and sports facilities close by, and direct access to Málaga and its airport.

Quality specifications

What the homes are built with

The developer’s own quality specification, summarised by part of the home.

Structure, facade and roofs
  • A reinforced-concrete structure, with foundations designed to the geotechnical study, and a multilayer facade of outer brickwork, interior insulation, an air cavity and an inner lining, finished in an exterior mortar render.
  • Flat inverted roofs, waterproofed and insulated with rigid boards, finished in gravel where there is no access and in non-slip ceramic tiling where there is.
Windows and insulation
  • Lacquered aluminium frames with a thermal break, opening, tilting or sliding by location, with double glazing with a dehydrated air chamber chosen by orientation, and motorised bedroom shutters operated by a push button.
  • Dry plasterboard partitions on metal framing with mineral-wool thermal and acoustic insulation, moisture-resistant boards in wet areas, and walls between homes and common areas in perforated brick with an insulated lining.
Doors and wardrobes
  • A reinforced security front door with a three-point lock, lacquered flush interior doors with stainless-steel or matte-chrome ironmongery, and built-in modular wardrobes fully lined, with drawers, a shelf and a hanging rail.
Floors and ceilings
  • Large-format rectified porcelain stoneware throughout, non-slip rectified porcelain on the terraces, porcelain on the bathroom floors and walls, and plasterboard ceilings with mineral-wool insulation.
Kitchen
  • A fully fitted kitchen with laminate wall and base units, a worktop of high-resistance porcelain material about 12 mm thick or similar, an undermount stainless-steel sink and a chrome single-lever tap.
  • Appliances included: an electric oven, a microwave, an induction hob, an extractor hood, an integrated dishwasher, a fridge and a washing machine.
Bathrooms
  • Washbasins set into the countertop with wall-mounted vanity units, low-profile shower trays with glass screens, chrome thermostatic shower mixers with a sliding rail and hand shower, and toilets with concealed cisterns, dual flush and soft-close seats.
Climate, hot water and electrics
  • Ducted air conditioning for cooling and heating, zoned by room, hot water from an aerothermal system, electric underfloor heating in the bathrooms and a ventilation system with continuous air renewal.
  • Recessed LED lighting in the kitchen, hallways and bathrooms, LED lighting on the terraces, TV, radio and telephone points in the living rooms, kitchens and bedrooms, a colour video entry phone, and water points in the solarium terraces and ground-floor gardens.
Building and garage
  • A communal pool is planned on the roof terrace, with restroom facilities and a plant room. The entrance lobbies are finished in stone, ceramic or engineered stone and lit by LED with presence detectors.
  • Lifts serve every floor and connect directly to the garage levels, with automatic doors, overload detection and a telephone link. The garage floor is polished concrete.

Source: the developer’s quality specification for this development, which carries no version or date. It says the materials may be changed by the project management as long as the quality is not reduced, and that the information is not contractual.

Architecture

Curved white lines, planted terraces

In the developer’s computer-generated images the building has curved white balcony slabs, planted terraces on every level and a pergola on the roof, in a contemporary design that aims to bring in as much natural light as possible.

The homes have large glazed doors to their terraces, and the glazing in the living and dining room is set into the floor so that the room runs on into the terrace.

  • Structure — Reinforced concrete built to the Spanish Technical Building Code.
  • Facade — Multilayer: outer brickwork, interior insulation, an air cavity and an inner lining, finished in an exterior mortar render.
  • Glazing — Lacquered aluminium frames with a thermal break and double glazing, and motorised shutters in the bedrooms.
  • Terraces — Large-format non-slip porcelain paving, with LED lighting.

FAQ

Common buyer questions

Can foreigners buy property in Spain?

Yes. There are no restrictions on non-Spanish nationals buying property in Spain, whether as a resident or non-resident.

What is an NIE number and do I need one?

An NIE (Numero de Identificacion de Extranjero) is a tax ID number required for any property purchase in Spain by a non-Spanish national. Nueva Living can guide you through obtaining one before you reserve.

What costs should I budget for on top of the purchase price?

Buyers typically budget for transfer tax or VAT, notary fees, land registry fees and legal fees on top of the purchase price. Nueva Living provides a full, current cost breakdown for your chosen residence before you reserve.

Can I get a mortgage in Spain as a non-resident?

Many Spanish banks offer mortgages to non-resident buyers, typically financing a portion of the purchase price. Exact terms depend on the bank and your personal financial profile.

What is the difference between off-plan and completed properties?

Off-plan means the development is still under construction and is usually sold with staged payments through to completion. A completed property is ready to view and move into now.

How does the reservation and payment process work?

Reservation and payment structures vary by development and are set out in full before you reserve. Nueva Living reconfirms the current schedule for your chosen residence at every step.

Can I rent out the property after purchase?

This depends on the individual development and local regulations, which can vary by community and municipality. Nueva Living will confirm the specific rules for a development before you reserve.

Do I need a lawyer?

Yes, we strongly recommend independent legal representation for any property purchase in Spain. Nueva Living can put you in touch with independent lawyers experienced in Costa del Sol property.
