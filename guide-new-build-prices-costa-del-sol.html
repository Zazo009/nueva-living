How to Read This

Listed prices, not sale prices

Every figure on this page is a developer’s current list price for a new-build project on the Costa del Sol, read from the project files behind this site. Each project is represented by its from price, the lowest price in its published price list, and the tables compare those prices by area, by type of home and by delivery year.

These are asking prices of homes still for sale, not prices paid and not a price per square metre. A from price is the cheapest home in a project and says nothing about the rest of it. The method, including how tax is treated, is set out at the end of the page.

Method

How the figures are produced

What is counted

Every published project with a listed price and an area that is not archived. Projects are not weighted by size or by number of homes.

From price

The lowest price in the developer’s current price list for the project, as transcribed on its page. A list price for the cheapest home: not an average, not a typical home and not a sale price.

Tax

Prices are compared as each developer publishes them. Tax stated as excluded: 83. Stated as included: 0. Not stated: 18. Our guide to purchase costs sets out IVA at 10% and AJD at 1.2% on a new-build, 11.2% in taxes, before notary, registry and legal fees.

Purchase costs in Andalucia

Statistics

Lowest, median and highest are taken across the projects’ from prices. With an even number of projects the median is the mean of the middle two, rounded to the euro.

Types and delivery

Types are the property types each project publishes; duplexes count as apartments and duplex penthouses as penthouses. Delivery is the quarter or year the developer states.

Data date

Prices were last checked between May 2026 and October 2026. The page is rebuilt from the project files whenever the site is built.

Not included

Price per square metre, sale or transaction prices, resale homes, rental yields and return projections. Nueva Living provides current, factual information rather than return projections.

Common Questions

Questions about these figures

What does a from price mean?

It is the lowest price in a developer’s published price list for a project, for the cheapest home listed. It shows where a project starts, not what a typical home in it costs, and the tax column of the project table shows how each price is stated.

Why do prices differ so much between areas?

Part of the answer is what is being built. Projects selling only villas have a median from price of €2,375,000, against €511,000 for projects selling only apartments. Size, plot, setting and specification also differ, and a from price does not capture them.

Are these the prices buyers pay?

No. They are developers’ list prices, which can change without notice, not transaction prices. Nueva Living confirms the current price and availability of a home in writing before any reservation.

How often is this page updated?

The figures are produced from the project files each time the site is built, so a change to a project’s price list reaches this page with the next build. Each project page shows when its price was last checked; the checks behind this edition run from May 2026 to October 2026.
