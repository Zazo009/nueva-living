Overview

A furnished home, ready to move into

La Morelia de Marbella is a boutique gated development of apartments, duplexes and penthouses on an elevated plot in Nueva Andalucia's Golf Valley, each with its own private plunge pool and set around sub-tropical communal gardens and two cascading infinity pools.

Apartment 102 is a fully furnished, key-ready two-bedroom home with both bedrooms en suite, an open-plan living and dining area that opens onto a 73.90 sqm terrace, and a private 3 x 3 metre plunge pool. Unlike an off-plan purchase, this is a resale: what you see in the photographs is what you buy, and a straightforward reservation-to-completion process typically closes within weeks rather than years.

Architecture

Contemporary styling, built to a high specification

The apartments were designed with floor-to-ceiling glazed doors that retract fully into the wall, opening the living space onto the terrace and bringing the outside in. Interiors run in Crema Marfil marble from the entrance through to the terrace, with a matching non-slip finish outdoors.

Kitchens are fitted with white lacquered soft-close units, Silestone worktops and Siemens or Bosch appliances. Bathrooms carry Crema Marfil marble, Villeroy & Boch sanitaryware and Grohe taps, with water-based underfloor heating throughout, including the bathrooms.

  • Siemens/Bosch kitchen appliances and Silestone worktops.
  • Underfloor heating by water throughout, including the bathrooms.

FAQ

Common buyer questions

Can foreigners buy property in Spain?

Yes. There are no restrictions on non-Spanish nationals buying property in Spain, whether as a resident or non-resident.

What is an NIE number and do I need one?

An NIE (Numero de Identificacion de Extranjero) is a tax ID number required for any property purchase in Spain by a non-Spanish national. Nueva Living can guide you through obtaining one before you reserve.

What costs should I budget for on top of the purchase price?

Buyers typically budget for transfer tax or VAT, notary fees, land registry fees and legal fees on top of the purchase price. Nueva Living provides a full, current cost breakdown for your chosen residence before you reserve.

Can I get a mortgage in Spain as a non-resident?

Many Spanish banks offer mortgages to non-resident buyers, typically financing a portion of the purchase price. Exact terms depend on the bank and your personal financial profile.

What is the difference between off-plan and completed properties?

Off-plan means the development is still under construction and is usually sold with staged payments through to completion. A completed property is ready to view and move into now.

How does the reservation and payment process work?

Reservation and payment structures vary by development and are set out in full before you reserve. Nueva Living reconfirms the current schedule for your chosen residence at every step.

Can I rent out the property after purchase?

This depends on the individual development and local regulations, which can vary by community and municipality. Nueva Living will confirm the specific rules for a development before you reserve.

Do I need a lawyer?

Yes, we strongly recommend independent legal representation for any property purchase in Spain. Nueva Living can put you in touch with independent lawyers experienced in Costa del Sol property.

Is this apartment off-plan or ready now?

Ready now. This is a resale: the apartment is complete, fully furnished and available to view immediately.

Does it come furnished?

Yes. The apartment is sold fully furnished, as shown in the photographs.

Is the private pool really private?

Yes. In addition to the development's two communal infinity pools, Apartment 102 has its own private 3 x 3 metre plunge pool on the terrace.

Does it allow short-term rental?

The documents supplied do not confirm a short-term rental licence for this apartment. Buyers who plan to let the property should confirm current licensing requirements with their own lawyer before purchase.

Is parking included?

Yes. The apartment is sold with underground parking and a storage room.

How does the purchase process work for a resale?

A refundable reservation deposit, followed by a private purchase contract with 10% paid within four weeks, then the remaining 90% at completion, typically within around 45 days.
