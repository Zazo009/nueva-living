The development

Arosa is a complex of 54 apartments in La Cala de Mijas, 13 with one bedroom, 11 with two and 30 with three, according to the developer’s dossier. The dossier describes open-plan homes with natural light and sea views, south-facing, in six numbered portals, with landscaped gardens, outdoor pools, an indoor pool, a gym and chill-out areas. Thirteen homes are listed with a price: three one-bedroom homes and one two-bedroom home on the ground floor with a garden, two three-bedroom homes on the first floor, and seven homes on the third floor with a solarium.

The infosheet gives the building licence as granted and the start of construction as expected for the second quarter of 2026, with the end of construction expected for the fourth quarter of 2028, and the availability list gives the stage as under construction. The infosheet carries no issue date and the end of construction is an estimate, so we ask how far the build has come.

Architecture · Stepped terraces, open to the sea

In the renders the block rises in stepped white terraces with glass balustrades, timber-toned screens and pergolas on the upper levels, set among gardens and pools.

The developer’s dossier describes terraces designed as a natural extension of the interior, and south-facing solariums with sea views.

  • Structure: Reinforced concrete flat slabs, with reinforced concrete walls in the basement, to the Technical Building Code.
  • Facade: A double exterior wall, with perforated solid brick outside and a self-supporting dry lining with thermal insulation inside.
  • Glazing: Lacquered aluminium frames with a thermal break and double thermo-acoustic glazing, with sliding doors recessed in the floor to the terraces on the main facade.
  • Roofs: Flat roofs, waterproofed and insulated, in gravel where nobody walks and non-slip ceramic and porcelain paving on the terraces.

Residences · Thirteen homes, four kinds

The thirteen listed homes fall into four groups by floor and outdoor space. The table below lists each one with its price.

One-bedroom homes with a garden

  • Three one-bedroom homes on the ground floor, 5.0.A and 5.0.C in portal 5 and 6.0.A in portal 6, each with one bathroom, a covered terrace, an uncovered terrace and a garden.
  • Interior areas of 42.45 to 48.92 sqm, terraces of 22.23 to 25.45 sqm and gardens of 29.17 to 33.26 sqm.
  • The plans draw a storage room beside each home, of 48.62, 39.73 and 40.09 sqm in that order. The prices are EUR 705,000, EUR 705,000 and EUR 715,000.

A two-bedroom home with a garden

  • Home 5.0.B, on the ground floor of portal 5, has two bedrooms, two bathrooms, 67.20 sqm interior, terraces of 35.17 sqm and a garden of 45.63 sqm.
  • The plan draws a storage room of 28.43 sqm beside the home. The price is EUR 805,000.

Three-bedroom homes on the first floor

  • Homes 5.1.B in portal 5 and 6.1.A in portal 6 have three bedrooms, two bathrooms and 97.23 and 97.28 sqm interior.
  • Each has a terrace of 66.67 sqm on the plan, which includes a patio of 7.34 sqm, with a covered part of 25.99 sqm. Both are priced at EUR 1,090,000.

Third-floor homes with a solarium

  • Seven homes on the third floor, each with a solarium on its own level, reached by the stair shown on the plan: 2.3.B in portal 2, 3.3.A and 3.3.B in portal 3, 4.3.A and 4.3.B in portal 4, and 5.3.A and 5.3.B in portal 5. Two have two bedrooms, 2.3.B and 3.3.A, and five have three, all with two bathrooms.
  • Interior areas of 82.51 to 100.35 sqm, terraces of 27.87 to 37.70 sqm and solariums of 77.41 to 104.23 sqm. The plan of every one of them draws a small pool on the solarium.
  • The prices run from EUR 1,095,000 to EUR 1,570,000.

Specifications

The developer’s own quality specification, summarised by part of the home.

  • Reinforced concrete basement walls and flat slabs to the Technical Building Code, with a double exterior wall of perforated solid brick and a self-supporting dry lining with thermal insulation.
  • Homes separated by half a foot of perforated solid brick with a double self-supporting lining and acoustic insulation, and rooms divided by self-supporting dry partitions.
  • Waterproofed roofs and terraces, flat roofs in gravel where nobody walks, and non-slip ceramic and porcelain stoneware paving on porches and terraces.
  • Thermal and acoustic insulation in the exterior walls, and anti-impact sheeting between floors.
  • Large-format rectified porcelain stoneware with a skirting board in the living areas, and a floating vinyl floor in the bedroom area.
  • Suspended ceilings throughout the home, and tiled bathrooms and toilets.
  • A security front door with a three-point lock and anti-lever hinges, white interior doors, and built-in wardrobes lined in melamine board, with a shelf, a hanging rail and drawers.
  • Lacquered aluminium frames with a thermal break, in a colour the developer sets, and thermo-acoustic double glazing in all living areas, with automatic lacquered aluminium slat blinds in the bedrooms.
  • A fitted kitchen with high and low units, a fridge, a dishwasher, a microwave, an oven, an induction hob, an extractor, a sink and a washing machine.
  • Vitrified porcelain sanitary ware, black mixer taps and glass shower screens.
  • Water and drainage connections for a washing machine and a dishwasher.
  • Hot water from an aerothermal system, underfloor heating, and air conditioning through ducts in the suspended ceilings of the living room and the bedrooms, connected to the aerothermal system.
  • Mechanical ventilation inside the homes.
  • LED lighting designed by the architect, TV and telephone points in the kitchen, living room and bedrooms, sockets with USB ports in the main rooms, and a video intercom with a screen.
  • An automatic garage door, large landscaped gardens with automatic irrigation, and a communal indoor and outdoor pool with a gym and a sauna.

Source: the quality specification in the developer’s documents, which is undated. It says that it is at the development stage and that its references may change for technical reasons without any reduction in the quality or design of the result.

Questions about Arosa

How many homes are available?

The complex has 54 homes and 13 are listed with a price: three one-bedroom homes, three two-bedroom homes and seven three-bedroom homes, from EUR 705,000 to EUR 1,570,000 plus VAT.

Is VAT included?

No. The list gives the developer’s net prices and the infosheet adds VAT to each instalment. The rate is not stated, so we confirm it and the other purchase costs in writing.

Is there parking and storage?

The plans of the four ground-floor homes draw a storage room beside the home, and the specification mentions a car park with an automatic door. No document says whether a storage room or a garage space is included in each price, and we confirm it.

Do the third-floor homes have a private pool?

The plans of the seven listed third-floor homes draw a small pool on the solarium, but the availability list does not mention one. We confirm whether it is built and included.

When will it be finished?

The infosheet gives the end of construction as expected for the fourth quarter of 2028 and carries no issue date, so it is an estimate. We ask how far the build has come and for the contractual date.
