The development

This page lists one home: a three-bedroom home of 170.90 sqm at EUR 891,000, reference EH-20. It belongs to a development of 80 townhouses with three and four bedrooms in Mijas Costa, on the first line of a golf course. The other homes in the development are not listed here, and we hold no size, price or plan for them.

The sales portal lists the development as built, and the photographs we hold show finished buildings and a communal pool. The developer’s infosheet gives construction as started in Q3 2022 and finished in Q3 2024, and says that the delivery date in the private purchase contract prevails.

Architecture · Two-storey rows, slatted canopies and stone walls

In the photographs the homes are white, flat-roofed rows of two storeys, with canopies and screens of golden slats, glass balustrades on the upper terraces and stone-filled retaining walls between the terraces and the paths.

The specification says that its references and pictures are indicative, and that the developer may change materials for technical or legal reasons, replacing them with others of equal or higher quality.

  • Structure: Reinforced concrete built to current regulations and the Technical Building Code, on a foundation designed from the geotechnical study.
  • Facade: Multi-layer brick with interior insulation, an air chamber and a plasterboard lining, finished in rendered mortar with cladding or panelled elements.
  • Glazing: Dark lacquered aluminium frames with a thermal break and Climalit-type double glazing with a low-emissivity treatment, sliding doors from the living rooms and the bedrooms onto the terraces, and motorised aluminium blinds, with security blinds on the ground floor.
  • Roofs: Inverted flat roofs, waterproofed and insulated, with gravel in the areas nobody walks on and non-slip ceramic flooring where they are walkable.

Specifications

The developer’s own quality specification, summarised by part of the home.

  • Rectified porcelain stoneware tiles of 75 x 75 cm throughout the home, with white timber skirting, and non-slip tiles of the same size on the terraces. The bathroom floors are tiled in the same size, in a different model in bathrooms 2 and 3.
  • In the bathrooms, rectified white-paste tiles of 40 x 120 cm on the shower and basin walls, with plain paint elsewhere, and porcelain tiles of 30 x 90 cm at the basin in the guest toilet. Plasterboard suspended ceilings throughout, with a recessed perimeter detail in the living room and the main bedroom, in soft-coloured paint on the walls and white on the ceilings.
  • A security entrance door with a wide-angle spyhole, smooth lacquered solid-wood interior doors with a rubber acoustic seal and concealed hinges, and built-in wardrobes finished like the doors, with a luggage shelf and a hanging rail.
  • An island kitchen delivered furnished, with high-capacity upper and lower units in a laminate finish, and a Silestone compact quartz worktop with the same material as the splashback.
  • A large stainless-steel Franke undermount sink with a pull-out tap, and Siemens appliances: an integrated extractor, an oven, a microwave, an induction hob, a free-standing washing machine, an integrated dishwasher and an integrated combination fridge-freezer.
  • In the main bathroom, a double washbasin by Porcelanosa, 120 x 50 cm or 150 x 50 cm depending on the type of home, on a vanity unit, with a shower on a mineral-resin tray, a built-in thermostatic mixer with a ceiling rain head and a hand shower, a fixed screen and an anti-mist mirror.
  • Depending on the type of home, bathroom 2 has a single basin and a shower or a bath, bathroom 3 a basin and a bath, and there may be a guest toilet. All the toilets are wall-hung, with built-in cisterns.
  • Hot and cold air conditioning through ducts hidden in the ceiling, with supply grilles in the living room and the bedroom and a digital thermostat, hot water from an aerothermal system, and electric underfloor heating in the bathrooms.
  • Sockets with USB charging in the living room and the bedrooms, LED lighting in the kitchen and the bathrooms, a colour video door entry, TV, radio and telephone points, basic home automation, water taps on the terraces and the private outdoor spaces, and a parking space with an electric-vehicle charging point for each home.
  • Communal areas planted with low-water native species on automatic drip irrigation, artificial grass in the communal gardens and the private gardens, a communal swimming pool for adults, green areas and a sunbathing area, a control booth and closed-circuit TV.
  • A vehicle entrance with an automatic gate on remote control, and lifts with automatic doors in the complex. The specification says that the common areas are designed to keep the community fees as low as possible; it gives no figure.

What to plan for

  • Private purchase contractThe private purchase contract (CPCV) is signed with a payment of EUR 9,000. The infosheet says that the amounts paid are guaranteed by a bank guarantee, and does not say whether the EUR 9,000 includes VAT.

  • VATThe infosheet states the completion payment as plus VAT, which indicates that the listed price is before VAT. The sales portal does not say, so we confirm it in writing.
  • What the price includesThe documents do not say whether a parking space or a storage room is included. They list a parking space with a charging point for each home, and a furnished kitchen with appliances.

Checks before any money moves

  • LicencesThe infosheet lists the building licence as granted on 10 June 2022 and leaves the first-occupation licence blank. We ask for a copy of both and for the certificate of completion.
  • The datesThe infosheet is an early document: it still announces construction as started, with the start in Q3 2022 and the end in Q3 2024, and says that the date in the contract prevails. The sales portal lists the development as built. We ask for the contractual date and for confirmation that the home is ready to occupy.
