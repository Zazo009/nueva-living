Referral & Ambassador Program

Your introduction deserves more than a thank you

The people you introduce to Nueva Living get the same private, unhurried search every client receives. When their search ends in a home, yours ends in a genuine reward.

The best homes on the Costa del Sol rarely reach the open market through advertising alone. They move through conversations — a friend who already trusts us, a colleague who found the right place and wants someone they care about to find theirs too. That is the introduction Nueva Living exists to reward.

Before You Introduce Someone

What makes a strong introduction

The introductions that go furthest are usually simple. Someone has already mentioned, in a conversation that happened naturally, that they are thinking about a home on the Costa del Sol. You do not need to sell them on Nueva Living — just let us pick up the conversation from there.

A few things tend to make an introduction genuinely useful to us: they are seriously considering a purchase, not just idly browsing; they have not already been in contact with another Costa del Sol agency about new-build options; and they are open to us reaching out directly, on your recommendation.

A simple way to say thank you

Introductions built on trust tend to find homes built to last.
The Reward

A share of our commission, paid on completion

Not a discount off someone else's purchase and not a finder's fee for a name on a list -- a genuine share of what we earn, paid once your introduction has actually bought a home through us. We confirm the exact percentage and payment terms with you directly, in writing, before any introduction is made.

Who Can Take Part

Open to anyone

Past Clients & Friends

If someone in your life is thinking about a new home on the Costa del Sol, this program is open to you, whether or not you have bought through us before.

A Genuine Introduction

The program rewards real introductions to people who are not already in touch with Nueva Living. We will always tell you honestly if a contact is already known to us.

Good to Know

The practical details

When You Get Paid

The reward is paid after your introduction's purchase legally completes, not at reservation stage. We will keep you updated on where things stand.

Taxes & Reporting

You are responsible for declaring any reward you receive under your own local tax rules. We recommend checking with an advisor if you are unsure.

Common Questions

What ambassadors usually ask us

How is my referral tracked once I submit it?

The moment you submit the form below, we record the introduction against your name and contact details before we reach out to your friend. That record is what confirms your reward if a purchase later completes.

Is there a limit to how many people I can refer?

No. You can introduce as many people as you like, and each genuine introduction is tracked and rewarded on its own.

What if my friend already has a Nueva Living project in mind?

We will still help them find the right home. The reward itself applies specifically to introductions to people who are not already in touch with Nueva Living, since it is there to recognise bringing us a genuinely new client.

Can I refer someone who lives outside Spain?

Yes. Many of our clients buy from abroad, so there is no restriction on where you or the person you introduce are based.

What if my friend contacts Nueva Living directly instead of through this form?

Submitting the introduction yourself, before we hear from them independently, is what establishes it as your referral. If you are planning to introduce someone, let us know first.

How will I know if my referral leads to a sale?

We keep you updated on the general progress of your introduction, within the bounds of your friend's own privacy, and confirm directly with you once a reward is due.
