The development

Higueron Parkside Residences is part of a complex of 214 two- and three-bedroom homes at El Higuerón, in Fuengirola, in 11 buildings of four levels. The top floors are Sky Villas, the ground floors are Garden Homes with a private garden, and every home has a terrace.

38 homes are listed as available, from EUR 499,000 to EUR 995,000, in portals 2 to 11. The developer’s portal lists the homes as ready to move in, and the dossier gives several outdoor pools, landscaped gardens, rest areas and a children’s play area.

Architecture · White blocks, terraces and gardens

The complex is eleven buildings of four levels, in white render, with private gardens on the ground floors and a terrace on every home.

Terrace railings combine metalwork and glass, and the windows are coloured aluminium with sliding openings in every room except the bathrooms.

  • Facades: White mortar render on the exterior.
  • Terraces: A terrace with every home, with railings of metalwork and glass.
  • Garden homes: Ground-floor homes with a private garden and a covered terrace.
  • Sky Villas: Top-floor homes with a terrace and a view of the sea.

Specifications

The developer’s own specification, summarised by part of the home.

  • Porcelain floor tiles of 75 by 75 cm throughout the home.
  • Bathroom walls tiled in some areas, with five ranges of formats and tones to choose from.
  • A combined aerothermal system for hot and cold air conditioning in the bedrooms and the living room, and for hot water.
  • Electric underfloor heating in the bathrooms, with the option of hydronic underfloor heating through the rest of the home.
  • Fitted kitchens with high and low cabinets and a Silestone-type worktop, integrated in the living room.
  • Appliances: ceramic hob, oven, microwave, extractor hood, fridge, dishwasher and washer-dryer, with an energy rating of A or higher, and the option to upgrade the kitchen.
  • Wall-hung toilets with integrated cisterns and Teka-type taps, extra-flat shower trays and screens.
  • A double vanity in the main bathroom and a wall-hung cabinet with drawers in the second.
  • Coloured aluminium windows with high thermal performance, sliding openings in every room except the bathrooms, and thermal and acoustic double glazing.
  • Lacquered interior doors 2.10 m high, built-in wardrobes with lined interiors, and pre-installation for motorised blinds in the bedrooms and living rooms.
  • Home automation with smoke detection and a flood sensor, which can be upgraded to control from a smartphone app.
  • TV outlets in the living room, the bedrooms and the terrace, with Jung-type switches and sockets.
  • Two parking spaces and a storeroom, according to the specification; the dossier says the storeroom is available in most homes.
  • Pre-installation for electric-vehicle charging, presence-detector lighting in the shared areas, swimming pools with a solarium and landscaped gardens with rest areas.

Source: the developer’s quality specifications for phases 5A and 5B, which are identical, and its dossier. They say that finishes may be replaced by others of equal or higher quality, and Nueva Living asks for the current version in writing before any reservation.

Questions about Higueron Parkside

Are the homes ready?

What comes with the home?

According to the specification, two parking spaces and a storeroom; the dossier says the storeroom is available in most homes. Every home has a terrace, and the ground-floor homes a private garden.
