The development

Lentisco Residences is a new building of 15 two-bedroom, two-bathroom homes in Los Pacos, Fuengirola, built as the second phase of an established development. The brochure says the pool, the gym, the sauna, the laundry and the garage of the earlier phase are already there, and every home in the new building has a terrace.

The price list of 5 November 2026 shows 11 of the 15 homes as sold and one as reserved. The three that remain are the penthouses on the top floor: Penthouses C and F with a private roof solarium of 96.72 and 104.96 sqm, and Penthouse G with a covered terrace of 46.65 sqm, from EUR 363,000 to EUR 460,000 before VAT.

Architecture · Curved balconies, a deep white roof

In the renders the building rises five storeys above the street on a sloping plot, with terracotta walls, white curved balconies on every floor and a deep white roof over the penthouse level. The ground-floor homes open onto stepped gardens above a planted retaining wall.

The plans show each penthouse with an open living room, dining area and kitchen onto a covered terrace. Penthouse C reaches its roof solarium by a spiral staircase from the terrace, and Penthouse F by a stair beside the entrance.

  • Envelope: Thermal insulation in the facade and improved waterproofing of the roof and floors, to cut heat loss and noise.
  • Glazing: Exterior joinery with a thermal break and double glazing with a dehydrated air chamber.
  • Ventilation: A single-flow ventilation system that brings fresh air in and takes stale air out continuously.
  • Energy: Photovoltaic panels on the roof for the shared areas, LED lighting in the common parts and hot water from an aerothermal heat pump.

Location

The building stands on Calle María de las Mercedes, in the residential area of Los Pacos on the hillside above Fuengirola. The brochure gives 2 km to the sea, about eight minutes by car, and a five-minute walk to the centre of Los Pacos, and marks schools, a park, padel courts and the Los Boliches commuter train station on its map. It gives 30 km to Marbella and 23 km to Málaga airport. The map pin is placed from the brochure’s site map and is approximate.

Our assessment

What we would weigh before reserving here, and what we check for you.

Why it stands out

  • Who it is forBuyers who want a new two-bedroom home on the top floor in Fuengirola, with a roof solarium or a large covered terrace, to live in or as a second home.
  • What makes it differentThe roof solariums of Penthouses C and F are larger than the homes beneath them, and Penthouse G has a covered terrace of 46.65 sqm. The pool, gym and sauna belong to the earlier phase and are already in use.
  • Why location mattersThe brochure puts the building 2 km from the sea, about eight minutes by car, and a five-minute walk from the centre of Los Pacos, with schools, shops, parks, sports facilities and a bus stop nearby.
  • Why check availabilityOnly the three penthouses remain of the 15 homes in this phase. Prices are before VAT, and we confirm the current list, the licence and the delivery date before any reservation.

What to plan for

  • VAT and taxesThe price list says that prices are net and do not include VAT, transfer tax or other charges. At the current 10 per cent VAT on new homes, the EUR 363,000 penthouse comes to EUR 399,300 and the EUR 460,000 penthouse to EUR 506,000, before stamp duty, notary and land registry fees.
  • Payment scheduleEUR 10,000 to reserve; 30 per cent at the private contract, which the schedule ties to the building licence; and 70 per cent at the title deed, on the first-occupation licence. The schedule says VAT is added to these amounts.
  • Garage spaceThe price list gives each penthouse a garage space by number, and the brochure lists parking with every home. We confirm that the space is included in the price.
  • Money before completionThirty per cent is paid long before the keys. No document we hold says how payments before completion are guaranteed, so we ask for the bank guarantee or insurance before any money moves.

Checks before any money moves

  • Licence and datesBuilding licence granted. The developer’s portal says the building licence is granted, that construction started in October 2025 and that completion is planned for September 2027. We ask for the licence and the delivery date that goes into the contract.
  • The areasThe price list gives built areas, and the plans add useful areas: 60.90, 72.79 and 68.58 sqm inside for Penthouses C, F and G. On the plan of Penthouse C the room areas add up to 60.36 sqm, not the 60.90 sqm printed as its total. The solariums appear only on the price list. We confirm which figures the contract uses.
  • The shared facilitiesThe pool, the gym, the sauna, the laundry and the garage belong to the earlier phase. We ask for the community rules and fees, and how the new building shares them.
  • The imagesThe views of the building and the rooms are computer-generated and approximate, and the furniture is not included. The pool and gym are photographs of the existing facilities.
