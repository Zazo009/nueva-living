The development

Torrevigía Residences is a gated scheme of apartment blocks on the hillside at Torrevigía, Estepona, beside Selwo Aventura, built in two phases. The developer’s agent portal lists eighteen homes for sale, seven in the first phase and eleven in the second, all turnkey with immediate delivery.

The available homes have two, three or four bedrooms and two or three bathrooms, and run from 131.05 to 196.08 sqm built with 103.73 to 155.32 sqm of usable interior. Every home with a drawing has a terrace on it, from 33.10 to 50.45 sqm, covered on the ground and first floors and part covered on the second.

Within the scheme the brochure lists a swimming pool, a co-working space, landscaped gardens, bicycle storage, a gated entrance and perimeter video surveillance. Buyers also have access to the residents’ club of the wider estate, with a heated indoor pool, a gym, saunas and a dining room.

Architecture · Blocks that step down the slope

The blocks are tiered over the slope so that the homes look towards the sea, with rounded corners and façades in stone, white concrete and glass, and planted roofs and terraces.

  • The blocks: Several low blocks on the hillside, each with its own portals, lifts and a communal garage.
  • The terraces: Covered terraces of 33.10 to 45.35 sqm on the ground and first floors, and of about 50 sqm, part open, on the second.
  • Inside: Porcelain stoneware floors, suspended ceilings, a fitted and equipped kitchen and a main bathroom en suite.
  • Energy: Hot-water underfloor heating, ducted cooling, an aerothermal heat pump for hot water and double glazing; the drawings carry a provisional A rating.

Residences · Two bedrooms to four

Thirteen of the eighteen homes have three bedrooms; three have four bedrooms, and the two on the second floor have two. The table below follows the developer’s list of 13 November 2026.

Three bedrooms

  • Thirteen homes of 153.46 to 159.85 sqm built, with two bathrooms, the main one en suite.
  • An open living room, dining area and kitchen of about 52 sqm on the drawings, opening onto the terrace.
  • On the ground and first floors, a covered terrace of 33.10 to 37.45 sqm across the front of the home.
  • Prices from EUR 638,000 to EUR 777,000.

Four bedrooms

  • Three ground-floor homes of 196.08 sqm built and 155.32 sqm usable, with three bathrooms.
  • A living room, dining area and kitchen of 57.45 sqm on the drawings, and a covered terrace of 45.35 sqm.
  • Each comes with two parking spaces and a storeroom on the developer’s list.
  • Prices from EUR 898,000 to EUR 900,000.

Two bedrooms

  • Two second-floor homes of 131.05 and 137.21 sqm built, with two bathrooms.
  • A terrace of 49.15 and 50.45 sqm on the drawings, part covered and part open.
  • Prices of EUR 761,000 and EUR 745,000.

Specifications

The developer’s own specification, from its brochure, summarised by part of the home.

  • Porcelain stoneware floors in the hall, living room, bedrooms and kitchen, with white skirting.
  • Suspended ceilings throughout, finished in emulsion paint.
  • Hot-water underfloor heating, electric in the bathrooms, with a thermostat in the main rooms.
  • Ducted cooling to the living room and bedrooms, and an aerothermal heat pump for hot water.
  • Fitted wall and base units, with a ceramic hob on an island or peninsula and a ceiling extractor hood.
  • An oven and microwave column, a panelled dishwasher and fridge, and a washer-dryer.
  • Ceramic wall tiles, porcelain floors, resin shower trays and screens.
  • In the main bathroom a thermostatic shower, a vanity unit with a compact quartz top and a wall-hung toilet.
  • Thermally broken aluminium windows with double glazing, and motorised insulated blinds in the bedrooms.
  • An armoured front door, white lacquered interior doors 220 cm high and fitted wardrobes.
  • Non-slip stoneware paving, with a water point, drainage, lighting, a power socket and a TV socket on every terrace.

Questions about Torrevigía

How many are available?

Eighteen: seven in the first phase and eleven in the second.

Are the homes finished?

The developer’s portal lists both phases as turnkey with immediate delivery. We ask for the first-occupation licence and the energy certificate for the home you choose.

How big are the homes?

From 131.05 to 196.08 sqm built, with 103.73 to 155.32 sqm of usable interior and terraces of 33.10 to 50.45 sqm on the drawings.

Is parking included?

The price shown is for the home alone. The developer’s list sells a parking space and a storeroom with most homes for EUR 22,000, and two spaces and a storeroom with four homes for EUR 38,000.

What does the residents’ club offer?

The brochure lists a heated indoor pool, a gym, saunas, a dining room and gardens, open to the scheme’s residents.
