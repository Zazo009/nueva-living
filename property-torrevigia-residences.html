Overview

Eighteen homes, two phases

Torrevigía Residences is a gated scheme of apartment blocks on the hillside at Torrevigía, Estepona, beside Selwo Aventura, built in two phases. The developer’s agent portal lists eighteen homes for sale, seven in the first phase and eleven in the second, all turnkey with immediate delivery.

The available homes have two, three or four bedrooms and two or three bathrooms, and run from 131.05 to 196.08 sqm built with 103.73 to 155.32 sqm of usable interior. Every home with a drawing has a terrace on it, from 33.10 to 50.45 sqm, covered on the ground and first floors and part covered on the second.

Within the scheme the brochure lists a swimming pool, a co-working space, landscaped gardens, bicycle storage, a gated entrance and perimeter video surveillance. Buyers also have access to the residents’ club of the wider estate, with a heated indoor pool, a gym, saunas and a dining room.

What the homes are built with

The developer’s own specification, from its brochure, summarised by part of the home.

Floors and ceilings
  • Porcelain stoneware floors in the hall, living room, bedrooms and kitchen, with white skirting.
  • Suspended ceilings throughout, finished in emulsion paint.
Climate and energy
  • Hot-water underfloor heating, electric in the bathrooms, with a thermostat in the main rooms.
  • Ducted cooling to the living room and bedrooms, and an aerothermal heat pump for hot water.
Kitchen
  • Fitted wall and base units, with a ceramic hob on an island or peninsula and a ceiling extractor hood.

Bathrooms

Architecture

Blocks that step down the slope

The blocks are tiered over the slope so that the homes look towards the sea, with rounded corners and façades in stone, white concrete and glass, and planted roofs and terraces.

  • The blocks — Several low blocks on the hillside, each with its own portals, lifts and a communal garage.
  • The terraces — Covered terraces of 33.10 to 45.35 sqm on the ground and first floors, and of about 50 sqm, part open, on the second.
  • Inside — Porcelain stoneware floors, suspended ceilings, a fitted and equipped kitchen and a main bathroom en suite.
  • Energy — Hot-water underfloor heating, ducted cooling, an aerothermal heat pump for hot water and double glazing; the drawings carry a provisional A rating.

FAQ

Common buyer questions

Can foreigners buy property in Spain?

Yes. There are no restrictions on non-Spanish nationals buying property in Spain, whether as a resident or non-resident.

What is an NIE number and do I need one?

An NIE (Numero de Identificacion de Extranjero) is a tax ID number required for any property purchase in Spain by a non-Spanish national. Nueva Living can guide you through obtaining one before you reserve.

What costs should I budget for on top of the purchase price?

Buyers typically budget for transfer tax or VAT, notary fees, land registry fees and legal fees on top of the purchase price. Nueva Living provides a full, current cost breakdown for your chosen residence before you reserve.

Can I get a mortgage in Spain as a non-resident?

Many Spanish banks offer mortgages to non-resident buyers, typically financing a portion of the purchase price. Exact terms depend on the bank and your personal financial profile.

What is the difference between off-plan and completed properties?

Off-plan means the development is still under construction and is usually sold with staged payments through to completion. A completed property is ready to view and move into now.

How does the reservation and payment process work?

Reservation and payment structures vary by development and are set out in full before you reserve. Nueva Living reconfirms the current schedule for your chosen residence at every step.

Can I rent out the property after purchase?

This depends on the individual development and local regulations, which can vary by community and municipality. Nueva Living will confirm the specific rules for a development before you reserve.

Do I need a lawyer?

Yes, we strongly recommend independent legal representation for any property purchase in Spain. Nueva Living can put you in touch with independent lawyers experienced in Costa del Sol property.
