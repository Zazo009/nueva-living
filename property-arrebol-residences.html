Overview

Sixteen homes, three still available

A single terraced building of sixteen homes with three or four bedrooms on a hillside on the coast of Benalmádena. The developer's brochure places it about 300 metres from Malibú beach and describes large, south-facing terraces, and its location plan marks the plot above the N-340 coast road.

The price list of 27 October 2026 shows thirteen of the sixteen homes reserved. Three remain: a three-bedroom home on two levels at EUR 740,000, a three-bedroom penthouse at EUR 1,040,000 and a four-bedroom duplex penthouse at EUR 1,145,000, all plus VAT, each with two parking spaces and a storage room.

Residents share a salt-chlorinated pool with a glass wall, an outdoor kitchen with a barbecue and a chill-out area, a gym, and a Finnish sauna with a changing room. The developer's portal gives the building licence as granted, the works as started in June 2025 and completion in the third quarter of 2027.

Quality specifications

What the homes are built with

The developer's own building specification, summarised by part of the home.

Structure and envelope
  • Reinforced-concrete foundations and structure, to the Spanish Technical Building Code.
  • A double-leaf facade of perforated brick with water-repellent render and acrylic paint, projected polyurethane insulation, an air cavity and an inner leaf of plasterboard with mineral wool.
  • Walkable terrace roofs built as inverted roofs, waterproofed and insulated, finished in non-slip porcelain stoneware.
Walls and floors
  • A triple wall between homes, of double hollow brick with an acoustic membrane, lined with plasterboard on both sides and insulated with mineral wool.
  • Interior partitions of plasterboard on both sides with mineral wool, with water-repellent boards in the wet rooms.
  • Large-format porcelain stoneware throughout, the same tile on the terraces, and large-format rectified porcelain on the bathroom walls.
Windows and doors
  • Lacquered aluminium windows with a thermal break and double glazing, low-emission glass in the living rooms.
  • Aluminium roller blinds in the bedrooms, motorised in the main bedrooms and by strap in the others.
  • A security entrance door with a three-point lock, white lacquered interior doors, and built-in wardrobes with sliding or folding doors, a shelf and a hanging rail.
Kitchen
  • Wall and floor units in a choice of two colours, with a Silestone, Compac or similar worktop and splashback.
  • Bosch, Teka or similar appliances: an extractor hood, an induction hob, an electric oven, a microwave, a dishwasher, a fridge and a steel sink.
Bathrooms
  • A vanity unit with an integrated basin, wall-hung toilets with concealed cisterns in the main bathroom, and low resin shower trays.
  • Single-lever chrome taps with water savers, and taps built into the wall in the showers.
  • Electric underfloor heating in the bathrooms.
Climate and energy
  • Ducted heat-pump air conditioning, hot and cold, with room-by-room temperature control through motorised grilles, or split units in each room, depending on the home.
  • Hot water from an aerothermal heat pump in each home, and a single-flow ventilation system that brings fresh air in continuously.
  • Photovoltaic panels for the community's own consumption, which also cover part of the aerothermal system's needs.
Communal areas and garage
  • A pool with a glass wall, saline chlorination and underwater lighting, a chill-out area with a barbecue beside it, a Finnish sauna with a changing room and shower, and a gym with large windows.
  • A fully enclosed complex with access control for pedestrians and vehicles by closed-circuit television, and lifts for eight people serving every floor.
  • A garage with an automatic door, polished concrete floors, and pre-installation for charging an electric car from the home's own meter.

Source: the developer's building specification, in English and in Spanish, of July 2025. It says the finishes may be changed or replaced by others of equal or higher quality for technical reasons, changes in manufacturers' models or new regulations. The two versions differ on one point: the English places the photovoltaic panels on the roof of the car parks, the Spanish and the brochure on the roof of the wellness building.

FAQ

Common buyer questions

Can foreigners buy property in Spain?

Yes. There are no restrictions on non-Spanish nationals buying property in Spain, whether as a resident or non-resident.

What is an NIE number and do I need one?

An NIE (Numero de Identificacion de Extranjero) is a tax ID number required for any property purchase in Spain by a non-Spanish national. Nueva Living can guide you through obtaining one before you reserve.

What costs should I budget for on top of the purchase price?

Buyers typically budget for transfer tax or VAT, notary fees, land registry fees and legal fees on top of the purchase price. Nueva Living provides a full, current cost breakdown for your chosen residence before you reserve.

Can I get a mortgage in Spain as a non-resident?

Many Spanish banks offer mortgages to non-resident buyers, typically financing a portion of the purchase price. Exact terms depend on the bank and your personal financial profile.

What is the difference between off-plan and completed properties?

Off-plan means the development is still under construction and is usually sold with staged payments through to completion. A completed property is ready to view and move into now.

How does the reservation and payment process work?

Reservation and payment structures vary by development and are set out in full before you reserve. Nueva Living reconfirms the current schedule for your chosen residence at every step.

Can I rent out the property after purchase?

This depends on the individual development and local regulations, which can vary by community and municipality. Nueva Living will confirm the specific rules for a development before you reserve.

Do I need a lawyer?

Yes, we strongly recommend independent legal representation for any property purchase in Spain. Nueva Living can put you in touch with independent lawyers experienced in Costa del Sol property.
