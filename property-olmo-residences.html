Overview

Twenty-five homes, eighteen listed

Contemporary townhouses on a hillside at El Higuerón, Fuengirola, in five groups of homes at different levels, so that the architecture steps down the slope. The developer’s brochure describes just 25 homes, with large windows, terraces that are part of daily life and open views of the Mediterranean.

Each home has its own outdoor space and private pool. Residents share a gym, an outdoor pool with a sunbathing area and a multi-purpose space for working or studying. The developer’s documents give no completion date, so none is quoted here.

Location

El Higuerón, Fuengirola

El Higuerón is in Fuengirola, on the Costa del Sol. The developer’s brochure says it combines elevation, open views of the Mediterranean and a direct connection to everything that matters. Its map marks the A-7 and AP-7 roads, the beaches of Carvajal, Torreblanca, Fuengirola and Torremuelle and the golf courses of Chaparral, Mijas, Alhaurín, La Noria and Calanova. It gives no distances; the ones shown here are measured by road from the site.

What the homes are built with

The developer’s own building specification, summarised by part of the home.

Floors and walls
  • Large-format porcelain flooring throughout, including the bathrooms and kitchen, anti-slip ceramic flooring on the terraces, ceramic tiles in the bathrooms and smooth white paint on the other walls and ceilings.
Doors and wardrobes
  • A reinforced security door with satin chrome handles, white lacquered sliding or hinged interior doors with concealed hinges, and modular wardrobes lined in textile-finish melamine with a top shelf, a hanging rail and drawers.
Kitchen
  • Delivered fitted with cabinetry, depending on the home type, and with appliances. The specification does not list the appliances.
Bathrooms
  • White sanitaryware, a wall-hung toilet with a concealed cistern and soft-close seat, chrome single-lever taps, a vanity unit with a washbasin and a backlit anti-fog mirror, and a shower tray built in the same finish as the floor with a sliding glass screen.
Climate and energy
  • Hot water by an aerothermal system and underfloor heating throughout the home, with a climate system as the regulations require.
  • The development is to have an energy performance certificate rated A, which is a project-stage rating.
Electrical and telecoms
  • A protected electrical installation, television, telephone and fibre-optic points, an automatic video entry phone and a home-automation hub with general on and off lighting control.
Private pool and communal areas
  • A private swimming pool in each home, built with high-end materials and fitted with a filtration system.

Garage and lifts
  • A garage with vehicle access, a polished concrete floor and a fire-fighting system, electric lifts, and a pre-installation for electric-vehicle charging through an accessible conduit to each parking space.

Architecture

Five groups of homes, stepping down the hill

The development is five groups of homes on different levels of the slope, which the brochure calls a staggered architecture that follows the terrain. The homes are townhouses with a living level and a bedroom level and, in types C, D and E, a third level with a solarium and the private pool.

The brochure describes clean lines, continuous materials, neutral tones and interiors that open to the landscape. The images are illustrations of that intention.

  • Structure — Reinforced concrete foundations and structure to the Technical Building Code, with independent quality control and a ten-year building warranty insurance.
  • Roofs — Flat, waterproofed and insulated roofs, accessible only for maintenance except on usable terraces.
  • Facade — A selection of materials and finishes for durability and energy efficiency. The specification does not name them.
  • Glazing — Thermally broken aluminium or PVC joinery with double glazing, motorised blinds in the bedrooms and motorised exterior blinds in the living room.

FAQ

Common buyer questions

Can foreigners buy property in Spain?

Yes. There are no restrictions on non-Spanish nationals buying property in Spain, whether as a resident or non-resident.

What is an NIE number and do I need one?

An NIE (Numero de Identificacion de Extranjero) is a tax ID number required for any property purchase in Spain by a non-Spanish national. Nueva Living can guide you through obtaining one before you reserve.

What costs should I budget for on top of the purchase price?

Buyers typically budget for transfer tax or VAT, notary fees, land registry fees and legal fees on top of the purchase price. Nueva Living provides a full, current cost breakdown for your chosen residence before you reserve.

Can I get a mortgage in Spain as a non-resident?

Many Spanish banks offer mortgages to non-resident buyers, typically financing a portion of the purchase price. Exact terms depend on the bank and your personal financial profile.

What is the difference between off-plan and completed properties?

Off-plan means the development is still under construction and is usually sold with staged payments through to completion. A completed property is ready to view and move into now.

How does the reservation and payment process work?

Reservation and payment structures vary by development and are set out in full before you reserve. Nueva Living reconfirms the current schedule for your chosen residence at every step.

Can I rent out the property after purchase?

This depends on the individual development and local regulations, which can vary by community and municipality. Nueva Living will confirm the specific rules for a development before you reserve.

Do I need a lawyer?

Yes, we strongly recommend independent legal representation for any property purchase in Spain. Nueva Living can put you in touch with independent lawyers experienced in Costa del Sol property.

How many homes are there?

The development has 25 homes. The developer lists 18 of them with a price: 11 with three bedrooms and 7 with four, from EUR 789,000 to EUR 1,327,000.

Does every home have a pool?

Yes. The developer’s documents say that each home has its own outdoor space and a private swimming pool.
