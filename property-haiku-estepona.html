The development

Haiku Estepona is a residential complex in Cancelada, on Estepona’s New Golden Mile, with two types of home: 36 apartments, called Haiku Suites, and 15 townhouses, called Haiku Residences. The developer’s dossier describes a hillside setting where the sea and the mountains meet, with Mediterranean gardens, walking paths, a swimming pool, a community lounge and a social club. The quality specifications add that the landscaped open areas form a central zone that separates the two types.

The price list of 26 October 2026 marks 28 of the 51 homes as reserved and lists 23 with a price: 13 two-bedroom apartments and 10 three-bedroom townhouses. None of the documents gives a completion date.

Architecture · Contemporary design, on a hillside

The dossier describes a contemporary and functional design, with open-plan interiors, large windows and spacious southwest-facing terraces, on a Mediterranean hillside where the sea and the mountains meet. In the developer’s computer-generated images the apartment blocks have stepped, planted terraces with glass balustrades, and the townhouses stand in terraced rows in front of them.

The dossier says the apartments include ground-floor homes with gardens or large terraces, duplexes and penthouses with solariums, and that the townhouses have terraces and solariums. The list does not say which apartments are duplexes or penthouses. On their plans the 13 apartments listed with a price have two bedrooms and two bathrooms, and the townhouses three bedrooms.

  • Facade: Exterior render of mortar with a concrete finish, with composite or timber cladding in a metal or wood finish.
  • Glazing: Tilting or sliding doors and windows in lacquered aluminium from a leading brand, with double glazing, and glass balustrades with bevelled edges fixed to the slab.
  • Floors: Rectified porcelain tiles of 90 x 90 cm inside and, in non-slip C3 grade, on the terraces, with a 10 x 90 cm porcelain skirting.
  • Outdoor areas: A central strip of gardens with large palms, water features and a waterfall between the buildings, pools with organic shapes, and chill-out and yoga areas with changing rooms.

Residences · Two types, apartments and townhouses

The 23 homes listed with a price are of two types, and the table below lists each one with its price. The areas are the list’s built areas, with the terraces and, where there is one, the garden.

Haiku Suites: apartments with two bedrooms

  • 13 of the 36 apartments are listed with a price, all with two bedrooms and two bathrooms. They are in two buildings of two staircases each, on the ground, first and second floors. The built area is 94.15 to 102.08 sqm and the terraces 27.58 to 94.71 sqm.
  • Apartments 10 and 28, on the ground floor, also have gardens of 25.13 and 66.27 sqm. The prices run from EUR 505,000 for apartment 32 to EUR 675,000 for apartments 12 and 28.
  • Apartment 35, on the second floor, has a second plan for a roof terrace of 45.53 sqm with a jacuzzi, reached by a spiral staircase. On the list each apartment has two numbered parking spaces and a numbered storage room.

Haiku Residences: townhouses with three bedrooms

  • 10 of the 15 townhouses are listed with a price, all with three bedrooms. On the plans each has four levels: a basement with a garage and a storage room, a bedroom level with a garden, a living level with the kitchen and terraces, and a solarium with a terrace.
  • The built area is 200.56 to 208.92 sqm, the terraces 116.59 to 171.84 sqm and the gardens 10.80 to 42.88 sqm. The prices run from EUR 962,000 for R2 to EUR 1,165,000 for R1, which has the largest garden.
  • The list gives each townhouse a garage numbered GR1 to GR15 and marked “(ext)”, and each plan shows a parking space with the same number in the basement.

The developer’s payment schedule is on request; we send it with the price list.

Specifications

The developer’s own quality specifications, summarised by part of the home.

  • Rectified porcelain tiles of 90 x 90 cm throughout, in non-slip C3 grade on the terraces, with a 10 x 90 cm porcelain skirting.
  • In the bathrooms, rectified porcelain of 60 x 120 cm around the washbasin and the shower, with the other walls painted white. Large-format rectified porcelain of 120 x 280 cm, matt, in beige or gold, is an option.
  • Matt black taps, Siemens appliances and a porcelain stoneware worktop. An upgrade to Gaggenau appliances is an option.
  • Solid-surface washbasins on oak units: 70 cm in the main bathrooms, 100 cm in the second bathrooms, and a 60 cm unit with a 60 x 42 cm basin in the cloakroom toilets.
  • Wall-hung toilets with a concealed cistern, brushed graphite taps with a thermostatic shower and a hygienic shower, a resin shower tray with a fixed 8 mm anti-limescale glass screen, and backlit mirrors.
  • Lacquered aluminium doors and windows, tilting or sliding, with double glazing as the manufacturer indicates, and glass balustrades with bevelled edges.
  • Wardrobes with sliding or hinged fronts in thick board, a walk-in closet with an interior lining in the main bedroom and wardrobes with drawers in the others, with interior doors in the same material. Black or timber finishes are options.
  • Air conditioning by direct expansion, with eco-efficient units in the false ceiling and adjustable linear grilles, aerothermal hot water, water underfloor heating throughout the home and electric underfloor heating in the bathrooms.
  • Mechanical ventilation with ducts hidden in the false ceilings, to the requirements of the Technical Building Code. In the apartments, photovoltaic panels support the supply to the common areas.
  • Plasterboard false ceilings with recesses for curtain tracks and headboards, and a pre-installation of LED strips for indirect lighting in the bedrooms.
  • Jung LS990 switches or similar in white, a Fermax video door entry or similar, television and telephone points in the main rooms, and climate control from a smartphone app. Other home-automation items are options.
  • For the townhouses the specification lists as standard a private pool on the solarium, a bar on the solarium, a private lift in each home and a box-type garage enclosure on level -1 of the home, and says that pool options are to be consulted.
  • Options in both types: jacuzzis on terraces or solariums, a bar upgraded to a kitchen with a barbecue, a charger for electric vehicles in the interior garages and motorised blinds in the bedrooms. For the apartments, private pools and a bar on the solarium are options too, subject to feasibility for each home.

Location

The dossier places the complex in Cancelada, on Estepona’s New Golden Mile, which it describes as a tranquil area with access to the amenities of the Costa del Sol. It names the golf courses Flamingos Golf, Cancelada Club Golf, El Campanario Golf & Country House and Atalaya Golf & Country Club, and puts Real Club Valderrama in Sotogrande 20 minutes from the complex. Its location map shows an airport, a train station, medical centres, golf courses, shopping centres, schools and marinas, without distances. The documents give no address and no coordinate for the plot, so the map shows the Cancelada area.

Questions about Haiku Estepona

Is there parking and storage?

On the list each apartment has two numbered parking spaces and a storage room, although the dossier mentions one parking space. Each townhouse has a garage on its basement plan. We confirm what the price includes.

Is there a private pool or jacuzzi?

The quality specification for the townhouses lists a private pool on the solarium as standard, while their plans show a jacuzzi, so we confirm which is included. For the apartments, pools and jacuzzis are options subject to feasibility, and the plan of apartment 35 shows a jacuzzi on its roof terrace.

Is furniture included?

No. The furniture and decoration in the images are not included. The kitchens are delivered with the appliances in the specification.

When will it be finished?

None of the developer’s documents gives a completion date. We ask for the contractual date, the licence status and the stage of the works.
