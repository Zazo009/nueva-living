Overview

Fifty semi-detached homes, thirteen listed

This is a development of 50 contemporary semi-detached homes at Atalaya Golf in Estepona, next to the Atalaya Golf & Country Club and close to commercial areas, with Puerto Banús nearby. The developer’s page describes a large living room, an open kitchen supplied furnished and fitted with branded appliances, a terrace and a garden for every home.

The communal gardens have two saltwater pools, one of them for children with a beach-style entry. The developer’s page calls the last homes “turnkey” in phase 1, while the plans we hold are project-stage plans with no delivery date, so we ask for the status of the works before any reservation.

Architecture

Contemporary houses, built in pairs

The developer describes the homes as contemporary semi-detached villas. The site model on the plans shows them built in pairs that share a party wall, and the renders show white houses with a grey upper floor and a pergola over the rooftop terrace.

The plans show the living room and kitchen opening onto a covered terrace on one floor, the three bedrooms on another (the lower of the two main floors on F1V37 and F1V38), and a basement with a cellar room, a laundry and a bathroom.

  • Semi-detached or terraced — The developer’s page says semi-detached (“pareadas”), while its price table labels every home “Adosado”, the Spanish word for a terraced or attached house. The plans’ site model shows pairs of homes, so we call them semi-detached.
  • Levels — A basement and two main floors on every plan, with a rooftop solarium on ten of the 13. Homes F1V35, F1V37 and F3V1 show no solarium on the sheets we hold.
  • Basement — Every plan shows a basement with a cellar room (“bodega”), a laundry and a bathroom. On F1V35 and F3V1 it also holds a garage for two cars.
  • Extras on two plans — The plan of FIIIV7 shows a private pool of 12.2 sqm in the garden, and the plan of F3V1 shows a jacuzzi of 3.8 sqm on the terrace.

FAQ

Common buyer questions

Can foreigners buy property in Spain?

Yes. There are no restrictions on non-Spanish nationals buying property in Spain, whether as a resident or non-resident.

What is an NIE number and do I need one?

An NIE (Numero de Identificacion de Extranjero) is a tax ID number required for any property purchase in Spain by a non-Spanish national. Nueva Living can guide you through obtaining one before you reserve.

What costs should I budget for on top of the purchase price?

Buyers typically budget for transfer tax or VAT, notary fees, land registry fees and legal fees on top of the purchase price. Nueva Living provides a full, current cost breakdown for your chosen residence before you reserve.

Can I get a mortgage in Spain as a non-resident?

Many Spanish banks offer mortgages to non-resident buyers, typically financing a portion of the purchase price. Exact terms depend on the bank and your personal financial profile.

What is the difference between off-plan and completed properties?

Off-plan means the development is still under construction and is usually sold with staged payments through to completion. A completed property is ready to view and move into now.

How does the reservation and payment process work?

Reservation and payment structures vary by development and are set out in full before you reserve. Nueva Living reconfirms the current schedule for your chosen residence at every step.

Can I rent out the property after purchase?

This depends on the individual development and local regulations, which can vary by community and municipality. Nueva Living will confirm the specific rules for a development before you reserve.

Do I need a lawyer?

Yes, we strongly recommend independent legal representation for any property purchase in Spain. Nueva Living can put you in touch with independent lawyers experienced in Costa del Sol property.

How many homes are there?

The development has 50 semi-detached homes. The developer lists 13 of them with a price and marks all 13 available, from EUR 775,000 to EUR 950,000.

Are the homes semi-detached or terraced?

The developer’s page says semi-detached and its price table says “Adosado”, which means terraced or attached. The plans show the homes built in pairs, so we call them semi-detached and confirm the party walls of your plot.

How many bedrooms do they have?

The plans of all 13 homes show three bedrooms on one floor and a cellar room in the basement. The price table lists four bedrooms for F1V23 and F2V42, which we check with the developer.

Is VAT included?

When will it be finished?
