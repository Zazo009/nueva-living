The development

La Gaspara Residences is the second phase of a gated scheme in La Gaspara, beside the A-7 on the western side of Estepona. The developer's dossier describes 105 homes over the two phases; fifteen apartments in this second phase are available.

The available homes have two or three bedrooms and two bathrooms, from 107.45 to 144.67 sqm built with 81.35 to 110.67 sqm of usable interior. Every one has a terrace, from 27.79 to 98.60 sqm, and twelve of the fifteen face south-west, the other three south.

The communal areas described for the scheme are two adult pools, a children's pool, a gym, a social club, landscaped gardens, bicycle storage and a gatehouse at the entrance. Each home is listed with a parking space and a storeroom, priced as separate lines.

Architecture · Terraces, floor by floor

Long, low blocks step back floor by floor, so the terraces run the length of each façade. The façades are a painted render over a double leaf of brick and drywall, with an insulated air cavity between them.

  • The scheme: 105 homes over two phases in the developer's dossier, of two, three and four bedrooms; this phase lists fifteen apartments of two and three bedrooms.
  • The terraces: From 27.79 to 98.60 sqm on the available homes, paved in non-slip stoneware, with water, drainage, lighting, a power socket and a TV socket on each.
  • Inside: Porcelain stoneware floors, suspended ceilings throughout, white lacquered doors 220 cm high and fitted wardrobes with modular interiors.
  • Climate: Warm-water underfloor heating, electric in the bathrooms, with ducted cooling to the living room and bedrooms.
  • Energy: An aerothermal heat pump for hot water, thermally broken aluminium with double glazing and motorised insulated blinds; the drawings carry a provisional A rating.
  • The garage: Automatic garage doors on a remote control, a parking space and a storeroom listed with every home, and pre-installation for electric vehicle charging.

Residences · Two bedrooms and three

Seven two-bedroom and eight three-bedroom apartments, on the ground to third floors of four blocks. The table below follows the developer's current list.

The apartments

  • Two or three bedrooms and two bathrooms, with the living room, dining area and kitchen as one room opening to the terrace, and a separate utility room.
  • A terrace on every home, from 27.79 to 98.60 sqm; on some of the drawings a jacuzzi is drawn on the terrace as an option, not included in the price.
  • Twelve of the fifteen face south-west and three face south, according to the developer's list.
  • Warm-water underfloor heating, electric in the bathrooms, with ducted cooling to the living room and bedrooms, and hot water from an aerothermal heat pump.
  • A fitted kitchen with induction hob, extractor hood, oven and microwave in a column, panelled dishwasher and fridge, and a washing machine.
  • Motorised insulated blinds in the living room and bedrooms, and basic home automation for lights and blinds, controlled from a phone.
