The development

Las Brisas Pavilion Villa is a single detached house under construction in Las Brisas, on its own 1,014.96 sqm plot in Nueva Andalucía, with a private pool, two parking spaces and a lift between the two levels.

The house measures 451.06 sqm of living area and 544.87 sqm built, with 318.16 sqm of terraces, which comes to 769.22 sqm in total. Three of the five bedrooms are on the ground floor, including the principal bedroom with a 21.54 sqm bathroom and dressing room; the other two are on the lower level, alongside a gym, a sauna, a massage room, a home cinema of 22.48 sqm and a second living room with its own kitchen and bar. The building licence has been granted and the house is under construction, with completion estimated for the third quarter of 2026.

Architecture · Pavilions over one living level

Designed by ER Arquitectos. The house is a group of pavilions under wide, low-pitched roofs, with the living rooms opening through full-height glass onto covered terraces.

  • Roofs: Wide pavilion roofs with a raised clerestory and concealed lighting along the eaves.
  • Glazing: Full-height sliding glass to the living rooms, with glazed courtyards bringing daylight into the plan.
  • Daylight below: A glazed walkway and light wells carry daylight down to the lower level, so the cinema, gym and second living room are not blind rooms.
  • Outdoor rooms: Covered terraces on more than one side, a sunken seating area at the pool and an outdoor lounge under its own pavilion.
  • Vertical circulation: A lift and a stair link the two levels.
  • Landscaping: Native planting to the garden, with water running beside the steps down to the garage.
