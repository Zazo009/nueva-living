The development

This is a gated complex of contemporary apartments and penthouses beside a golf club in Estepona. The developer’s portal describes it as 135 homes with one, two and three bedrooms, facing south, with gardens for the ground-floor homes, solariums for the penthouses, a pool with a sun terrace, a gym and gardens planted with native species. It calls this the first phase and does not say how many of the 135 homes belong to it.

The price list for this phase shows 12 homes, all with two bedrooms and two bathrooms: four on the ground floor, one on the first floor, two on the second floor and five penthouses, in blocks 2, 3, 5, 7, 8 and 9. Each is listed with one garage space and one storage room. None of the developer’s documents gives a completion date.

Architecture · Contemporary blocks, built around terraces

The developer’s portal describes the design as contemporary. In the computer-generated views the blocks rise three and four levels in long bands of terraces with glass fronts, set among lawns, with a pool and a glazed gym building in the gardens.

On the plans the ground-floor homes open onto a porch and, in some, a green-shaded area, and the upper floors onto a terrace. The December 2024 sheets note that the green-shaded areas are laid with artificial turf on a structure.

  • The homes: Two bedrooms, two bathrooms, an entrance hall and a living and dining room with the kitchen, on 76.44 to 87.71 sqm of interior area.
  • Outdoors: Ground-floor homes have a porch of 56.54 to 56.61 sqm, or a 38.87 sqm porch and a 31.00 sqm garden. The upper floors have terraces of 15.83 to 39.75 sqm.
  • Communal areas: A pool with a sun terrace, a gym and gardens planted with native species, inside a gated complex, as the portal describes them.
  • Parking and storage: One garage space and one storage room are listed with each home.

Residences · Three kinds of home, two bedrooms each

The 12 listed homes all have two bedrooms and two bathrooms and fall into three groups by floor. The table below lists all of them with their prices.

Ground floor, with a porch or a garden

  • Four homes: 2-2-BAJ-A and 2-2-BAJ-B with 111.86 and 111.85 sqm built, 3-2-BAJ-A with 110.62 sqm and 5-1-BAJ-A with 115.18 sqm.
  • Three of them have a porch of 56.54 to 56.61 sqm; 5-1-BAJ-A has a 38.87 sqm porch and a 31.00 sqm garden.

First and second floors

  • Three homes: 5-1-1-A on the first floor with 115.31 sqm built and a 39.75 sqm terrace, and 7-3-2-B and 8-3-2-B on the second floor with 114.12 and 114.16 sqm built and a 29.91 sqm terrace.
  • The first-floor home costs EUR 373,000 and each second-floor home EUR 408,000.

Penthouses

  • Five penthouses of 107.82 to 126.50 sqm built and 76.44 to 87.71 sqm interior.
  • The two in block 3 have a 29.32 sqm terrace; the three in blocks 5 and 9 have terraces of 15.83 to 15.91 sqm.
  • Prices run from EUR 360,000 to EUR 455,000. The portal text mentions solariums on the penthouses, but the plans of these five show terraces only.

Our assessment

What we would weigh before reserving here, and what we check for you.

Why it stands out

  • Who it is forBuyers who want a new two-bedroom, two-bathroom home with a large terrace or porch, a pool and a gym in the complex, and a golf club next door.
  • What makes it differentPorches of about 56 sqm or a garden on the ground floor, terraces on every other floor, a south orientation and prices from EUR 332,000 plus VAT.
  • Why location mattersThe portal places the complex beside a golf club in Estepona. The developer’s documents give no distances, so we measure the drive to the beach, the town and the airport with you.
  • Why check the detailsThe documents give no completion date, and the portal mentions solariums on the penthouses while the plans of the listed ones show terraces, so we confirm both before any reservation.

What to plan for

  • ReservationThe portal lists no reservation payment.
  • Buyers resident outside Spain30% plus VAT on signing the private purchase contract and 70% plus VAT on completion at the notary.
  • Buyers resident in Spain20% plus VAT on signing the private purchase contract and 80% plus VAT on completion at the notary.
  • VAT and guaranteeThe price list excludes VAT and does not give the rate. The documents do not mention a guarantee on advance payments, so we ask for the policy before any payment.

Checks before any money moves

  • Licence, works and completionThe documents we hold give no completion date and do not say whether the building licence is granted or how far the works have gone. The portal has a works-status page that was not part of what we received. We ask for the licence, the stage of the works and the contractual date.
  • The areasThe built and interior areas on the price list match the plans to within 0.01 sqm, but the list’s terrace and porch figures are 0.6 to 4.2 sqm larger than the plans’ exterior areas: for home 5-1-1-A the list gives 33.24 sqm covered and 6.51 sqm uncovered terrace (39.75 sqm), the plan 35.53 sqm. The plans also give larger areas under Decree 218/2005, for example 120.94 sqm built for home 2-2-BAJ-B against 111.85 sqm. We confirm which figure the contract uses.
  • Solariums on the penthousesThe portal text mentions penthouses with a solarium. The plans of the five listed penthouses show one level with a terrace and no solarium. We ask whether any listed penthouse has one.
  • The plans and the phaseThe plans say they are not final. The ground-floor sheets are dated April 2024 and follow the basic project; the others are dated December 2024 and follow the execution project. The portal describes 135 homes without saying how many are in this phase.

Questions about Almendro

How many homes are there?

The developer’s portal describes the complex as 135 homes with one, two and three bedrooms. The price list for this phase shows 12, all with two bedrooms and two bathrooms, from EUR 332,000 to EUR 455,000 plus VAT.

Is VAT included?

No. The price list says prices exclude VAT and does not give the rate. We confirm the taxes and purchase costs in writing.

Is there parking?

The price list shows one garage space and one storage room for each home. It does not say whether they are in the price, so we confirm it.

When will it be finished?

No document we hold gives a completion date. The portal has a works-status page that we did not receive, so we ask for the stage of the works and the contractual date.

What is the payment schedule?

The portal lists no reservation payment. Buyers resident outside Spain pay 30% plus VAT on signing the private purchase contract and 70% plus VAT on completion at the notary; buyers resident in Spain pay 20% plus VAT and 80% plus VAT.
