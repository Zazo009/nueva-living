Overview

Seventy-two homes, five listed

This is a gated residential complex of 72 homes with two, three and four bedrooms, on the ground, first and second floors and in penthouses, at Cala de Mijas between Fuengirola and La Cala. The developer says every home faces south or south-west and that many have sea views. The ground-floor homes have a garden and the penthouses large terraces.

Residents share a pool, a gym and a social room with a kitchen, and every home has at least one parking space and a storage room. Five homes are listed with a price, and the developer’s documents do not give a completion date.

Location

Cala de Mijas, by the sea between Fuengirola and La Cala

The homes are at Cala de Mijas, on the coast between Fuengirola and La Cala. The developer’s brochure puts the beach two minutes away on foot, the centre of Cala de Mijas 1.6 km away, a three-minute drive, and the A-7 and the AP-7 close by. It lists Chaparral Golf Club under two kilometres away, with shopping centres, supermarkets, restaurants, a health centre and pharmacies in the area, Málaga airport under 25 minutes by car and the María Zambrano station 40 minutes. Fuengirola is about ten minutes by car, as the developer’s portal says.

Quality specifications

What the homes are built with

The developer’s own quality specification, summarised by part of the home.

Structure, facade and roofs
  • A reinforced-concrete structure, and a facade of brickwork clad on the outside, with interior thermal insulation, an air cavity and a plasterboard lining on metal fixings.
  • Flat roofs finished in gravel where there is no access, and non-slip stoneware paving on the roofs that can be walked on.
Partitions and windows
  • Plasterboard partitions on a metal frame with thermal and acoustic insulation inside the home, and brick walls lined with plasterboard and insulation between homes and between a home and the common areas.
  • PVC window frames with double glazing and an air chamber, to reduce heat exchange and outside noise, and blinds in the colour of the frames on all bedroom and living-room windows.
Doors and wardrobes
  • A white-lacquered armoured front door with security fittings, an outer handle and a peephole, flat white interior doors, and modular hinged or sliding wardrobes to match, with a melamine interior, a suitcase shelf and a hanging rail.
Floors and walls
  • Stoneware flooring in the living room, bedrooms, hall, corridor and bathrooms, and non-slip ceramic stoneware on the terraces.
  • Smooth plastic paint in a soft colour on the walls and in white on the ceilings, with paint or ceramic tiles on the wet walls of the kitchen and bathrooms, and an accessible false ceiling in the bathroom that houses the climate equipment.
Kitchen
  • A furnished kitchen with base and wall units, a postformed worktop in the work area, a stainless-steel sink and a single-lever tap.
  • Appliances: an electric oven, a ceramic hob, an extractor hood, a fridge, a washing machine and a dishwasher.
Bathrooms
  • White sanitaryware, a washbasin built into a vanity unit in the main bathroom and a wall-hung washbasin in the second, a mirror in both, chrome taps, single-lever mixers on the bath and washbasins and thermostatic mixers on the showers.
Climate and energy
  • Hot water from an aerothermal system in every home, heating and cooling from a ducted heat pump, and an individual mechanical ventilation system.
  • The building is listed with an energy rating of B, achieved with a better thermal envelope, efficient glazing and high-efficiency installations. The developer estimates the saving against a home rated F.
Electrical and telecoms
  • An electrical installation to the low-voltage regulations, telecom sockets in the living room, bedrooms and kitchen, a satellite-dish pre-installation, a video entry phone at the entrances to the complex and the blocks, and a light, a power socket and a TV aerial socket on the living-room terrace.
Shared areas
  • A fully enclosed and fenced complex with low-energy lighting on the access routes, internal roads and gardens, motion detectors in the entrance halls, floor lobbies and garage, and landscaped gardens with irrigation and native shrubs and trees.
  • A sunny communal outdoor pool for adults with salt chlorination and night lighting, an equipped gym, a social room with a kitchen, toilets and wifi, and a space with an external defibrillator.
  • A garage with forced ventilation, carbon-monoxide detection, fire protection and emergency lighting, and a pre-installation for electric-car charging as the regulations require.

Source: the developer’s quality specification, edition 00. It is provisional and may change with licence requirements or at the discretion of the project management, without loss of quality.

FAQ

Common buyer questions

Can foreigners buy property in Spain?

Yes. There are no restrictions on non-Spanish nationals buying property in Spain, whether as a resident or non-resident.

What is an NIE number and do I need one?

An NIE (Numero de Identificacion de Extranjero) is a tax ID number required for any property purchase in Spain by a non-Spanish national. Nueva Living can guide you through obtaining one before you reserve.

What costs should I budget for on top of the purchase price?

Buyers typically budget for transfer tax or VAT, notary fees, land registry fees and legal fees on top of the purchase price. Nueva Living provides a full, current cost breakdown for your chosen residence before you reserve.

Can I get a mortgage in Spain as a non-resident?

Many Spanish banks offer mortgages to non-resident buyers, typically financing a portion of the purchase price. Exact terms depend on the bank and your personal financial profile.

What is the difference between off-plan and completed properties?

Off-plan means the development is still under construction and is usually sold with staged payments through to completion. A completed property is ready to view and move into now.

How does the reservation and payment process work?

Reservation and payment structures vary by development and are set out in full before you reserve. Nueva Living reconfirms the current schedule for your chosen residence at every step.

Can I rent out the property after purchase?

This depends on the individual development and local regulations, which can vary by community and municipality. Nueva Living will confirm the specific rules for a development before you reserve.

Do I need a lawyer?

Yes, we strongly recommend independent legal representation for any property purchase in Spain. Nueva Living can put you in touch with independent lawyers experienced in Costa del Sol property.

How many homes are there?

The development has 72 homes with two, three and four bedrooms, and five are listed with a price: three with two bedrooms and two with three, from EUR 483,500 to EUR 749,000 plus VAT.

Is VAT included?

No. The prices exclude VAT, which the developer gives as 10% on the reservation. We confirm the taxes and purchase costs in writing.

Is there parking?

Yes. The developer says every home has at least one parking space and a storage room.

When will it be finished?

None of the developer’s documents gives a completion date. We ask for the contractual date, the licence status and the stage of the works.

What is the payment schedule?

The reservation is EUR 6,000 plus 10% VAT. Then 30% plus VAT on the private contract and 70% plus VAT at the notary on completion.
