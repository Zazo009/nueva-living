Overview

A hillside of terraces, looking at the sea

Selwo Sea Terraces is a gated development of apartments, penthouses and semi-detached villas on a hillside at Selwo, Estepona, in the middle of the Costa del Sol’s Golden Triangle of Marbella, Estepona and Benahavís. The blocks step down the slope so that each looks over the one below, towards the sea.

65 homes are listed as available, from one to three bedrooms, in blocks delivered between November 2026 and September 2028. Each of the 55 types has its own floorplan, and the shared grounds include a climatisable infinity pool, a summer pool, a children’s pool, two jacuzzis, a gym, a spa and a coworking room.

Quality specifications

What the homes are built with

The developer’s own specification, summarised by part of the home.

Floors and interior
  • Large-format porcelain floors and ceramic wall tiling in the bathrooms.
  • Fitted wardrobes with lined and divided interiors, and a storeroom and shoe cupboard inside every home.
Climate and energy
  • Ducted air conditioning and hot water from an aerothermal heat pump.
  • Underfloor heating from the aerothermal system in the penthouses and the three- and four-bedroom homes, and electric underfloor heating in the bathrooms.
  • The developer’s energy certificates for Blocks 1 and 3 give emissions of 3.45 and 2.61 kgCO2 per sqm a year, bands B and A.
Kitchen
  • Kitchen with a quartz worktop, an island or peninsula, an under-mounted sink and LED lighting under the wall units.
  • Appliances included: extractor hood, oven, fridge, induction hob, dishwasher, microwave and washing machine; a tumble dryer is not included.
Bathrooms
  • Resin shower trays with a slate finish and a built-in screen, concealed cisterns, and eco-efficient taps.
  • Wall-hung vanity units with a mirror and LED lighting, with a double basin in the penthouses and the three- and four-bedroom homes.
Windows and doors
  • PVC windows with motorised aluminium roller shutters, and sliding doors in the living room and main bedroom.
Outdoors and solarium
  • Ground-floor homes have a covered porch and an open terrace of artificial lawn, with pre-installation for a jacuzzi.
  • Private solariums on the penthouses, reached by a private staircase with a motorised roof hatch, with a barbecue, a sink, a hot and cold shower, power and TV outlets, a storage cupboard and pre-installation for a jacuzzi.
Parking and technology
  • A parking space and a storeroom in the basement for every apartment and, for most penthouses and duplexes, two spaces and a storeroom, with a pre-installation for an electric-car charger at every space. The price list names the spaces for each home and lists none for the villas.
  • A Wi-Fi router in every home and community Wi-Fi in the shared areas.

Architecture

Stepped terraces, framed in white

The blocks sit on platforms that follow the slope, with large terraces framed by volumes in the manner of porticoes and finished with glass balustrades, so that each block looks over the next towards the sea. Some top-floor terraces are covered by pergolas that filter the light, and the ground floors have covered porches and open terraces.

The facades are cement render and paint in combined colours, with ceramic cladding on some volumes and lacquered metal screens on the stairwells.

  • Facades — Cement render and paint in combined colours, with ceramic cladding on some volumes and lacquered metal screens on the stairwells.
  • Terraces — Large terraces and covered porches, glass balustrades, and pergolas that filter the light on the top floors.
  • Orientation — Most homes face south, south-west or south-east, with views of the sea, the shared grounds and the green surroundings.
  • Gardens — Ground-floor homes have a terrace of artificial lawn, and the garden homes on the lower levels and the villas have private gardens.

FAQ

Common buyer questions

Can foreigners buy property in Spain?

Yes. There are no restrictions on non-Spanish nationals buying property in Spain, whether as a resident or non-resident.

What is an NIE number and do I need one?

An NIE (Numero de Identificacion de Extranjero) is a tax ID number required for any property purchase in Spain by a non-Spanish national. Nueva Living can guide you through obtaining one before you reserve.

What costs should I budget for on top of the purchase price?

Buyers typically budget for transfer tax or VAT, notary fees, land registry fees and legal fees on top of the purchase price. Nueva Living provides a full, current cost breakdown for your chosen residence before you reserve.

Can I get a mortgage in Spain as a non-resident?

Many Spanish banks offer mortgages to non-resident buyers, typically financing a portion of the purchase price. Exact terms depend on the bank and your personal financial profile.

What is the difference between off-plan and completed properties?

Off-plan means the development is still under construction and is usually sold with staged payments through to completion. A completed property is ready to view and move into now.

How does the reservation and payment process work?

Reservation and payment structures vary by development and are set out in full before you reserve. Nueva Living reconfirms the current schedule for your chosen residence at every step.

Can I rent out the property after purchase?

This depends on the individual development and local regulations, which can vary by community and municipality. Nueva Living will confirm the specific rules for a development before you reserve.

Do I need a lawyer?

Yes, we strongly recommend independent legal representation for any property purchase in Spain. Nueva Living can put you in touch with independent lawyers experienced in Costa del Sol property.

How many homes are available?

65 homes are listed as available: 42 apartments, 13 penthouses, 2 duplex penthouses and 8 semi-detached villas, from EUR 464,000 for a one-bedroom apartment to EUR 1,638,000 for a villa.

When will the homes be ready?

The developer’s price list gives a delivery month for each block: November 2026 for Blocks 2A and 3A, December 2026 for Block 1A, April 2027 for Block 4B, May 2027 for Block 5B, August and September 2027 for the villas, and September 2028 for Blocks 10A to 12B. Its April 2024 circular gave February and March 2026 for the first blocks, which the price list supersedes.

Is there a floorplan for each home?

Yes. Each of the 55 types has its own floorplan, and every home in the availability table links to it. The villas have two drawings, one for each floor and the solarium.

What comes with the price?

The price list names the basement parking and the storeroom included with each home: one space and a storeroom for the apartments, and two spaces for most penthouses and the duplexes, and none for the villas. It shows a separate equipment figure on top of the price, from EUR 18,100 to EUR 26,400, which we confirm before any reservation.

Which licence covers the blocks?

The licence in the developer’s documents, Estepona file 9243/2024, covers 112 apartments and 8 semi-detached villas and does not include the swimming pools. We ask the developer which licence covers Blocks 10A to 12B and the pools before any reservation.
