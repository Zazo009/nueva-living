The development

Maralto Estepona is a gated collection of 164 apartments and penthouses across ten buildings in Las Mesas, on the eastern edge of Estepona, with 1, 2 and 3-bedroom layouts.

Residents share two outdoor swimming pools, a spa and wellness zone, a fully equipped gymnasium and an open-air calisthenics area, within a gated, controlled-access community with underground parking, private storage and a car wash area for every home.

Architecture · Ten buildings, framed by sea and mountains

The complex is designed across ten buildings, with apartments on the ground and first floors, some on a third floor, and duplex penthouses with a private solarium on the top floor of each building.

Layouts are open-plan, with the living-dining room and kitchen opening directly onto a covered terrace, and floor-to-ceiling windows framing the sea or Sierra Bermeja mountain views.
