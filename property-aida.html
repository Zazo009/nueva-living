The idea

Where Bentley Home meets the Golden Mile

AÍDA is a boutique collection of just eight residences on Marbella’s Golden Mile. The developer’s brochure describes it as the union of two forces in excellence: RGZ Developers, in luxury real estate, and Bentley Home.

The RGZ Architecture studio designed it in collaboration with Bentley Home. The brochure says the development carries Bentley’s legacy of timeless elegance, precision and impeccable design, and brings the brand’s automotive artistry into the world of refined living.

There are two types of residence: the Skyline Penthouse, with sea views, a solarium and a private pool, and the Duplex Ground Floor, with a private garden and pool. Each owner has a personal pitbox on a private pit lane.

Eight residences furnished by Bentley Home, each owner with a pitbox on a private pit lane.

The Golden Mile

Steps from Marbella’s best-known addresses

The brochure places AÍDA steps from the Puente Romano Beach Resort, the Marbella Club Hotel and the Forum leisure centre, and five minutes from Puerto Banús.

01

Puente Romano Beach Resort

Four minutes’ walk away, according to the brochure’s distance table: the Tennis and Padel Club, a gym and Pilates studio, the Six Senses Spa, boutique-lined promenades, and restaurants including Nobu, COYA and Cipriani.

The Owners Club

Residents are invited to join the Owners Club at Puente Romano, a three-minute walk away: private lounges, a concierge, daily breakfast and access to beach clubs, pools, padel and tennis, a gym and the Six Senses Spa. The brochure marks it subject to validation of the Owners Club.

03

Marbella Club Hotel

Founded in 1954 and seven minutes’ walk away, with gardens, a spa, a fitness centre, a beach club and its El Patio restaurant.

04

Forum and Puerto Banús

The Forum leisure centre is steps away, and the brochure puts Puerto Banús five minutes from the residences by car.

FAQ

Common buyer questions

Can foreigners buy property in Spain?

Yes. There are no restrictions on non-Spanish nationals buying property in Spain, whether as a resident or non-resident.

What is an NIE number and do I need one?

An NIE (Numero de Identificacion de Extranjero) is a tax ID number required for any property purchase in Spain by a non-Spanish national. Nueva Living can guide you through obtaining one before you reserve.

What costs should I budget for on top of the purchase price?

Buyers typically budget for transfer tax or VAT, notary fees, land registry fees and legal fees on top of the purchase price. Nueva Living provides a full, current cost breakdown for your chosen residence before you reserve.

Can I get a mortgage in Spain as a non-resident?

Many Spanish banks offer mortgages to non-resident buyers, typically financing a portion of the purchase price. Exact terms depend on the bank and your personal financial profile.

What is the difference between off-plan and completed properties?

Off-plan means the development is still under construction and is usually sold with staged payments through to completion. A completed property is ready to view and move into now.

How does the reservation and payment process work?

Reservation and payment structures vary by development and are set out in full before you reserve. Nueva Living reconfirms the current schedule for your chosen residence at every step.

Can I rent out the property after purchase?

This depends on the individual development and local regulations, which can vary by community and municipality. Nueva Living will confirm the specific rules for a development before you reserve.

Do I need a lawyer?

Yes, we strongly recommend independent legal representation for any property purchase in Spain. Nueva Living can put you in touch with independent lawyers experienced in Costa del Sol property.

What is AÍDA, furnished by Bentley Home?

AÍDA is a collection of eight residences on Marbella’s Golden Mile, developed by RGZ Developers. Bentley Home, through Luxury Living Group, furnishes the residences, and the RGZ Architecture studio designed them together with Bentley Home.

Is the Bentley Home furnishing included in the price?

No. The prices on the developer’s price list start at €4,500,000 + VAT and include 2 parking spaces and a storage room. The brochure presents the residences furnished by Bentley Home, but the price list sells the furnishing as a separate, mandatory package valued at €500,000 + VAT and offered at €300,000 + VAT. We confirm the scope in writing before any reservation.

How many residences are available?

The collection has eight residences. Two are on the developer’s current price list, a Skyline Duplex Penthouse at €4,500,000 + VAT and a Garden-Level Duplex at €4,800,000 + VAT; the other six are reserved.

What is the pit lane?

The brochure describes a private pitlane that gives each owner a personal pitbox, with a private bar and a curated lounge. It does not say how the pitbox relates to the parking spaces in the price list, so we confirm that before any reservation.

What are the areas of the residences?

RGZ’s plans print built areas of 257.68 m² inside and 351.09 m² outside for residence 0D, and 121.42 m² inside and 185.58 m² outside for residence 1A, with garage and storage on top (67.94 m² and 91.59 m²). The usable areas are smaller: 217.91 m² and 99.86 m² inside. The price list’s 428 m² matches none of these, so we use the plans.
