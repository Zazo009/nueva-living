The development

The developer’s brochure presents two- and three-bedroom homes in Estepona, between the sea and the hills and very close to golf courses, with large terraces, two swimming pools, a spa, a gym and a coworking area.

The price list of November 2026 names 19 homes in four blocks. It shows 17 with a price and marks 2 as reserved. Eleven of the 17 have two bedrooms and six have three, and each is sold with its parking space or spaces and its storage room. No document we hold gives a completion date, a licence or the stage of the works.

Architecture · Curved terraces, stone, timber and glass

In the developer’s computer-generated images the blocks have long, curved terrace slabs in a pale stone colour, timber soffits and glass balustrades, set on a hillside with stone walls and planting. The brochure describes horizontal lines, balanced volumes and spacious terraces that work as outdoor living areas.

The price list numbers the homes by block and portal, in four blocks with up to three portals each. The brochure says the buildings are laid out to make the most of views and natural light. None of the documents gives the number of homes or of floors in all.

  • Facade: A bespoke facade combining a variety of finishes with continuous cladding in warm tones, with thermal and acoustic insulation that meets the Spanish Building Code.
  • Between homes: Dividing walls of sound-resistant brick, with insulated plasterboard cladding on both sides.
  • Glazing: Thermally broken aluminium frames, fixed, hinged or sliding, with double glazing, blinds in the living room and bedrooms, and glass balustrades.
  • Roofs: Walkable flat-roof terraces on the residences, paved for outdoor use; the other flat roofs are finished in gravel or an equivalent.

Our assessment

What we would weigh before reserving here, and what we check for you.

Why it stands out

  • Who it is forBuyers who want a new two- or three-bedroom apartment in Estepona, with a terrace, a parking space and a storage room, to live in or as a second home.
  • What makes it differentThe brochure sets the homes between the sea and the hills, very close to some of the best golf courses on the coast, with terraces designed as outdoor living areas. The terraces on the price list run from 16.42 to 79.42 sqm.
  • The payment scheduleThe reservation is EUR 10,000 + VAT, 35% + VAT falls due at the private contract and 65% + VAT at the signature of the title deeds.
  • Why check availability2 of the 19 homes on the list are marked reserved, and the list says availability and prices are subject to change without prior notice. We confirm price and availability before any reservation.

What to plan for

  • Availability17 of the 19 homes on the list have a price: eleven two-bedroom apartments from EUR 473,000 to EUR 572,000 and six three-bedroom apartments from EUR 560,000 to EUR 687,000, plus VAT. The other 2 are marked reserved.
  • Tax is not in the priceThe list says 10% VAT is not included. With VAT at 10% and stamp duty at 1.20%, EUR 473,000 becomes about EUR 526,000 and EUR 687,000 about EUR 764,000, before notary and land registry fees.
  • The deed carries most of the price65% + VAT falls due only at the signature of the title deeds, after the reservation and 35% + VAT at the private contract. The list gives no date for either and says nothing about a bank guarantee for payments on account.
  • Prices can moveThe list says availability and prices are subject to change without prior notice. We reconfirm the figure with the seller before you reserve, and it may be different by then.

Checks before any money moves

  • The licence and the worksNone of the documents gives a building licence, a start of works, a stage of construction or a completion date, and the brochure says it is based on the preliminary project. We ask for the licence, the works programme and the contractual completion date in writing.
  • The plansWe hold plans for 5 of the 17 homes: 01-03-00-C, 04-01-00-B, 04-01-02-A, 04-03-00-A and 04-03-01-B. Their interior areas, terraces and built areas match the price list exactly. The other 12 homes have no plan, and we ask for them.
  • The poolsThe brochure counts two swimming pools; the quality specification lists outdoor pools in the plural and a heated pool in the spa. We ask how many pools there are and which are outdoors.
  • Golf and distancesThe brochure puts the site a few minutes’ drive from four golf courses, while its own map draws two of them on the far side of Estepona town. We measure the drive times ourselves.
  • The documentsThe price list is dated November 2026, without a day, and the plans carry the date 16 October 2026. The brochure, the quality specification and the plans all say they are preliminary and not contractual. We use the list for prices and availability and the other documents to describe the homes.

Questions about Estepona West

Are parking and storage included?

Yes, the price list says each price includes the assigned parking space or spaces and the storage room, which it numbers by level, S-1 or S-2. We confirm them in writing for the home you choose.

How does the payment schedule work?

EUR 10,000 + VAT on reservation (EUR 11,000 with VAT), 35% + VAT at the private contract and 65% + VAT at the signature of the title deeds. The list gives no dates.

When will it be finished?

No document gives a completion date, a licence date or the stage of the works, so this page says only that completion is to be confirmed. We ask for the contractual date in writing before any reservation.

Is there a plan for every home?

No. There are plans for 5 of the 17: 01-03-00-C, 04-01-00-B, 04-01-02-A, 04-03-00-A and 04-03-01-B. The other 12 have none, and we ask the developer for them. The plans are preliminary, with approximate areas.

Is furniture included?

No. The brochure says furniture is shown only to illustrate the use of the spaces and is not included. The kitchens come with the appliances listed in the quality specification.
